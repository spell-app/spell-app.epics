Epics: their pages, the review loop, comments, agents, and the skills around them.

Epics grew from a plan doc per session into pages Owen reviews, comments on and sends from, with agents and skills around them.

  • Details Pages: a question that needs a picture becomes a page in the side bar, answered on the page.
  • Epic Review, merged: /epic review walks an epic's open items a section at a time, answers landing in the epic page at once.
  • Review Review, 7 phases of 10: Owen marks items right on the page, and Claude picks the marks up in one batch, or at once in the background.
  • Windows and Review: notes saved as typed, Review Now, Symptom / Changes, and VS Code windows that say which worktree they show.
  • Epic Pages as Components: every epic page drawn by <epic-*> elements from a component pack, so a page holds only its data and prose.
  • Isolate & Epic Changes: /isolate and /epic open their new window at once, never losing what was typed.
  • Airplane Mode, merged: marks, notes and new items on a plane with no Claude, gathered by /airplane land.
  • Skillz: /bg, merged: /bg hands a task to a named background helper, and each epic page shows its running agents.
  • Epic Workflow, a standing epic: requests from any session reach the tooling epic, the epic command, comments with Send on every page, Epic UI, and these saga pages.

Two future epics, and the parked goals' own tooling, wait their turn.

  • Epic Eject, 1 phase of 8: the epic tooling moves to its own repo, a Claude Code plugin every repo and window runs as one copy.
  • Epic Goals, a future epic: goals rebuilt on what epics have now; these saga pages are its foundation. The parked first draft is in the parked goals guide.
  • Epic Workflow's open todos, such as making the wide layout every page's default.
