 1.3's examples first (a minute), then the questions. 1.1, 1.2 and 1.5 are there if you want the why.
  • A colour should mean ONE thing, so you can read a page at a glance. Today most colours mean several.
  • Orange means about nine things on a plan doc:
    • an item in progress, a review choice not sent, your note box, your reply card, a sent note
    • an UPDATE note, the active phase, an agent blocked, "nobody is listening", your pick
    • your note box, an UPDATE note and "Plan changes" are the very same orange box
  • Buttons contradict what they make:
    • Make Todo is a green button, but the item it makes turns orange
    • Do Now is blue in the note box, but the mark it makes shows as an orange Revisit on the line
    • the chosen option is green on an answered question, orange while you pick, violet on a details page
  • Outlined vs solid flips: on review buttons solid means "not sent"; in the note box it means "the current mark, sent or not"; on the Choose pill, outlined means both "not picked" and "picked and sent".
  • One meaning, several colours: Send is blue in the plan doc header, violet on details pages; "needs attention" is red on chips, orange in the contents and the rail.
