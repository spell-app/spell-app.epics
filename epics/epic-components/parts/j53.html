The rebuilt Agents running panel (AgentsPanel.tsx) takes every word it shows from <epic-page>'s vocabulary: eight new texts (agents, agentStarted, agentYou, agentTold, agentWaiting, agentNote, agentNoteLabel, agentSend), as every other string in the pack is. No new attribute, part or state: the panel is styled by its own sheet (agents-panel.css), adopted by the page.

Why: the pack localizes like a Spell UI family; an inline string would be the only one. A part (::part(agents)) would let a page restyle it, but nothing needs that yet.
