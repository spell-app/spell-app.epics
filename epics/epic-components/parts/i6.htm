Fixed at the source, SourceBody in Spell UI, so every element that loads a part gets it: items, sections, phases, and <ui-section source> / <ui-accordion source> too.

  • The cause: a body is held hidden while it's on its way (veiled()), and a re-fetch counted as "on its way" again, so an open item hid its details, note box included, until the new part arrived.
  • Now only the FIRST load is held. Once a body has been shown, a re-fetch keeps the old one on screen and swaps the new one in, in place, in one step: nothing is hidden in between.
  • The note box lives in the item's shadow root, so the swap never touches it: text, focus and caret stay as they were.

Evidence:

  • packages/epics/demo/check-live-epics.mjs now also watches for a blink, from the moment the note is typed: every change to the item's shadow root (a MutationObserver, so even a sub-frame flash counts) and every animation frame, flagging "details hidden", "note box gone" or "note box unfocused".
  • Before the fix it failed on the part edit: blinked: details hidden (on a change). After: exit 0, all three edits landed, no blink.
  • A new ui-section test, "reload() keeps the open body shown until the new one replaces it", fails without the fix and passes with it; ui-section, ui-accordion and elements tests all pass (437).

Net effect:

  • A part edited while you read the item updates under your eyes, with no flicker; a half-typed note keeps its text, focus and caret.
  • Files: packages/ui/src/elements/SourceBody.ts, its test in ui-section.test.tsx, the check script; the docs bundle (spell-ui.js) rebuilt so pages get it.
