Chose to draw each folding block (epic-overview, epic-section, epic-phase) as a ui-section (sticky, collapsible, chevron at the end) inside its shadow root, its children slotted in, over writing a new fold and sticky stack, because ui-section already does the hard parts, and its owner lookup climbs the flat tree, so nested titles stack across the shadow roots.

  • the element cancels the inner section's ui-open / ui-close and fires its own, so the page sees one event per block
  • a top-level title sticks below the site header plus the page header (measured by epic-page); every block hands its children --epic-stack, the bottom of the stuck titles, which epic-item's sticky line reads
  • fold state is an open attribute, page state never written in a doc, as epic-item's

Net effect:

  • chevron at the title's far end, up when open, down when folded, as today
  • a link to a block, into it, or to an id in its part-ids opens every block around it, loads the part and lands below the stuck titles (it follows the target while folds animate open)
