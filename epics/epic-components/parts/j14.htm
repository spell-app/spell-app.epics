Chose <p slot="summary"> and <blockquote slot="prompt"> inside <epic-overview>, and estimate="4h-5h in all, 2h left" on it.

  • summaries can't be attributes: many hold <code> or links; the prompt is several paragraphs
  • slots, not new elements (<epic-summary>): the element draws the folded Kickoff prompt around its slot, and there are two fewer tags
  • the estimate stays a line the script adds up and writes, as today, rather than the browser adding up the phases' estimates

Net effect:

  • <epic-overview id="overview" estimate="..."><p slot="summary">...</p><blockquote slot="prompt">...</blockquote> <epic-section kind="overview-part" ...>
