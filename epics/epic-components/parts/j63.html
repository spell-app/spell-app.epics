A details page's Send (the paper plane in its sticky header) is now blue, as Send is everywhere, and drawn by the fill rule:

  • nothing on the page yet: a grey outline (available); the line says "Not sent"; the status label "waiting for your answer" is RED (needs you)
  • something picked or typed and not sent, or changed since the send: DASHED blue; the line "Not sent" / "Changes not sent" in blue; the label "changes not sent" blue
  • sent, and the page as sent: a blue OUTLINE (recorded, Claude's to act on); the line "Sent 10/8/26 14:34 · 5 of 17 decided" green; the label "answered" green
  • never SOLID: the page can't tell when Claude is done with the answer

A basic ui-button draws its edge as an inset shadow, which can't dash: the dashed state is an outline through ::part(button) (details.css).
