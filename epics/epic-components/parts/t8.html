Elements carry their title as title="..." (Owen's sketch). The browser shows an element's title as a tooltip over everything inside it, slotted prose included, so hovering anywhere in an open item might show its title.

  • check it in Drawing Elements (P5), by hand: tooltips can't be tested headless
  • if it shows: try title="" on the element's shadow wrapper, since an empty title stops the tooltip
  • else rename the attribute through the converter: header, as the section says
