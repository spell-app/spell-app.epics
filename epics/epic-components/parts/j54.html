The old runtime kept the reader's place with keepAnchor() on the first section. The pack had no such helper, so the panel does it itself (AgentsPanel follow()): when the client changes while the panel's bottom edge is above the reading line (the reader is below it), it measures that edge, lets Solid draw (a microtask after the write), and scrolls by how far the edge moved.

Why: the panel sits in the page's shadow root right before its blocks, so its bottom edge moves exactly as far as everything below it. Measured, not assumed: a browser that anchors the scroll itself leaves nothing to make up. At the top of the page nothing scrolls: the panel pushes the page down, in view. Pinned by a browser test (within a pixel).
