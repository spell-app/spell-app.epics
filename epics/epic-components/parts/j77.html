A question's text as asked is its leading prose: everything before its Net effect, its options (<epic-choices>, a grid, The options:) or its first card. Code, asides and notes inside it stay in it. The same rule runs in each version of its Original Discussion (4 in skillz).

Not on a question born answered (answered, with no choices and no answer card): its title IS the decision and its text the why, so a "Question" label over it would be wrong. That leaves 167 questions wrapped of 634 (394 have no text before their answer card).
