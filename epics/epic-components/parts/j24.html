Chose to make elements only of what the script writes, and keep everything hand-written as the prose it is, over converting every look-alike.

  • an option grid (ui-grid.spell-pros-cons) straight in a QUESTION's details becomes <epic-choices>; one in a judgement call, issue, reply or answer is options weighed in an explanation, often mid-text between headings (outline-spell J3, J8): it stays a ui-grid
  • only the script's UPDATE note (header="UPDATE" with data-phase) becomes <epic-update phase>; hand-written ones (DONE · option A, 2026-10-06, DEFERRED, an UPDATE with no phase) keep their header and stay ui-messages
  • an UPDATE label on an item's line goes into a slot="title" span with the title, so it still shows on the line without loading the body (shared-content, 5)
  • option titles with no letter (markdown's Choices: New package, Inside parser) get their position's letter, A, B ...

Net effect:

  • no hand-written header or note text is lost to a drawn label
  • judgement calls' grids won't get Choose pills when P8 draws them for questions

What Owen sees for a kept grid

  • Plain cards side by side, each with its label as written, colours included (J1's green "chosen").
  • No Choose pill on any card while the page is reviewed.
  • No folding under a Choices button: the grid stays open in the middle of the text.

The alternative: make every grid an <epic-choices>

  • The content model would have to change. Today an item may hold ONE <epic-choices>, right after its text, and never inside prose or a reply. Q17 would need two inside replies, and J2 of wwod-spell-ui one in its text and one in a reply.
  • Choose pills would show on options that are an explanation, not a question. A pick on J1 would go to the review inbox for an item that decide can't answer.
  • Hand-written labels would be lost: "(chosen)" and the green and blue label colours have no place in <epic-option>.

Net effect:

  • 20 grids in 5 docs keep their old markup and look as they always have
  • 13 question grids (7 in claude-design, 5 in wwod-spell-ui, 1 in review-review) become <epic-choices>
  • a question's grid inside a reply stays a grid, with no Choose pill (Q17, and both Q4s)
