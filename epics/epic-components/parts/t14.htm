  • A (recommended): the plan-doc tool writes the box, as updatePlanChanges() did: a copy of each <epic-updated> of a phase still to do, as a slot="changes" child of the Phases section (<epic-updated slot="changes" phase="3" at="...">), rewritten on every edit. <epic-section kind="phases"> gets a changes slot, drawn as a "Plan changes" box above its phases (nothing without one); the converter writes the same copies instead of dropping the box.
  • B: <epic-section kind="phases"> fetches the part of each phase still to do that has updates (a marker attribute on <epic-phase>, e.g. updates="2", written by the tool) and lists their lines: no copies in the doc, but a fetch per such phase on every load, and nothing from file://.
