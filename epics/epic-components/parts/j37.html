The review client marks <epic-page reviewing> (declared in its vocabulary as page state) once the page is served with a token and its inbox answers: the heir of body.plan-reviewing. The review routes accept only epics/<name>/<name>.plan.html paths, so preview-epics/ copies are refused (403), and check-review.js can't target a copy (it reads the shared epics/ and the old markup). So a new check, packages/epics/demo/check-review-epics.mjs, copies the sample or a preview doc to demo/shots/epics/<name>/ (git-ignored), starts a page server of its own (no pid file) and clicks through every flow.
