The elements' content models are strict (an item reads text, Choices, answer, More Details, replies, Original Discussion, commits; a phase only takes its named fields). Old docs put text where those don't allow it. Chose to MOVE it, never drop it, and to say so in the run's notes, over refusing those docs (which would leave 12 of 26 unconvertible).

  • text after a question's option cards (its Net effect) moves up before the options, as decide already lays an answered question out (claude-design Q1-Q7, wwod-spell-ui Q22-Q26, review-review Q17, the fixtures)
  • text after a reply joins the end of that reply (outline-spell J9, J11, output-targets T1, J10): the reading order stays exactly as it was
  • a phase field the vocabulary has no name for (Judgement calls: on about 50 phases, Outcome:, The snapshot diff to look at:) is kept whole, label and all, at the end of the phase's Done, else its Goal
  • an answer card whose title has markup (4 in details: <code>, a link) can't be an attribute and <epic-answer> has no title slot: the title leads the answer as a bold paragraph
  • a reply's title line becomes from / at / re; markup in re (a few <code>s) is kept as its text
  • claude-design's answer-card-first layout: the answer moves after the question and its choices

Net effect:

  • every doc converts, with no word lost (the proof checks it per item)
  • about 60 items read in a slightly different order: convert --verbose lists each as a note, the proof lists them as reordered
