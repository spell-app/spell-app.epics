  • Why people chose axios: mostly to smooth over browsers and node. Node 24 and every browser Spell targets have fetch, so that reason has gone.
  • Where the size lands: in every Spell app that uses a place, plus the runtime dependency you'd be approving.
  • Trust: its npm account was hijacked in March 2026, and two releases carried a remote-access trojan through an added dependency. The maintainers recovered, and 1.21.0 doesn't list that dependency, but it's a real cost for a package every app would ship. InfoQ's report, Vercel's notice.
  • What's fair in your worry: home-grown code is ours to keep working. Here it's 170 lines, already in use in Spell UI, with a thin base under them.
