Owen asked for a design pass over the server examples ("they are waaay too crowded, especially when full screen"). This piece fixed what Spell UI and core drew wrong, for every page, not only this example. Commit 1472bc1b6.

  • A paged list draws its pager (Previous, Page 1, Next) AFTER its cards. Before, the pager was one more card in the grid: a fifth slot on Open Library, a card floating in the Met mosaic, two pixels past the left edge on GitHub.
  • A mosaic no longer overlaps what follows it. Before, the credits under the Met mosaic sat on top of its last row.
  • A row of tabs scrolls with arrows, and keeps the chosen tab in view on a phone. Before, on a phone the chosen tab could be scrolled out of sight, with no arrows. The toolbar already scrolled like this: that scrolling is now shared by both, as ScrollingRow.
  • A flip card is as tall as its tallest side. Before, turning one changed its height by about 37 pixels, and the mosaic around it jumped.
  • A bar of tabs leaves a 1em gap under it, before the page it shows.
  • A link that is only an icon sits 0.35em from the word before it, and never wraps onto a line by itself. This closes I86 (GitHub icon pressed against the login).
  • every page with a paged list, a mosaic, tabs, flip cards or icon links draws more cleanly, not only Public Servers
  • I86 is done
