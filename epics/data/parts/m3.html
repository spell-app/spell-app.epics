• An AT Protocol account you don't mind being public. A throwaway one is best: at bsky.app, Create account, gives you a handle like someone.bsky.social. Nothing here has used your real one.
  • Everything you save is public, on your account, until you remove it. It goes under the collection app.spell.demo.recipes, and the sign-in page will ask to let this page change your records.
  • Two browsers on this Mac, Chrome and Safari. Each has its own sign-in.
  1. In VS Code, in window ⎇ data, menu Terminal, New Terminal, run: 
    spell server start
spell server url
    The last line it prints is an address starting http://127.0.0.1:: this server's. It has to be 127.0.0.1, not localhost.
  2. In Chrome, open that address followed by /guides/data/experiments/recipe-box/recipe-box-account.html. It shows "You aren't signed in", with a field and Sign in, and no recipes.
  3. Type your handle, press Sign in. Chrome goes to your account's host. Sign in there, and press to allow it.
  4. Add a recipe called M3 soup.
  5. Back in the terminal, with your handle in place of HANDLE: 
    curl "https://bsky.social/xrpc/com.atproto.repo.listRecords?repo=HANDLE&collection=app.spell.demo.recipes"
  6. In Safari, open the same address, and sign in as the same person. Change the title to M3 stew.
  7. Look at Chrome. In Chrome, press Sign out, then reload the page, and sign in again.
  8. Press the ✕ on the recipe, so nothing's left on your account, and run the curl again.
  • Signing in comes back to the same page, saying "Signed in as" your handle, with Sign out beside it.
  • The first curl prints your recipe's record, with its title, M3 soup.
  • Safari's change shows in Chrome within a few seconds, with no reload.
  • After signing out, Chrome says "You aren't signed in", and the add box goes.
  • The last curl prints {"records":[]}.
