core.dateShown() uses timeZone: "UTC"; the hour of is getHours(), local. Open-Meteo's times have no offset, so they're read as local: west of UTC, a late hour may show the next day's date.

the reader's own: a date drawn on a page (`dateShown()`) shows its day where the reader is, as `the hour of` and `the day of` already read it; text with no time zone reads in the reader's time, a day alone (`"1977-05-25"`) as that day's midnight there, not UTC's
