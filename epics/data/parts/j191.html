Chose - forecasts are asked for by their latitude and longitude (es - los pronósticos se piden por su latitud y longitud), read as every forecast where its latitude is ... and its longitude is ..., over a new read form (the forecast at ...). It reuses the filter a where already hands a place: RestPlace sends it as the query and takes it as done, so a forecast for the nearest place (52.5126) still counts as 52.52's. A read that doesn't say each is refused at compile time, as a search-only read is (P29).
