Needs a personal Google account (a work one's admin can block step 3's key). Google Cloud is free for this: no billing account, up to 60 reads and 60 writes a minute.

  1. In a browser, open Google Cloud's new project page and create a project named spell-recipes.
  2. Open the Sheets API's page, with that project picked at the top, and press Enable.
  3. Open Service accounts and press Create service account. Name it spell-recipes, skip the roles, press Done. Click its row, then Keys, Add key, Create new key, JSON. A file lands in your Downloads: it's the key, so keep it out of every repo.
  4. Open a new sheet. Rename the tab at the bottom to recipes. In row 1 type these, a cell each: id, title, cuisine, servings, notes. In row 2 type r-1, Soup, Italian, 4, Roast it.
  5. In a VS Code terminal in the ⎇ data window, print the account's address, with your file's name: 
    node -p 'JSON.parse(require("fs").readFileSync(process.argv[1])).client_email' ~/Downloads/<the-key-file>.json
  6. In the sheet press Share, paste that address, pick Editor, and press Send.
  7. Copy the sheet's id: in its address, what's between /d/ and /edit.
  8. In the same terminal, at the worktree's root, with your file's name and that id, start the servers, and leave them running. It should end by saying your Google Sheet is on /api/places/google-sheet. 
    export GOOGLE_SHEETS_KEY="$(cat ~/Downloads/<the-key-file>.json)"
export GOOGLE_SHEET_ID="<the sheet's id>"
yarn tsx scripts/recipe-servers.ts
  9. In a browser window beside the sheet, open: 
    http://127.0.0.1:4747/worktrees/data/guides/data/experiments/recipe-box/recipe-box.html?back-end=google-sheet
  10. On the page, change Soup's title, and wait for "saved".
  11. In the sheet, add a row: r-2, Dal, Indian, 6, Temper last. Wait 5 seconds.
  12. On the page, type a new recipe's title and press Add recipe. Press Remove on Dal.
  13. Now with no key at all: in the sheet press Share, set General access to "Anyone with the link", Viewer, then open this, with the id, in a browser: 
    http://127.0.0.1:4747/worktrees/data/guides/data/experiments/recipe-box/recipe-box.html?back-end=sheet&sheet=https%3A%2F%2Fdocs.google.com%2Fspreadsheets%2Fd%2F<the sheet's id>%2Fexport%3Fformat%3Dcsv%26gid%3D0
  14. In the sheet change a title, wait 5 seconds, then take General access back to Restricted. Press Ctrl-C in the terminal.
  • Step 9: the page says "Kept in a Google Sheet", with Soup in a box you can type in. Nothing in the page's source or its network answers holds the key or a token.
  • Step 10: the sheet's cell changes within a second or two, its formatting kept.
  • Step 11: Dal shows on the page.
  • Step 12: a new row appears at the bottom of the sheet, with an id starting r-, and Dal's row is gone.
  • Step 13: the same recipes, as text, marked Read only, with nothing to type in or press. It reached Google with no key and no server of ours.
  • Step 14: the page follows. Google's CSV address may lag its sheet a little, so give it up to a minute.

Tell Claude which step, and what the page or the terminal said. "share it with ..." means step 6 wasn't done for that address; a notice saying no key is set means step 8's exports were in another terminal.

The new row shows up in the demo, and your change shows up in the sheet.
