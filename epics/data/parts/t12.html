From Owen's question, 2026-10-31: how close are chunked files to AT Protocol, and should we switch? No switch, but a later phase borrows three ideas.

  • The address shape: who, collection, key, so moving data between the two stays a one-line change.
  • Keys made by the writer, which match the browser-made ids in the multiplayer model.
  • A write that says "only if it's still the version I read", like AT Protocol's swapRecord. When that check fails isn't fully confirmed yet.

Why not switch: everything on AT Protocol is public today, each account has one writer, it needs a server, and its fields can't be renamed.
