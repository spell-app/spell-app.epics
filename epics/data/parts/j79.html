A drawn view's element reads every declared property, to mirror it as an attribute for the inspector (SpellElement.shownValueOf()). In the prototype, 16 of a first draw's 149 recorded reads were only that, and it would have made every selection on a view fail at once.

Those reads now go through whileOnlyShowing(): they still follow the value, and don't count as needing it. An attribute shows its default until the field arrives. CONTEXT_VERSION went to 2 for the new field.
