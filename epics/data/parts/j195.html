hourly_units sets each hour field's unit and is left out: its "temperature" is a temperature in °C, stored as "temperature_2m", a length in mm, a speed in km/h, by Spell's own dimension table. A unit Spell has no dimension for is a number with // in %. A measured field drops a trailing 2m / 10m (how high it's measured) when no other field has the same words.
