The kinds places already tell apart, with the words a step would match:

Words Spanish What places throw today
"not found" "no encontrado" a place's not found, a 404
"not signed in" "sin sesión" a 401
"not allowed" "no permitido" a 403, or a place's refused write
"offline" "sin conexión" no network
"out of date" "desactualizado" someone changed it first, a 412
"cancelled" "cancelado" a cancel, if Q71 sends it here
"failed" "falló" anything else
  • Ambiguity: his "not authorized" covers two fixes, signing in (401) and asking for access (403). His example shows the login dialog, which only helps the first.
  • A: the words above, with "not authorized" also matching both. B: his words only, "not authorized" for both. C: the server's numbers, if the error is 404.
