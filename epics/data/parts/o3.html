Where these collide

  • every recipe: it's a scratch list today, of what the program made. For a saved type it would mean those in the place: a real change of meaning, though no existing program has a saved type.
  • when someone else changes it: it could parse as a property called "someone else". It needs a higher priority, and a test.
  • belongs to one: it refuses an owner that isn't a list today, and will accept one.
  • send: it keeps meaning events. A named operation on a place reads as an action, not as send.
  • source: taken by Spell UI's sources and by source code, so a "data source" would collide.
  • store: taken by several classes and the Solid store, and tienda is a shop.
  • record: taken by the undo history and the reactive engine. Data should be plain things.
  • sync: used all over, mostly for forms and the DOM. Avoid it.
  • find, load, fetch: people name their own actions with these (load the scores), so they must not be reserved.
  • Helpful errors: save, fetch, query, connect to, database and join should each get a message naming what to write.
  • "And" for "or": where its cook is "Ana" and its cook is "Bo" can never be true, so Spell should say so, and offer its cook is "Ana" or "Bo", which already works.
