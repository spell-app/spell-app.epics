What reads today: a draft (J24), from epic data's P8 and P15.

  • a draft of the recipe: a scratch copy for the form to change; the real recipe doesn't change while you type.
  • save the draft: every changed field goes out as one group, one Undo.
  • discard the draft: Cancel forgets the copy.
  • A form bound to the recipe itself is live instead: each field is kept as it's typed (RecipeBox's).
  • Spell UI's own form can hold edits too, <ui-form apply="on save">, with save() and cancel(), but Spell's programs don't use it: a draft is what reaches the place as one group.
