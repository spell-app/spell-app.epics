I106's details said a name holding another type's name trips Spell. Traced: Spell reads at most four words as a type's name (SpellType.MAX_NAME_WORDS); 'red green blue yellow pink' fails the same way with no other type, and 'front port rear port' worked only because it is four. So inside types now try names in order -- schema's, field's, owner + field, + entry -- and take the first of at most four words that isn't taken. A collection's own type name is not capped yet: a list path of five words (virtual-circuit-termination-role-sets) is still left out whole; none of NetBox's 143 is.
