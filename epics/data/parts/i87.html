A <ui-toolbar> draws a menu inside it, and that menu keeps its own 1em margin above and below. Found in the Public Servers design pass.

  • Measured: the try-chips toolbars are 68 pixels tall, where 40 was meant.
  • So the gap from the chips to the results is only 1.3 to 1.7 times the gap from the title to the chips, not the 2 times the header asks for.

Net effect (once fixed):

  • every toolbar 28 pixels shorter, and the Public Servers headers spaced as designed
