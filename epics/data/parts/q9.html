When a form is bound to a saved thing, do its changes go out as people type, or when they press Save? Spell's forms write straight into the thing today, in memory. 

<ui-form value={it}>                    // live
<ui-form value={a draft of the recipe}>  // held until "save the draft"

As Notion, Airtable and HyperCard do, and as in-memory programs do today.

A draft is for an Edit screen with a Cancel button. Others see keystrokes, unless Spell batches them.

Familiar, but every form needs a Save, so the back-end shows through, and local code stops looking like remote code.

  • Existing forms need no change to work with saved things.
  • a draft of the recipe is a new phrase, kept apart from a draft property.
