Chose keeping an ingredient INSIDE its recipe's row, in the recipe's list of them, over a collection of its own with a reference back, because that's what Saver already does by default, it's one change on every kind of place, and removing a recipe then removes its ingredients and undo restores them with no second write. the recipe of an ingredient is who holds it, not a stored field. Cost: you can't query ingredients by themselves from a place, and an owner must declare its list, which it's told if it doesn't.
