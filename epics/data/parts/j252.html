The lexicon reader (spellTypeFor(), spellKindOf(), spellLineOf() in the core's lexicon.ts) already reads a JSON-Schema-like shape into Spell: required, enums as one of, lengths, a datetime as a date, lists. But it writes Spell text by hand, and the ingest's spellPartsFor() can't say required, choices or lengths.

  • The one: a schema node read into the ingest's field description, which gains required, choices and lengths (and date-time and uuid formats); spellPartsFor() writes them in the lexicon's wording; then the lexicon reader moves onto it, one writer of type lines (about 80 lines out of lexicon.ts).
  • Watch: $ref is Spell's own reference key in saved data (REF_KEY, the core's saving.ts), so a schema's pointer is kept under another name.
