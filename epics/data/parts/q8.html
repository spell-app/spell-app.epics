An action may change something, wait on a read, then change something else.

Once a write is sent, the only way back is a reversing change (1.6). Holding writes back is what makes cancelling real, at a cost. 

- to move the recipe:
	set the shelf of the recipe to the new shelf    // shows at once
	set the note to the lookup of the shelf         // waits on a read
	set the moved of the recipe to yes

Cancelling really cancels, and all of an action's writes arrive together. The cost: a long action reaches other people late.

Others see each step at once, but a cancelled action has to send reversals, and others may see a half-finished state.

  • Moving away during an unfinished action drops its writes, since none has left.
  • A long action's changes arrive at other people all together, when it ends.
