His answer to Q45 was C, the states only in .api.json5. This form puts unverified in the Spell. 

	job sessions live at "/api/job-session" disabled, unverified     <- every state
	job sessions live at "/api/job-session" disabled                 <- Q45 kept:  disabled only
  • Every state: disabled, unverified, verified, failed, untyped. spell place verify keeps them in step with .api.json5, rewriting your line each run, but never touching disabled. Ambiguity: failed may read as failing now, and a word you wrote by hand is replaced on the next verify.
  • Only disabled: Q45 stands, and the line holds only what a person decides, never what a tool found.
