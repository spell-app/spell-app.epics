  • Rows into things, under node: 12 microseconds and 5.25 KB a thing, the same at every size; 50,000 takes 0.6 s. Reading again: 5 microseconds a row. A filter and sort here over 50,000: 0.5 s.
Drawn in headless Chromium, every row 1,000 5,000 50,000
a view, its own element: until all drawn 0.34 s 1.7 s 119 s
a view: page's memory 103 MB 402 MB 3.7 GB
a plain thing: until all drawn 0.23 s 1.0 s 77 s
a plain thing: page's memory 65 MB 237 MB 2.3 GB

So 5,000 rows are fine. 50,000 reads in under a second and can't be drawn whole: per row it's about 7 times slower than at 5,000, though DEFAULT_CEILING allows it. The limit is drawing, not things or cells; a list that draws only what shows would be the fix. A waiting value lives as long as its read: one SavedRead per type and query, for the run. One machine, one run each.

Nobody has measured the cost per thing at thousands of rows, nor how long each waiting value lives.
