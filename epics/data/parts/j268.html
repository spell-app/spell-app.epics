P49 step 1. NetBox nests BriefTenantGroup / NestedTag objects. When a nested object's schema is Brief<X> or Nested<X>, a collection's item schema is X, and it carries a url, the field becomes a named link to that collection (its "group" is a tenant group), as PokéAPI's {name, url} are, instead of an inside type. Without it 30 of NetBox's 143 lists were left out (inside type names like 'console port module module bay' don't parse); with it, 5.
