  • Trust, then verify: every collection, field and write carries a state (unverified, untyped, verified with date and server, failed with status and reason, disabled), kept only in .api.json5: the Spell doesn't say it, but a type says whether it was verified on its own lines: this type is verified or this type is failed, no line meaning OK. spell place verify <file>, its states verified and failed; "check" stays the word for asking what changed. It reads each list and each thing by its id, fills an untyped collection's fields from what it reads, and never writes unless asked.
  • Writes come from the spec, every operation enabled by default; a thing's type narrows them with enabled operations are add, change, or says all, on the thing, never on the web server's lines; action routes with ids, to complete a job is at "/jobs/{id}/complete", its braces filled from the thing, its method from the description; request bodies typed from their schemas.
