Owen, 2026-10-31: "where its cook is them = where they are the cook". Both forms, one meaning: recipes where they are the cook.

Inside a where, they is the person using the program, and the cook is the thing's own field, not a program's cook.

`recipes where they are the cook` compiles to just what `recipes where its cook is them` does, `the cook` being the thing's own, and a field it hasn't got says so
