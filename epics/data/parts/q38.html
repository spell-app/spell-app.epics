Owen's comment on the guide's "Where the code is" (C22, "Places, the cache, the copy on this device, writes, healing"): "this is worth its own top-level folder next to util". Whether the browser half of places becomes a folder of the kit's own src/, named spell/places.

Earlier: J5 chose a folder of util over a new kit folder, because a new one means more exports, TypeScript settings and test projects "for no gain". The census below is that cost.

  • Its tests, tests/util/places/: 34 files, to tests/places/.
  • util's AGENTS.md section "Where data lives" becomes the new folder's own AGENTS.md (and its CLAUDE.md).
