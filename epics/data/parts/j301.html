I141: Spanish's inflector now keeps a borrowed plural (an -s after a consonant Spanish words don't end in: jobs, steps) as it is, and makes a later word plural only where the first word changed, mirroring how it makes a name singular. So 'jobs archive' stays 'los jobs archive' (compiles), not 'los jobses archives' (didn't). 'vals' still becomes 'valses'; 'step' still 'stepes'.
