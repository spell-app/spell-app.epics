The survey's model (Figma) numbers first and writes later, with a journal. I chose to write first, one batch at a time, over a journal: a store that refuses is then a plain refusal, one that fails is no answer and resends with its changeId, and there's no window where the room holds what the store won't. The cost is that a slow store, Airtable at 5 requests a second, slows the room. A batch is one step with one number, all or none, as an action's changes have to be.
