A page should fetch what it needs in one trip.

The compiler can see most reads, while the running page sees all of them, but later (1.5). Neither has been tried on Spell.

One trip where the compiler can prove it, always right otherwise. It's two mechanisms, so it starts with a prototype over the built-in projects to measure what the static pass finds.

One mechanism and no surprises at run time, but a read it can't see needs widening to the whole thing, or a miss.

No compiler work and always right, but a first load goes one missing field at a time.

  • What it finds decides how much the run-time half matters.
