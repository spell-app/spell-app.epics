From I81 (issue), marked "Make Todo" on the page: A draft kept in a property can't be saved or discarded

An Edit screen with separate Save and Cancel buttons can't be written in Spell today: a draft only works while it sits in a local name, inside one action. Checked again on 2026-11-08, on the data branch: still broken.

  • A draft is a scratch copy of a saved thing: the form changes the copy, and nothing goes out until it's saved.
  • Save and Cancel are two buttons, so two actions. The draft has to live between them, in the view's own property.
  • That's exactly the case that fails.

What a person writes

a recipe editor is a view: - its "recipe" is a recipe - its "draft" is a draft of its recipe - to keep it: save its draft - to drop it: discard its draft
  • Today:
    • save its draft is refused: "its draft" isn't a draft: a change is kept as it's made, with no save step.
    • discard its draft is Don't understand "discard its draft".
    • The property also starts empty: is a draft of its recipe makes no draft when the view opens.
  • Should: both lines compile, as they already do for a local name, and the property holds a fresh draft of the recipe.
    • Save sends every changed field as one group, one Undo.
    • Cancel forgets the copy; the real recipe never changed.
  • What works today, in one action only: set d to a draft of the recipe, then save d or discard d.

Why it happens

  • save and discard only accept something whose type is "a draft".
  • A local name gets that type from a draft of ....
  • A property line doesn't: its "draft" is a draft of its recipe reads like its "suit" is a suit of its deck, so "draft" becomes a type named Draft that doesn't exist.
    • So the property's type is Draft, where the rules look for the draft type.
    • save then says "no save step"; discard has no such message, so it's "Don't understand".
  • A trap beside it: name the actions save and cancel, as a form's buttons would be, and save its draft is read as the view's own save action, with no error at all.

Why it matters

  • The whole-form question (Q36) was answered B: no new words, a form over a draft kept in a property. That answer only works once this is fixed.
  • Showing it in the guide and in RecipeBox's Edit screen (T134) waits on this.

The fix, roughly

  • Teach the property line is a draft of {thing}: the property gets the draft type, and starts as a fresh draft of that thing.
  • Then save its draft and discard its draft compile as they do for a local name.
  • The same in Spanish, with its word for a draft, borrador.
  • Make the action-name trap say so, or prefer the draft rules over an action of the same name.
  • Size: small to medium, a day or less. Two rules and the property line, plus tests in English and Spanish.
  • a view can keep a draft in a property, made when the view opens
  • save its draft and discard its draft work from separate Save and Cancel actions
  • an action named save or cancel no longer silently swallows those lines
  • Q36's answer (B) can be shown, and T134 can start
