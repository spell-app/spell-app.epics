When a read fails, core draws its own message, always in red.

  • A rate limit isn't something the reader did wrong: "try again in a minute" should be quiet.
  • Today each example must write its own <failed> body to say that (Public Servers does, for GitHub).

Net effect (once fixed):

  • a rate-limit or "try later" failure reads quietly by default, with no <failed> body in each example
