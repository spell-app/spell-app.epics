  • <ui-tabs parameter="server"> and any form control, <ui-search parameter="s"> among them, mirror their value into the address: ?server=met&s=van+gogh.
  • A tab click or a settled search is a new history entry, so Back steps through them.
  • Opening that address puts them back.
  • One shared mechanism for every control, not one per tag.
