  2. T73: where a key goes
    • Any header's name: - its key goes in its "X-Api-Key" header, Spanish - su clave va en su cabecera "X-Api-Key".
    • Or the address, Google's ?key= and the like: - its key goes in the address as "api_key", Spanish - su clave va en la dirección como "api_key".
    • With neither, it goes as Authorization: Bearer, as now.
    • A running program: WebPlace already takes how to send a key, for headers only, so it widens to change the address too.
    • The ingest: PoliteReader sends the key the same way; spell place ingest takes --key-header and --key-in-address, and writes the line.
    • A key in an address must not show up in a message: errors and logs print the address with its key hidden.
    • Checked on a stand-in server for each form, English and Spanish.
