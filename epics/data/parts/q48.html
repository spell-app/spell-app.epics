For P49 · OpenAPI Ingest. The API has about 67 of these. to X is at sends POST only, and fills no braces. A list under a thing already fills {id} from the thing (a post's comments live at "/posts/{id}/comments"). 

	to complete a job is at "/api/jobs/{id}/complete"
	to cancel a job is a DELETE at "/api/jobs/{id}/run"
  • The ambiguity: which field fills the braces. {id} is the thing's id; a name like {job_id} could be its id, or a field of that name.
  • And the method: POST unless a line says so, or the method from the description.
  • Recommended: the same line, the braces filled from the thing sent (its id for {id} and {<type>_id}, else the field by that name), the method from the description unless it's POST.
