P1's swap() only changes where the next read goes, and its test pins that. A sample needs more: pending changes held apart, rows out of the cache, every reader reading again. So Places.useInstead() and backToDeclared() do that, and swap() stays. PendingChanges.setAside() holds on to its own place, because it finds its place by name and a sample's changes must never find the real one.

  • Two ways to change what's behind a name, each named in the coherence catalog.
