1. In VS Code, open a terminal at the data worktree (window ⎇ data, menu Terminal, New Terminal) and start the servers, leaving them running: 
    yarn tsx scripts/recipe-servers.ts
  2. In Chrome, open this address in two windows side by side (File, New Window for the second): 
    http://127.0.0.1:4747/worktrees/data/guides/data/experiments/recipe-box/recipe-box.html?back-end=room
    Each says "Connected to the room". If they say they can't reach the servers, step 1 is not running.
  3. Pick Tomato soup. Type a title in it in the left window, then at once a different title in the right window, and stop.
  4. Paste this into its Notes in the left window, and at once a different long sentence in the right window: 
    Roast the tomatoes slowly until the edges start to catch and they smell sweet.
  5. In the left window press Add recipe with a title typed, and in the right window the same with another title.
  6. Press One more serving on one recipe in both windows, quickly. Press Up on Margherita in both, then Down on Dal in the left and Up on Dal in the right.
  7. Type in Dal's title in the right window and, while you are, press Remove on Dal in the left window.
  8. In the left window press Go offline. Change Tacos al pastor's title there. In the right window change its Notes. Wait 5 seconds, then press Back online in the left window.
  9. Stop the servers: Ctrl-C in the terminal. Change a title in the left window. Wait 5 seconds, then start them again with the same command as step 1.
  • Step 3: each window may flicker for up to a second or two, then both show one title, whole, never a blend.
  • Step 4: both show one sentence, and the window that lost has a notice holding its own sentence, with Use mine. Pressing Use mine puts it back in both.
  • Step 5: both new recipes are in both windows.
  • Step 6: both additions count (up by 2), and every recipe is there exactly once, in the same order in both, even if nobody chose that order.
  • Step 7: Dal goes from both. The right window has a notice, with Restore. Pressing it brings Dal back in both, with what you typed.
  • Step 8: while offline the left window says "Not connected" and shows your change. Back online, both show both changes within about a second.
  • Step 9: both say "Not connected" while the servers are down, and your change stays on the page. Once they are back, both reconnect by themselves and show it within a few seconds.
  • How it feels is yours to judge: "wonky for a second or two, then healed" is the standard. Measured headless: about half a second for anything typed, nearly all of it the half second a form waits for typing to stop.
