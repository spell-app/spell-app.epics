For P50 · Server Actions. Owen: "WHAT DO WE DO IF A CALL HAS MORE THAN ONE PARAMETER?"

Spell's actions already take several blanks: to put a card on a pile, called put the card on the pile. A server action can take its other parameters the same way. 

to move a job to a queue is at "/api/jobs/{id}/move/{queue}"
move the job to the night queue
move job 7151 to the night queue

para mover un trabajo a una cola está en "/api/jobs/{id}/move/{queue}"
mover el trabajo a la cola nocturna
 
to complete a job is at "/api/jobs/{id}/complete"
	- reason is text
complete the job with reason "done early"
completar el trabajo con razón "terminado antes"
 
to star a repository is at "/user/starred/{owner}/{repo}"
star the repository          <- {owner} and {repo} from the repository's own fields
  • These aren't rivals: each brace is filled from, in order, a blank of the action named like it, then a field of the thing, then a with value. What's left goes in the request's body, by the server's name.
  • Ambiguity: a blank and a field with the same name ({queue}, and a job that has a queue field). The blank wins, as it's what the call said.
  • Ambiguity in B: with reason "..." sits beside a call's existing with props on events.
