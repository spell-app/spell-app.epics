the first of a list will mean what the first item of a list means: the list's first item.

  • A new rule reads the {ordinal} of {list}, in English and Spanish, and compiles to core.getItemAt(list, 0).
  • It wins over the property reading only when what follows is a list, or its type isn't known.
  • A property the type really declares still wins: a first on your own type stays a property.
  • A thing that isn't a list stays a property read, so the top of its card keeps working.
  • top and bottom, which are also ordinary property names, count as items only when what follows is known to be a list.
