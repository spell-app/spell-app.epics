1. Open the editor on PokéSpell: 
spell app serve @spell/examples/PokeSpell
2. Click any Pokémon's card. 3. Click the blue speaker button beside its heart. 4. Click it again.

Each click plays the Pokémon's cry once, from the start, at once: the page loaded it as it opened, so even the first click doesn't wait for GitHub.

In every browser, Safari and phones too: the click itself plays it (play its sound, Q40 A, T145). Checked headlessly with Chrome's strictest setting, which refused the old player every time: each click played at once.
