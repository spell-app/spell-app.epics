Designer review of P52 (finding 9): on a wide window each fold's badge (a list's state label, a tag's count) is drawn at the far right end of the title bar, 800px or more from the title it belongs to, so the eye loses the pairing. It's ui-section's own layout (the badge after a flexible gap), so it's every ui-section's, not this page's. Reviewer's fix: in ui-section, put the badge right after the heading (a small gap, 8px) and keep only the actions at the far end; or a token / attribute choosing the place, used here.
