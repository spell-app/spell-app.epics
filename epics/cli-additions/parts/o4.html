The four language tools.

  • parse: a line's match tree, and its compiled output.
    • --in parses in a project's scope
  • repl: the same, line after line, in an Ink screen.
  • explain: the hover text, and the rule's syntax.
    • through workspaceSymbols() and describeRecord()
  • new: a project.json and a starter spell file (D4).
