Fomantic's calendar can disable hours, months and years, allow only some dates, and show a reason or highlight per day. Ours disables dates and weekdays only.

  • Fomantic's examples, on the calendar page:
    • Disabling specific Days: Single Dates, with a reason in a popup on hover
    • Hours (disabledHours)
    • Whole Months
    • Whole Years
    • Enable only specific Dates (enabledDates)
    • Highlighting specific Days: Event Dates (eventDates, a class and a tooltip per day)
  • Ours has only:
    • disabledDates: ISO strings, no message
    • disabledDaysOfWeek
  • What the page does instead: shows Days of Week and Single Dates (without reasons), and leaves the rest out, with gap comments.

Net effect:

  • once fixed: the calendar page can show every disabling and highlighting example
