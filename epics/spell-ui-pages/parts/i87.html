<ui-image> always renders its own <img src>, so an SVG drawing or a <picture> can't take the image look and sizes.

  • Fomantic's example: the image page, Image, second and third blocks: a div.ui.small.image around an <img>, and around an inline <svg><image>.
  • <ui-image> has no slot, so an SVG drawing (or a <picture> with sources) can't be put in it.
  • What the page does: shows the src form only, with a gap comment.
  • The fix: a default slot, used when there's no src; the slotted img, svg or picture sized by the image sheet.

Net effect:

  • once fixed: <ui-image size="small"><svg>...</svg></ui-image> works
