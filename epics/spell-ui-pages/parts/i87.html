<ui-image> always draws its own <img src>. So an SVG drawing or a <picture> can't take the image look and sizes.

  • Fomantic's example: the image page, Image, second and third blocks. A small image box wraps an <img>, and then an inline SVG. 
    <div class="ui small image"><img src="..."></div>
<div class="ui small image"><svg><image ...></svg></div>
  • <ui-image> has no slot, so an SVG drawing (or a picture with sources) can't be put in it.
  • What the page does: shows the src form only, with a gap comment.
  • The fix: a default slot, used when there's no src. The image sheet sizes whatever is slotted: an image, an SVG or a picture.
  • once fixed: <ui-image size="small"><svg>...</svg></ui-image> works
