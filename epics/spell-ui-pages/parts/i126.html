Our card lays out only a direct <img> as its full-width image, so Fomantic's reveal, dimmer and link images don't work.

  • Fomantic's examples, on the Card page:
    • Image: a ui slide masked reveal image
    • Image's special cards: a blurring dimmable image + a dimmer
    • Link: <a class="image" href>
  • The card lays out only a direct <img> (or the image shorthand) as its full-width image. A <ui-reveal>, or an <a> around the image, isn't.
  • What the page does instead: the plain image. For the dimmer, a not-ported note: the sheet leaves dimmers in cards out on purpose.

Net effect:

  • once fixed: a card's image can be a reveal or a link
