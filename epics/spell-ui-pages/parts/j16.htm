equal without fluid makes every item as wide as the widest, and the bar hugs them. It's done with a grid on the group's root.

  • Applies to equal without fluid or alignment="fluid". The root gets: The root's rules · 4 lines 
    display: grid;
grid-auto-columns: 1fr;
grid-auto-flow: column;
width: fit-content;
  • With fluid, every child is flex: 1 1 0 instead.
  • Why a grid: flexbox alone can't size items to the widest without knowing how many there are.
  • Why on the root, not an inner grid box in a full-width bar: <ui-tabs> draws its tab buttons straight into the tablist (no slot to turn into the grid), and a wrapper would change every tab set's DOM and ARIA tree.
  • Costs:
    • a packed equal tabular bar's rule ends at its last tab
    • a packed <ui-buttons equal>'s <ui-or> takes a column of its own (use fluid)

Net effect:

  • a packed equal group's root is a grid of 1fr columns, as wide as its content
