In a vertical menu, a <ui-item link> is only as wide as its text, so its hover fill, its right-floated icon and its click target stop short. Seen on the docs side bar's topic toggles.

  • Why: the item renders a <button>. The item box is display: block, but a block button doesn't stretch.
  • The workaround, in ui-docs-nav.css: --ui-menu-item-width: 100% on the menu.
  • The fix, in ui-menu.css: .ui.vertical.menu { --_ui-menu-item-width: 100% }. It needs the menu visual baselines updated.
  • once fixed: link items fill a vertical menu's width, and the nav's workaround goes
