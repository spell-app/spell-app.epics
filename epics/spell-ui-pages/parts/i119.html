Fomantic can choose how a toast animates in and out, and how long that takes. Every toast of ours scales in and out.

  • Fomantic's example: Toast, Examples, Transitions: transition: { showMethod: 'zoom', showDuration: 1000, hideMethod: 'fade', hideDuration: 1000 }.
  • Ours: every toast scales in and out, timed by --ui-toast-duration.
  • What the page does instead: a note, with no example.
  • once fixed: UI.toast() takes show and hide methods and durations
