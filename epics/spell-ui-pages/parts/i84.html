Fomantic's slider labels can be restricted, highlight a range, be ui labels, or stay fixed. Ours always auto-adjust, with no attribute for any of that.

  • Fomantic's examples:
    • Types, Restricted Labels: restrictedLabels: [0, 10, 15, 35, 80, 90, 100], autoAdjustLabels: false
    • Examples, Highlight Range: highlightRange
    • UI Labels: className.label: 'ui tiny blue label'
    • Autoadjust Labels: autoAdjustLabels: 'fixed', labelDistance
  • Our labels always auto-adjust: every gap()-th label, the rest half ticks.
  • What the page does instead: the closest plain labeled ticked slider for each, with GAP comments.
