A page using our foundation CSS has its chrome 8px in from the window's edges, because nothing zeroes the body's margin.

  • Fomantic's reset zeroes the body margin.
  • Ours never touches body:
    • reset.css
    • typography.css
  • What site.css does: body { margin: 0 }.
  • The fix: :where(.ui-typography) on body (or the reset) sets margin 0.
  • once fixed: a page with ui-typography starts flush to the window, without its own rule
