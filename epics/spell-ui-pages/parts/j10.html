With stack-with="page", every responsive word on a grid follows the screen width, not just stacking.

  • The words that switch together (all screen-based in Fomantic):
    • stackable
    • doubling
    • the per-device widths (width-mobile ...)
    • reversed
  • Why all together: they share one range per grid. Switching only stacking could draw a mobile column (a container-mode width-mobile) inside a desktop layout.
  • The exception: only (show on some devices only) stays based on the viewport in both modes, as it was.
  • The other option: switch stacking alone.
  • a page-mode grid behaves as Fomantic's grid does, for every responsive word

Title, as first written: stack-with=page moves ALL of a grid's responsive words to the screen; only stays viewport

Chose to switch stackable, doubling, per-device widths AND reversed together (Fomantic's are all screen-based) over switching stacking alone, because they share one range per grid: a page-mode stack with container-mode width-mobile would draw a mobile column inside a desktop layout. only (device visibility) stays viewport-based in both modes, as before.
