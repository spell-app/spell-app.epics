Fomantic's popup loader takes the loader's style and speed words; ours has only loading.

  • Fomantic's example: States, Loading, second row: data-variation="blue double loading" and "green slow loading". The popup's loader takes the loader's double style, and its slow or fast speed.
  • <ui-popup> has only loading: no style or speed words.
  • What the page does: shows those two as plain loading popups in the hue, with the gap comment.
  • The same kind of gap as the button's double and elastic loaders (I22).
  • once fixed: <ui-popup loading> takes a style and a speed
