<ui-input type="color"> is the browser's native picker in Fomantic's input box: no hex or OKLCH text, and no transparency. The tokens playground on the docs pages needs a better one.

  • Fomantic has no colour input; <ui-docs-tokens playground> needs one for colour tokens.
  • The native picker takes #rrggbb only, so:
    • an OKLCH, light-dark() or translucent token can't be typed or kept
    • the value isn't shown as text
  • What was done:
    • ColorProbe resolves the token where it's used, and flattens it onto --ui-background to seed the picker
    • a picked colour overrides the token as an opaque hex
  • The fix belongs in <ui-input>, and it's not a 15-minute job. Either:
    • a color type with a text field beside the swatch
    • or a <ui-color> picker
  • once fixed: the playground can show and keep any colour token, including OKLCH and translucent ones
