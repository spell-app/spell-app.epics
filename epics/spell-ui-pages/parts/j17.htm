equal means slightly different things per family, depending on whether the group already spans its row.

  • Groups that span the row (no fluid attribute): <ui-statistics> and <ui-fields>. There equal means one row, an equal share each.
  • Groups that hug their children: <ui-buttons> and <ui-steps>. There:
    • equal means each as wide as the widest
    • equal fluid means an equal share of the row
  • <ui-fields equal> emits Fomantic's own equal width, an exact alias of widths="equal": no new CSS.
  • Elsewhere the class word is equal, and the rules leave out .width, so the older equal width keeps filling.
  • Not offered on vertical or circular steps, or horizontal statistics: their children already line up.

Net effect:

  • equal works on statistics, fields, buttons and steps, each as above
