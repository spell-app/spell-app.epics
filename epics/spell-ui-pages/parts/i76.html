Fomantic can hang a long floating label off one edge of its item; ours always centres it on the corner.

  • Fomantic's example: the Label page, Types, Floating aligned: left aligned floating and right aligned floating labels with long text.
  • Our floating takes "left", "bottom" and "bottom left", but no alignment.
  • What the page does instead: plain floating labels.
  • once fixed: a floating label can be left or right aligned
