Two gaps in vertical menus: item labels sit right after the text instead of at the right edge, and a small menu's input stays full size.

  • Fomantic's examples, on the menu page:
    • Types, Vertical Menu: Inbox + a ui teal left pointing label, Spam + a ui label
    • Variations, Size: vertical mini ... massive menus, with labels and an icon input
  • In Fomantic:
    • a label in a vertical menu item floats right: .ui.vertical.menu .item > .label { float: right }
    • a sized menu's inputs take its font size
  • Ours:
    • a slotted <ui-label> sits inline, right after the text (or before it, when written first)
    • a <ui-input> in a size="mini" menu keeps the page's size, so its placeholder is clipped
  • What the page does: shows them so.
  • The fix:
    • owner tokens on ui-item in a vertical menu (a label's float and margin)
    • the menu's scale reaching slotted controls

Net effect:

  • once fixed: vertical menu labels sit at the right edge, and inputs follow the menu's size
