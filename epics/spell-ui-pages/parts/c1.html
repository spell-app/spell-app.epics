The famfamfam theme draws flags from one sprite image, as Fomantic's does. Three things it can't do.

  • No sizes: the sprite is a fixed 16 x 11 cell, so <ui-flag size="large"> under famfamfam stays 16 x 11. Fomantic is the same: its famfamfam @size-map is empty.
  • No "unknown" flag: Fomantic draws a default cell (-108px -1976px) for an i.flag naming no country. Ours isn't drawn: our unknown flag stays an empty, unnamed box.
  • Countries the sprite lacks keep their emoji:
    • xk
    • ss
    • un
    • rainbow
    • pirate
  • The sprite is copied to flags.png; Fomantic's 244 rules became 244 codes.
