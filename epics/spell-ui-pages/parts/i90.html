Fomantic spaces only a statistic that directly follows another. Ours spaces every statistic after the first, even one that follows a divider.

  • Fomantic's example: the statistic page, Size, second block: a ui ignored divider between horizontal statistics.
  • Fomantic's .ui.statistic + .ui.statistic margin is for ADJACENT siblings only, so each statistic after a divider starts at the left edge.
  • Ours uses :host(:nth-child(n + 2 of :state(statistic))), which matches when there's ANY earlier statistic. A shadow root can't see its host's previous sibling.
    • So every statistic after the first is indented by --ui-statistic-horizontal-spacing (1.5em).
  • What the page does: the same markup as Fomantic.
  • A fix: a state the element sets from its previousElementSibling, kept current by the parent's child-list changes.

Net effect:

  • once fixed: a statistic after a divider starts at the left edge
