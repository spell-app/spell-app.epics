Emoji look different on every platform (Windows especially), and our <ui-emoji> has no way to swap in one consistent image set. Fomantic's joypixels theme, and its default Twemoji, draw emoji as images from a CDN.

  • <ui-emoji> only draws the platform's native glyph, so that look can't be themed in.
  • Proposal: an opt-in image registry on EmojiData, like the UI.icons packs:
    • a map from codepoint to URL, with a configurable base (self-hosted, or jsDelivr)
    • the element renders <img alt> while a set is active
  • It isn't a theme: it needs work in the element and the runtime.
  • What was done: the joypixels theme was skipped (see the joypixels decision).

Net effect:

  • once fixed: a page can turn on an image emoji set, and <ui-emoji> draws images
