Fomantic can colour a toast's progress bar separately from the toast; ours follows the toast's colour.

  • Fomantic's examples: Toast, Types, Progress Bar, second example: classProgress: 'red'; also Duration's 'blue'.
  • Neither UI.toast() nor <ui-toast> takes a bar colour.
  • What the page does instead: a note pointing at ::part(bar).

Net effect:

  • once fixed: a toast's bar can take its own colour
