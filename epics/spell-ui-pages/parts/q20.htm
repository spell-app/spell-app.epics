D19 · Themes may write a component's private variation tokens

A Fomantic variable that themes a VARIATION (secondary menu, styled accordion, basic button) has no public token to set: the variation writes the component's private alias itself (--_ui-menu-active-background, --_button-weight). Theme sheets (package code, pinned by themes.test.ts) may write those on the variation's box in their overrides half, and may READ --_button-edge* so a variation keeps its own look. Their own looks travel in theme-private tokens (--_raised-ring ...) declared on :root so groups hand them to members. Pages still theme with public tokens only. (T2, P6)
