Fomantic can put a dimmer over an image; our <ui-image> has no slot, so nothing can sit over it.

  • Fomantic's example: the dimmer page, Examples, Dimmer Events.
    • A medium image holds a dimmer and an img.
    • The same again, on a circular image.
    • The image is the box that dims.
    <div class="ui medium image">
  <div class="ui dimmer">...</div>
  <img src="...">
</div>
<!-- and on a ui circular image -->
  • What the page does instead:
    • it puts the <ui-image> and a hover dimmer inside a basic, compact segment (<ui-dimmer on="hover">)
    • the segment's padding shows round the dimmer
    • the circular variant is left out
  • once fixed: a <ui-dimmer> can sit inside a <ui-image>, round or square
