Fomantic can put a dimmer over an image; our <ui-image> has no slot, so nothing can sit over it.

  • Fomantic's example: the dimmer page, Examples, Dimmer Events. A ui medium image > ui dimmer + img, and the same on a ui circular image. The image is the box that dims.
  • What the page does instead:
    • the <ui-image> and a <ui-dimmer on="hover"> inside a <ui-segment basic compact>; the segment's padding shows round the dimmer
    • the circular variant is left out

Net effect:

  • once fixed: a <ui-dimmer> can sit inside a <ui-image>, round or square
