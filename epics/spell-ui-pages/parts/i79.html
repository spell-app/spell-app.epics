In Fomantic, a segment in a stretched column grows to the column's height. Ours keeps its content height.

  • Fomantic's examples:
    • the menu page, Tabbed: a twelve wide stretched right attached column holding a seamless right attached segment that matches the tabs' height
    • the grid page, Stretched
  • Ours: stretched makes the column a flex column and grows its slotted children. But <ui-segment>'s host is display: contents, so its inner box keeps its content height.
  • A known limit: the header NOTE in ui-grid.css.
  • What the pages do: render it; the segment stays short.
  • The fix: a flex owner token the segment's root reads (e.g. --_ui-owner-grow), set by a stretched column.
  • once fixed: a segment fills its stretched column, and the tabbed menu example lines up
