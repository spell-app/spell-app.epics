Fomantic draws a round 2.5em picture before a header's text. A <ui-image circular> in our header draws at the picture's full size.

  • Fomantic's example: the header page, Content, Image, second block. 
    <h2 class="ui header"><img class="ui circular image" src="patrick.png"> Patrick</h2>
  • The parts' sheet, ui-parts.css, sizes only a slotted plain image, by --ui-header-image-width.
  • A <ui-image circular> host has display: contents. The header can't reach the image inside it, so it drew at the picture's natural 300px and more.
  • What the page does: a plain <img>, sized but square (no circular).
  • The fix: the header sets the image's owner tokens on its slot, plus a margin, as a list item does. The tokens: --_ui-image-owner-width and --_ui-image-owner-align.
  • once fixed: <ui-image circular> in a header draws as a small round picture
