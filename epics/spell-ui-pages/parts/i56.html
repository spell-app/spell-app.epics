Fomantic draws a medium image 300px wide. Ours treats size="medium" as "no size", so a 1000px image fills the column.

  • Fomantic's examples: the dimmer page (ui medium image, ui medium wireframe image), and any page with a medium image.
  • The grammar makes size="medium" a no-op (the NOTE in ui-image.css's header).
  • What the pages do instead: size="large" (450px), or small, where the width matters.
  • once fixed: <ui-image size="medium"> is 300px wide, as in Fomantic
