alignment moves the items inside a menu bar that keeps spanning the row; only a segmented menu moves as a whole.

  • alignment="left|center|right" is justify-content inside the bar. The bar still spans the row, so a tab rule or a boxed menu runs across it.
  • fluid: the items grow from their own width (flex: 1 1 auto).
  • A segmented menu IS its items (it hugs them), so for it:
    • alignment moves the whole group (auto margins)
    • fluid spreads the group
  • Emitted as <value> aligned (the shape of Fomantic's phrases).
  • Ignored when the menu is vertical.
  • The other option: shrink every bar to its items and move the bar. Rejected because a tab rule or toolbar box across the row is the usual look for centred tabs.
  • <ui-menu alignment="center" tabular> centres the tabs; the rule still spans the row
