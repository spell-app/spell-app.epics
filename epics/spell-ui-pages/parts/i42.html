In Fomantic's feed, a date can sit at the right of an event's content. None of our feed parts can float there.

  • Fomantic's example: the feed page, Content, Additional information: <div class="right floated date"> inside an event's content.
  • What the page does: a plain <ui-date> above the summary.
  • The feed's sheet keeps Fomantic's static rule for a right-floated part (ui-feed.css, .ui.feed .right.floated).
    • but no part takes floated
    • <ui-content> does, but it's the wrong box
  • The fix: floated on <ui-date> (or on every feed part), reaching that rule.
  • once fixed: <ui-date floated="right"> sits at the right of the content
