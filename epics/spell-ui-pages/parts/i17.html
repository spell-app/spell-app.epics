The docs side bar's A-Z / Topics switch is two buttons, exactly one pressed, and should be announced that way. No button group does that today.

  • toggle gives aria-pressed, but a toggle owns its state:
    • clicking the pressed one un-presses it, and ui-toggle can't be cancelled
    • its pressed look is the positive green
  • What the nav does:
    • toggle buttons
    • the pressed one is set back to active inside its ui-toggle handler, so Controlled.request() keeps the host's value
    • the --ui-button-toggle-* tokens pointed at a light overlay
  • The fix: <ui-buttons value>, a radio-group mode. Fomantic has none either; its docs use plain active buttons.
  • Related: the single-choice buttons issue (I12); the call on the site's tabs (J18) left both open.

Net effect:

  • once fixed: <ui-buttons value> works as a radio group, and the nav's toggle workaround goes
