Fomantic's attached progress bars sit flush on a segment's top and bottom edges. Ours sit inside the segment's padding, short of the edges.

  • Fomantic's example: Variations, Attached. A ui top attached progress and a ui bottom attached progress as the first and last children of a ui segment: thin bars, full width, on the edges.
  • Ours: ui-progress.css leaves out the .ui.segment > .ui.attached.progress rule on purpose: a shadow root can't see its parent.
  • In a <ui-card> (the card example), the bottom bar does sit on the card's edge.
  • What the page does: shows the segment example as is, with the gap comment.
  • The fix: <ui-segment> pulls an attached progress child to its edges, as the owner, the way the card does.
  • once fixed: attached progress bars sit on a segment's edges
