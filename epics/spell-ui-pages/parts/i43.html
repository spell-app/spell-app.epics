In Fomantic, a loader inside an active dimmer shows by itself, white. Ours stays hidden unless the author marks it active, and then shows dark on the dark dimmer.

  • Fomantic's examples are on the Loader page: Loader, Text Loader, Indeterminate, Size, inverted dimmer.
    • a loader in an active dimmer shows white, with no active on it
    • the rule that does it: .ui.dimmer > .ui.loader
  • Our loader's sheet, ui-loader.css, keeps that rule for static markup only.
    • It says <ui-dimmer> MUST make its loader active and hand it the scheme.
    • The dimmer doesn't.
  • What the page does instead: every loader in a dimmer says active, plus inverted in a dark (non-inverted) dimmer.
  • once fixed: a plain <ui-loader> in an active dimmer shows, in the right scheme
