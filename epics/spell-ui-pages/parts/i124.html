Fomantic's loading form can show a blue, double-ringed spinner; ours only has the plain grey one.

  • Fomantic's example: the form page, Form States, Loading, second form: ui blue double loading form.
  • Ours: the form has no color, and its loading is a boolean. The form's loader tokens (--ui-form-loader-*) can recolour it from CSS.
  • What the page does: shows the first form only.
  • Suggested: loading="double" and a color, as for the button (I22), segment and popup loaders.
  • once fixed: <ui-form loading="double" color="blue"> works
