Fomantic's calendar can format its text any way you like; ours always uses the locale's own numeric format.

  • Fomantic's examples, on the calendar page, Examples:
    • 24 hour format: formatter.time: "H:mm"
    • Custom format: token strings like YYYY-MM-DD, or a function
    • Century Calculation, for short years
  • Our field's text is the locale's own numeric format (Intl.DateTimeFormat); nothing overrides it.
  • What the page does instead:
    • 24 hours and day-first through locale (en-GB, de-DE)
    • custom formats and the short-year century left out, with a note
  • once fixed: a calendar can take a format, and the page gets the custom format examples
