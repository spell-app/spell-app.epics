stack-with says what a layout stacks by: the page (the screen) or its own container. It goes only on the elements that OWN a layout, never on their parts.

  • The owners that take it:
    • <ui-grid>
    • <ui-cards>
    • <ui-steps>
    • <ui-form>
    • <ui-items>
    • <ui-statistics>
  • The parts follow their owner:
    • a row or a column follows its grid (<ui-row>, <ui-column>)
    • a <ui-fields> follows its form
  • Why not on parts too: a part measured differently from its owner mixes two ranges in one layout, e.g. a row in page mode inside a grid in container mode.
  • Each grid works out its range ONCE, for everything inside it (--_grid-range).
  • A row's own stackable, doubling and reversed words follow the grid's range too.
  • <ui-row stack-with> and <ui-fields stack-with> don't exist
  • a whole grid always switches at one width
