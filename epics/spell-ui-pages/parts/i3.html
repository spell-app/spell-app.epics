Under direction: rtl (the rtl theme, or dir=rtl), text and flex / grid order flip, but much of the component styling stays on its left-to-right side.

  • Why: 445 declarations in 40 component sheets use physical properties, which don't flip: margin-left, left:, float, text-align: left, corner radii ...
  • The most affected sheets (count of declarations):
    • list 108
    • menu 43
    • label 31, button 31
    • slider 26
    • dropdown 16, checkbox 16
    • step 15, parts 15
    • segment, rail, grid: 10 each
  • Seen in screenshots:
    • icon inputs keep the icon on its physical side, over the text
    • corner labels and the loading spinner don't move
    • labeled-icon buttons keep the icon on the right
  • The fix: convert the component sheets to logical properties, family by family, with a right-to-left visual-test pass. It's not a theme's job.
    • Where Fomantic's left / right words mean physical sides (e.g. left floated), keep them physical.
