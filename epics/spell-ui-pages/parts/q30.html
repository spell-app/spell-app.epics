tools/ThemeFamilies.ts (run by yarn site:data) reads every src/styles/themes/*.css and writes components.json themes: [{ name, title, families, global }]. A sheet touches a family through a class-grammar compound naming a noun (.ui.labeled.icon.button: a noun that is an attribute of another noun's tag is a modifier), a tag selector, or a declared --ui-<tag>-* token; global means it re-declares a foundation token on :root or styles page markup. Titles are hand-kept in pages.json themes (seeded from each sheet's header comment). for lists only themes touching that family, like Fomantic's per-page N Themes; site-wide-only themes (systemfont, rtl, resetcss) aren't listed under any for. Why build time: reading the sheets in the browser would load every theme chunk to fill one menu. Pinned in tools/SiteDataBuilder.test.ts.
