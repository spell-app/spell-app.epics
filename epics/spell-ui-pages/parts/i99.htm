Fomantic can centre or left-align a modal's actions, and make the header and actions plain. Ours can't.

  • Fomantic's examples, on the modal page, Variations:
    • Center Aligned: center aligned actions
    • Left Actions: left actions
    • Basic Header and Actions: basic header, basic actions (no tinted background, no rule)
  • Ours:
    • <ui-actions> has no attributes at all
    • <ui-header> has no basic
    • only the whole modal's basic clears the parts
    • <ui-header text-align> and <ui-content text-align> do work
  • What the page does: shows all three with the closest markup (the actions stay right-aligned and tinted), with a gap comment.
  • Wanted, in a modal (and a flyout):
    • <ui-actions text-align="left|center" basic>
    • <ui-header basic>

Net effect:

  • once fixed: the three modal examples match Fomantic's
