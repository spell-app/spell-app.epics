Fomantic can centre one tab in a tabbed menu; our tabs can't.

  • Fomantic's example: Tab, Centered Item: <div class="center item">Two</div> in a tabbed menu.
  • <ui-tab> has no attribute to centre its tab, and the tab buttons live in the tab set's shadow menu.
  • What the page does instead: the three tabs in line.

Net effect:

  • once fixed: a <ui-tab> can ask for its tab to be centred
