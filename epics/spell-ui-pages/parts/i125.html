Fomantic can float a card's meta, extra or author to the right, and centre a description. In ours, only the header and content can.

  • Fomantic's examples, on the Card page:
    • Card, second card: right floated meta ("14h"), right floated likes
    • Floated Content: right floated time, right floated author
    • Text Alignment: center aligned description / author
    • Size: a right floated author
  • Ours:
    • only the header and content parts take floated and text-align
    • the meta, extra and description parts don't
    • plain spans in a card's light DOM can't reach the card sheet's [class*=floated] rules
  • What the page does instead: the pieces sit in line; text alignment goes on the <ui-content>.
  • once fixed: card parts take floated and text-align
