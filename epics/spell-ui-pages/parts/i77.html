In Fomantic, a label attached to a segment's edge or corner pushes the content after it clear. Ours overlays it.

  • Fomantic's example: the Label page, Types, Attached. A top attached, bottom attached or corner-attached label in a segment, with the wireframe paragraph pushed clear of it by:
    • a margin on .ui.attached.label:first-child + *
    • the segment's extra padding
  • Ours: the label overlays the paragraph image, in all six cases.
  • What the page does instead: shows it as is, overlapping.
  • once fixed: an attached label leaves room for the content after it
