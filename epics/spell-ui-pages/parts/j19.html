The details of the new segmented menu look, chosen during the Menu Appearance phase (P11).

  • Border and dividers: --ui-border-color-strong.
  • Item padding: like a button's.
  • The fill's inner corners: radius - border.
  • Margins: the ordinary menu margins, not compact's zero.
  • The selected item's focus ring is its on-colour (the text colour on the fill), not the focus colour.
    • Why: the default focus colour IS the primary fill, and would vanish on it.
    • New tokens for it: private --_ui-menu-active-focus-color, public --ui-menu-active-focus-color.
  • Inverted: the dark surface, the fill kept.
  • a new public token, --ui-menu-active-focus-color
  • keyboard focus stays visible on the selected item of a segmented menu
