A few parts of Fomantic's own classic theme weren't ported, and its font isn't loaded.

  • card.overrides: it holds variables, not rules, so Fomantic compiles it to nothing.
  • The table's @boxShadow: @subtleGradient: a gradient as a box shadow is invalid CSS.
  • The button group offsets.
  • The loading button's background.
  • Open Sans isn't loaded: the page must load it.
  • The full list: the header of fomantic-classic.css.
