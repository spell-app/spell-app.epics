Fomantic's circular steps can put the title on the connecting line, or under it. Ours always draw the default top-aligned content.

  • Fomantic's examples, on the step page, Content:
    • Center Aligned (center aligned content): the title sits ON the connecting line, beside the ring, and the last step draws no line
    • Bottom Aligned (bottom aligned content): the title and description under the line
    • also used by Circular Step, Disabled, Evenly Divided, Size and Color
  • ui-step.css leaves both out on purpose (header NOTE: "the parts have no alignment attributes").
  • But <ui-content> now HAS text-align and vertical-align, which do nothing in a step.
  • What the page does: writes <ui-content text-align="center"> / vertical-align="bottom" (the natural translation), which render as the default top-aligned content.
  • The fix: the step reads its content's alignment (a part state or a token) in ui-step.css.
  • once fixed: the alignment the page already writes takes effect
