The docs side bar's Topics view wants groups that open and close, like Fomantic's accordion menu, and no menu item can do that.

  • Fomantic's markup: .item > .header + .menu.
  • Why ours can't:
    • an item that is a <button> can't hold its sub-menu (interactive content inside a button)
    • <ui-accordion> is a part barrier
  • What <ui-docs-nav> does instead, per topic:
    1. a toggle item: link, aria-expanded, an angle icon, a count label
    2. while open, a SECOND item (fitted="vertically") holding the sub-menu
    3. the divider between the two is hidden from the nav sheet: .topic-rows::part(item)::before { display: none }
  • A real fix, either:
    • a menu item that renders a header button plus its sub-menu, e.g. <ui-item type="group" collapsible>
    • or <ui-accordion menu>

Net effect:

  • once fixed: the nav's topics are one item each, and its two-item workaround goes
