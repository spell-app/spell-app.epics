The Material theme doesn't load its font, and leaves out a few details.

  • Roboto isn't loaded: Fomantic @imported it from Google Fonts, and a runtime sheet drops @import. The page must load Roboto itself.
  • Not ported:
    • the Material Icons font
    • the text-selection highlight colours
    • the body font size
    • the header icon's offset
  • The full list: the header of material.css, "NOT ported".
