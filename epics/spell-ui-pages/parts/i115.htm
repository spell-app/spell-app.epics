Fomantic draws a round 2.5em picture before a header's text. A <ui-image circular> in our header draws at the picture's full size.

  • Fomantic's example: the header page, Content, Image, second block: <h2 class="ui header"><img class="ui circular image" src="patrick.png"> Patrick</h2>.
  • ui-parts.css sizes only a slotted <img> (--ui-header-image-width).
  • A <ui-image circular> is a display: contents host, whose inner <img> the header can't reach, so it drew at the picture's natural 300px and more.
  • What the page does: a plain <img>, sized but square (no circular).
  • The fix: the header sets <ui-image>'s owner tokens on its slot (--_ui-image-owner-width / -align, plus a margin), as a list item does.

Net effect:

  • once fixed: <ui-image circular> in a header draws as a small round picture
