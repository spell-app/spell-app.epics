Eight linked docs, read before any planning. Each ends with its open questions, which come here as Q items.

  • What people do with an agent: the flows, step by step.

  • How agents hand work to cheaper agents: what Claude does first (sub-agents on cheaper models, effort levels, orchestrator and workers), then todos T2-T6 to evaluate the other frontier agents.

  • Hosting cheaply, and bring your own agent: who pays on spell.app, your own key or subscription, and your own Claude Code or Codex driving Spell.
