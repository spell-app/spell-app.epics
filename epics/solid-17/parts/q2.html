The Solid packages, the Vite plugin and the router each require the others at a minimum version, so none of them can move alone. So each repo moves them in ONE commit.

  • The Vite plugin next.51 needs Solid rc.16 or later.
  • The router next.39 and later needs Solid rc.15 or later.
  • solid-js rc.17 needs seroval 1.6.7 exactly.
  • @solidjs/web rc.17 needs @solidjs/signals rc.17 or later.
  • One commit per repo moves every Solid package, the plugin and the router together (Bump Versions).
  • seroval is pinned to 1.6.7 in the kit's resolutions.
