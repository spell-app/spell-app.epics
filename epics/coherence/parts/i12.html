Spell UI's components rebuild six of Spell UI's own runtime pieces, and one copy probably sends English event names from a Spanish page.

  • Likely bug (not checked): each native fallback (the plain input shown before a component loads) sends its events itself.
    • None translates the event's name, or lets it be cancelled, as the components' own send does.
    • So a Spanish page's fallback may send ui-change where the page listens for its Spanish name.
  • Drift: the read-only attribute is a plain switch in the input, and a keyword, read-only, in the dropdown and the slider.
