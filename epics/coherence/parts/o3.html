The spellx reader: a parser written by hand

  • elemental-spell's P3 wrote SpellxReader: 1,270 lines that walk the text one character at a time, with its own word list beside the rules' own.
  • The parser already lets a language bring its own tokenizer: the markdown and rulex tokenizers each subclass it in about 40 lines.
  • Why it happened: the plan's option B ("one parser for both") was costed without knowing the tokenizer could be subclassed. Owen asked after the fact (its Q69), and its P6 now moves it onto rules.
  • What a review would have needed: a catalog entry, "a new syntax: a tokenizer subclass plus rules", read while the plan was still in its Overview.

Open and close: six words for one idea

  • Today, showing and hiding content is called open, show, fold, collapse, expand, toggle, reveal or dismiss, depending on the component.
  • elemental-spell settled it on 2026-10-24 (its Q65 and Q68): anything that shows and hides opens and closes, as <details open> does.
    • Owen: "folding is a programmer concept, showing and hiding are people concepts ... better to reuse terms for analogous things"
    • Its P9 makes the renames. Coherence keeps the rule, and catches the next one.
