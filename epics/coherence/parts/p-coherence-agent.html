No reviewer asks "do we already have this?", so a second way of doing something gets built, as elemental-spell's spellx reader was.

  • spell-epic gains the coherence agent, beside the designer's, and the /coherence skill that runs it: a sweep of code, or a review of an epic's plan.
  • Its findings are ranked, and each lands with the epic it's about, by that epic's stage.
  • Owen's verdict on a finding teaches it: a catalog entry, a word, or "stop flagging this".
  • The coherence agent, registered in spell-epic's plugin as the designer's is: read-only, it finds and ranks, the session that asked decides (Q1).
  • /coherence sweep [area] reads one of the five areas, or all of them, for one mechanism done more than one way.
    • each finding names every place it's in, what they share, what differs, and the one mechanism that would replace them
    • ranked: most code saved, and most likely to be copied again, first
  • /coherence epic <name> reads an epic's kickoff and Overview against the catalog and the word list, and searches the code for the nouns it names.
  • Each finding lands by the stage of the epic it's about (Q4), found from the epic list and that epic's own stage:
    • planning: worked into its plan, with change marks
    • building: a question on its page, "(coherence)"
    • none, or done: a calm issue on this page
  • Whoever owns the code fixes it: an open epic that owns it, else a later phase here (Q5).
  • Learning, as the designer's last step does: Owen's verdict becomes a catalog entry, a word, or a "stop flagging it" rule.
  • A row for /coherence on the commands page, its check passing.
  • Tried on elemental-spell's kickoff as a dry run: would it have flagged the hand-written parser? The answer goes in this phase's Done.
  • Doc pass: /fussbudget on the agent, the skill and the changed text, spell-epic's README and the commands page brought up to date, then merged into main.
  1. The agent: spell-epic's coherence/coherence-agent.md, registered in its plugin beside the designer's; read-only tools (search, read, run commands), it finds and ranks, changes nothing (Q1).
    • its rules beside it, as the designer's are: coherence/procedures.md, what a finding is (every place, what they share, what differs, the one mechanism), how it's ranked (code saved, then how likely it's copied again), at most 15 per run, and a "Considered, left out" line
  2. The skill, /coherence in spell-epic's skills, two modes:
    • /coherence sweep [area]: one of the five areas, or all, an agent each
    • /coherence epic <name>: an epic's kickoff, Overview and build plans, against the catalog and word list, then spell map uses / explain / --docs for each thing the plan names
    • it reads the catalog, word list and the five areas from the repo it runs in (the kit's agents/coherence/); a repo without them gets the search alone
  3. Where a finding lands (Q4, Q5): a new epic owns <paths...> says which open epic's branch changes those files; then the skill:
    • that epic is planning: the finding worked into its Overview or plans, with change marks
    • building: a question on its page, "(coherence)"
    • none, or done: a calm issue here
  4. Learning: Owen's verdict on a finding becomes a catalog entry, a word, or a line in the catalog's "Fine as it is" list, which later runs skip.
  5. The commands page: rows for /coherence and epic owns; spell commands check.
  6. The dry run (J1): the agent on elemental-spell's kickoff and Overview as they were before its Q69, from the content repo's history. Did it flag the hand-written parser? The answer, word for word, goes in the Done.
  7. Checked by: a test of epic owns in spell-epic; the dry run; spell-epic's tests and lint.
  8. Left out: running by itself from /epic (P4); the sweep of every area (P5).
  9. Then the doc pass (spell-epic's README, the commands page), the commit, and the merge into main.
