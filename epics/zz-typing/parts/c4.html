A wake finds an epic's session by its title: 🚧 feedback, or the epic's name as a whole word in it (titleNames()). "feedback" is a common word, so a session titled, say, "UI feedback pass" would get /epic review feedback typed into it by an unowned page's Send (two such sessions: neither, and the toast says which). Accepted: the prompt only waits there for Enter, and while feedback's own session listens no wake happens at all.
