Open phases: how do you reorder them? (What "in parallel" and "at bedtime" do is now its own question, Q21: one pick per question.)

  • Today phases run in order. add-phase --before N can insert one, but nothing moves a phase once written, and nothing says "these two can run at once".
  • "At bedtime": /bedtime already runs a range of phases overnight (epic bedtime <name> start "P3-P6").
  • "In parallel": background agents (/bg), each in a worktree of its own, then merged in.

The reorder control:

  • Two small arrows on an open phase's title row; a mark each, applied by the next listening session.
  • Works in the narrow side bar, and keyboard-reachable. Ids follow the new order, links kept (as --before does).
  • Drag a folded phase by its title. Cost: fiddly in the side bar, where you review; and a drop is easy to make by mistake.
  • open phases get up / down arrows on their title rows
  • a reorder renumbers the open phases and their links; done phases never move
