To let Spell UI's pages load the epics pack (T14), its site bundle took SitePacks (globalThis.SpellUI: registerPack, packModules), which only the other sets' own build of the site had. With it, the two builds were the same file, so the other one is gone: site/_src/sets.ts, the sets mode, SETS_BUNDLE and site/_assets/sets/; every set in COMPONENT_SETS now loads SITE_BUNDLE. spell-epic reads Epic UI's bundle from that list (epicUiMounts()) instead of naming SETS_BUNDLE, so it works with spell-app before and after the change.

The cost Owen took with "let its site bundle load packs", measured on a scratch server:

  • Spell UI's eager JS: 305 KB to 555 KB raw (79 to 155 KB gzipped): the pack modules stay whole. First show of ui-button.html no slower with the bundle alone (about 480 ms both).
  • With the comments and the epics pack, first show about 200-300 ms later on the big element pages (ui-button 480 to 700 ms, ui-dropdown 620 to 920 ms): the same cost App UI's and Brand's pages took in T13.

The alternative: keep two builds and serve Spell UI's pages the sets' one; the same bytes on the page, a second build kept for nothing.
