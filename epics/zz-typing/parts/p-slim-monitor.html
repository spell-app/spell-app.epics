  • What changed in how the page counts a listener: the listener holds the monitor's process id: live while the monitor runs, no heartbeat, gone the moment it stops (a Send then wakes the session the old way).
  • The same behaviour as P18: the epic from the session's title, else its worktree; one 📬 line per batch of work; quiet otherwise; stands back for another session's listener or this session's own /epic review.
