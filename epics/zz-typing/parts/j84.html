The check that refuses an epic's worktree in the wrong repo (I47, EpicRepo) now treats the top and every child repo cloned in it as ONE repo. So epic-engine, whose page names its old spell-epic worktree, is still "own" from ~/www/spell, and its worktree there is the top's. The other way, refusing until each page's worktree= is rewritten, would block every running epic at the switch.
