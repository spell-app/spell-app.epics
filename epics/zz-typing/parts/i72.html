Found from your screenshot: the tab wasn't stray. It was the tab you opened to type /epic solid-17.

  • Typed in another epic's window, /epic <name> always launched a NEW session in the new window, and this session stayed where it was, as another epic's session.
  • So a fresh tab opened just for it was left behind. It then did epic-engine work (asked about merging main) and got an epic-engine title.

The fix: a fresh session moves instead.

  • The prompt hook tells when a session is fresh: Claude hasn't answered anything in it yet. It titles the session 🚧 <name> and tells Claude to move it.
  • epic window launch <name> --move makes the worktree and window file, then hands this session off. When its turn ends, it opens in the epic's window with /epic <name> typed in, and its tab here closes.
  • A session that has already done work in its window keeps the old behaviour: a new session in the new window, and it stays put.
  • The epic skill's "From another worktree" has the new step 5.
