I59 asked for real paths on both sides of every compare, with "a path that doesn't exist kept as given". RealPath.of() goes one step further: for a path that isn't there, it follows the links of its nearest folder that is, then adds the rest as given.

  • Why: a session's recorded cwd in a worktree that's gone, or a page not made yet, sits UNDER a compat link; kept as given, it would never match the real folder.
  • It's what RepoList and pages.js already did (realOr(), realOf()), now one helper; a path with no link above it comes out exactly as given (/no/such/place).
  • The alternative: the literal rule, as given when missing; then worktreeNameOf() of a removed worktree's old cwd says nothing.
