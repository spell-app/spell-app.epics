GET /api/epics/tests (the test run's "Next: <epic>", and epic test --all) lives in epicRoutes.ts, beside GET /api/epics/saga, which reads the epic pages the same way.

  • Why: a new route module would have to be listed in spell-app's own package.json (pageServer.routes) as well as the epic server's list, a change outside spell-epic.
  • The alternative: a testRoutes.ts of its own, and an edit to spell-app's root package.json.
