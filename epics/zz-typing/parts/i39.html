<epic-cards saga> (a saga page's Epics, from GET /api/epics/saga) draws its cards in its own markup and CSS: a list item per epic, its mark, title, summary and meta.

  • The Epics page's cards are <epic-card> since P3: Spell UI's card, the star, the saga label and the last-worked day.
  • So the same epic looks different on the two pages, and a fix to one isn't a fix to the other.
  • The fix: <epic-cards> draws an <epic-card> per epic from the server's answer (its facts are the same EpicFacts), and its groups as <epic-list>'s.

Fixed in P3: a saga page's Epics (<epic-cards saga>) now draw the Epics page's own list and cards, an <epic-list no-favorites> of <epic-card>s in its shadow root. So one epic looks and behaves the same on both pages.
