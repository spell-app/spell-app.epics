The nav panel takes its colours from the theme's tokens, so it follows whichever theme is picked. Only the ivory fill is a fixed colour.

  • Panel fill: light-dark(#fffdf8, var(--ui-surface)), the one hex value.
  • Header band: --ui-brown-background. In Spell that is Polished Ivory accent-100 in light mode, the dark warm surface #2c2152 in dark mode.
  • Group bands: halfway between the two.
  • Band titles: --ui-brown-text.
  • Hairline: a shade under the band (dark mode: --ui-border-color).
  • In Classic and the Fomantic themes, the bands read as a light tan.

The overnight run recorded no other options for this one.

Net effect:

  • the panel follows the picked theme
  • non-Spell themes show tan bands
