The nav's groups fold like its topics do, and each viewer's folds are remembered. Every group starts open.

  • Get started, Favourites, Components and Foundation are collapsible bands.
  • A folded group is remembered per viewer, under a new key, spell-ui-site:closed-groups.
  • While you search, Favourites and Components open regardless. Clicking a band then folds it for that search only.

The options:

  1. Fold and remember (chosen).
  2. Fold, but don't remember group folds.

Net effect:

  • a viewer who folds Foundation finds it folded on the next page and the next visit
  • a search always shows its matches, whatever is folded
