The theme menu now starts with Spell, and the old "Default" entry is called "Plain". You'd see it in the theme overlay on the Spell UI site.

  • With Spell the default, an entry called "Default" for our own unthemed look would mislead.
  • The menu now reads:
    1. Spell, "The Spell brand"
    2. Plain, "Spell UI, unthemed"
    3. then the rest, as before
  • How it's stored:
    • no theme stored: Spell
    • Plain: stored as default (DOCS_PLAIN_THEME)
    • an unknown stored name: falls back to Spell

The options:

  1. Rename "Default" to "Plain", and put Spell first (chosen).
  2. Keep "Default", pointing at Spell, and drop the unthemed look from the menu.

Net effect:

  • the menu starts Spell, Plain, Classic, then Fomantic's themes
  • a viewer who had picked the unthemed look keeps it (still stored as default)
