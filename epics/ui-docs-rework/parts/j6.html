The spell theme is applied the way a Fomantic theme is: the classic look underneath, Spell's sheet on top. You'd only notice it when editing spell.css: it's long, because it restates much of classic.

  • Chose: on top of classic (the lead's brief allowed that), listed apart from Fomantic's themes in a new ThemeSheets.OWN.
  • Over: a standalone theme, on top of our own plain look.
  • The cost: spell.css restates every classic token the brand replaces:
    • type and the size ladder
    • radii
    • ink (text colours) and borders
    • shadows
    • the palette and the message colours
    • links and focus
themes.ts · the new list · 1 line 
static readonly OWN: readonly string[] = ["spell"]

The options:

  1. On top of classic, in its own OWN list (chosen).
  2. A third apply() mode with no base sheet at all.
  3. Make spell one of the Fomantic names.

Net effect:

  • Spell loads like any theme: classic, then spell.css
  • the theme menu lists it apart from Fomantic's themes
  • spell.css carries a long list of classic overrides
