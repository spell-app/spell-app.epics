The converter gives each named example a section of its own. That section holds the example and any unnamed examples right after it; anything else goes back to the section around it.

  • <ui-docs-example header=X> becomes <ui-section id="<parent>-x" header=X> around the example. The example loses its header and keeps its description.
  • Header-less examples that follow (Fomantic's .another.example) join that section.
  • Any other content closes it and lands in the enclosing section, e.g. a markdown paragraph, or a "not ported" message.
  • A comment goes with the node after it: a GAP note before an example lands just before that example's section.
  • A generator marker (<!-- kitchen:start -->) closes every section and stays at the root.

<!-- before -->
<ui-docs-example header="Emphasis" description="...">...</ui-docs-example>

<!-- after -->
<ui-section id="examples-types-emphasis" header="Emphasis" sticky collapsible dividing>
  <ui-docs-example description="...">...</ui-docs-example>
</ui-section>

The options:

  1. Only examples in an example's section; prose goes to the parent (chosen).
  2. Prose right after an example goes into its section too.

Net effect:

  • folding an example's section hides only examples
  • explanations between examples stay visible in the parent section
