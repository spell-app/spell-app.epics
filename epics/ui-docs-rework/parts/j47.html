The pages for the 12 parts tags (e.g. ui-meta) have Examples, Usage and API tabs, but no Theming tab. The site check accepts that.

  • The parts have no tokens of their own: their looks are their owners' tokens, like --ui-card-header-font-size.
  • SiteCheck.OPTIONAL_TABS lets the Theming tab be absent.
  • yarn site:new TAG keeps the Theming tab for a split tag only when a family token names it, e.g. --ui-checkbox-radio-size.
  • Radio's and textarea's Theming tabs show a filtered tokens= subset of their family's table, with a live preview of the tag, and link the family page for the full list.
