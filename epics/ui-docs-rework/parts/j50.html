The site check's deep-link test now accepts a short page that can't scroll its target up to the sticky line, because the page ends first.

  • On short pages (ui-summary, ui-avatar), the first nested section can't scroll up to its sticky line: the page runs out.
  • landedState now reports bottom.
  • A target below its line, on a page scrolled to the bottom, is not counted as a problem.

The options:

  1. Accept "scrolled to the bottom" (chosen).
  2. Pad those pages with filler examples.

Net effect:

  • site:check passes on short pages, without filler
