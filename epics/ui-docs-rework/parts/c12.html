Opening a page with a link to a section (a hash in the URL) can show the page about 45px off for a frame or two, then settle on the right line.

  • On a first load with a hash, <ui-docs-toc> still scrolls to the target itself (scrollIntoView, a frame after the root is ready).
  • Then SiteSections lands it on its exact line, 2 frames later, and again at 400ms unless the reader has moved.
  • Page swaps and links within the page land once.
  • A possible fix: a toc attribute that leaves landing to the page.
