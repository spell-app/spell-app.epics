The palette button's overlay (the theme list and "Match system") is a <ui-popup> holding a hand-built menu, so it can look like the brand rather than Fomantic. Picking a theme leaves it open.

  • Built on <ui-popup on="click" basic>. Escape, an outside click and returning focus come from UI.overlays.
  • Its box is restyled through ::part(popup): 14px radius, the theme's surface, the floating shadow.
  • The list is a role=menu of native menuitemradio buttons:
    • one tab stop for the list (roving tabindex)
    • arrows, Home and End move
    • Enter, Space or a click picks
  • Chose that over <ui-menu> or <ui-dropdown>, which bring Fomantic's look.
  • A pick keeps the overlay open, so themes can be compared; Escape or an outside click closes it.
  • The tooltips are <ui-popup inverted size=mini>; the palette button's tooltip hides while the overlay is open.

The options:

  1. A popup with a native menu, open after a pick (chosen).
  2. <ui-menu> or <ui-dropdown>: Fomantic's look.
  • the overlay looks like the brand, and works from the keyboard
  • you can click through themes without reopening it
