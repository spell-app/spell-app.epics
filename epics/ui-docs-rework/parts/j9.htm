ui has no slot for an extra accent colour, so the brand's Polished Ivory rides on the brown hue, and info messages are lavender rather than ivory.

  • Polished Ivory is mapped onto brown. The brand's own Theme Creator did the same.
  • Info messages take lavender (the brand's aside tint), not ivory: ivory and the amber warning tint are too close to tell apart.
  • Secondary and grey use the brand's grey-blue at brand-700: white text on brand-600 is only 4.1:1.

The options for info messages:

  1. Ivory, like the TIP box in the brand's docs1 mockup.
  2. Lavender (chosen).
  3. Sky blue.

Net effect:

  • in the Spell theme, anything drawn in the brown hue shows as ivory
  • info and warning messages are easy to tell apart
  • secondary and grey buttons are a shade darker than brand-600
