The Spell theme keeps body text at 16px, not the brand's 15px. You'd notice that widgets and the docs' prose are the same size.

  • --ui-font-size: 16px is ui's default, and also the body size spell-doc.css sets for docs pages.
  • The brand's --text-base is 15px.
  • Why 16px: docs prose and widgets agree, and reading stays comfortable.

The options:

  1. 16px (chosen).
  2. 15px: the brand's density, but widgets a notch smaller than the docs' prose.
  • every doc site reads at 16px, widgets included
  • to go to the brand's size: one token, --ui-font-size in spell.css
