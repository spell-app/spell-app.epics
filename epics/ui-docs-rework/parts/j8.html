When you tab through a page, the focused control gets a solid 3px violet ring, 2px out from its edge, instead of the brand's soft halo.

  • The brand's focus is a 3px halo of violet at 28% opacity: about 1.5:1 contrast on white. That fails WCAG's 3:1 minimum for non-text contrast.
  • Chose a solid 3px ring:
    • light mode: violet-500 (4.3:1)
    • dark mode: lilac, violet-300
    • 2px out from the box (ui's usual offset)
  • Set through two tokens, --ui-focus-width and --ui-focus-color.
--ui-focus-width: 3px;
--ui-focus-color: light-dark(var(--spell-violet-500), var(--spell-violet-300));

The options:

  1. The brand's halo, as it is: too faint.
  2. A solid ring (chosen).
  3. The halo at 55% opacity.
  • keyboard focus is clearly visible and meets 3:1
  • it looks stronger than the brand's mockups
