Below 1200px, one slim top bar in the page's own colours replaces Fomantic's dark Spell UI menu. You'd see it in a narrow window or on a phone.

  • What the bar holds, by width:
    • below 1200px (the right column is hidden): the docked site header and the look controls
    • below 992px: also the menu button (it opens the nav flyout) and a "Spell UI" brand
    • below 768px: the site row moves into the flyout (no room left)
  • The old menu's Getting started, Grammar, Theming and Icons links are gone from the bar: the nav panel lists them.

The options:

  1. A slim bar in the page's colours (chosen).
  2. Keep Fomantic's dark menu: off-brand.
  3. A ui-menu: brings Fomantic's menu look back.
  • narrow screens show one light, on-brand bar instead of a dark menu
  • the page links live only in the nav panel
