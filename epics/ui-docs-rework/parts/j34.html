Sections on the Spell UI site start open. What you fold or unfold is remembered per page; a link that unfolds a section for you is not.

  • Your fold or unfold (a cancelable ui-open / ui-close from a page section) is saved in localStorage under spell-ui-site:folds:<path>. That's how the docs runtime does it too.
  • It's restored on every visit: before the sections draw on a first load, and without animation after a swap.
  • Everything else starts OPEN: Owen asked for collapsible, not collapsed.
  • A deep link into a folded section unfolds it for that visit only. Find-in-page and link landings are never saved, so your fold comes back next time.

The options:

  1. Save only the reader's own folds (chosen).
  2. Save a landing's unfold too.
  • a first visit shows every section open
  • a section you folded stays folded, even after a link opened it once
