On a first load, the page now stays hidden until the theme's stylesheet is in, as well as the layout and the elements. You'd notice that the page doesn't restyle itself a moment after it appears.

  • Before, the stored or default theme (Spell) restyled the page after the first paint.
  • Layout shift (CLS: how much content moves after it's first drawn; 0 is none) went from 0.03 to 0.0002.
  • The page shows at about 250ms, measured locally.

The options:

  1. Wait for the theme too (chosen).
  2. Show the page before the theme lands: a visible restyle, 0.03 shift.
  • a first load appears once, already in its theme
  • a slow theme sheet delays the whole page (not checked: whether ui-root's 5s cap, from the no-flash decision D2, covers this wait)
