The light / dark button in the docs, plan docs and goals header now has two states, sun and moon, showing what the page shows. Two clicks always bring you back to following the OS.

  • This is the non-docked <spell-site-header>.
  • Landing on the OS's own scheme forgets your choice, so the page follows the OS again. The tooltip says so: Light (click: dark, as the OS).
  • Chose this over:
    • a third "auto" state: every other click would look like it did nothing
    • a hidden gesture for "auto" (long-press, alt-click)
  • The catch: someone who wants light pinned while the OS goes dark at night can't pin it from this header. Only Spell UI's theme overlay can (with "Match system" off).
  • On the Spell UI site the icon click always stores the choice, as the prompt asked.

Example, with the OS in dark mode:

Click Page shows Stored
none yet dark (the OS) nothing
1st light light
2nd dark, the OS's scheme nothing: following the OS again

The options:

  1. Two states, and the OS's own scheme means "follow the OS" (chosen).
  2. A third "auto" state in the cycle.
  3. A hidden gesture for "auto".

Net effect:

  • one click always flips what you see
  • pinning the OS's opposite at night needs Spell UI's overlay
