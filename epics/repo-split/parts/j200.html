spellRepos() looked for a child repo in the kit or in the kit's own node_modules/. Installed from npm, the kit is node_modules/spell/ and the others sit beside it, so it now looks there too. The published spell ships repos.json5 (the order), and the published bins import the built entries: spell-pack and spell-types got entry files of their own (packMain.ts, typesMain.ts), which the checkout's bins import too.
