Chose to give element/tools/ its own TypeScript program, over folding the tools into element/'s:

  • its own configs:
    • tsconfig.json (node)
    • tsconfig.node.json
    • vite.config.ts
    • vitest.config.ts
  • a "spellTypes" entry
  • a row in the kit's kit.ts

Why, and how they fit:

  • element/src is browser-safe (no node types), and the tools are node code: the same split as server's.
  • The tools read element/src by its npm name, so by its declarations: e.g. spell-kit/element/SkeletonText.
  • Their tests are in element's tests' tools folder. They're excluded from tests/element's browser tsconfig.
  • Inside the kit, two aliases:
    • $/spell-element/tools/*
    • $/spell-element/test-helpers/* (was test/*)
  • yarn kit:ts | test | lint list element/tools beside element
