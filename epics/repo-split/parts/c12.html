spell-lang's typecheck() (the ts/solid target's check, used by spell compile and typescript.test.ts) reads app's SpellElement and <ui-*> JSX types (app/src/runner/spellElements.ts, app/src/solid/solid.types.ts). It imports nothing of the app, but finds the installed @spell-app/app at run time (APP_DIR). With yarn clone spell-lang alone those tests skip themselves and typecheck() throws. The way out: move those element types down (spell-lang's own, or spell-element), later work.
