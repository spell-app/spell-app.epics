spell-lang's typecheck() needs the app's element types, so it fails without spell-app.

  • It's the ts/solid target's check, used by spell compile and typescript.test.ts.
  • It reads the app's SpellElement and the JSX types of <ui-*> tags, from:
    • spellElements.ts
    • solid.types.ts
  • It imports nothing of the app, but finds the installed @spell-app/app at run time (APP_DIR).
  • With yarn clone spell-lang alone, those tests skip themselves and typecheck() throws.
  • The way out: move those element types down (spell-lang's own, or spell-element), later work.
