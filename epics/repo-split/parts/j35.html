The codemod turns $/x/rest into @spell-app/x/rest, with one exception: a family's barrel, $/ui/components/ui-code, becomes @spell-app/ui/ui-code. That's the name Spell UI already publishes, so these imports won't change at release. One pattern in ui's exports covers every family: "./ui-*": "./components/ui-*/index.ts". The brief's example said @spell-app/ui/components/ui-code, which isn't published; it's 10 imports in the app and brand if you'd rather have that.
