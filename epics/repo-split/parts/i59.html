spell-ui has its own vite-plus and vitest: same version, a second install. That breaks spell-app's type check of its Vitest config.

  • The app's shared Vite settings (vite.shared.ts) import spell-ui's vite.config.ts.
  • Its declarations bring that copy's vitest augmentation in.
  • When it loads before spell-app's, test.browser.provider: playwright() fails to type-check in vitest.config.ts. It gets a BrowserProviderOption from two vitests.

Worked round: tsconfig.node.json lists the configs FIRST in include, with a comment.

The fix is either:

  • one copy of vite-plus in the install (the kit's resolutions)
  • or emitIconPacks() moved out of spell-ui's vite.config.ts
