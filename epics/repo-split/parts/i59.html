spell-ui has its own node_modules/vite-plus and vitest (same version, a second install). app/vite.shared.ts imports spell-ui/vite.config.ts, whose declarations bring that copy's vitest augmentation in; when it loads before spell-app's, test.browser.provider: playwright() in vitest.config.ts fails to type-check (BrowserProviderOption from two vitests).

Worked round: tsconfig.node.json lists the configs FIRST in include, with a comment. The fix is one copy of vite-plus in the install (the kit's resolutions), or emitIconPacks() moved out of spell-ui's vite.config.ts.
