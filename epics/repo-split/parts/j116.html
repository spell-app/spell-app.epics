spell pack add <pack> <tag> (was spell dev pack element) writes a new family into a pack repo:

  • the family, into the pack's components/<tag>/
  • its test, into that pack repo's own tests/components/<tag>/, where spell-brand and spell-epic keep theirs
  • it went to the monorepo's tests/<pack>/ before
  • A pack is found by its pack name among the kit's child repos whose package.json has spellPack.
    • brand is spell-brand
    • epics is spell-epic
  • spell pack check with no name checks every one of them.
