packModuleKey() is gone. A page now offers each shared module under the exact name the source imports:

  • spell-kit/element, spell-ui/core and spell-ui/forms
  • the families as spell-ui/components/ui-code, ui-markdown and ui-root

A pack written outside the repos that imports the published short name (spell-ui/ui-code) would not match a family key. No such pack exists today.

PACK_FORMAT is now spell-pack 2, so every pack was rebuilt once. The generated banners now say spell pack build.

The run-time Symbol.for keys are renamed the same way: @spell-app/ui:runtime became spell-ui:runtime, and likewise spell-kit:cells, spell-lang:element-tag / element-class, spell-ui:docs-example-sources and spell-app:spell-elements. Every bundle that carries them was rebuilt.
