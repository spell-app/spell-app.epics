packModuleKey() is gone. A page now offers each shared module under the exact name the source imports:

  • spell-kit/element
  • spell-ui/core
  • spell-ui/forms
  • the families, e.g. spell-ui/components/ui-code, and likewise ui-markdown and ui-root

A pack written outside the repos that imports the published short name (spell-ui/ui-code) would not match a family key. No such pack exists today.

PACK_FORMAT is now spell-pack 2, so every pack was rebuilt once. The generated banners now say spell pack build.

The run-time Symbol.for keys are renamed the same way:

  • @spell-app/ui:runtime became spell-ui:runtime
  • and likewise:
    • spell-kit:cells
    • spell-lang:element-tag / element-class
    • spell-ui:docs-example-sources
    • spell-app:spell-elements
  • Every bundle that carries them was rebuilt.
