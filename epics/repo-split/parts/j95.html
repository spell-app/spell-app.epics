Chose to make DevServer.start() take root (the package's folder, whose vite.config.ts it runs) and NodePackage's lookups start in process.cwd() by default, over their old defaults (Spell UI's folder, from import.meta.url), because in the kit those would point at the kit. Spell UI's three DevServer callers pass their root; NodePackage's callers run as Spell UI's yarn scripts, in its folder, so they find what they found.

  • DevServer.start({ root, port })
