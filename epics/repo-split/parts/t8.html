  • WWOD is shared: every checkout reads it, main included.
  • main has no exports maps yet.
  • In WWOD.md §4, a new first bullet group, "Package names across repos":
  • How to name a package:
    • Inside ONE future repo, its alias, $/name: spell imports $/parser.
    • From ANOTHER repo, its npm name, @spell-app/ui/core: an entry its package file's exports names.
    • A Spell UI family by its published entry, @spell-app/ui/ui-code.
  • Lint fails a $/ alias into another repo: spell/no-cross-repo-alias.
    • The repos are REPO_OF, in the toolchain's lint.imports.ts.
  • A string that only names a module (a packModules key) keeps its $/ name.
