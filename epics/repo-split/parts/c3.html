Cutting the repos (P8) and the multi-repo tooling (P12) change main itself, once the seam phases (P1 to P6) have merged.

  • A worktree cut from main before then still has every package in one repo.
  • Its branch must merge main before the cutover, or its changes to the moved packages have to be carried into the new repos by hand.
  • So, before P8 starts: every running epic merges main, or parks.
  • P8 starts only once the other open worktrees have merged main or parked
