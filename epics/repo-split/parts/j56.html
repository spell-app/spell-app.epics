Decided over 'use the built ones': a repo's root yarn ts (and a flat repo's) runs spell-types --below first, which builds every package of the LOWER repos its packages depend on (found by their @spell-app/* dependencies through node_modules), lowest first. Incremental, so an up-to-date one costs about a second, and what a repo checks against is never stale. A single package's yarn ts (cd spell-lang/core) uses what's built.

spell-types --below still builds the lower repos first. It finds them by their workspace: dependencies (spell-kit, spell-ui ...), no longer by @spell-app/* names: the kit, and spell-ui for the repos above it (J79).
