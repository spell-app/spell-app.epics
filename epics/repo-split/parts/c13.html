spell dev pack new writes packages/<name>/ and wires it into a monorepo root (workspaces, the alias table, vitest, lint, generators). In the split a new component pack would be a repo of its own (like spell-brand) or a package of one. Its tests now copy a frozen fixture of the old root files (tests/cli/src/dev/fixtures/monorepo/). P9 or a later epic decides what pack new makes.
