The rename covers the package, its folders, its alias and every mention of it as a package. Two names kept the old word:

  • SpellSolid (E.SpellSolid, type E.SpellSolidSettings): the settings a set's runtime hands the package as it loads. Its natural new name, SpellElement, is already the app's runner's class, the element a drawable thing draws as (packages/app/src/runner/SpellElement.tsx), and the app imports both.
  • The app's spell-solid.js and spell-solid-shared.js (vite.solid.config.ts): bundles that carry Solid itself, plus Spell UI's core, not this package.

Either is a rename of its own later, if you want one (e.g. ElementSettings).
