Each package's exports lists, one by one, the subpaths another future repo imports: 71 found by the codemod, plus the ones named by npm name outside it (configs, spell-epic's plain-node imports, tests). The list is the package's public surface, and tsc fails a new crossing import until its entry is added. Every package gets at least its barrel, ".". The two patterns: Spell UI's families (./ui-*) and its icon packs (./icons/*).

Stand-ins: ./src/* in server and assembler, because spell-epic still imports @spell-app/server/src/page/settings.ts and the like by path. New entries plain node loads replace them (/page/settings, /page/prune, /checkout, @spell-app/assembler/DataFile); they go once spell-epic switches (its T).
