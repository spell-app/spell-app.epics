Why not:

  • a custom 'source' condition: every Vite config, tsc, tsx and spell-epic's esbuild would have to turn it on, and one that didn't would quietly read a stale dist/
  • separate source subpaths: the barrel (@spell-app/ui) can't have two

The catch: npm publish ignores publishConfig.exports, so publish with yarn.
