Fixed in P7. Found by P6's design review: after picks in later columns, the first column kept .peek, covering the next column (and catching clicks on a phone) until a click outside the tree.

Why: the tree heard pointerover, focusin and focusout on its own element. A move between two boxes inside its shadow root (the rows from items) never reaches the element, as both ends of it are the element there, so the column under the pointer or focus was never updated after the first.

The fix: those three are heard on the shadow root (markup rows pass through it by their slots), and a column that turns narrow as a later one is picked forgets the pointer and focus. Test: real clicks down three columns, the pointer leaves, no column peeks.
