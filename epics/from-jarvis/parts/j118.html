Seen re-making the dropdown's pictures (I31). Since debut P13 a menu opens on whichever side has more room (position-try-order: most-block-size), so on the dropdown page's lower examples, Chromium opens a short menu upward even though it fits below, over the heading and example above it.

  • Firefox and WebKit still open them below: they don't follow that rule yet.
  • The pictures keep it as built. If menus should open below whenever they fit, as a Mac's popup menus do, that's a small CSS change in the dropdown's sheet.

Open every Spell UI dropdown-style popup below whenever it fits whole, like a Mac's popup menus. Only when it fits on neither side does it shrink and scroll, on the side with more room.

  • Why not just drop position-try-order: most-block-size: the menu's height is capped to the room beside its button, so it always "fits" below. Dropped alone, a menu by the window's bottom would open below as a sliver that scrolls.
  • So the browser tries, in order: below at full height, then above at full height, then a capped, scrolling menu. The capped try asks for at least half the window's room (less its button), so it lands below only when below is the bigger side, else above.
  • The same four popups that open on the roomier side today, each in its own sheet:
    • the dropdown's menu (UIDropdown.css, the one debut P13 changed)
    • the select's picker (UISelect.css)
    • the icon picker's panel (UIIconPicker.css)
    • a shrinkable menu's overflow menu (UIMenu.css)
  • Left as it is: the editor's Inspect Other Views side menu (the app's Palette.css), which asks for the roomier side on purpose, since it's often long.
