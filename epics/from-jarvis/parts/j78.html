The designer found the button look's popup stayed open the whole upload, came loose when its button scrolled off, and covered the page's title, tabs and next section.

  • Picked: a 2-second glimpse as an upload starts, then the popup shows only on hover or focus (POPUP_GLIMPSE). It stays while the pointer or the focus is on it.
  • Also set: position-visibility: anchors-visible on the popup, so a hover-opened popup hides when its button scrolls out of view.
  • Not picked: anchors-visible alone. It hides the popup only when the button scrolls out, so the popup would still sit over the next section for the whole upload.
  • ui-popup's pointer arrow is drawn (it's on unless appearance="basic"), but it's white on the near-white example frame, so it's hard to see. Making it clearer is a ui-popup change (its arrow's shadow is a private token), so it's left as it is.
