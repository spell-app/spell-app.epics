Designer finding 2 (P7 fix pass): the tools row is in the tree's shadow root, so a page's field can only reach it through a slot. <ui-tree> gains a tools slot (Spanish herramientas), as <ui-example> has one. Slotted without the tools attribute, the line holds the field alone. The buttons scroll sideways and never wrap; the field drops to its own line when there's no room. In the docs, the field is 2.5em short of the line's end, so the example's code button doesn't cover it.
