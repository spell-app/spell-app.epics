For the uploader's input look (File Upload In Spell), the Browse button sits joined to a <ui-input>, written the way its docs page writes one. Your note on the mockup (C18): the borders don't line up.

Measured: the two are the same height and their edges meet, but they don't look like one control.

  • The field has a thin grey border; the button has none, only a pale fill, so the border stops where the button starts.
  • The field's corners are 10px round; the button's are 8px.
  • The input docs page's own Search example shows the same thing: it's the input's look, not the mockup's.

By the house style, a look an element can't give is your call, not a style written around it.

  • the input's joined action buttons take the field's border and corner size, everywhere they're used
  • the uploader's input look then needs no styles of its own for the join
