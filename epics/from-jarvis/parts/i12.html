The matches under "Elsewhere in the tree" are clicked, not reached with the arrow keys: up and down stop at the filtered column's last row. A screen reader still finds them (a labelled group of tree items). Left for later.

The matches become part of the filtered column for the keys, in both kinds of tree (rows from markup, and from data).

  • Down on the column's last row moves the focus onto the first match; up and down then walk the matches, and up on the first goes back to the last row.
  • Home and End on a match go to the first and last match.
  • Moving onto a match only moves the focus: nothing is picked, so the columns stay put.
  • Enter or Space on a match opens its path and selects it, as a click does.
  • The matches keep tabindex="-1": the tree is still one tab stop, its picked row's.
  • A screen reader hears each match with its path ("loaf.jpg, in Acme › Menu › photos"), a new text, matchIn, in English and Spanish.
