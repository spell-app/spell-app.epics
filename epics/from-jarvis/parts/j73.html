In a column view, the keys pick each row they land on, as a Mac's Finder does. On the matches under Elsewhere in the tree (I12), they only move the focus: picking one opens its whole path, which would move the columns and the filtered column at every key press. Enter or Space opens it, as a click does.

Up from the first match goes back to the column's last row, and picks it only when it isn't the column's pick already.
