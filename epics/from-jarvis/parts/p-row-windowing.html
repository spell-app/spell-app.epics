A column draws every row: fine at 400, slow at several thousand.

  • A column of 5,000 rows opens as fast as one of 300
  • Keys, selection, filter and the breadcrumb still work

A column draws only what's needed, in data mode, through the kit's one way of drawing a long list, or its own windowing: this phase's choice (coherence).

  • drawnInWindow() is how every long list in Spell draws: the first 24, then more as you scroll near the end. The coherence catalog's "a long list, drawn as you scroll" says never a scroll listener of a list's own.
  • It lives in the parser's core today, which Spell UI can't use; it moves down into spell/element, and the core imports it from there.
  • Opening a 5,000-row column draws 24 rows: as fast as a 300-row one.
  • The catch: what's drawn stays drawn, so a column scrolled to its end (or End pressed) is 5,000 elements, as every Spell list is.
  • Only the rows in view plus a margin are drawn, with spacers above and below; rows scrolled past are taken down again.
  • Stays fast at the end of the column too.
  • A second mechanism: its own catalog entry, and an exception written into drawnInWindow()'s "never" line.
