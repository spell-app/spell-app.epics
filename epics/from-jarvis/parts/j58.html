openTo(2) opens the top rows, so they and the rows inside them show; openTo(1) is closeAll(). Deeper rows close, so Open 2 levels after Open 3 levels goes back.

In columns it shows that many columns: each column's pick stays, else its first row that holds rows is picked, and the last is selected as a click would (ui-selecting, ui-changed). Close all goes back to the first column, nothing picked, with no ui-changed.
