A subclass like <netlist-columns> works, but nothing documents how, and the column view has rough edges the netlists epic listed.

  • A documented way to tweak the look from a subclass
  • The five rough edges fixed, or each one said why not
  • Column mode has its own unit tests

The docs page shows a supported subclass; the rough edges fixed: a chevron on empty children, a settled event, fewer hidden shadow roots, and unit tests for column mode.

  1. A supported subclass: the docs page shows a small subclass (its own sheet in elementSetup, its rowClass, its tokens), written as <netlist-columns> does it, and the tree's docstring says which members are for subclasses.
  2. A children function that returns nothing loses its chevron once opened.
  3. A settled event: ui-settled, sent once the arrow keys stop for a moment, for pages doing heavy work per pick.
  4. Rows from markup: replace the "rebuild up to 5 times" wait with waiting for each row's tag to be defined.
  5. Hidden shadow roots: look at whether a row in column mode can skip building its own; if not, say why in its docstring.
  6. Unit tests for column mode, and the visual baselines brought up to date.
  7. Checked by: ui's full tests and lint.
