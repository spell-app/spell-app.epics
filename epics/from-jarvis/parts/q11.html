This is for Subclass Elements In Spell too. A project's tree view overrides the tree's hooks: rowClass(item) gives each row its classes, for its stylesheet.

Those hooks have only code names today. The two sided view's hooks have Spell words of their own (to draw its front), because it's written in Spell. A Spell UI element is written in TypeScript, so its hooks need words from somewhere. New words need your OK.

    its "row class" of (an item) is
