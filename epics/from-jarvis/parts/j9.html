<ui-tree-diagram> and <ui-diagram> read a <ui-json> child as their data, and both have a default <slot>.

  • Now that <ui-json> draws, that child would show inside the diagram.
  • So each diagram's sheet hides a slotted ui-json / es-json: pages and Spell programs written that way look the same, with no hidden to add.
  • Rejected: making every such page add hidden, and having <ui-json> guess whether its parent reads it.
