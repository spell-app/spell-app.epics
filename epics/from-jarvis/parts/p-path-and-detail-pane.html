A reload loses where you were in the tree, and what you picked can only be shown somewhere else on the page.

  • A reload opens the tree where you left it
  • A detail pane at the end of the columns shows the picked item

The tree remembers its last path for the session, and opens it again when the page sets none; its last pane can be any view, which changes with what's picked, sizes to fit, and can stick to the side.
