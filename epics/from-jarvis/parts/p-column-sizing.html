Every column is the same width, and a resize can't fit a column to its rows or move them all at once.

  • Columns can size to their contents, and needn't be equal
  • Resizing works as it does in the Finder
  • A size you dragged stays after a reload

A size setting (pixels, a percentage, or auto to fit what's shown), and the Finder's resizing: drag one edge, Option-drag for all, double-click to fit the longest row; what you set is remembered.
