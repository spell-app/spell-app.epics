When the open path is deeper than the tree is wide, the first columns scroll out of view, and nothing says where you are.

  • You always see the whole path, and can click back to any column
  • Deep paths keep the last columns readable; the early ones narrow to a strip with the picked row's name

The tree's sticky-path line shows above the columns too, each part a way back to its column, styled like the editor's sticky header; and the columns before the last few narrow to a strip (coherence: narrow, not collapse), widening on hover or focus.

  • The path line becomes a nav a screen reader announces, its parts real buttons you can Tab to, like <ui-breadcrumbs>.
  • The same in the outline, where sticky-path is hidden from screen readers today.
  • It stays a picture of where you are; the columns' own keys are the way back.
  • Less to change in the outline view.
