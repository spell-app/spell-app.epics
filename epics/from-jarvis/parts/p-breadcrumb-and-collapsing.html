When the open path is deeper than the tree is wide, the first columns scroll out of view, and nothing says where you are.

  • You always see the whole path, and can click back to any column
  • Deep paths keep the last columns readable; the early ones are a strip with the picked row's name

A breadcrumb above the columns, each part a way back to its column, styled like the editor's sticky header; and the columns before the last few shrink to a narrow strip, widening on hover or focus.
