columnsRoomPx = the tree's width less the pane's, at least 80px: narrow strips and shared widths work out of that. The pane's own content width is capped by --ui-tree-detail-max-width (50%); a dragged width goes from 80px to the tree's width less 80px. With sticky-detail, showing the selected column takes the pane's width off the box, so that column never hides under it.
