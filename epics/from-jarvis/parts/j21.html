One column view, two sources of rows.

  • Rows written as <ui-tree-item>s stay main's column view: each row draws itself, with its actions slot and icons.
  • Rows given as items are drawn by the tree itself, in the same box, with the same titles, widths and keys.
  • The handoff instead turned markup rows into data. Not doing that drops two of its rough edges: no waiting for rows' tags (nothing is converted), and no hidden shadow roots (every markup row shows what it builds).
