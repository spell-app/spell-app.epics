Which deployments belong on the Jarvis Jobs page: system deployments, which can be read now, or job deployments, which wait for a sign-in token?

"Deployment" means two things in Jarvis.

  • Job deployments: the Prefect deployment each job runs from. Listed only by the Control System, which refuses the API-key tokens Spell can use (ingest-jarvis-api's I3).
  • System deployments: a system design on real hardware. Readable now, but two of their routes answer one record per instance, which neither converter reads yet.

The Jobs page's subtitle says "Deployments, In-Flight, Completed". The Claude in ingest-jarvis-api recommended job deployments.

  • P16 teaches the reader one record per thing, so the page can show each instance's status.
  • Job deployments follow once there's a token the Control System takes: a Jarvis todo.
  • Why recommended: something real on the page now, and the Spell work helps any API.
  • No Spell phase: P16 is dropped. The page waits for the sign-in token.
  • P16 as in A; the job list waits on the token.
  • P16 stays
  • Jarvis todos: the page's list, job deployments after I3, ingest-jarvis-api's P9 marked dropped
