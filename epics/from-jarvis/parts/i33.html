On a labeled button (the icon in its own tinted block), the icon fills the whole block. The copy glyph comes out 25 x 28.6px beside 15px words; the cart on Checkout, 24 x 22px. A plain button's icon is 1em (15px).

  • The icon in the block is the same size as a plain button's icon, 1em tall, centred in the block. The block keeps its size and tint.
  • In the component (<ui-button>), for every labeled icon button, not only the teal Copy one.
