The outline view has no Open 2 levels, Open 3 levels or Close all; and its filter, which already searches the whole outline (coherence), can't reach data rows not opened yet, nor search across columns.

Methods and a small toolbar for opening to a depth and closing all (open / close, Spell's words, not expand / collapse: coherence); and the existing filter reaching lazy data rows and working across columns, instead of a second search.

  1. Methods on the tree: openTo(depth) and closeAll() (coherence: Spell's word list says open / close, never expand / collapse), for markup rows and data rows alike (a data row's children function runs as it opens).
  2. An optional toolbar part with "Open 2 levels", "Open 3 levels" and "Close all", in the vocabulary and Spanish.
  3. No new search (coherence): main's filter already searches the whole outline (applyFilter()). It's taken further instead:
    • it reaches data rows' lazy children, opening only as far as a match needs, with a limit
    • in columns, it shows the matches at any depth, and picking one opens its path; the one-column filter Land Tree Columns brought in becomes its first step
  4. Checked by: unit tests; a filter over a generated tree the size of the paste's sample (7,715 nodes), timed.
  5. Left out: fuzzy matching.
  • openTo(levels) and closeAll() on the tree, through a new DOMTreeElement (J58). openTo opens level by level and waits for lazy or promised rows; it returns a promise, and picks the path so far while a list loads (J67).
  • The tools attribute: 'Open 2 levels', 'Open 3 levels' and 'Close all', drawn as Spell UI's mini basic buttons on one line, with a tools slot at its end for a page's own field (J55, J66). English and Spanish.
  • One filter, no new search: in columns it filters the last column and lists every other match at any depth under 'Elsewhere in the tree', each with its icon and path; a click opens the path to it (J56). It runs lazy children one level at a time with a spinner and the words 'Looking through the tree', lists 200 matches and stops after 100,000 rows (J57). A kept pick that doesn't match is dimmed (J68).
