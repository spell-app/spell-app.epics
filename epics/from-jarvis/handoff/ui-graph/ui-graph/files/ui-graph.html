<ui-graph>

A graph draws nodes joined by edges, laid out left to right; a node may hold a graph of its own, opened in place, in a modal, or in its stead.

Flat graph

A few nodes with ports, as JSON in the graph attribute. Drag to pan, wheel to zoom; click a node, an edge or a port to select it; hover an edge to light its ends.

Children: inline

child-view="inline", the default: the node grows into a container, its graph inside it. CPU starts open; ALU loads its children lazily when it opens. Edges with no node reach the enclosing node's ports.

Children: modal

child-view="modal": the children open in a <ui-modal> holding another graph, the node's ports as pins on its sides.

Children: drill

child-view="drill": the drawing shows the children instead, with breadcrumbs back up.

Big

150 generated nodes and about 300 edges in one level, laid out as it loads. Some nodes hold a generated graph of their own.
