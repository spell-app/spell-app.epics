A person refers to friends, who refer back: loading must keep the person before reading its friends, or it would go round forever.

  • Chose: each thing is made with its text, numbers and yes-or-no values, kept, then given its lists and the things it refers to.
  • The catch: a type's own setup (create()) sees the plain values only, not its references.
  • Over: reading every reference before making the thing, as a page's state does, which can't handle a person and friend who refer to each other.
