Another project or space: yes, but there's a snag with a bare @org at the end. A reference has to say WHERE the thing is saved, and a place is a space AND a project:

  • @three-degrees is the org's space; matt@three-degrees is Matt's own space there.
  • A card in Matt's game is in project bubble-trouble, in Matt's space.
  • So the end of the reference is a whole address, which can have its own @.
                          A · place first                              B · thing first (yours)
a person of an org        @three-degrees:Person#matt                   Person#matt@three-degrees
a card in Matt's game     matt@three-degrees:bubble-trouble/Card#26    Card#26@matt@three-degrees:bubble-trouble
a thing in the library    @spell:library/people/Person#owen            Person#owen@spell:library/people
