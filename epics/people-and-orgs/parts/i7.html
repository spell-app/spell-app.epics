With --target, spell compile --stale decides whether a project is out of date from ALL its targets' output, not just the one asked for.

  • The check, staleBecause(), looks at each of the project's own targets, from its project.json
  • So spell compile --stale --target py @examples/Solitaire on a project whose targets don't include Python says up to date, and writes no Python, even though there is none
  • And when only another target's file is missing, it compiles just X, and the next run still finds the project out of date

Net effect (once fixed):

  • with --target X, the check looks at X's output, plus the declarations and words
