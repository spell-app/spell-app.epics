Owen, after P1 (2026-10-12): "is TypeSpecifierList necessary? should be is a list of people -- if I said as it's a typo. Was this added just for the example I gave?"

  • Yes: the sentence form as a list of people was added only for that example, and is gone.
  • Instead, in a type's outline body, - its "people" is a list of people declares a list property.
  • It didn't work before, not even as is a new list of people: both now do, and mean the same, an empty list to start with.
  • The sentence form stays as it was: a friend-list has people as a new list of people.
