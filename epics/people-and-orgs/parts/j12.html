Your note on the language decision (Q2) wrote a reference as "@ref": "matt@spell", an address. Addresses come in P4, and the people store that uses them in P9.

  • Chose: the saver takes a function that names each entry and reference; by default it says the kind and the id, Person/matt.
  • The people store will pass one that says the address, matt@spell, so its files read as you wrote.
  • Over: addresses in the saver itself. It's in the core, which knows nothing of people, orgs or spaces.
"friends": [{ "@ref": "Person/matt" }]     // the saver's default
"friends": [{ "@ref": "matt@spell" }]      // the people store's, from P9
  • the core's saving needs no addresses
  • P9 decides the files' references
