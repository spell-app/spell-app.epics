Chose to move App UI's _images/ and _examples/ folders into its docs folder under their own names, over renaming them with two underscores.

  • they were in the app's components/ folder
  • the other names would have been __images/ and __examples/
  • Q35 gives two underscores to only two of a set's folders: its page frame (__parts/) and data (__data/)
  • pictures and snippets are the pages' own content, which a page links like any file
  • App UI's docs folder:
    • __parts/
    • __data/
    • _images/
    • _examples/
    • and the pages
  • yarn site:pictures writes into docs/_images/
