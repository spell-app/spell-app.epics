The phase plan said declarations and words, which stay checked in beside the source, would be marked generated and regenerated on a merge; neither was built.

  • The plan's goal, for Name.declarations.json and Name.en.js:
    • the root .gitattributes marks them generated
    • the merge command's table, GENERATORS, regenerates them
  • Today: neither file kind is in either place; only a library's .lib.ts is
  • What happens: when both sides of a merge changed a project, its declarations and words are merged line by line, which can leave a file that matches neither side's source
  • Less pressing than before this phase: the compiled programs and scope packs, which churned most, are no longer checked in, so they never conflict

Net effect (once fixed):

  • declarations and words collapse in GitHub's diffs, and a merge regenerates them from the merged source
