The names compiled files had before this phase are still on the list of names a project's file list leaves out: .compiled.js, .compiled.tsx, .compiled.py and .elements.js.

  • Why: a checkout that compiled before this phase still has, say, Solitaire.compiled.js beside its source, no longer tracked
  • Without them on the list, the app would show that file as one of the project's own, and take a .js one for one of its imports
  • Cost: four names, OLD_BUILT_SUFFIXES
  • Nothing deletes the old files: they're harmless, and someone may want to compare
  • old compiled files left in a checkout are ignored: never listed, never imported
  • delete them by hand when convenient; the list can go once no checkout has any
