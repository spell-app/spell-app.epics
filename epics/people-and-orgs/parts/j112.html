The comment above a property is its statement's text, as written. An outline line's dash isn't part of what the statement matched, so the writer puts it back on any line starting it or its.

  • e.g. // - its "friend-lists" is a list of friend-lists, as in Owen's example
  • a sentence stays as written: // cards have a suit as one of clubs, diamonds, hearts or spades
  • Every target's comment reads like the line Owen wrote.
  • A sentence starting "it" outside an outline would get a dash too. None does today.
