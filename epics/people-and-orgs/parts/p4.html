References to things in another place are built here too: Type#id@place, as in Person#matt@three-degrees. Owen settled it in the decision on references to another place (Q18), answering the issue about two ways to write a reference (I2).

  • Why here: a reference to another place names that place by its address, and addresses arrive in this phase.
  • Inside one project nothing changes: a saved reference stays Person#matt.
  • The place is a whole address (Q15), which can hold an @ of its own: Card#26@matt@three-degrees:bubble-trouble.
  • So the reader splits at the FIRST @ after the #, and an id containing @ is refused.
  • The saver writes this form for a thing saved in another place; the files list gains both cores’ savers.
