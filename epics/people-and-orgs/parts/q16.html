How a person records who someone is to them. Owen, in a comment on the plan:

relationships - type ("father"), person (ref), notes (another type)

  • A relationship is its own kind, with three parts.
  • Its type, in words: "father", "colleague".
  • The other person, by reference: saved as their address, never a copy.
  • Notes, a kind of their own, so a relationship can hold several.
  • Friend lists hold relationships: the "family" list holds "father: matt", with notes.
a relationship is a thing where:
	- it has a type as text           // father, colleague ...
	- it has a person as a person     // saved as a reference
	- it has notes as a new list of notes

a note is a thing where:
	- it has a body as text

a friend-list is a thing where:
	- it has a name as text           // family, work ...
	- it has relationships as a new list of relationships

  • A friend list holds relationships, not bare people
