The Solid router matches no route for a URL with an empty part (/edit/a//b; its issue #567, on purpose). So the app's URLs (Q40) go through a small wrapper round the browser's history, addressHistory(): the router reads /edit/@spell/examples/solitaire/%2FCard.spell, and the address bar keeps /edit/@spell/examples/solitaire//Card.spell.

  • The app navigates in the router's form (toRouter(), in its navigator), and each URL the router writes goes back to //.
  • Pasting a URL with //, back and forward all work: a test drives each.
