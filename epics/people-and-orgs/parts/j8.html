The sentence a dated-event is saved inline compiles to one line of code that marks the class.

  • Chose: the compiler puts it INSIDE the class, as static savedInline = true, the way it already places other class-wide values.
  • Over: a line after the class, DatedEvent.savedInline = true, which is how "belongs to one pile" compiles. That one must come after, to win over other code; this one has nothing to win over.
  • reading the compiled class shows it's saved inline, at a glance
