The library is packages/spaces/@spell/library/people/People.spell, compiled to people.lib.ts (committed; a test fails while it's stale).

  • A person: names as one text, a nickname, emails, dated events, friend lists (each a list of relationships: a kind, a person by reference, notes), notes. A person is saved in their own file.
  • An org: a name and its memberships; saved in its own file. A membership is a person (by reference) and a role, one of member, admin, spell-admin (the SpellAdmin role, J46).
  • Memberships are kept inside the org's file for now; People On Disk (P9) may give them the __members/ folder the spaces page named.
  • VS Code checks __me.json5 and __org.json5 against Person's and Org's schemas, through the repo's settings, once the library has been compiled in a checkout
