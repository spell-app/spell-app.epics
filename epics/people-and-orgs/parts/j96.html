The library is spaces' People.spell, compiled to people.lib.ts (committed; a test fails while it's stale).

  • A person, saved in their own file:
    • names, as one text
    • a nickname, emails, dated events, notes
    • friend lists, each a list of relationships: a kind, a person by reference, notes
  • An org, saved in its own file: a name and its memberships.
    • A membership is a person (by reference) and a role.
    • the roles: member, admin
    • spell-admin, the SpellAdmin role (J46)
  • Memberships are kept inside the org's file for now; People On Disk (P9) may give them the __members/ folder the spaces page named.
  • VS Code checks __me.json5 and __org.json5 against Person's and Org's schemas, through the repo's settings, once the library has been compiled in a checkout
