Owen, on the spaces page (2026-10-13, his note on "One layout"): "use $parts and $data".

With the __ names (Q33), a set's two docs folders are renamed:

  • docs/_parts/ becomes docs/__parts/
  • docs/_data/ becomes docs/__data/
  • They are the set's own, never a docs page, so they take the same mark as every special name.
  • The docs site's script, laid over at /components/<set>/_assets/ by the page server, is not in a set's folder, and keeps its name.
  • Component Sets Layout (P18) moves each set's pages with __parts/ and __data/
  • spell pack build writes docs/__data/
  • Spell UI's site script reads a set's frame and data from the new names
