    • __org.json5
    • __project.json5
    • components/__pack/
    • __members/
    • __settings/
    • __sessions/
    • __docs/

  • The keys inside a file keep their $ (J50), and a type's version is Person__v2 (J51): my calls.
  • Sorting, checked:
    • VS Code's Explorer lists __ names first.
    • A terminal's ls (byte order) lists capitalised names first, then __ names, then lower-case ones.
      • e.g. Errors.spell above __project.json5
    • A space's folders are lower-case, so there they're on top.
    • The dollar sign sorted first in ls, second in VS Code.
