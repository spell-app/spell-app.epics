RunningEpics: a worktree's epic pages are the content folder's (url /epics/...) unless it has its own (a real folder, or an old place: EPICS_DIRS), at /worktrees/<w>/... as before. The Epics list page is recognised by real path too (isIndex()), so it renders whether the server found it through the content mount or an older checkout's link. Worktrees.epicsDir() likewise: a checkout's own REAL epics folder first, else the content folder's.
