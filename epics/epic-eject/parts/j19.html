Chose one absolute prefix for the engine's files, /_epic/, over a mount of /packages/epics/ or relative paths.

  • Why: the engine is one copy for every repo, and a repo's content folder can't reach it by a relative path.
  • The prefix starts with _, so no content folder's own folder takes it.
  • Works at any depth, under /r/<repo>/ and /worktrees/<w>/.
  • A migrated page opened from file:// no longer finds its assets: check-spell answers the routes from the engine's folders itself.
  • Same URL for every repo and depth
  • No migration
  • A repo's own packages/ folder would clash
  • Every page loads the engine from /_epic/ once migrated
