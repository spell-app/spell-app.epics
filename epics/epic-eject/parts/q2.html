  • What's at stake: whether epic work done in a worktree is live at once, and how easy the kit is to release.
  • A new repo, ~/www/epic-kit, loaded as a local plugin.

  • The catch: two repos to keep in step while spell's packages change under it (Q5).
  • The epics package stays where it is, loaded as a plugin from MAIN's working tree.
  • Still one copy for every window.
  • The catch: epic work done in a worktree still isn't live until it merges into main.
  • The catch: harder to release on its own, and other projects reach into spell-app for it.
