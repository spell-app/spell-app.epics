Every spell-* tag inside a <spell-app> loads as <tag>.js, beside the app's own script: spell-editor loads spell-editor.js.

  • The rule is SpellApp.ownTagLoader().
  • Why a naming rule, not a list of spell's tags:
    • each family's script is already named after its tag (the element build)
    • the light panels of T12 will follow it, without a change here
  • The rule (chosen): an unknown spell-foo is tried, and reported if it fails (ui-error, reason failed).
  • A catalog baked into spell-app.js: an unknown tag is reported (unknown) without a request, but it's one more thing to keep in step.
  • a page with only <spell-app>s never downloads the editor: checked in a browser, on the demo and on spell run's page
