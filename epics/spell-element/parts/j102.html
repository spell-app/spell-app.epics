Once built, <spell-app> loads its icon packs from beside its own script; in dev and tests, from Spell UI's default.

  • Built: the icon-packs/ folder beside spell-app.js, or in its assets.
    • The switch is import.meta.env.PROD.
  • Why not always the default: on a docs page, the default is the docs bundle's, which has no packs to load.
  • The packs sit beside spell-solid.js, whose chunk holds BuiltInPacks.
    • Pinned by a build test (element.build.test.ts).
  • A new protected UIRoot.iconAssets (default: assets) lets a subclass say so.
  • <spell-app assets> now also says where the icon packs are
