  • Click each <ui-counter>: the count goes up. At its maximum, the inspector beside it shows :state(at-max).
  • Click the toggle <ui-button>: the inspector shows active coming and going, and the row flashes as it changes.
  • Pick a value in the <ui-select>: the inspector shows its new value.
