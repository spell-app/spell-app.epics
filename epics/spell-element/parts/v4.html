On Spell UI's site (/ui/ on the page server), in the browser's dev tools, add attributes to live examples.

  • <ui-card disabled>: dimmed; clicks on it and its links do nothing; Tab skips everything inside it; a screen reader no longer reads its text
  • <ui-segment loading>: its own veil and spinner, as before; on an element without a loading of its own (e.g. <ui-message loading>): dimmed, inert, a spinner over it
  • visible="false" on any element: it fades out, then takes no space; removing it fades it back in
  • hidden on any element: gone at once, even one with its own display
  • a form control with readonly: shows its value, refuses changes
