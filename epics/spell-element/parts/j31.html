Chose to move only the methods and effects that read isConnected ALONE, over giving @E.whileConnected extra members, because the multi-member ones each mix the connection with other conditions (isReady, isOpen, a setting) and read clearer as @onChange.

  • Decorated @onChange("isConnected") (6): UIDocsSearch, UIDocsThemes, UIDocsToc, UIDocsApi, UIMarkdown, UIRoot. UIDocsToc and UIRoot: the decorator went onto their watch(), their one-line onConnectedChanged() gone.
  • Hand-written createEffect(() => this.isConnected, ...) (5): UIForm (its onMount() override gone), UIBrandFlourish (was created in render()), EpicPage, EpicItem, EpicSection (its overview-part check moved into the method).
  • Not moved, as the brief says: the 8 labels.refresh() form-control sites (6 in ui, 2 in brand).
  • Not moved: UINag, UIToast, UISticky, UICalendar, DialogComponent, UIDimmer x2, UIRoot onSettingsChanged, UIDocsTokens (several members).
  • Same timing: @whileConnected entries go in the @onChange list, so UIRoot's loading still starts before its settings are handed out.
  • A hand-written effect now starts in startEffects() instead of before super.onMount().
