Chose to keep the ui-root tag check beside the new elementSetup.root lookup (UIComponent.isRootTag()), over the setting alone, because the root family loads on demand: an app may set appContext on a <ui-root> that isn't defined yet, and the elements inside must still see it (an existing test pins it).

  • both (chosen): any defined root by its class's setting (<spell-app>, a translated <ui-root>), plus <ui-root> before it's defined
  • the setting alone: that early appContext would be lost
  • a subclass of UIRoot under any tag is a root; UIComponent.rootTags lists them as they're registered
