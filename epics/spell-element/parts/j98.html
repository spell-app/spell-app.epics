A root is found by its class's setting (elementSetup.root), and <ui-root> by its tag too, until its family loads.

  • Both checks live in UIComponent.isRootTag().
  • Why keep the tag check: the root family loads on demand.
    • An app may set appContext on a <ui-root> that isn't defined yet.
    • The elements inside must still see it. An existing test pins it.
  • Both (chosen): any defined root by its class's setting, plus <ui-root> before it's defined.
    • Defined roots include <spell-app> and a translated <ui-root>.
  • The setting alone: that early appContext would be lost.
  • a subclass of UIRoot under any tag is a root
  • UIComponent.rootTags lists them as they're registered
