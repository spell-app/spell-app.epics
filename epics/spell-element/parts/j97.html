<spell-app> draws at once by default (display is immediately), not with the root's skeletons.

  • Why: that's how it drew before, through its inner <ui-root display="immediately">.
  • And a program drawing into a hidden box could measure itself wrong: the box is display: none while skeletons show.
  • immediately (chosen): the runner draws as its widgets arrive, as today.
  • skeleton or when-ready: no flash of undefined widgets, but the app waits, up to the timeout.
  • a page can still ask for display="when-ready" on a <spell-app>
