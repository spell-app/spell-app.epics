Chose to let <spell-app>'s own width, height and assets replace the root's same-named attributes, over renaming its own, because its meanings are public and differ: assets is where Semantic UI, Lato, spell-app.css (and now the icon packs) are; width / height take fluid and set its inline style. It never draws the root's scrolling box (scrolls is false): the runner scrolls its own panes.

  • The whole vocabulary (root's names, then its own) is built in SpellApp.tsx, not SpellApp.en.ts: node reads that file for the pack's catalog, and can't import Spell UI's vocabulary there
  • Its own also restates display and icons, for their defaults (immediately, fomantic)
  • every root attribute works on <spell-app> (timeout, theme, size, emoji, loading ...), and its own five keep their meaning
