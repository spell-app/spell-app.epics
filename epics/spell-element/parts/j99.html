<spell-app>'s own width, height and assets win over the root's attributes of the same names.

  • Why not rename its own: their meanings are public, and differ from the root's.
    • assets is where its files are: Semantic UI, Lato, spell-app.css, and now the icon packs.
    • width and height take fluid, and set its inline style.
  • It never draws the root's scrolling box (scrolls is false): the runner scrolls its own panes.
  • The whole vocabulary (the root's names, then its own) is built in SpellApp.tsx, not in its English dictionary (SpellApp.en.ts).
    • Node reads that file for the pack's catalog, and can't import Spell UI's vocabulary there.
  • Its own also restates display and icons, for their defaults: immediately, fomantic.
  • every root attribute works on <spell-app>: timeout, theme, size, emoji, loading ...
  • and its own five keep their meaning
