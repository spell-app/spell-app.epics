A component hears the page through methods on UIComponent (the lifecycle methods decision, Q1, named in Q19). Three of them do work in the base class, so a component that overrides one calls super first, as onMount() already works.

  • the three with base work:
    • onConnect() sets isConnected a microtask later, and a part re-checks its owner (PartContext.connected())
    • onDisconnect() undoes that
    • onFormDisabled() sets formIsDisabled
  • the three that are empty: onFormAssociated(), onFormReset(), onFormStateRestore()
  • who overrides what today:
    • UIRadio overrides three of them
    • rating, calendar and slider override onFormStateRestore()
    • FormComponent’s onFormReset() is called directly
  • an override looks like this: 
    onConnect() {
  super.onConnect()
  this.group.join(this)
}
  • the one difference from Q1’s answer: Q1 said “an empty default” for every method; the base bookkeeping had to live somewhere, and a separate hidden hook beside each method would be two ways to hear the same event
  • an override of onConnect(), onDisconnect() or onFormDisabled() that forgets super breaks isConnected, parts or formIsDisabled
  • the other three form methods are empty, as Q1 said
