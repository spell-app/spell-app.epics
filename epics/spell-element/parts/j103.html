A dialog, popup or dimmer keeps its own sheet's transition as its default animation; any OTHER animation it names runs as keyframes.

  • The family defaults (elementSetup.animation):
    • modal and popup: scale
    • dimmer: fade
    • flyout: fly right
  • Any other animation runs on the element's <dialog> or popup box, through UI.transitions.
    • Its keyframes are found by UIT.AnimationLookup.keyframesBeside().
  • Why not rewrite the three sheets to run everything through keyframes:
    • J59 says the overlays keep the look they have now.
    • The sheets' transitions also carry the backdrop fade and the top-layer exit.
  • A: the sheet's transition for the family's name, keyframes for any other (chosen).
  • B: every animation through UI.transitions, the sheets' transitions removed.

The flyout's family name is "fly right", the nearest Fomantic name.

  • The flyout slides in from its position's edge, which no Fomantic name says.
  • fly right comes in from the left, the default position.
  • So <ui-flyout animation="fly right"> gets the sheet's slide, whatever the position.
  • <ui-modal animation="fly down"> flies; a plain modal looks as before
  • animation="none" or reduced motion: at once
