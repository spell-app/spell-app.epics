  • nothing visible: no doc sets those attributes on epic-* tags

do 'em now, let's be consistent

Here's what each of the 8 would say. The split is by what's inside: the ones with something to click keep the default, the ones that are only text get "its own".

  • "unusable" (the default) makes everything inside inert: dimmed, nothing clickable.
    • but the browser's find-in-page skips inert text, and it can't be selected or copied
    • a plan doc's prose must stay findable (Q12)
  • "its own" for text: the family's sheet only dims it, as <ui-text> and <ui-header> do; links in it still work
  • "loader" (the default) everywhere: none of them loads anything itself, so the shared spinner is right
