A plan doc's fold now hears its inner section open and close through a decorator, not through listeners added by hand.

  • Its onInnerToggle() method listens with @E.on, on the fold's own shadow root, for ui-open and ui-close.
  • It takes only an event whose sender sits in that shadow root.
  • Why: the decorator is the house way to listen for the element's whole life.
    • The ref callback is left with one job: reading the inner section's sticky stack.
  • Options:
    • the decorator on the shadow root (chosen)
    • keep the two addEventListener() calls in the ref callback, with a comment
  • The test of the sender changes, from the inner section to the shadow root: 
    target === currentTarget                  // before:  the inner section
target.getRootNode() === currentTarget    // after:  the shadow root
    • Plus the same detail.section check as before.
    • A nested fold's events, and a light-DOM <ui-section>'s, still pass through untouched.

The full decorator: @E.on("ui-open", { target: "renderRoot" }), and the same for ui-close.

  • No visible change: folding by the title, cancelling ui-open, find-in-page all behave as before
    • checked by the EpicSection / EpicPhase tests
    • and a phase folded open and shut on the spell-element plan doc
