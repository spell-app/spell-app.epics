readonly means what the platform means: the value can't be edited, and it's still submitted. So a readonly composer still casts, and a readonly colour picker still copies.

  • Why not block cast and copy too: casting submits the text unchanged, and copying only reads it.
  • The composer: the textarea's own readonly; cast() and the button still work.
  • The colour picker:
    • every edit goes through move(), which refuses
    • the hue slider and the square's hidden sliders spring back: a range has no readonly
    • the fields are readonly; copying works
  • The fallbacks:
    • the composer's textarea: readonly
    • the colour input: aria-readonly, and its picker never opens
    • <ui-select>'s undoes a change, as the component does
    • <ui-search>'s input: readonly
  • readonly in both brand vocabularies (property: "readOnly")
  • :state(readonly) from FormComponent
