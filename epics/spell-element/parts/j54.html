Chose two values over Q25's wording (unusable vs look only), because the base class does exactly the same for a look-only family and for one whose disabled means something else: it sets :state(disabled) and nothing more.

  • "unusable": isDisabled (clicks swallowed), aria-disabled, everything inside inert and dimmed, focus inside moves on (UI.focus.moveOutOf())
  • "its own": only a look (<ui-icon>, <ui-segment>), unusable its own way (form controls, <ui-button>, <ui-step>, <ui-item> override isDisabled), or another meaning (<ui-transition> pauses, <ui-dimmer> never shows)
  • :state(disabled) now comes from UIComponent for every element (isMarkedDisabled: the attribute, or a disabled fieldset); the P3 @E.cssStates("disabled") lines are gone
  • each family's elementSetup says in one commented line what disabled means for it
