Owen's answer to analysis Q8, "Errors: keep each element's safety net?" (2026-10-08): B · Keep it, drop the switch.

Every element always draws inside its own safety net.

  • why the net exists: in Solid 2, an error while one element draws stops Solid for the whole page, and every other element freezes
  • with the net (Solid's <Errored>), a broken element gets :state(errored), sends ui-error and shows its fallback
  • the net moves into UIComponent's mount; onError() and renderFallback() are called directly
  • today's steps: 1.3.5
  • not chosen: A (keep the ISOLATE_ERRORS switch, for measuring), C (let tiny leaf elements opt out)
  • same behaviour on every page
  • UIComponent.ISOLATE_ERRORS and the test that measures drawing without the net go
  • no easy way left to measure what the net costs
