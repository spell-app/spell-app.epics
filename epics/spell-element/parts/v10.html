Turn motion off, and show and hide the same overlays again: each should appear and go at once, while a loader keeps spinning.

  • Two ways to turn it off, try both:
    • the person's setting: macOS's Reduce motion (System Settings, Accessibility, Display), or Chrome's dev tools (Rendering, "Emulate CSS media feature prefers-reduced-motion: reduce")
    • the page's: animation="none" on the page's <ui-root>, in dev tools

What should happen, in Chrome:

  • The modal, popup, flyout, dimmer and sidebar show and hide at once: no fade, no slide.
  • A loader still spins: motion that shows progress stays.
  • An element's own animation can't turn it back on: animation="fade" on a modal inside the none root still shows at once (J107).
  • Motion back on (the setting off, or the root's animation removed): the animations come back, with no reload.
  • Nothing in the console.

Known limit, not a failure: in a browser without CSS style queries, a family's own CSS motion still moves with motion off; showing and hiding are still at once (J112).
