Chose declare readonly domElement: DOMFormControl on FormComponent (and declare readonly domElement: DOMBrandColorElement on UIBrandColor) over keeping the domFormElement getter and the cast inside it, because the getter only existed to cast: with the declare, this.domElement already IS the form control, and two names for one object is one too many.

  • Kept the getter: no churn in 11 files, but a member that only re-types another.
  • A second type parameter on UIComponent<V, D>: rejected, not simple (every subclass and UIComponent<any> use).
  • ui: FormComponent, UIRating, CheckControl, UISearch, UICalendar, UISlider, TextControl, UIInput, UISelect; brand: UIBrandColorPicker, UIBrandComposer, UIBrandColor.
  • LoadableComponent keeps its 5 as DOMLoadableElement casts: two of its field initializers read this.domElement, and TypeScript 7 refuses a same-class declared field there (TS2729 'used before its initialization'); a NOTE in the class says so.
