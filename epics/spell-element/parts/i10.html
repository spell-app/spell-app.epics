Since T9 <ui-form>'s disabled is the base class's (unusable). UIComponent.onSharedStatesChanged() sets inert on EVERY top-level shadow box when the shared inert state ends, so a form both disabled and loading that loses disabled first has its root un-inerted while its own veil (inert={this.disabled || this.loading}, unchanged value, so Solid never rewrites it) still shows. No other family sets inert for its own loading.
