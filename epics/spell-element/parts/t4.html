On Spell UI's site (/ui/, from this branch's page server), add the shared attributes to live examples in the browser's dev tools: each should look and act the same on every family.

  • disabled on a card, a segment, a label and a form (C15, J94):
    • dimmed
    • clicks on it and its links do nothing
    • Tab skips everything inside it
    • a screen reader no longer reads its text
    • removing it brings it all back
  • disabled on a button or an input keeps its own look: the native control is disabled, still announced as disabled, and its text stays readable.
  • <ui-segment loading>: its own veil and spinner, as before.
    • On an element without a loading look of its own (<ui-message loading>, say): dimmed, inert, a spinner over it.
  • visible="false" on a message: it fades out, then takes no space. Writing visible back fades it in.
  • hidden on the same message: it fades out the same way (since P12, hidden and visible are one fact).
    • Removing visible instead hands the choice back to the family: the message stays shown (J108).
  • A form control with readonly: shows its value, refuses changes.

On Spell UI's site (/ui/ on the page server), add attributes to live examples, in the browser's dev tools.

  • <ui-card disabled>:
    • dimmed
    • clicks on it and its links do nothing
    • Tab skips everything inside it
    • a screen reader no longer reads its text
  • <ui-segment loading>: its own veil and spinner, as before.
    • On an element without a loading look of its own (<ui-message loading>, say): dimmed, inert, a spinner over it.
  • visible="false" on any element: it fades out, then takes no space. Removing it fades it back in.
  • hidden on any element: gone at once, even one with its own display.
  • A form control with readonly: shows its value, refuses changes.
