The new-item button and its form are ONE element, <epic-new-item>, as P10 says; the page header's copy is <epic-new-item compact>.

  • Why one, not two elements (a button piece and a form piece sharing an open state): a section's and the page's differ only in where the form goes.
    • In a section, the form takes the button's place.
    • In the page header, the round + stays, and the form is a row of its own.
  • compact lays the button and the form into the header's own row.
    • The host and its box are display: contents, so both are items of the header's flex row.
    • The page's sheet (EpicPage.css) puts the form on a full row of its own, after the rest (::part(form)).
    • It moves the button 4px left (::part(button)), so it sits 6px from Send, as when it was in Send's box (tested).
  • Open state: its own open (@E.controlled).
    • A section's Edit on a waiting card sets the element's editing (the item's key) and open.
  • The section keeps the waiting cards: newItemList(), its private render method, as P10 says.
  • Its kind attribute is called adds (todo or question).
    • Same reason as the review controls' buttons (J77): a kind enum clashed.
  • The page's isAdding state is gone.
  • The same look and flow, with one change: the form's green Add is white text, not black.
    • Its text colour (--_epic-act-green-on) was never declared.
    • axe failed it, at 4.3:1, once a test opened the form.
