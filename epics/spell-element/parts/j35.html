Chose to leave about 70 untrack() calls as they are, over forcing a decorator onto them.

  • Getters (@E.untracked is for methods and fields): DialogComponent closedBy, UITransition animationName, UIShape defaultFlip, UIForm nativeForm, UIMarkdown headings ...
  • Field initializers and constructors (they run once, at build): every initialValue = untrack(...), UINag, UIRadio, CheckControl, UICalendar.
  • Render-time code: onMount() / render() bodies and JSX (value={untrack(() => this.query)}), LoadableBody config closures, function components (AgentsPanel, ReviewControls), static helpers (StaticRender, PartContext.ownerFor()).
  • Half-tracked ON PURPOSE, called from a computation that must follow part of what it reads: UIMenu.itemContext() (tracks the top menu's context, not topMenu) and UIItem.isOwnerOf(). Decorating them would stop items following their owner.
  • Untracks inside deferred callbacks (afterSolidUpdate, a promise's then, after an await) went WITHOUT a decorator: nothing tracks there (UIDimmer / UISidebar / DialogComponent after() callbacks, CheckControl.choose(), UIMenu.queueRefresh(), LoadableComponent.scheduleLoad(), UIDocsThemes' toggle listener).
  • About 250 untrack() calls gone, 140 @E.untracked decorations in 46 files; the rest above stay.
