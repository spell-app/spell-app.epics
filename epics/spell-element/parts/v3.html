Build the app's two elements, and open their demo pages on the editor's dev server.

  1. Start the servers: /spell-serve.
  2. Build the elements: yarn build:element, in the app package (app).
  3. Open /demo/spell-app.html and /demo/spell-editor.html.
  • <spell-app> runs its project, with its toolbar.
  • <spell-editor> opens Monaco, and compiles as it opens.
    • Type: 2 seconds later (or on Cmd+Enter), the linked app redraws.
  • An app linked with editor="..." hears the editor too: the spell-compiled event, fixed in P4.
  • The web app itself (yarn start) edits and runs a project as before.
  • Nothing in the console.
  • Careful: edits SAVE to the example projects on disk.
