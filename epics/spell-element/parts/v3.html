Start the servers (/spell-serve), then yarn build:element in packages/app, and open /demo/spell-app.html and /demo/spell-editor.html on the editor's dev server.

  • <spell-app> runs its project, with its toolbar
  • <spell-editor> opens Monaco and compiles as it opens; type, and 2 seconds later (or on Cmd+Enter) the linked app redraws
  • an app linked with editor="..." hears the editor too (the spell-compiled event, fixed in P4)
  • the web app itself (yarn start) edits and runs a project as before
  • nothing in the console. Careful: edits SAVE to the example projects on disk
