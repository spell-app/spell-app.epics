The plan doc's elements now watch only while they're on the page: decorators that follow isConnected, instead of the old once-per-life onSettled() blocks.

  • It's what p10-epics-rest did for EpicFold (J70, J71).

An item (EpicItem):

  • Its part loads when its source, its open mark or its connection changes: @E.onChange("source", "isMarkedOpen", "isConnected").
    • A source means it has details, so the open mark stands for isOpen().
  • Following the address's #hash is an @E.whileConnected; the hash already in the address is followed on the first connect only.
  • Find-in-page reaches it through @E.on("beforematch", { target: "renderRoot" }).
  • Its source is read through an @E.untracked method.

The page (EpicPage):

  • Its three watchers start once it's connected and drawn, since the header must be drawn to be measured (@E.onChange("isConnected", "isReady")):
    • its MutationObserver: not @E.fromContent, because it bumps the page-wide layout signal
    • its ResizeObserver
    • its resize listener

Two smaller calls:

  • The agents panel (<epic-agents>) reads where titles stick from the page's --epic-stack custom property, not from EpicPage.signalsOf().
    • So it imports nothing from the page's class, which draws it.
  • A section's render() keeps its two untrack()s, commented: they're reads in a body.
    • Its chosen starts from this.domElement.id.
  • A removed page or item stops watching until it's back.
  • Nothing visible changes.

Chose decorators that follow isConnected over the old once-per-life onSettled() blocks, as p10-epics-rest did for EpicFold (J70, J71).

  • EpicItem: its part loads by @E.onChange("source", "isMarkedOpen", "isConnected") (a source means details, so isMarkedOpen stands for isOpen()); hashchange is an @E.whileConnected, the address's own #hash followed on the first connect only; find-in-page by @E.on("beforematch", { target: "renderRoot" }); its source read through an @E.untracked method
  • EpicPage: its MutationObserver (it bumps the page-wide layout signal, so not @E.fromContent), ResizeObserver and resize listener are @E.onChange("isConnected", "isReady") (the header must be drawn to be measured)
  • <epic-agents> reads where titles stick from the page's --epic-stack custom property, not EpicPage.signalsOf(): no import of the page's class from a family the page draws
  • EpicSection.render()'s two untrack()s stay, commented (reads in a body); its chosen starts from this.domElement.id
  • a removed page or item stops watching until it's back; nothing visible
