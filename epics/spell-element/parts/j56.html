Q28 is still open: took the recommended option of its question 4.

  • loading, "loader": aria-busy, everything inside inert and dimmed, a spinner over it (:state(busy), a ::after placed by anchor positioning on the first box: most DOM elements have no box of their own); :state(loading) on every element
  • "its own" for the 16 components and 3 form controls that draw their own (button, segment, card, form, root's message ...)
  • readonly: FormComponent.isReadOnly and :state(readonly); the 8 vocabularies that had it keep it, <ui-select> (undoes a change) and <ui-search> (read-only input, results never open) gain it; brand's two controls not yet (todo)
  • inert: works as the platform's, but left UNSTYLED, against the recommendation's "dimmed": overlays set inert on what they cover, which has a look of its own (<ui-pushable> on its pusher, under the sidebar's dimmer), and a dim there doubled it (the sidebar's visual tests caught it); families set it on boxes with a look of their own too (<ui-form loading>'s veil). The base class's own dim keys on :state(dimmed) instead.
  • <ui-menu loading> greys out under a spinner
