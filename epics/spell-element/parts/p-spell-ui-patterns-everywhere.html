  • The Spell UI pattern, for a reader new to it (ui's AGENTS.md, "Solid authoring" and "Classes"):
    • one FAMILY per tag: <Name>.tsx, with its vocabulary (.en.ts), its sheet (.css) and its tests
    • reactive members by decorator:
      • values: @E.state, @E.controlled, @E.derived
      • listening: @E.on, @E.untracked
      • watching: @E.fromContent, @E.whileConnected, @E.onChange
      • ARIA and states: @E.aria, @E.cssStates
    • timing by helper: E.afterSolidUpdate, E.after ...
    • set-up in @E.protoMerged static elementSetup
    • events through the vocabulary-checked send()
    • render pieces as private methods of the family
      • WWOD §17's inner functions are for app function components, not families.
    • a raw createEffect only where ui's rules allow one
      • a conditional, per-item or object-building effect, created in onMount()
  • The five epics function components, decided per file:
    • The review controls (ReviewControls.tsx) become a family of their own, <epic-review>.
      • Today: ReviewButtons, NoteBox, SaidNote.

      • The four families draw <epic-review>, and listen for its events.
    • The new-item button and form (NewItems.tsx) become one family, <epic-new-item>.
      • NewItemButton and NewItemForm are drawn by the page and the section.
      • NewItemList, drawn only by the section, becomes a private render method of EpicSection.
    • The agents panel (AgentsPanel.tsx) becomes a family of its own, <epic-agents>.
      • It's drawn only by the page, but has state of its own: its fold, the agents client, keeping the reader's place.
      • Its client subscription is an @E.whileConnected.
    • The fold button and the chevron (FoldButton.tsx, Chevron.tsx) are NOT tags of their own:
      • The fold button is named by aria-labelledby, and those ids must be in the same shadow root as the card's heading. A tag would put a shadow root between them.
      • The chevron must be on screen in the first frame, with no wait for an element to upgrade.
      • So both become methods of Fold, the open / folded state every card already holds.
        • Called from each family's private render methods.
        • Written fold.button({ controls, labelledBy, part }) and Fold.chevron().
