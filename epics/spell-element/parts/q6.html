Owen's answer to analysis Q6, "Properties, reflection and early properties: move as is, or change the rules?" (2026-10-08): B · Move, and tidy the definitions. The rules page authors and frameworks see stay the same; how each attribute is defined gets simpler.

  • the rules that stay:
    • a getter and setter per attribute, on the class
    • a property write is copied back to its attribute at once ("reflection"); defaults never are
    • a property a framework sets BEFORE the tag is defined is kept, and wins
  • what changes: each attribute's three converters (from the attribute, to the attribute, from a property) become one converter per kind of value
  • an attribute whose property would hide a member of the DOM element becomes one clear error when the tag is defined
  • the two flows today, step by step: 1.3.4
  • not chosen: A (move them as they are), C (reflect later, a microtask batched, as Lit does)
  • every vocabulary's converters are touched: more to check than a plain move
  • the 17 property tests are ported, plus one for the define-time clash error
  • no change in timing: after el.size = "x", getAttribute("size") gives "x" at once
