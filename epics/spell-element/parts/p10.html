The plan docs’ <epic-*> elements and the brand pages’ <ui-brand-*> elements don’t follow Spell UI’s pattern: five epics files are plain Solid function components, and about 83 hand-written effects, observers, listeners and timers do what a decorator already does in packages/ui.Every component outside the app (packages/epics, packages/brand, and packages/ui itself) is written the way packages/ui/AGENTS.md says (“Solid authoring”, “Classes”): one family per tag, reactive members by decorator, render pieces as private methods; a lint rule keeps new code from drifting back. packages/app stays as it is (Owen: “other than app components”). Doc Review (P9) is already done, so a second Doc Review follows the new phases.
  • Owen's ask (2026-10-09, reviewing): "I think that the epic- components are not following the same patterns as spell/ui? If not, add a phase in this epic to make them do it. Everything other than app components should use spell-ui pattern."
  • The Spell UI pattern, for a reader new to it (packages/ui/AGENTS.md, "Solid authoring" and "Classes"):
    • one FAMILY per tag: <Name>.tsx, its .en.ts vocabulary, its .css and its tests
    • reactive members by decorator: @E.state, @E.controlled, @E.derived, @E.on, @E.untracked, @E.fromContent, @E.whileConnected, @E.aria, @E.cssStates, @E.onChange
    • timing by helper (E.afterSolidUpdate, E.after ...), set-up in @E.protoMerged static elementSetup, events through the vocabulary-checked send()
    • render pieces as private methods of the family (WWOD §17's inner functions are for app function components, not families)
    • a raw createEffect only where packages/ui/AGENTS.md allows one: a conditional, per-item or object-building effect, created in onMount()
  • The five epics function components, decided per file:
    • ReviewControls.tsx (ReviewButtons, NoteBox, SaidNote), drawn by four families (<epic-item>, <epic-phase>, <epic-section>, <epic-summary>): a family of its own, <epic-review> (folder epic-review/); the note box and the said note become its private render methods, and the four families draw <epic-review> and listen for its events
    • NewItems.tsx: NewItemButton and NewItemForm, drawn by <epic-page> and <epic-section>, become one family, <epic-new-item>; NewItemList, drawn only by <epic-section>, becomes a private render method of EpicSection
    • AgentsPanel.tsx, drawn only by <epic-page> but with state of its own (its fold, the agents client, keeping the reader's place): a family of its own, <epic-agents>, its client subscription an @E.whileConnected
    • FoldButton.tsx and Chevron.tsx are NOT tags of their own:
      • the fold button is named by aria-labelledby, and those ids must be in the same shadow root as the card's heading: a tag would put a shadow root between them
      • the chevron must be on screen in the first frame, with no wait for an element to upgrade
      • so both become methods of Fold, the open / folded state every card already holds: fold.button({ controls, labelledBy, part }) and Fold.chevron(), called from each family's private render methods
  • Every hand-written effect, observer, listener, untrack( and timer moved to its decorator or helper, or kept with a one-line comment saying why no decorator fits:
    • in packages/epics/components/: about 53, in 11 families (EpicFold 8, EpicPage 7, ReviewControls 7, EpicItem 6, NewItems 5, AgentsPanel 5, EpicSection 4, EpicChoices 3, EpicOption 3, EpicVersion 3, EpicField 2)
    • in packages/brand/components/: about 30, in 10 families (UIBrandColorPicker 9, UIBrandChecklist 5, UIBrandCheck 3, UIBrandField 3 ...)
  • Brand's hand-made Cells become @E.state members: UIBrandChecklist's checks and announcement, UIBrandColor's choice and copied, UIBrandColorPicker's working, drafts, dragging and copied, UIBrandFlourish's
  • New code can't drift back: a lint rule in the root vite.lint.ts for the components of packages/ui, packages/epics and packages/brand
    • it flags a raw createEffect, onSettled or MutationObserver, and an exported function component
    • each use that stays carries an oxlint-disable-next-line with its reason
    • where oxlint can't say it, a test that greps for it
    • anything it finds in packages/ui is fixed or justified the same way
  • The epics pack rebuilt; every plan doc, the Epics index and the brand pages draw and behave as before
