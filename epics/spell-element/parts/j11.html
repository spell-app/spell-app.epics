When hot reload refuses to swap a tag’s class, it now warns through ui’s Warnings, not console.warn, as ui’s rule says.

  • the code: HotDefinitions.ts, which took in solid-element’s hot reload
  • a refusal prints like this: 
    [@spell-app/ui] HotDefinitions:  …
  • hot reload’s warnings carry the [@spell-app/ui] prefix, and tests can catch them as any other
