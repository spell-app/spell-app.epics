r1, "still open": "fold <ui-panel> into <ui-section style="panel">?". (Its style= is the look word, whatever the style question picks.)

  • <ui-panel> is an inspector: a tinted box of fields under a full-width header band; a panel inside it is a smaller sub-head band. Every band folds.
  • It already IS a section: its component extends the section's, its vocabulary is the section's with two descriptions changed, and only its look differs (its own style sheet).
  • Two defaults differ: the fold chevron sits at the band's end, and no color means primary.
  • Its other names, for search: inspector, property panel, settings panel, fieldset, property sheet.
 
<ui-panel title="Colour" color="violet"> ... </ui-panel>                       <!-- today -->
<ui-section appearance="panel" title="Colour" color="violet"> ... </ui-section>  <!-- A -->
  • One element, one word ("one word, one element"): a panel is a section that looks like a panel.
  • The panel's docs become a section of the section's page; its other names move to the section.
  • <ui-panel> keeps working until the aliases go.
  • Cost: the two defaults ride on the look; a sub-panel is "a panel-look section inside a panel-look section".
  • <panel> is easy to find and to write in a drawing.
  • Cost: two tags for one component, and two docs pages to keep in step.
  • <ui-section appearance="panel"> draws today's panel; <ui-panel> is an old tag name until the aliases go.
  • ui-panel's docs page folds into ui-section's.
