With <ui-row>s, the <ui-table> is itself the table box, so its caption draws at the top inside the border, muted, over the header row. A native table's <caption> sits above the border. Moving it out would mean drawing the box on the inner scroller instead, and porting every box rule (basic, colours, inverted) a second time.

  • Rows mode looks a little different from a native table with a caption.
