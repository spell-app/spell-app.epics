1. Open the page docs' first example in Safari. 2. Turn VoiceOver on (Cmd + F5), open its rotor (Ctrl + Option + U) and pick Landmarks with the arrow keys. 3. Click just above the example's box, then press Tab until 'Skip to content' shows over the box's corner; press Enter.

The rotor lists banner, navigation (Folders), main, complementary and content information. Enter on Skip to content moves focus to the messages, past the header and the folders.
