--ui-menu-item-min (34px, J36's name): with the icons only, each button counts at its own width or the token, whichever is wider; once they add up to more than the row, the items fold into the overflow button. A page that raises it folds sooner. Read as a length: px, or em of the menu's font size.

  • --ui-menu-title-min (50px) works the same way for the icons-only stage
