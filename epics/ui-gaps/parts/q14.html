How a drawing puts a caption under an image.

  • HTML's is <figure> around the image and a <figcaption>.
  • Today ui-image has no slots and no caption.
  • Spell UI's rule is "one word, one element": a caption is part of the image, the way a field's label is part of the field.
  • <image src="…" caption="The deck"/>; a caption slot for one with markup.
  • With a caption, it draws a <figure> and <figcaption>, so screen readers tie the two.
  • Cost: about 1h.
<image src="deck.png" caption="The deck"/>
  • <figure caption="…"> around anything: an image, a table, a code block, a video.
  • Cost: a new tag; about 2h.
<figure caption="Scores">
  <table>…</table>
</figure>
  • ui-image gains caption, and a caption slot.
  • No new tag; a figure around other things waits until something needs it.
