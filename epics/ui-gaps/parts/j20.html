• <ui-segment vertical> is a band of a page (no box, a rule above), not a direction its content runs: it became a word of appearance. The divider's vertical and horizontal, and the card, segment group and statistic's horizontal, are directions: direction.
  • A card's link was a look (rises on hover) and its href the link. Now link="..." is the link, so link written BARE still draws the old look, on <ui-card> and <ui-cards>; appearance="link" is the new way.
  • A card's looks now JOIN its group's (<ui-cards appearance="raised"><ui-card appearance="basic"> is basic and raised), as the old switches did one by one.
  • Left as they are: <ui-container text>, <ui-extra text>, <ui-value text> (a look, not words), and <ui-statistic text> (its value is a word).
  • <ui-card link> still rises on hover; <ui-card link="#x"> goes to #x
