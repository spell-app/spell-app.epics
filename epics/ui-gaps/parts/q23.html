r1's concept 13: "animated -> PROPOSED reveal="slide|fade|vertical" (the button's hover icon reveal; animation, on every tag, is show / hide motion, so animated can't become animation)". Where animated is today:

  • ui-button animated: on hover, the icon slot slides in over the button's text, sideways, fade or vertical. This is r1's case.
  • ui-list animated: items indent a little when hovered. A second meaning r1 didn't name.
  • Accordion and section have an animated STATE (folding with a height transition), for CSS, not an attribute: it stays. 
<ui-button animated="fade">Shop<ui-icon slot="icon" name="cart"></ui-icon></ui-button>  <!-- today -->
<ui-button reveal="fade">Shop<ui-icon slot="icon" name="cart"></ui-icon></ui-button>    <!-- A -->
<ui-list animated> ... </ui-list>            <!-- today -->
<ui-list hover="indent"> ... </ui-list>      <!-- A -->
  • reveal="slide | fade | vertical": r1's proposal, with sideways named slide.
  • hover="indent" on the list: says what happens, and leaves room for other hover effects.
  • Cost: two renames.
  • Only r1's rename.
  • Cost: animated survives on one family, meaning something else.
  • ui-button reveal and ui-list hover="indent"; animated their old name.
  • The accordion's and section's animated state stays.
