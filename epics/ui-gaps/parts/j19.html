Two places where a rule of the renames met a family whose words mean something else (P4, batch B).

  • On <ui-grid> and <ui-row>, compact and relaxed are the same thing: the room between columns and rows. So both became values of spacing (compact, xs for very compact, lg, xl), not compact into padding. The grid's padded (outer gutters kept inside its box) became padding="md" (md-x, md-y for one axis).
  • On a segment, compact means only as wide as its content; it went into padding="compact" anyway, as r1 says.
  • Grid, column and row have centered (the columns centred in their line) beside text-align (their text): align took text-align's job, and centered stays as it is.
  • <ui-grid spacing="compact">, <ui-grid padding="md">, <ui-grid centered align="center">
