The overflow button opens <div role="menu" popover part="overflow-menu">, a <button> row per item, drawn in the menu's own shadow root and anchored under the button (above it without the room).

Why: a vertical <ui-menu> is a <nav> or a menubar, never the role="menu" the plan's ARIA asks for; and the menu family would load the popup family on every page.

  • rows: the item's icon (or a copy of its icon slot, else its first letter), its title, a copy of its counts; the selected one a menuitemradio, ticked
  • Escape and a click outside go through UI.overlays, as every Spell UI popover's do
