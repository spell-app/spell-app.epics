r1 removes floated, and P8 removes it from ten families: button, buttons, header, content, segment, statistic, list, menu, grid column, images. A <ui-stack direction="horizontal" justify="between"> places those left and right now.

An image is different: <ui-image floated="left"> inside a paragraph lets the text flow round it, as a magazine does. A stack puts the image BESIDE the text, never inside its flow. So the image keeps floated.

  • Kept: <ui-image floated="left|right">, text wrapping round it.
  • Gone: floated on every other tag. Markup that still writes it gets nothing (no old name: there's nothing for it to become).
