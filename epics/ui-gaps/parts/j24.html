  • Taken out, every form's submit button would stop submitting.
  • So it stays, until there's another way to say it.
  • Forms keep working; revisit with a form-specific word later.
  • As HTML's own button does inside a form; type gone. Cost: a button meant only to do something in a form submits it unless told not to.
