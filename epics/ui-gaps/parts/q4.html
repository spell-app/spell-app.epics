How much of the drawing surface (gap 7) to build here: the one large gap.

  • Nothing in any of the repos draws on a canvas today, and nothing draws a chart. The only shapes are inside ui-tree-diagram, drawn from data.
  • Shapes are tags: <circle>, <rect> ... inside a <drawing>, each one clickable, and moved by changing its attributes. They suit a drawing, which is written, not scripted.
  • A canvas is a blank surface a script paints on, frame by frame: fast for many moving things, but nothing on it is an element you can click or style.
  • ui-shape is taken: it's Fomantic's 3D flip box.
  • <drawing view-box="0 0 100 100"> with <rect>, <circle>, <line>, <path>, <polygon> and a text shape.
  • <canvas>, sharp on every screen, with an event every frame for a script to paint in.
  • Cost: about 8-11h, in two phases. Charts later, built on the shapes.
<drawing view-box="0 0 100 100" label="A red dot">
  <rect x="0" y="0" width="100" height="100" color="green"/>
  <circle x="50" y="50" radius="10" color="red"/>
</drawing>
  • The part a drawing can write; the canvas waits for code spell to drive it.
  • Cost: about 6-8h.
  • A's two, plus <chart type="bar" items="..."> drawn from a list.
  • Cost: about 16-20h more; charts are a design job of their own (axes, legends, colours).
  • New: ui-drawing, ui-rect, ui-circle, ui-line, ui-path, ui-polygon, a text shape, ui-canvas.
  • Charts become a todo, for a later epic.
