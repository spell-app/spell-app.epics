How a drawing plays a video or a sound of the app's own.

  • Today ui-embed plays YouTube and Vimeo only, behind a play button so nothing loads before the click. Nothing plays a file the app ships, and nothing plays a sound.
  • The pattern to copy is ui-image: the browser's own element inside, its src relative to the page.
  • What they'd take:
    • src, controls, autoplay, muted, loop
    • video only: poster (the picture before it plays), captions (a captions file)
    • for scripts: play(), pause(), the time; events when it plays, pauses and ends
  • A sound for a game: an <audio> with no controls, played on a click.
  • Browsers block sound until the person has clicked or tapped once, or the video is muted: the docs say so.
 
<video src="intro.mp4" controls poster="intro.jpg"/>
<audio name="deal" src="deal.mp3"/>
<button on click="play deal">Deal</button>
  • The on click is elemental-spell's, sketched only to show a sound played by a button.
  • ui-video and ui-audio, the browser's controls inside.
  • Keyboard, full screen and captions work everywhere, for free.
  • Cost: about 3h; the controls look like the browser's, not Spell UI's.
  • A's tags, with play, a timeline and volume drawn by Spell UI, themed.
  • Cost: a day or more: keyboard, full screen and captions rebuilt by hand.
