  • ui-loader speed="slow | fast": how fast it spins.
  • ui-progress speed="slow | fast": with indeterminate, how fast its animation runs (not how fast it fills: the fill follows the value).
  • Both are how fast the motion goes, with the same two values.
  • One word, one idea: how fast its motion runs.
