Owen named the settings (epic-engine Q22, J36) but no default for the shrink. Half size is what the old screenshots' thumbnails looked like, and what a newcomer expects a zoom to do; shrink="100%" keeps content full size, only cut off by max-width / max-height.

  • <ui-zoom> with no settings shows its content at half size
  • max-width / max-height are CSS lengths; past them the content is cut off until it's shown full size
  • Spanish: ancho-max, alto-max, reduccion, beside Q23's filas-max
