font is on every tag, as Owen asked (M5). On <text> each word draws its HTML element (<strong>, <em>, <code>, <mark>, <sub>, <sup>). On any other tag:

  • bold, italic, underline, strikethrough and code show: the look alone
  • highlight, subscript and superscript don't: a raised, shrunk or yellow-backed button or card isn't a thing anyone means; they belong to words in a sentence
  • <button font="bold"> is bold; <button font="superscript"> looks as it was
