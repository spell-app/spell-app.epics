Chose: source on an accordion is for ONE title (a plan item line); the body goes into the first title's content child. When the title has no content after it, the accordion adds an empty <ui-content> right after it, at load time (not on connect: the parser may not have added the children yet). A <ui-content> already there is kept (its class and attributes too) and its children are the placeholder.

  • Owen's skeleton line works as written: <ui-accordion class="plan-item" source="parts/q2.html"><ui-title>...</ui-title></ui-accordion>
  • an accordion with several panels and a source loads only the first panel's content
  • states :state(loading) / loaded / error on the accordion
