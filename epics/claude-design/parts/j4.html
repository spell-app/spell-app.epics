An accordion's source fills its FIRST panel: one title, like a plan item's line.

  • The body goes into the content after that first title.
  • No content after the title: the accordion adds an empty <ui-content> right after it.
    • At load time, not on connect: the parser may not have added the children yet.
  • A <ui-content> already there is kept, with its class and attributes.
    • Its children are the placeholder.
  • Owen's skeleton line works as written: <ui-accordion class="plan-item" source="parts/q2.html"><ui-title>...</ui-title></ui-accordion>
  • An accordion with several panels and a source loads only the first panel's content.
  • The accordion has the states :state(loading), loaded and error.
