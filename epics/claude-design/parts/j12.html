The design system's README says to wrap pages in a plain <ui-root>. Its theme only picks light or dark: the Spell look comes from the bundle.

  • The P7 brief said <ui-root theme="spell">, as the spike wrote it.
  • But theme is the colour scheme: light or dark.
    • "spell" isn't a value, so it did nothing in the spike.
  • The Spell look comes from the bundle itself: its entry (spell-ui.entry.js) runs ThemeSheets.apply("spell") when it loads.
  • The README: "Wrap a page or an artboard in <ui-root>." And: "theme=\"dark\" / \"light\" forces a scheme".
  • Every preview wraps in a bare <ui-root>.
  • P8's design entry must keep applying the spell theme itself, or nothing does.
