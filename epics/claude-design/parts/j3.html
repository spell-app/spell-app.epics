When a section's body arrives, it replaces the placeholder; the pieces of its title stay.

  • The placeholder: every child WITHOUT a slot attribute.
    • Text, or a <p>Loading…</p>.
    • It's removed when the body goes in.
  • Children with slot="header", icon, badge or actions stay.
  • reload() removes exactly the nodes the last load inserted.
    • So a body with a top-level slot child never doubles.
  • A skeleton can carry a rich title: <ui-section source="parts/p3.html"><ui-icon slot="icon" name="circle half stroke"></ui-icon></ui-section>
  • Anything else written inside a source section is thrown away on its first unfold.
