• Failure: the cancelable ui-error ({ kind, source, error }), :state(error), and unless cancelled one line <p class="source error" part="error" role="alert">Couldn't load parts/p3.html.</p> above the placeholder (in the shadow content box, not a <ui-message>, which would pull in another family). el.load() rejects. A failure isn't remembered: fold and unfold again and it fetches again.
  • The ui-* families a body uses are NOT loaded by the section (the include does, through RootLoader): the body is light DOM, the page's own, so a <ui-root> (it watches its subtree) or the page's bundle defines its tags, as for any markup in the page. Plan docs load the one-file spell-ui.js, which defines every tag.
  • No ui-insert (edit the fragment before it goes in) and no load="visible|idle" modes: P3 doesn't need them; listen for ui-load to touch the body after.
