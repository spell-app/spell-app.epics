The one-file bundle loads its lazy engines by adding a <script src> next to itself. Claude Design's previews refuse a relative <script src> (only the CDNs, Google Fonts, and the system's files by fetch). So <ui-code> colouring, <ui-markdown> and emoji names draw nothing there unless the design build inlines them (about 720 KB more) or loads them with fetch. To decide in P8.
