Owen's brief said fetch, insert, THEN unfold. Chose to hold only the content box, not the state: collapsed, aria-expanded and ui-open change at once as before, but the content stays hidden while the body is on its way (SourceBody.veiled()), for at most SOURCE_BODY_HOLD_MS = 300. Over that, the placeholder shows under the section's existing loading look (dimmer + spinner, aria-busy) until the body arrives.

Why not wait for the fetch inside toggle(): the page runtime unfolds by removing collapsed (a #id link), with no toggle at all; holding the box works the same for every route, and the fold animates once, to the real height.

  • a same-origin body (a few ms) appears with the unfold, no flash of the placeholder
  • a slow or failing server never leaves a click looking dead for more than 300 ms
  • an accordion holds its first <details> closed the same way
