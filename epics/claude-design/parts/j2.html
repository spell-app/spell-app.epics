A section that's opening shows its body with the unfold, if it arrives within 300 ms. Slower than that, it opens on the placeholder, with a spinner, until the body comes.

  • Owen's brief said: fetch, insert, THEN unfold.
  • Chosen: hold back only the content box, not the section's state.
    • collapsed, aria-expanded and ui-open change at once, as before.
    • The content stays hidden while the body is on its way (SourceBody.veiled()).
    • At most 300 ms (SOURCE_BODY_HOLD_MS).
    • Over that, the placeholder shows under the section's own loading look: a dimmer and a spinner, aria-busy.
  • Why not wait for the fetch inside toggle():
    • the page runtime unfolds a section by removing collapsed (on a #id link), with no toggle at all
    • holding the box works the same for every route
    • the fold animates once, to the real height
  • A same-origin body (a few ms) appears with the unfold: no flash of the placeholder.
  • A slow or failing server never leaves a click looking dead for more than 300 ms.
  • An accordion holds its first <details> closed the same way.
