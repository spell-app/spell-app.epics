A brand family's design-system examples come from its docs page's Examples tab, not from separate example files.

  • The pages: one per brand element, in the shared brand folder's components.
  • Not chosen: example files per brand family, as Spell UI's families have.
  • Why: the pages already hold 3-10 working examples each, and a second copy would drift.
  • Each <ui-docs-example> in the Examples tab is an example.
    • It's titled by the <ui-section> around it.
    • The card shows the first three.
  • The preview gets the page's own styles, and the example rules of brand-docs.css.
    • So the stage, app card and docs row classes lay out as on the page.
  • No page scripts: the colour picker's family chips show, but do nothing.
  • A change to a brand docs page's examples reaches the design system on the next build.
  • The pages are shared: a checkout without spell-app-dev gets bare-tag previews (still valid cards).
