Chose to read each brand/components/<tag>.html page's Examples tab over writing examples/elements/*.html per brand family (as Spell UI's families have), because the pages already hold 3-10 working examples each, and a second copy would drift.

  • each <ui-docs-example> in the Examples tab is an example, titled by the <ui-section> around it; the card shows the first three
  • the page's own <style>s and brand-docs.css's example rules go into the preview, so .stage, .app-card, .brand-docs-row lay out as on the page
  • no page scripts: the colour picker's family chips show but do nothing

Net effect:

  • a change to a brand docs page's examples reaches the design system on the next build
  • the pages are shared: a checkout without spell-app-dev gets bare-tag previews (still valid cards)
