The design system's colours are the Spell theme as it ships, worked out in node: computed colours become sRGB hex, and light and dark come from light-dark().

  • It works out every :root value of five style sheets, in the order the spell theme stacks them:
    1. tokens.css
    2. sizes.css
    3. colors.css
    4. the classic theme
    5. then the spell theme
  • The order is ThemeSheets.apply("spell")'s.
  • The maths is in DesignColor:
    • OKLab
    • relative colours, oklch(from ...), with their maths (calc / min / max)
    • color-mix()
  • Not chosen: skipping everything computed.
    • About half the palette is relative colours: hover, text, background tints.
  • 369 colours, 0 left out.
    • A value that is just var(--x) of another exported colour stays an alias, {x}.
    • Plain hex, rgb() and oklch() stay as written.
    • Computed ones are hex, clipped to sRGB: e.g. red-hover is #cf0000 in light.
  • Spacing and radii in px: em times the 16px base.
  • Shadows per theme, with every layer's colour as hex.
  • Plus the size, motion, zIndex and breakpoint families.
  • Type: the three font stacks, and 10 styles.
    • h1-h4, lede, body, ui, caption, eyebrow, code.
    • From the theme's ratios, and its header comment's rules.
    • No font files: local Palatino only.
  • Anything that can't be worked out would be listed under the README's "Not synced".
