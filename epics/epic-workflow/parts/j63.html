links-agree rewrites only what it can show was an old name:

  • A link's words are fixed only when they name a title the log says the target had (P6 renamed: "Old" -> "New"); other words stay.
  • A phase link naming another phase's exact title (unique) follows that phase: the insert case.
  • A comment's anchor moves only when exactly one block on the page starts with its excerpt. Several matches, or changed words, are reported, never guessed.
  • The anchor rules are a copy of BlockAnchors.js's (PlanAnchors), since epics may not import docs. A docs test checks the two agree on a page.
  • Because add-phase --before now runs it, comments on phases that move down follow them by excerpt. That covers I7's symptom before P15's stable ids land.
