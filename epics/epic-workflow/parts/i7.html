When a phase is inserted with add-phase --before, the phases after it move down a number, but Owen's comments on them stay on the old numbers. So a comment shows on the wrong phase.

  • Seen 2026-10-11: Owen's comment CM3 was written on Doc Review, then P10. Guide Comments Sent was inserted as P10 minutes later, and CM3 showed on it instead (J29).
  • Why: a comment is stored in the inbox by an anchor such as p10#field-1. Renumbering a phase rewrites the doc, the links and the phase attributes, but never the inbox.
  • Today's workaround: Claude moved CM2 and CM3 by hand, through the inbox's own locked update, after adding App UI Docs. 
// PlanDoc.makeRoomForPhase():  renumbers the phases, not the inbox
for (let k = phases.length; k >= n; k--) this.renumberPhase(k, k + 1)

Net effect (once fixed):

  • inserting or removing a phase moves every comment, mark and draft keyed by a later phase's id along with it
  • a test pins it: a comment on P10 is on P11 after add-phase --before 10
