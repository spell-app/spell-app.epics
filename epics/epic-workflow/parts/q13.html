The See Also lists every page an epic made or leans on. It has to stay current as the epic writes more pages, or it goes stale the way hand-kept lists do.

  • Example: python-target wrote a durable doc, rewrote the targets guide, made a bedtime details page, has two changelog entries, spun off a future epic, and waits on drawable-thing.
  • Most of those are already linked somewhere in the doc (a phase's Done list, an item's details); some aren't (the bedtime page).
  • plan-doc see-also <name> add <page> --as durable | guide | details | future | depends, and remove.
  • The tools that make pages add them by themselves: Doc Review's durable doc, /details, /epic future, the changelog entry.
  • check lists pages the doc links to that the See Also lacks, and listed pages that are gone.
  • Owen decides what belongs; the tool just won't let it rot.
  • Every page the doc or its parts link to, sorted by kind.
  • No upkeep, but noisy: every guide a reply mentions in passing shows up, and an epic owns pages it only quoted.
  • A plain list in the plan doc; the session adds to it as it goes.
  • Simplest, and the first to go stale.
  • A new see-also command, called by the tools that make pages.
  • check warns about pages missing from it, or listed and gone.
