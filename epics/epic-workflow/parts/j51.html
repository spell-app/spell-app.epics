My reading of Owen's 2026-10-11 ask ("Make these a new <epic-update> web component"): the existing <epic-update> becomes the ONE element, its own family (components/epic-update/).

  • Attributes: at (the date), summary (one line), phase (the phase active then); what changed is its children.
  • With a summary, a date or children it's a BLOCK: Update: <summary> on its title row, the date at the right as a comment thread's message shows its time (smaller, tabular, muted), what changed under it, a faint orange box, folding by its title row.
  • Empty, it stays the bare orange UPDATE label the tool puts in a changed item's title: that use wasn't in the ask, and dropping it would lose the flag.
  • The date floats right of the title's first line only, so at phone width the title's later lines run full width (a flex column squeezed the title to a few words at 280px).
  • <epic-updated> is retired: still drawn until migrated.
<epic-update> replaces the Plan changes box and <epic-updated>: an Update block (summary, date at right) at the end of its phase or after the block it changed, bullhorn-able; a migrations registry, first step updates-as-epic-update
