About 50 links in epic pages, details pages and guides point at data files by their old names (commands.json, favorites.json, a rows file ...): renamed without them, every one breaks (doc-links.js --check). So the epic page step json5-data-files also points its page's links at the .json5 names (its only change to the page, logged as a migration), and the docs tool's data files pass (migrate.js) does every other page's links, text only, every other byte kept. Words in prose and <code> names are left: they're the record.

  • epic migrate --all: the epics' own files and their pages' links
  • epic migrate (every kind) or --step json5-data-files: also the shared files outside the epics' folders, and every other page's links
