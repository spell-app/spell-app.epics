The page SERVER decides whether to wake, inside the Send or Do Now request, rather than the page asking for it afterwards.

  • Every kind of page (epic page, guide, goal page) wakes the same way, through the same helper.
  • The "already woken" record lives in the epic's own inbox, under its lock: two Sends at once (two pages, or the epic page and one of its guides) open one window, never two.
  • The cooldown: 10 minutes, or until a session listens. Long enough to find the window and press Enter; short enough that a forgotten tab doesn't block the next Send for long.
  • The "No Claude session is reviewing" pill still types the command on purpose, cooldown or not.
  • the inbox gains a woken record: when, how, where
  • Send's and Do Now's answers carry what the wake did, for the page's notice
