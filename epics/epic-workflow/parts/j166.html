Per-verb help (epic ask new --help) needs commander to know each group's verbs, so packages/cli/src/epic/epicGroups.ts lists them, with a usage, a sentence and an example each. The tools (details.js, comments.ts ...) still read their own arguments. A verb added to a tool must be added there too, or epic refuses it as unknown; its header says so.

  • a new group verb is two edits, the tool and epicGroups.ts
