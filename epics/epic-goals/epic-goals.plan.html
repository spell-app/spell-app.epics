Rebuild goals on top of the epic tools.

Goals was a "master plan" for a project, as pages Claude keeps up to date: one GOAL SET per project (spell is the first), one topic per front (motivation, goals, audience, brand, syntax, app, AI, native, spell/ui, docs, thingverse, web-components, translation, programmers, cli). Each topic has a page for people, the source of truth, and a .md brief beside it that agents can build from. Items have short ids to talk about: goals (G1) on three horizons, ideas, questions, risks, decisions and work.

How it was meant to be used:
- Talk a topic, section or item through with Claude (`/goals spell/motivation/G1`): Claude presents it, then asks questions one or two at a time, and writes every answer onto the page and its brief.
- Jot a thought on any section or item, from a bubble on the page or `/goals-thought`, without a dialog.
- Later, have Claude digest the thoughts (`/goals-update`) into goals, questions, decisions, risks and work.
- Agreed work goes to agents, with the topic's .md as their brief; what they find comes back as history.
- Buttons on the pages start these sessions; the pages open in the browser or in VS Code's side bar; `spell goals ...` runs them from a terminal.

Built 2026-10-01 as an early draft, and parked 2026-10-11 by epic `epic-workflow` P6, to be redone on what epics have now: plan docs, the review inbox, comments, `spell epic`.
