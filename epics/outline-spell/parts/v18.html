1. In VS Code's main window (the kit's, spell), open the Explorer (⇧⌘E) and open any file in spell-app/spaces/@spell/examples/Solitaire/.
  2. Open the Command Palette (⇧⌘P) and run: 
    Spell: Run Project
  3. Wait for the game to show in the Spell panel.
  4. Click the stock.
  5. Click a face-up card that can be played.
  • The stock turns a card over onto the discards.
  • The clicked card goes to the first pile that takes it, as before P28.
  • A tiny wobble while clicking (less than 6 pixels) is still a click, not a drag.
