A foundation's cards can't be dragged, even its face-up top card.

  • Owen said "dragging the top card in a stacked pile should work if it is face up" (P28's Changes).
  • But a foundation's own rule never lets a card go, and what can be dragged is the list's own can give up rule.
  • So the game's rule wins: nothing drags off a foundation, as nothing is played off it by a click.
  • To allow it, the foundation's rule would change to let its top card go.
  • Since J48, a card must also say it can be dragged.
  • Tableau: any face-up card, with the cards on it. Discards: the top card. Stock and foundations: nothing.
