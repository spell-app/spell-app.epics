This settles the phase's design question: each pile keeps drawing its own cards, and a card that moves glides from where it was to where it lands.

  • How (the FLIP way: note where it was, let it move, slide it back from there):
    • core calls a new hook, core.beforeMove, just before things move between lists of a family
    • the page notes where each card's element is on screen
    • after the move, the card's new element starts at the old place and slides to its new one, over 250ms
  • It slides by translate, so a fanned pile's turn (rotate) stays as it is.
  • With the system's reduced-motion setting on, cards just move.
  • Not chosen: the board placing every card absolutely on the page. That would take the piles' drawing away from them, and the gliding gives the same look.
  • the piles still draw their own cards, in their own layouts
  • every move, the player's or the game's, glides
  • dragging (P28) builds on the same piles
