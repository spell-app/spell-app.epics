  • <> children </> is a FRAGMENT: its children drawn side by side, with no element around them.
  • Elements written one after another are one fragment, as if wrapped in <>...</>:
    • on the same line
    • or on the next line, indented at least as far as the first one's line
    • before, the second element was dropped silently, with no error
  • How it compiles:
    • JavaScript, and Python's page side, write a list of the children
    • TypeScript writes <>...</>
  • The code: the tokenizer's Tokenizer.matchJSXElements(), and the jsxFragment rule.
  • Checked: drawn in a headless browser; the JSX rule tests and core's drawing tests.
