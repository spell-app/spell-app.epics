  • Markup text: your Q2 answer said brackets fill in, always: I took "always" to include markup.
    • markup text: <span>[rank] of [suit]</span>
    • attribute values: source="images/[rank].png"
    • as well as ordinary text
    • no existing project has a [ in any of them
  • A one-line indented body now parses in the body's scope, as an inline body always did.
    • e.g. to "draw its front": with markup on the next line, or return + markup
    • without it, [rank] in the indented markup wasn't the card's
    • every saved project still compiles the same
  • not yet: hover and go-to-definition inside a fill-in (it's parsed on its own)
