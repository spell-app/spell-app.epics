Two choices made in P4 · Text Fill-ins.

  • Markup text: your Q2 answer said brackets fill in, always. I took "always" to include markup text, <span>[rank] of [suit]</span>, and attribute values, source="images/[rank].png", as well as ordinary text. No existing project has a [ in any of them
  • A one-line indented body (to "draw its front": + markup on the next line, or return + markup) now parses in the body's scope, as an inline body always did. Without it, [rank] in the indented markup wasn't the card's. Every saved project still compiles the same
  • not yet: hover and go-to-definition inside a fill-in (it's parsed on its own)
