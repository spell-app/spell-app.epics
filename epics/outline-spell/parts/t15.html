1. In VS Code's main window (the kit's, spell), open the Explorer (⇧⌘E) and open any file in spell-app/spaces/@spell/examples/Solitaire/.
  2. Open the Command Palette (⇧⌘P) and run: 
    Spell: Run Project
  3. Wait for the game to show in the Spell panel.
  4. Press on a face-up tableau card that can go somewhere (an ace to a foundation, or a card onto a column), and drag it there with the mouse.
  5. Let go over that pile.
  • The card follows the pointer, with the cards on it, above the board.
  • It lands on the pile.
  • The tableau it left turns its next card face up.
  • The score goes up.
