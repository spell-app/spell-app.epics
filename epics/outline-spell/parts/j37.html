Owen asked for a horizontal pile "with 10% overlap". Built: each card covers the last 10% of the card before it, so 90% of each card shows.

  • The row shrinks its cards to fit the pile's width, rather than running past it.
  • The amount is one token, --card-overlap, in styles.css: a bigger number packs the cards tighter.
  • a horizontal pile shows nearly every card whole, side by side
  • a long row gets smaller cards, never a row wider than its pile
