Owen asked for each card in a vertical pile to appear "10% below the last card", and that's what was built.

  • At 10%, a face-up card's rank, in its top corner, is hidden under the next card: you see the edge of each card, not what it is.
  • One token, --card-step-down, in styles.css changes it: 0.2 (20%) shows each card's rank.
  • tableaus are compact, but a face-up run shows only its top card's face
  • to see the ranks, set --card-step-down: 0.2 in styles.css
