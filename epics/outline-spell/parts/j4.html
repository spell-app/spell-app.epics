Chose to drop a leading - (a dash with a space after it) on EVERY line, over only inside a type's body.

  • why: no statement starts - today, so nothing changes meaning; and a list of steps in a method body can be bulleted too
  • not dropped: -5 or a lone -
  • the other way: bullets only in an outline body, a parse error elsewhere
  • - a card is a thing at the top level compiles as a card is a thing (a test pins it)
