In a type's outline body, - its clubs is a new foundation with ... doesn't parse ("Don't understand"), and gives the property the type "New". Only the long form gives a property its starting value: a solitaire has a hearts as a new foundation with name = hearts, symbol = "♥️".

A body line like - its clubs is a new foundation with ... gives the property its starting value, the outline twin of has ... as a new ..., compiled the same. And with name T1 means name = "T1".
