"images/(rank)-of-(suit).png" means "put the card's rank and suit in here". Spell had no way to do that: you wrote "images/" + its rank + "-of-" + its suit + ".png". Now "images/[rank]-of-[suit].png" does, in text, in a markup attribute and in markup text, and any expression fits, [rank + 1]; a real bracket is [[ or \[ (P4).

  • Parentheses already mean "fill in" in spell: a card "is a (suit)" for its suits. Using them in text too is one idea, not two.
  • The catch: ordinary text has parentheses too: "Draw a card (any card)". So either only a name the parser knows fills in ((any card) is left alone), or brackets are used (Q2).
  • Curly braces stay out, as Owen asked. Markup's own {expression} keeps working for anything bigger than a name.
