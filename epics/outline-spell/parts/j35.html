Each pile that has a placeholder draws a plain <div part="placeholder">, and the project's styles.css sizes and colours it.

  • Spell UI's <ui-text>, which draws the cards themselves, is display: contents: it has no box of its own, so nothing outside it can size it.
  • A placeholder has to be exactly a card's size in an empty pile, so it needs a real box.
  • styles.css reaches it through ::part(placeholder), the same way it reaches a card's picture.
  • the stock's and foundations' placeholders are plain boxes holding the card letter, sized like a card
  • their look (light grey) lives in each project's styles.css, beside the cards'
