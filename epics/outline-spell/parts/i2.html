  • works today in the sentence style: a card has a short rank as text (the article is optional after as)
  • cause: outline_specifier_datatype's syntax is (a|an) {datatype:singular_type}; the article should be optional, as in type_specifier_datatype
  • careful: with the article optional, its "x" is total (a getter returning the variable total) must stay a getter: only a TYPE word (text, number, a declared type) should make it a declaration
