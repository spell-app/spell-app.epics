Cards are drawn as playing-card letters, and their size and placement come from measuring that letter in one browser on one system.

  • Measured in Chrome on a Mac, whose playing-card letters come from its symbols font:
    • a card letter is 0.604em wide and 0.805em tall
    • its ink starts 0.049em in from the side and 0.224em down from the top
  • Another system's font may draw the letter a different size, so a card's edge may be cropped, or show a sliver of the card below.
  • All four numbers are in each project's styles.css, so another font is a change there.
