A type's heading needs its colon.

  • under a card is a thing, each indented bullet errors: "it" and "its" mean a type only in its outline
  • an indented print 1 under a game is an app runs at the top level

A type's heading takes the indented block under it as its body, with or without its colon (T8, point 1).

  • a stock-pile is a pile, then - can give up a card if: ... indented under it: the bullet is the stock-pile's.
  • where:, with: and a bare : work as before.
  • where without its colon opens the body too.
  • A heading with nothing indented under it is declared as before.
  • The outline guide says what changed for code indented under a heading: a variable set in a body isn't exported.
