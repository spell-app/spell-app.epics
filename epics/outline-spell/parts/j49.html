  • - when a card is dragged out of it:

  • Spanish: cuando una carta se arrastra sobre ella:, ... se arrastra fuera de ella:, ... se suelta sobre ella:
  • the card is typed as a card in the body, and it is the list.
  • A sub-type's thing (a joker) runs the card's handlers too.

  • dragged over runs for any list that can be dropped on, whether it takes the card or not.
  • A drop the list won't take runs dragged out of it.
  • dropped on it takes the place of the program's own drop event; with neither, the card moves by the lists' rules.
