1. In VS Code's main window (the kit's, spell), open the Explorer (⇧⌘E) and open any file in spell-app/spaces/@spell/examples/Solitaire/.
  2. Open the Command Palette (⇧⌘P) and run: 
    Spell: Run Project
  3. Wait for the game to show in the Spell panel.
  4. Hover a face-up tableau card, then press and drag it.
  5. Hover a face-down card, and the stock.
  6. Hover a foundation's card.
  • A face-up tableau card shows the open hand (grab); while dragged, the closed hand (grabbing).
  • Face-down cards and the stock show the pointing hand: they can be clicked.
  • Card text never gets selected while clicking or dragging.
