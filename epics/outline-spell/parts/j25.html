When the type in a gmae: doesn't exist, the heading is an error AND each of its bullets is "Don't understand": there's no type for them to add to.

  • The heading: There's no type "gmae": declare it, e.g. "a gmae is a thing"
  • Each bullet under it: Don't understand "score is a number"
  • Why not hide the bullets' errors: the heading didn't parse, so its body isn't read as a body at all. Hiding them would mean a special case for "lines under a broken heading".
  • Fixing the typo clears every error at once.
