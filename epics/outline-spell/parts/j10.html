Your Q14 answer: to "draw its front": / to "draw its back": are markup properties, and drawing the card picks one by its direction. How it's built:

  • each side compiles to a getter, get front() { return <markup> }
  • whichever side is declared SECOND also gives the card draw() { return (this.direction === 'down' ? this.back : this.front) }
  • so the card needs its direction is up or down; without one, it draws its front
  • a card with its own to draw a card: too gets two draw()s: the later one wins
  • the other way: a draw() only when the type also says it has a direction
