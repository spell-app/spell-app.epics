How a bullet declares a property, and the list of values it can take. The sketch has three shapes:

  • - its “suit” is a suit of its deck: with its
  • - “rank” is a rank of its deck: without
  • - “directions” are up or down: declares the values; the property direction is implied, since the next lines read its direction
A · Always its “name” (recommended)
  • its “rank” is a rank of its deck, its “direction” is up or down
  • one shape; its tells a property from a new kind of thing
  • cost: one more word per line
 B · its optional
  • “rank” is a rank of its deck also works
  • cost: two ways to say it; a bare “x” is ... could also be a new kind of thing
 C · As sketched: plural declares the values
  • “directions” are up or down makes the kind direction AND the property
  • cost: magic: a property appears that no line names

Net effect (A):

  • bullets with its “x” declare properties; “xs” as one of ... (on the deck) declares a list of values
