Owen asked, after P28, for can be dragged and can be dropped on, and for standard drag events.

  • a card can be dragged (in a body, - it can be dragged; Spanish se puede arrastrar) and a pile can be dropped on (se puede soltar encima) compile to flags on the class.
  • Without them nothing drags. With them the lists' rules still decide each move: give up, then take.
  • The page still drags with pointer events, so a mouse and a finger work alike, and the real cards follow. But it sends the web's own DragEvents, with no dataTransfer: dragstart, drag, dragend on the card; dragenter, dragover, dragleave, drop on the pile.
  • A pile that would take the card glows gold while it's over it (:state(accepting-drop); --spell-drop-glow changes it). A drop on a pile that wouldn't take it does nothing, and the program hears nothing.
  • A tableau glows its whole column: the grid stretches each cell to its row, and the whole cell takes the drop.
  • A program says which things drag and where they drop; dragging over a pile that takes the card shows it.
