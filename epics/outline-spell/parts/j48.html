Owen asked, after P28's first round, for can be dragged and can be dropped on, and for the web's standard drag events.

  • New words, which compile to flags on the class:
    • a card can be dragged (in a body, - it can be dragged; Spanish se puede arrastrar)
    • a pile can be dropped on (Spanish se puede soltar encima)
  • Without them nothing drags.
  • With them, the lists' rules still decide each move: give up, then take.
  • The page still drags with pointer events, so a mouse and a finger work alike, and the real cards follow.
    • but it sends the web's own DragEvents, with no dataTransfer
    • on the card: dragstart, drag, dragend
    • on the pile: dragenter, dragover, dragleave, drop
  • A pile that would take the card glows while the card is over it (:state(accepting-drop); --spell-drop-glow changes it).
    • built gold, then made a soft purple, outside and inset, 0.6em around a pile's cards (7a3898ed, 30c63944)
  • A drop on a pile that wouldn't take the card does nothing.
  • A tableau glows its whole column: the grid stretches each cell to its row, and the whole cell takes the drop.
  • A program says which things drag and where they drop; dragging over a pile that takes the card shows it.
