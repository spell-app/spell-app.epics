1. In VS Code's main window (the kit's, spell), open the Explorer (⇧⌘E) and open any file in spell-app/spaces/@spell/examples/Solitaire/.
  2. Open the Command Palette (⇧⌘P) and run: 
    Spell: Run Project
  3. Wait for the game to show in the Spell panel.
  4. Look at the four foundations (top right) before playing anything.
  5. Click the stock until it's empty.
  6. Click the empty stock once more.
  • Each empty foundation shows a light grey ace of its suit.
  • The empty stock shows a light grey card back.
  • Clicking it turns the discards back over onto the stock.
