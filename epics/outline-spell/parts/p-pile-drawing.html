Today (2026-10-20) a pile is laid out one of two ways, an empty pile shows nothing of its own, and a card that moves just jumps:

  • a pile's layout is - layout is one of stacked or vertical, in Pile.spell
  • it's drawn as a class on the pile's <div className="Pile ...">, and styled in Pile.spell's <css> block (a drawable pile draws in its own element)
  • a vertical pile stacks its cards in the page's flow, with a margin under each
  • the only placeholders are the stock's and the foundations', a <ui-button className="Placeholder"> each, made by hand in Solitaire.spell
  • a card moving from one pile to another jumps there
  • core now has List.drawItsPlaceholder() (Owen added it): draw() shows it while a list is empty
    • in spell, - to draw its placeholder: writes it, and {draw its placeholder} calls it
    • since today, a call that's all words beats a built-in reading

Each kind of pile draws its own placeholder, piles get four layouts done in CSS, and cards animate as they move.
