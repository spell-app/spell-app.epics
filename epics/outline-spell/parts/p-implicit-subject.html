In a type's outline body every bullet had to start with it or its, and one line without them compiled to a stray return outside the class.

Gave each line that starts with it or its a twin without them, directly in a type's body, and fixed the stray return (I9).

  • In a type's body, a line starting with a quoted name or a verb can drop it / its: - "rank" is a number, - "is face up" if its direction is up, - belongs to a pile.
  • A list type's guards take it or nothing: - can take a card if: ....
  • Both styles compile to the same JavaScript; outside a body nothing changes.
  • The hover's Reads as puts the subject back: - has a deck reads as a card has a deck.
  • A getter line outside any getter is an error, and the colour-style getter is written once, on its value_if line.
  • How: a rule that takes no words, subject_implied, and a small addition to the generic parser.
  • Committed with P9, as 7da9c3b6.
  • Where to see it: the guide's new "Leaving out it / its" section, under Reference (both styles side by side), and the tests' new group "the subject left out (P8)" in outline.test.ts.
