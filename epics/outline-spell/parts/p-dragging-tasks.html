Tasks couldn't be dragged, and a drop had no place in a list: it went on at the end.

Tasks reorder by dragging, one or several at once.

  • A list type says - its items can be reordered (Spanish sus elementos se pueden reordenar), with a wait: 2 seconds by default, Spell's own line, which the page's move-delay attribute overrides.
  • With a wait, the dragged task dims, a blue line shows where it would go, and resting there moves it.
  • Or immediately: the item moves as the pointer does; Task List and Lista de tareas reorder this way.
  • A reordered list's items glide to their new places.
  • Spell says nothing for a place in a list: a list that reorders moves dropped items itself.
  • Solitaire's piles keep their glow, not the blue line.
  • New Spell on a list type: - its items can be reordered, or ... after 1.5 seconds
    • Spanish: - sus elementos se pueden reordenar, ... después de 1 segundo
    • the wait defaults to 2 seconds
    • the page's move-delay attribute, on the list's element, overrides it (Q21)
  • Dragging a task, with a wait (J56):
    • it stays where it is, dimmed
    • a blue line shows where it would go
    • resting the pointer there for the wait moves it, the drag still going
    • letting go moves it at once
  • Then, at Owen's ask: - its items can be reordered immediately (J57)
    • also right away; Spanish de inmediato, inmediatamente, enseguida
    • no wait and no line: the item moves as the pointer does
    • Task List and Lista de tareas reorder this way now
  • A reordered list's items glide to their new places (J57).
    • the list notes where its items are as it moves them, and glides them when its children are drawn again
    • on Python pages too, when Python's patch arrives
  • Where a dragged item lands, as a sortable list does it:
    • under the pointer, as it was held: the list places it by the box it would fill
    • a neighbour swaps with it once the item covers half of it
    • places are measured where the layout puts the items, not where they're gliding, so nothing flickers
    • a place where the items already are, or a drop there, moves nothing
  • Selected tasks drag together, in the list's order.
  • A press in a task's title field or checkbox is theirs: no drag starts there.
    • each task has a grip to drag it by
  • A drag no longer selects the page's text.
  • The move is the list's own: moveBefore() in both cores (Python's move_before()).
    • on a Python page it runs in Python
  • The list's when a task is dropped on it still runs after the move, with no place to read (Q22).
    • Solitaire's piles keep their glow, and don't reorder (Q24)
