Today (2026-10-23) tasks can't be dragged, and a drop has no place in a list:

  • the Task List's tasks stay in the order they were added
  • dragging (P28) drops onto a LIST, and the thing is added at its end: there's no "drop it here, between these two"
  • while dragging, the pile that would take the drop glows purple; nothing shows a place inside it
  • one thing is dragged at a time, with the cards below it in its pile; nothing can be selected

Tasks reorder by dragging, one or several at once: a blue line shows where they'd go, and after a short rest there they move, even before they're dropped.
