— Definition of a card —
A “card” is a two sided thing where:
- it has a deck

— suit and rank properties
- its “suit” is a suit of its deck
- its “color” is the color of its suit
- it “is a suit” — e.g. “the card is a spade”

- “rank” is a rank of its deck
— e.g. “the card is a queen”
- it “is a rank”
- it “is the rank of suits” — e.g. “the card is the queen of spades”

— direction
- “directions” are up or down
- it “is is face up” if: its direction is up
- it “is is face down”if: its direction is down

drawing a card

- to “draw its back”:

— Definition of a deck —
A “deck” is a list of cards with:
- “suits” as one of clubs, diamonds, hearts or spades
- the color of a suit is:
red if it is diamonds or hearts
black otherwise
- “ranks” as one of ace, 2, 3, 4 ... 10, jack, queen, king
- a rank “is a face card” if it is jack, queen, king
