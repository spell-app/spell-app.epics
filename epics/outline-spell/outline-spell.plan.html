Let spell be written as an outline: a type's heading line, then a bulleted list of what's true of it, with words in quotes teaching spell new language, so a non-programmer can read it at a glance. Most of the meaning exists today; the work is the outline layout, lists of values as real kinds of things, and the parser filling in the words the outline leaves out.

Kickoff prompt

So I'm thinking about spell language particulars again.
The language reads like a novel, and I see people glaze over quickly when they look.
I'm thinking about supporting/moving to an outline style, with drastically simpler phrasing (e.g. more magic to get simpler syntax to work).

Attached is an example that Lauren, my partner who is not a programmer but thinks logically (and probably could program in spell) likes. I showed it to my 70 year old Chiropractor and he (kinda) got it. Does it all make sense? If we get types working fully, can we make this work?

The phrase “something in quotes” means “I’m teaching you new language”
The parenthesis in the <image source> made intuitive sense to Lauren -- she'd be just as happy with `[xxx]` and even says `{xxx}` would be fine -- but I want to avoid curly braces, 'cause they are a sucky part of so many languages.

Thoughts?

— Definition of a card —
A “card” is a two sided thing where:
- it has a deck

— suit and rank properties
- its “suit” is a suit of its deck
- its “color” is the color of its suit
- it “is a suit” — e.g. “the card is a spade”

- “rank” is a rank of its deck
— e.g. “the card is a queen”
- it “is a rank”
- it “is the rank of suits” — e.g. “the card is the queen of spades”

— direction
- “directions” are up or down
- it “is is face up” if: its direction is up
- it “is is face down”if: its direction is down

drawing a card
- to “draw its front”:
<ui-image source=“images/(rank)-of-(suit).png“ />
- to “draw its back”:
<ui-image source=“images/card-back.png“ />

— Definition of a deck —
A “deck” is a list of cards with:
- “suits” as one of clubs, diamonds, hearts or spades
- the color of a suit is:
red if it is diamonds or hearts
black otherwise
- “ranks” as one of ace, 2, 3, 4 ... 10, jack, queen, king
- a rank “is a face card” if it is jack, queen, king
