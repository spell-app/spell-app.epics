Nothing turns a vocabulary into fields.

Added the three elements to Spell UI, each passing through the words of what it wraps:

  • <ui-property-editor>: a form drawn from its schema, with a value that may be a list
  • <ui-property-group>: a folding section, on <ui-section>
  • <ui-property>: a field that makes its own control
  • Labels, tips, choices and rules come from the schema; an empty editor makes a property per attribute
  • A control by as, else by type, from the registry UI.propertyControls; a name may be a dotted path
  • The registry also holds choice sources, on-change rules (run after a write) and named schemas
  • readonly nests; a group shows its properties' errors and held edits
  • Spell UI's Property Editor docs page shows it from a schema, laid out, with Save and Cancel, and on many things

The words Owen approved (Q8, Q10, Q13):

  • Each element passes through most of what it wraps: the editor, ui-section’s and ui-form’s words; the group, ui-section’s (so it sits on ui-section, not ui-panel: J1); the property, ui-field’s.
  • The editor’s source is its layout page, and select picks from it; the group has no source of its own.
  • Control kinds come from a registry, UI.propertyControls.add(); a property’s name may be a dotted path.
  • readonly on the editor or a group reaches every property inside, as disabled does.
  • Where to see it: Spell UI's new Property Editor page (/components/ui/ui-property-editor.html): From a schema, Laid out, Save and Cancel, Many things; all live, checked headlessly.
  • <ui-property-editor>: a form drawn from its schema.
    • The schema: a tag's vocabulary, a schema the page added, or an object.
    • Empty, it makes a property per attribute.
    • With a header, it draws a section around its properties, passing the section's attributes on.
    • Save and Cancel with apply="on save".
  • <ui-property-group>: a folding section.
    • While a property in it fails: :state(error) and ui-invalid.
    • While one holds an edit: :state(changed).
    • And ui-success.
  • <ui-property>: a field whose label, tip, choices and rules come from the schema.
    • It makes its own control: by as, else by its type's kind.
    • Its name may be dotted.
    • readonly nests (J8).
  • UI.propertyControls: control kinds, choice sources, on-change rules (run after a write), named schemas.

    • Layout pages (source / select) are P6.
