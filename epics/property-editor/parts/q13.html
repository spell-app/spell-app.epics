Approved on the vocabulary review page, 2.4, and its Q10.

  • A new control kind is added through a registry: UI.propertyControls.add().
  • <ui-property as> picks the control:
    • input, text, number, select, buttons, range
    • checkbox, toggle, color, color-set, editor
    • or one added later through the registry
    • left out, the property’s type picks it
  • <ui-property name> may be a dotted path, reaching into a nested thing: font.size.
  • options: the choices, when not the schema’s; a registered source (fonts, maybe async), or a list.
