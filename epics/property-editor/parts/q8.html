Approved on the vocabulary review page, section 2.

  • <ui-property-editor> passes through most of ui-section and ui-form.
  • <ui-property-group> passes through most of <ui-section>, so it sits on ui-section.
  • <ui-property> passes through most of <ui-field>, the shell it draws in.
  • A name an element shares with what it wraps, meaning the same there, is shown as passed through (↳ on the page), not as a new word.
  • The group also takes, from Owen’s notes:
    • attributes: readonly, state, output, error
    • a changed state
    • events: ui-success, ui-invalid
  • The group has no source, no load events and no loaded state: its select picks from the editor’s source page.
