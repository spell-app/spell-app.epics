Approved on the vocabulary review page, 2.5, and its Q14.

  • label: the label; it names the control for screen readers.
  • description: the tip behind the “i” icon at the end of the label row (was info).
  • hint: text under the control (the review page’s Q11).
  • output: a value at the end of the label row, “100%” (was readout).
  • error: a plain line under the control, and the error state.
    • On EVERY form now, not only the property editor’s (C5).
  • apply="immediately | delayed [ms] | on leave": when a change is written.
    • at once (the default)
    • after a pause: delayed 300ms, delayed 100
    • when the cursor leaves the field
  • mixed: the things edited disagree; the control is blank, with a “Mixed” placeholder. The form sets it.
  • rules: validation rules for its control, in <ui-form rules>’s shape, added to the form’s own.
  • readonly: shows the value, which can’t be changed (its Q14, D).
    • It NESTS like disabled: a readonly form, editor or group passes it to every field and property inside it (Owen’s note on its Q14).
  • Slots for the rich versions: label, description, hint, output; and actions, small buttons at the end of the label row (Reset, Auto).
