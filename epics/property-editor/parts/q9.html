Approved on the vocabulary review page, 2.6.

  • apply="immediately | on save": when edits reach the thing.
    • at once (the default)
    • or together on Save; Cancel drops them
  • value takes a list: many things, edited at once.
  • validate="immediately | on leave | on save", the default on save (was validate-on).
    • A field showing an error always checks again as it changes.
  • Events: ui-saving (can be cancelled) and ui-saved; ui-cancelling (can be cancelled) and ui-cancelled.
  • Methods: save(), cancel().
  • A changed state: edits are held, not saved yet.
