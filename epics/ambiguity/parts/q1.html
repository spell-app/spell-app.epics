Spell's own messages use several words for one thing, which is the card worry in our own text:

  • Spell UI's pieces: component, element or widget
  • a to ... line: action (to people), method (in code), phrase (in the translation dictionary), "to rule" (in the kickoff)
  • the thing a line like a card is a thing makes: template (the hover) or type (the errors)
  • "alias" names four ideas: a type's short name, a quoted phrase that becomes a rule, a rule's other names, a variable alias

The review pages will each lean on these words; picking one each early keeps them, the book and the warnings consistent.
