Owen, 2026-11-21, on the analysis page's Words section: "I like your names except let's use formula for your calculated property."

  • view: Spell UI's pieces, and a program's own (a card is a view); "component" only for people building Spell UI
  • alias: kept, for a template's other name and quoted words that become a phrase
  • action: a to ... line
  • built-in phrase: words Spell already knows; "rule" stays the parser's word
  • formula: a property worked out each time it's read, in the hover, guides and messages only; the Spell you write doesn't change
