The question box Claude asks you in (the AskUserQuestion modal) holds at most 4 options, plus Other. That limit shapes three of the review's menus.

  • The Start menu ("What do you want to review?"): 3 sections or fewer, then "Finish Review"; with more, the top 2, then "More…" for the rest, then "Finish Review".
  • The epic list ("Which epic do you want to review?"): the top 4, and "More" on the rest, 2 at a time; any other epic can be typed in Other.
  • The modal for each item: Accept, Explore, Explain further, Defer.

Net effect:

  • nothing changes: long lists go through "More…", or a details page
