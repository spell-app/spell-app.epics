# 02 · Two questions

Two questions go onto the page, each with option cards.  Run straight after step 01, in the same turn.
After it:  4 working lines, a pause after each (about 10 seconds), then step 03.

## Commands

```sh
epic add demo#Q "When should Jarvis read the morning briefing?" --options "*The first time you say good morning each day | At a set time, like 7:30, even if you say nothing | Only when you ask what your day looks like" --details '<p>Jarvis could start talking by itself, or wait to be asked.</p><p>Starting by itself is handy, but it can be a surprise in a quiet room.  Waiting for "good morning" fits what Jarvis already does.</p>'
epic add demo#Q "How much of your inbox should the briefing mention?" --options "*Only mail from people you often write back to | A count of every unread email | No mail for now" --details '<p>Reading every subject line out loud takes too long.  Most mornings, only a few emails matter.</p><p>The people you often write back to are a good guess at what matters.</p>'
```

## Say

Two questions are on the page:  when Jarvis should speak, and how much mail it should mention.
