## Commands

```sh
epic add demo#Q "When should Jarvis read the morning briefing?" --options "*The first time you say good morning each day | At a set time, like 7:30, even if you say nothing | Only when you ask what your day looks like" --details '<p>Jarvis could start talking by itself, or wait to be asked.</p><p>We recommend waiting for "good morning":  it fits what Jarvis already does.  Jarvis starting on its own could startle someone in a quiet room.</p>'
epic add demo#Q "How much of your inbox should the briefing mention?" --options "*Only mail from people you often write back to | A count of every unread email | No mail for now" --details '<p>Reading every subject line out loud takes too long.  Most mornings, only a few emails matter.</p><p>We recommend mail from the people you often write back to:  a good guess at what matters.  The cost:  a rare urgent email from someone new is left out.</p>'
```
