Edit Text in Place: how it starts

What editing a guide's text in place looks like, and three ways to start it: a plain click, a pencil button, or ⌥-click.

session 🚧 epic-engine, answering your note on P60 2026-11-02
epic-engine -- the epic pages, guides and their tools. P60 · Edit Text in Place lets you change a guide's text by clicking it. You asked what it would look like, and whether a pencil button or ⌥-click would be easier than a plain click. How you start editing. The box itself, and how it saves, are the same whichever you pick.

The same in all three. The "Two paths" table of the LOE section, with its second row's text being edited:

First release, on files: about 3½ months Rows 1-13, with the data in files: 70 days. Nothing in it waits on the alpha.
Then ATSpaces First 16 days of investigation, to find out what works. Then 47½ more days if they go well ...

Enter or a click away saves · Tab next cell · Esc puts it back · saves by itself after 5 seconds without typing

  • The cell turns into a box where it is: the table doesn't move, and the page doesn't scroll.
  • It holds the cell's markdown, so formatting survives: the first cell would show <nobr>**Then ATSpaces**</nobr> while you type.
  • Only that cell's text changes in the file.

No button and no key: hovering outlines what a click would edit, and the click opens it.

Then ATSpaces First 16 days of investigation, to find out what works.
  • Good: nothing to learn.
  • Cost: every click on text is now an edit.
    • Clicking a word to start selecting it for a comment (⌘ I) opens the box instead, unless the code guesses which you meant.
    • A stray click while reading in the side bar opens a box.
    • The outline follows the mouse over every paragraph as you read.
  • Build: the hardest of the three, because of that guessing.

A pencil beside the expand / collapse button and the bullhorn turns editing on for the page. While it's on, a plain click edits, as in A.

  • Good: you can see it's there, and every editable piece shows while it's on.
  • Cost: one more click, and a mode to remember to turn off.
    • It stays on across reloads and moves, as the page's other state does.
    • While it's on, selecting text for a comment has A's problem.
  • Build: about the same as C, plus the button and remembering it.

A plain click reads, as it does now. Hold ⌥ and the editable text outlines; ⌥-click a piece to edit it.

Then ATSpaces First 16 days of investigation, to find out what works.
Straight to ATSpaces instead About 4, 6 or 9 months ...
  • Good: nothing changes for reading or commenting; no mode, no button.
  • Cost: you have to know about it.
    • The bullhorn's tooltip and the guides' help would say so.
    • A pencil (B) can be added later on top of it, with no other change.
  • Build: the easiest: the ⌥ key says it's an edit, so there's no guessing. It takes a little off P60's 4-6 hours.
  • ⌥-click on a link in Chrome normally downloads it: in editable text it opens the box instead.
