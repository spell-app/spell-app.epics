  1. Red means two opposite things.
    • "you have to do something"
    • and "you already did your part, Claude hasn't started": a pressed Do this stays red until handled
    • meanwhile a queued todo wears a green outline, and a handed-off mark a blue ring, for the same situation
  2. Orange means at least five things.
    • your turn to pick
    • your turn to review a fix
    • changed since you looked (phases)
    • a warning ("nobody is listening")
    • the box you typed over on a choice page, which is your own typing, not a turn
  3. One state, two colours.
    • a phase waiting for your Accept is red on its chip and circle
    • and orange on the plan's strip, at the same moment
  4. Green says done even when work is owed.
    • a queued todo (agreed, not started) wears a green outline
    • so do a pick Claude took, a Noted card, and the green "a session is listening" dot on Send
  5. Yellow means five unrelated things.
    • a calm call that needs nothing: 625 of the 952 yellows in open epics
    • a limit that is never work: 128
    • a manual test you should run: 102
    • a todo: 59
    • a reviewed issue, a queued todo
  6. Grey means four unrelated things.
    • canceled, handled in a phase, an issue Claude fixed itself, a green one past its day
    • all one chip, told apart only by "in P4", a strike-through, or nothing
  7. "Fresh" lasts different times.
    • an item's green lasts 1 day
    • a finished phase's 3 days
    • a phase's orange updates 3 days
  8. The word and the colour can disagree.
    • an aged green's tooltip says "Decided or done"
    • the plain grey's says "No longer relevant", though for a decided item it isn't
    • an epic in "errors" has nothing wrong with it
  9. Only text or colour separates some states.
    • calm from not-calm on an unreviewed call: colour alone (yellow vs red)
    • handled vs canceled vs aged: identical colour
