Found by T71's live trial. With the epic plugin loaded, a headless claude -p waits on the plugin's SessionStart hook monitors/listen.mjs --hook (asyncRewake, a timeout of 86400 seconds) and never starts. In the trial, 7 of its 8 session-start hooks answered, there was no init message after 180 seconds, and the same prompt with superpowers alone answered in seconds.

The trial worked around it with a copy of the plugin's hooks.json that leaves out the poller and the restart wake.

  • Any scripted claude -p (CI, a cron job, an agent harness) on a machine with epic turned on would hang until the poller ends.
  • A fix: the poller exits at once in a headless session, or skips the session-start run there.
