Chose: a live holder (its process running) is never taken over or killed by a waiter. Past 3 times its kind's usual run, epic turns list and its waiters say “longer than usual”; past 6 times, waiters give up with an error naming the holder. A holder whose process is gone, stopped (suspended) or a zombie is taken over at once.

Over: letting a waiter break a turn held past a hard limit. A long kit-wide review is still doing real work; breaking it would put two full runs on the machine at once, the load problem turns exist for.

Nothing kills another session's run. A stuck queue ends in a clear message, not a silent 30-minute timeout.

a waiter's ticket in turns/<kind>.queue/, the oldest live one next, a dead one dropped; a gone, stopped or zombie holder taken over at once, a live one never; waiters wait while the line moves and give up only past 6 times usual (history.jsonl's median), saying who; epic turns list shows the holder, how long, longer than usual, and the line; flags after -- pass through; epic's slow process-spawning tests are their own integration project, one file at a time, in the turn (yarn test:integration; review and CI run both)
