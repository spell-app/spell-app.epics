For I333 (epic pages hanging VS Code). An item draws its line's review buttons and marks once its line comes within a screen's height of the window; its clarify icon, marked note and note box from the first time it opens; an item whose details come from its part draws its details box from then too. A block's bullhorn button and a phase's gear menu are drawn the same way, an empty spot of their size till then.

  • Why: epic-engine's page built 191,000 nodes and held 476 MB, every item's controls drawn up front, most of them in folded sections. Now 56,000 nodes and 125 MB.
  • The cost: Find in the page no longer reaches the status cards of an item never opened whose details come from its part (its part's own text it never reached).
  • Keyboard: a bullhorn far down the page appears once its block is scrolled near.
