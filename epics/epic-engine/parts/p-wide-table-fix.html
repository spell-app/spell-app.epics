Expanding a wide table (the expand button over its top right corner, wireTableWidths() in spell-doc-runtime.js) blanks its first column and its spanning rows and cuts its last column off at the right: seen on an epic page's phase field table.

  • An expanded table draws every column and every spanning row exactly as it does unexpanded: only its width changes.
  • Expanded, it fits the window less its gutter, and scrolls sideways in its own box if it's still too wide.
  • Checked in a browser on an epic page and on a guide.

Cause found, in a browser at 1200px on repo-split's #p9. The expand button widened the table to the window less its gutter (27 to 1195px, margin-inline-start: -160px). But an ancestor inside the epic element clips its overflow: a div.reply with overflow-x: hidden, spanning 176 to 1024px. So about 150px of the table were hidden on the left (the Command column and the colspan row's "Top level") and about 170px on the right (most of Seam). The sticky header copy is not the cause.

The fix, in wireTableWidths(): a table widens only as far as its nearest clipping ancestor allows, and its expand button hides when that gives no extra room. Then spell-ui.js is rebuilt, since it bundles the runtime. Nothing in repo-split's three areas is touched.

  • Expanding a table no longer hides its edges. A table runs only as far as the boxes around it that clip it, such as an epic element's reply card. When that leaves less than 24px to gain on its width in the column, its expand button doesn't show.
  • Checked in a browser (Chromium) on r10's table, now in repo-split's P13 · CLI to the Kit:
    • with main's old bundle, the expanded table ran 27 to 1195px inside a 177 to 1023px clip, hiding its first column and cutting its right edge off
    • with the fix, no button there and the whole table readable, at 1200px (light and dark) and 390px
    • a guide's table (Solid 2) still widens at 1200px, to 16 to 1184px, and has no button at 390px, as before
