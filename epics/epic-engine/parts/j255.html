I321 refused a title with a double-spaced -- as a run-on. With 30 words allowed (I322), a dash aside reads fine, and Owen's own commit subjects use one ("short titles -- epic add ...").

So ShortTitle refuses only what is plainly more than one thing: a ; list, or a second sentence (a ., ! or ? then a capital, never after e.g., i.e., vs., etc.).
