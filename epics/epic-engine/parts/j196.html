Chose a native <select> (Paragraph, Heading 2-4) in the bar over a <ui-dropdown>: it opens without taking the box's selection away for good, and the bar stays plain DOM inside the rendered article. The formatted view's bold, italic, strikethrough, link and clear use the browser's editing commands (execCommand), written back as markdown as it's kept; a piece that wouldn't come back exactly stays in the faded-marks view, its switch off with a tooltip saying why. The bar's ground is the page's own (--ui-background) with a blur: the Spell theme's surface is see-through, and text showed through it.
