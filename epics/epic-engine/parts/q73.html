What each tab holds now (epic-engine P59 picks the tab from the page's path):

  • Review: epic pages and saga pages. The skills send here 24 times (epic open, --review).
  • Details: details, review and choice pages, the ones you answer on. It keeps a page you haven't answered, and queues the next (request r50: a choice page vanished under you). 90 such pages in the last 30 days.
  • Epic Docs: guides, the home page, the list pages. No skill sends a page here; it's where you browse. Review's own top bar already reaches Guides, Sagas and Epics.

My pick: two tabs. Epic Docs folds into Review, renamed Epic: one tab for reading, one for answering.

  • Gain: one tab fewer to explain in a demo, and no guessing which tab a guide went to.
  • Cost: a guide Claude opens replaces the epic page in view. Back brings it back, and Claude rarely opens guides.
  • One tab only would lose r50's protection: a page you're answering would be pushed out by the epic page.
