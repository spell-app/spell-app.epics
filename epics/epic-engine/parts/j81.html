The worktree hook's remove, and epic worktree remove <name>, check EVERY repo first: uncommitted changes, or commits its main lacks, in any one repo keep the whole worktree, naming the repo. Then the servers stop once (the whole folder), each child's worktree goes, then the top's.

  • Why: a half-removed epic worktree (the top gone, one child left inside a missing folder) would be worse than either.
  • epic worktree remove <name> --branches (new) also deletes each repo's merged branch, with git branch -d only: an unmerged one stays, and says so.
