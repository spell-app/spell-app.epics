<ui-textarea> learns to grow with its text: one line to start, taller as you type, then it scrolls. This decides whether every textarea does that, or only the ones that ask.

  • Owen's standing rule: every "tell me more" field grows with its text.
  • Not every textarea is one: a code editor or a fixed-size form box keeps its height on purpose.
  • A plain textarea keeps the browser's fixed height, as every page has it today.
  • Owen's rule is kept by writing grow on every "tell me more" field.
  • Cost: a field that forgets grow stays fixed.
  • Every <ui-textarea> grows; grow="off" keeps one fixed.
  • Cost: every existing textarea changes height at once, and each page must be looked at.
