An option may show a small picture (a screenshot, a diagram) inline, which opens full size on a click.

  1. The set says how it is answered. <epic-choices> takes three settings, any mix (Q52, A):
    • multiple: pick one or several; chosen may hold several letters (chosen="A C")
    • addable: Owen may add options
    • standing: never folds away as answered; each round's picks are stamped on their options, the rest stay open, and Claude may add options later
  2. Anywhere on the page. A set's picks are keyed by what holds it, nearest first: an item, a phase, or an Overview sub-section, all of which already take marks.
    • Today only an item draws Choose pills: EpicOption looks for the nearest item alone.
    • Inside a phase: Claude writes the set into the phase's body, a Choices field beside its Goal and Plan (epic update ...#P50 --choices "<epic-choices>..."). The phase's title row says "1 choice open" while one is, and the summary counts it.
    • Accept Phase on a phase whose set is still open takes its recommended option, as an approval does on a question; its tooltip says so.
    • A standing set: where it sits is Q54 (recommended: an Overview sub-section). The content epic's "Upgrades on offer" is the first one.
  3. The look: the epic page's own card (Owen's comment C2). No tick boxes, nothing from the details pages:
    • each option stays the card it is today: the band with its letter and title, the pros and cons, the Choose pill at the band's end
    • in a multiple set, several pills can be on at once, and the set's heading says "Pick any"
    • the fill rule as today: dashed once picked, outlined once sent, solid once applied
    • answered: the folded Choices heading names every chosen one
    • the designer review checks it beside an item card, light and dark, at the side bar's width
    • the details pages' cards: Q55
  4. Add choice. Under an addable set, an Add choice button adds a new card in the same look, open for Owen to fill: a title, a note (an <epic-text-box>), and an optional pasted or dropped picture, saved as the test screenshots are. As many as he likes; kept as a draft across reloads.
  5. Edit or delete any option in place (Owen's comment C1), his or Claude's, while the page is reviewed:
    • a pencil and an ✕ on the card's band, shown on hover and focus
    • the pencil turns the title and the text into boxes in the card; Save keeps the change as a draft, the card dashed until sent
    • ✕ draws the card faded, "Removed · not sent", with Undo
    • applied: an edited option's earlier text goes into a folded "Was" line on it; a removed option leaves the cards for a folded "Removed (1)" line at the set's end, never erased (the page's rule: earlier text is never lost)
    • Claude does the same from the tool: epic option add | edit | remove <epic>#<id> (a new noun: epic choices is the choice pages' command)
  6. The mark. ReviewClient's pick mark grows from one letter to a set's whole change: its picks, the options added (J134), edited and removed. One Send carries it all. Old marks (one pick) read as before.
  7. Apply. PlanDoc.applyMark() writes every letter into chosen, each added option with the next free letter (marked by="owen", counted as picked), each edit and removal as above; its line (chosen-note) names them all.
    • A standing set: its picks stamped on their options (picked-on), drawn under a folded "Picked" list; the set stays open, and inbox apply prints the round for the session to act on.
    • A phase's set: inbox apply prints it under the phase, so the session adjusts that phase's plan.
    • epic choose takes several letters (epic choose seo#Q4 A C "..."), and a phase or Overview sub-section as its target.
  8. Pictures in an option. An <img> (or a link to a picture or diagram) in an option draws as a small thumbnail in the card, opening full size in the page's one <ui-zoom>, as screenshots already do; the same for a picture Owen adds.
