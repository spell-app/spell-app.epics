node src/tools/check-spell.js goals/spell/motivation/motivation.html --base <epic server> exits 1 with "h2 #ideas not stuck at the top (its title's top at 80px, nothing showing at its middle)". This happens with the old goals-live.js as well as the new one, so it isn't P3's change. It is either the goal pages' sticky h2s under the sticky toolbar, or the check measuring section.s2 pages.

Fixed in P3. The cause was the check, not the page: the goal pages' h2s do stick, right under the toolbar at 80px.

  • stuckAndActive() in src/tools/check-spell.js looked for what shows at main's left edge plus 40px.
  • Since the wide layout became every page's default, main spans the whole window, with the reading column centred inside it. At 1440px that point was main's empty margin at x=40, while the title starts at x=292. So the check found "nothing" there.
  • <ui-section> pages passed only because a section's host is as wide as main.
  • The check now looks 40px into the title's own box. It still falls back to main when the title has no box.

Checked with check-spell.js --base against a scratch epic server:

  • All 17 pages under goals/ pass. motivation.html failed before the fix.
  • The commands page, wwod.html and the epic-engine epic page still pass.
  • To show the check still catches real problems, I added a fixed bar over the stuck title on a scratch copy of motivation.html. The check failed, as it should: "h2 #ideas not stuck at the top (its title's top at 80px, div#cover showing at its middle)".
