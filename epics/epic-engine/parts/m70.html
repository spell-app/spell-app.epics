1. In VS Code, open the side bar's Review tab and show epic-engine's epic page.
  2. Open the Issues and Todos sections, then scroll about half way down, so an item's line sits just under the toolbar. Note which item it is.
  3. In the toolbar at the top, click the grey count pill (hides everything settled), then click it again.
  4. Do the same with the green, orange and red pills, one at a time.
  5. Click the grey pill on the Issues section's own title, then again.
  6. Click the toolbar's collapse-all button (the double chevron at the right), then expand-all.
  7. Reload the page (Cmd+R in the side bar): the pills you left off are still off.

After every click the item you noted stays where it was on screen, or, when that item itself was hidden, the item just above it (or its section's title) takes its place. The page never jumps to its bottom or top. On epic-engine's page expand-all keeps loading for a while: the item stays put while it does, unless you scroll.
