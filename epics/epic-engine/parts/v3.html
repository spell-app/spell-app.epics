The hand test for Wake On Send: Send and Do Now, pressed with no Claude session listening, bring the epic's session or window forward with /epic review <epic> typed in, once.

  1. In the ⎇ epic-workflow window, check no session is reviewing: epic-workflow's epic page, in the side bar's Review tab, shows the orange "No Claude session is reviewing" pill.
  2. On any open todo, press Do Now (the wand). The 🚧 epic-workflow tab comes forward with /epic review epic-workflow typed in; the page says "Typed ... press Enter there"; the todo's spinner says "waiting for a session".
  3. Press Do Now on a second todo: nothing is typed again; the page says "Already typed ... at" and the time.
  4. Press Enter in the 🚧 epic-workflow tab. The page says epic-workflow is listening; both spinners turn into the working spinner.
  5. End that review (or close its tab), and wait 90 seconds for the pill to come back. Approve any item, then press Send: since no session is titled for the epic now, a NEW session tab opens in the ⎇ epic-workflow window, the command typed in; the window keeps its colours.
  6. Close that tab and the ⎇ epic-workflow window. After 10 minutes, approve another item and press Send: the window opens again, a new session in it with the command typed in.
  7. On a future epic's page (spell dev plan-doc list says future), press Do Now on an item: a new session opens in a main window, the command typed in; no worktree is made.
  8. With airplane mode on (/airplane), press Send with a mark: nothing opens; the page says it's queued for when you land.

Each step as written; never two tabs or windows for one press, and the page always says what it did.
