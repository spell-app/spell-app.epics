What attribute turns on title fitting for <ui-menu> and <ui-toolbar>? The plan first proposed fit="titles", with overflow="shrink" and a bare fit-titles as the others.

  • shrinkable on <ui-menu> and <ui-toolbar>; no fit attribute
  • a third stage in P48's plan: the overflow button and its menu

Owen, on the plan's Goal (comment C1): "if there is no other fit entry, use shrinkable. I want the 'if it's really really tiny, go to hamburger or ... menu'".

  • Nothing else would share a fit attribute, so the switch is a plain boolean: <ui-toolbar shrinkable>, <ui-menu shrinkable>.
  • Spanish: encogible.
  • It shrinks in three stages: titles cut with "…", then icons only, then (really tiny) one overflow button whose menu holds every item.
