  1. One rule for what hides. PageStages.hides() learns "empty", and the stage no longer counts.
    • Questions, Judgement calls, Todos, Issues and Manual tests hide with no item in them.
    • Phases hides with no phase in it.
    • The Log hides with no events, unless it loads them from a part file, as every current page's does.
    • Every other kind never hides: the Overview's sub-sections, While You Were Out, a report, Choices.
    • The per-stage lists (SHOWN, SHOWN_WHEN_HELD) and shows() go. The rest of PageStages stays: reading a stage, and moving to the next.
  2. Everything that reads the rule follows by itself.
    • Section numbers, the toolbar's chips and Focus mode already ask hides().

    • A link to something hidden still shows it.

    • Its state's name changes from offstage to empty, since it's no longer about the stage.
    • The "None yet" line goes: nothing can show it any more.
