Choices

A · Kept in the Log, hidden behind a toggle (recommended)

  • Both kinds stay in the one Log part file, the commits and the review lines.
  • The Log shows only the commits; a toggle in its header ("Review steps", off) shows the rest between them, by time.
  • Nothing that reads them changes.
  • Why recommended: the smallest change, and the record of when a question was answered is still one click away.

B · Moved to their own Activity section

  • The review lines go into a new section under the Log, "Activity", folded; the Log holds only commits.
  • Actual Time and "last worked on" are pointed at the new section.
  • Costs a second section and a migration that splits every epic's log.

C · Not shown on the page at all

  • Still written (Actual Time needs them), but the page never shows them; read only with epic.
  • The cleanest Log, but the answer times are gone from view.
  • the Log opens on commits only, newest first
  • a "Review steps" toggle in its header shows the old lines too, remembered per page
  • Actual Time and "last worked on" work as before
