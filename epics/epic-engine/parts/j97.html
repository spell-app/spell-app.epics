The session-start hook starts the patcher detached (hooks/patch-checkout.mjs) and says nothing; its lines go to epic-patches.log in the checkout's git folder.

  • Why: a run patch may take minutes (an install), and the hook has 10 seconds.
  • A copy patch lands within a second or two of the session starting.
  • Owen sees nothing; a failed patch is only in that log, and epic worktree patch prints it.
