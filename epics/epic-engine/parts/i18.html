Fixed in P1: the rebuilt bundle held TWO copies of Spell UI, so a button's page click ran twice. The cause was the bundler, not the runtime change or the new UI build.

  • bundle-spell-ui.js found spell-app's packages/ui through spell-epic's link, .spell-app. The entry's @spell-app/ui/* imports went in by the link's path, and UI's own chunk-to-chunk imports by the real path. So core.js, forms.js, index.js and four more were bundled twice, as two module instances. The bundle grew from about 2448 KB to 2592 KB.
  • With two copies of spell-solid's ShadowEvents, a click on a <ui-button> reached Do Now's handler twice: once for the real click, and once for the inner press <ui-button> makes (DOMElementClick). The second press saw the request already queued and called it off.
  • The takenInto import is harmless: CommentList.ts imports nothing.
  • The fix: UI_DIR is now UI's real path. A new build check, checkEachFileOnce(), fails when one file is bundled under two paths. It would have flagged 6 files in the bad build.

Checked: spell-ui.js was rebuilt and is 2391 KB, with one copy of everything. One click on Do Now now sends one /now. check-review-epics.mjs exits 0 on the sample, and on --doc epic-workflow in 3 runs.
