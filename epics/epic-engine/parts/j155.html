The filter rule (show only this state, hide that one) needs one state per phase, as it has one per item.

  • complete: done
  • updated: not done, with news (above)
  • undone: every other one, to do or under way

So the three chips add up to the phases shown in them. The phases with Claude (marked in this pass, or being worked on) count in the blue badge instead, and the filter never hides them, as with items. On the live page "only undone" therefore still shows the 2-3 phases Claude has, beside the undone ones.
