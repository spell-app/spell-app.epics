Epic UI's pages now have the docs pages' toolbar, and the site's "On this page" column stays beside it at 1200px and wider.

  • Why keep both: the column lists every section, nested ones too (With buttons in its title row under 1. Example), and its header holds the site's tabs and look controls; the toolbar holds the top-level sections and the comments' bullhorn. Hiding the column would leave an empty grey strip, or the site's header with nowhere to go.
  • They don't fight: each marks the section being read on its own, and a click on either lands through the site's own landing.
  • Below 1200px the site hides the column itself, so the toolbar is the only navigation there.
  • The other way: hide the column's list (keep its header) while the toolbar shows: one line of CSS in spell-toolbar.css.
