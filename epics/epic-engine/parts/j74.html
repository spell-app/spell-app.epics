repo-split P6 made @spell-app/toolchain, what every package's configs share, so spell-epic now uses it instead of its own copies (repo-split T7's after-merge part).

  • Gone: vite.lint.ts, vite.lint.patterns.ts, types/app.d.ts (the whole types/ folder).
  • vite.config.ts imports @spell-app/toolchain/lint; its spell-epic-only ignore lists (the generated src/tools/_assets/ files) stay in vite.config.ts, where they already were.
  • tsconfig.json extends ["@spell-app/toolchain/tsconfig.base.json", "./tsconfig.base.json"], as spell-app's packages do, and takes the global types from "types": @spell-app/toolchain/types. The settings the base already has (module, strict ...) left ours.
  • The toolchain is a portal: dev dependency through .spell-app, like the other spell-app packages.

So lint now also loads the toolchain's spell/* import rules. They don't fire on spell-epic's files: they only look at files under spell-app's own packages/ and tests/.

The other way: keep the copies and sync them by hand. That's what repo-split P6 was made to end.
