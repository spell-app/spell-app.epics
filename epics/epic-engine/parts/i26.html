Fixed in P1: the expand button now sits right of the bullhorn, every button the docs runtime and the comments draw is a Spell UI <ui-button>, and their icons are centred.

  • One actions row per block. A block with no title row (a table, a paragraph, a list ...) gets ONE row of its own controls, just before it, its buttons over the block's top right corner: the bullhorn first, then a table's expand button (addBlockAction() in spell-doc-helpers.js; the comments' addBullhorn() and the runtime's wireTableWidths() both use it). They can't overlap or drift apart, in a list item or an epic item too, and while a table runs wide the row moves with its right edge. A block's own top margin can sit between the row and it, so alignBlockActions() measures where the block's top edge is.
  • Components, not raw buttons. Now <ui-button circular basic>s: the table's expand button, the floating bullhorn, Send, the pen, the trash, the comment box's ×. The Epics page's sort is a <ui-buttons> group. The pen, trash and × stay plain (no ring) through the component's own tokens. Five stay raw buttons for now, each for a reason: J16.
  • Centred icons. ui-button already centres its icon: measured in the browser, the glyph's drawn centre against the button's centre, every control, at 1200 and 390px, light and dark. The worst is 0.37px (the check), the bullhorn 0.31px. The old raw buttons were 1.27-1.67px off: <ui-icon>'s icon cell carries Fomantic's 0.25em margin for text after it, so centring the cell put the glyph left of centre (spell-ui-findings.md, #28). Nothing in our CSS sizes or moves an icon any more, and ONE rule gives every titled ui-button's icon no pointer, so the tooltip shows in VS Code.
  • The rule is in src/AGENTS.md ("Widgets" › buttons, the runtime's notes in "Layout", and "Comments" › WHERE it sits): page controls are <ui-*> / <epic-*> components, never raw buttons. Icons are centred by the component. A block's controls share its one actions row, the bullhorn first.
