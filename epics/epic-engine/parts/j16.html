For I26 every control the docs runtime and the comments draw became a Spell UI <ui-button>: the table's expand button, the floating bullhorn, Send, the pen, the trash, the comment box's ×, and the Epics page's sort buttons. Five stayed raw <button>s, because a pill button isn't what they are:

  • A thread's fold (threadHead()) and a page note's fold (drawNoteCards()): each is the whole header row of a card -- chevron, bullhorn, id, the first words -- that folds the card when clicked. As a ui-button it would draw a pill round the header's text. The component for it is an accordion title (<ui-accordion> / <ui-title>); moving the threads onto it is a bigger change than this fix.
  • The toolbar's bullhorn (drawWaiting()): a cell of the page's toolbar band, with its orange count badge, beside the section links, which are <a>s styled as square cells. A round ui-button there would be the one odd cell out.
  • The state chips (stateButton(), the Epics page and saga lists): they copy the epics pack's own chips (<epic-section>'s StateFilter, also raw buttons in its shadow root), so a guide and an epic page look the same. They should change together, in the pack.
  • The jump dialog's lines (Cmd-K, jumpWaitingList()): a list of 'M3 · first words' rows to pick from, not buttons. A selectable <ui-list> / <ui-menu vertical> would fit, once its items take the focus and Enter.

None of them shows an icon off-centre: the measured miss was in the round icon buttons, all converted. The alternative was to force all five into ui-buttons now and restyle them back to their look through ::part(), which is the 'fighting the component' Owen's report is about.

P3: two of the five are no longer the runtime's markup. A thread's fold and a page note's fold are now drawn by <epic-thread> and <epic-page-note-head> in their shadow roots, the same header row and look as before (a button that is the whole row, as the pack's own cards' folds are). See J33.
