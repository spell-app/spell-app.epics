For I7. oxfmt folds runs of spaces in text, so migrate.js now asks oxfmt first (vp fmt --list-different) and formats afterwards only the pages oxfmt would have left alone. Every page it writes still gets its link targets.

  • Why: it is the rule page notes already follow (editNotes()), and it keeps every byte of a page nobody formatted. A formatted page can't hold a double space anyway.
  • The cost: a page that wasn't formatted stays unformatted, and the markup a step inserts isn't laid out.
  • The alternatives: never format, or protect titles (h1, <title>) from the formatter.
