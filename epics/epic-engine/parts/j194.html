Chose to make a comment only of a revisit's note with words and nothing else (ReviewInbox.isNoteToTalkOver()), because:

  • a pick with a note ("pick B, but ...") is answered about that option, and its pick is kept as chosen at clear: a thread would lose the pick
  • a note with a picture: a comment holds no picture, so it stays his reply card in the item
  • a revisit made of his comments in its text (I140): those are threads already
  • Do Now (revisit now, Add Details): a request a background agent takes, answered into the item, not a thread

A note no wait took (/airplane land lists marks without taking them) becomes a comment at inbox clear, answered with no words (it was talked over in the chat), so it's his turn on it.

`wait` makes it one as it hands it over (its mark gone), `clear` one no wait took; a box whose sent note left the inbox empties, even with the focus, keeping words typed after it
