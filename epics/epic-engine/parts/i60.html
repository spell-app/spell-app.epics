In src/command/Worktrees.ts, planSummaries() builds its loader from join(root, 'node_modules', 'tsx', 'dist', 'loader.mjs'). With spell-epic a workspace of the top spell install, everything is hoisted to ~/www/spell/node_modules, so the file isn't there and every epic page reads as not done, silently.

Fix: tsxImports(root) from src/tools/packageDir.ts (as EpicTools.TSX_IMPORTS does since P10). Left for the worktrees side (the trees agent's file).
