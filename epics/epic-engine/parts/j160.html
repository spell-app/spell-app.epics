Owen's comment C8 names three choices for the gear's Auto Send sub-menu: immediately, 2 minutes, 5 minutes. He also asked earlier for a way to turn it off, so the sub-menu has a fourth.

  • Immediately: each change goes as soon as you finish it -- a pick or a mark at once, a note or a comment when its box closes or loses focus. No ring.
  • 2 minutes (the default) and 5 minutes: the ring drains over that time after you last touched the page.
  • Off, last, under a divider: Send waits for your press, as today.
  • The row reads Auto Send · 2 minutes, so the choice shows without opening it.
  • four rows in the sub-menu, 2 minutes ticked to start with
