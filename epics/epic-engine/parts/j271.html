T95 put the Clarify button on every item's line.

  • Its glyph is Spell UI's info circle, a filled disc with an i, for your "(i)". The circle-question it had since T76 is gone.
  • It sits last on the line, after the review buttons, so it is in the same place on every kind: 24px square, centred with the review buttons, flush with the line's right edge.
  • It is muted grey until hovered, as T76's sheet always meant. The old icon drew in the link's blue by mistake: its class carried the word "link", which matched the Clarify link's rule.
  • Phases get none: Clarify replaces an item's unclear text, and a phase's body is its build plan, which details --more doesn't take.
