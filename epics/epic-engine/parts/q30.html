To cut a title with "…" and later hide it, <ui-item> needs a box around its title. This decides how a page tells the item which part is its title, and where a count that must stay visible goes.

  • Today the docs toolbar wraps each title in its own span.spell-toolbar-label, and its counts sit beside it.
  • A count (a badge): the "3" on Questions, which must stay when the title is cut or gone.
  • Only while its menu is shrinkable, the item wraps its text in part="title".
  • Counts go in a new badge slot (Spanish insignia).
  • Plain <ui-item>Questions</ui-item> keeps working: nothing to rewrite.
  • <ui-item><span slot="title">Questions</span></ui-item>
  • Cost: every item in a shrinkable row needs the extra markup, or its title never shrinks.
 
<ui-toolbar shrinkable>
  <ui-item icon="question">Questions
    <ui-label slot="badge">3</ui-label>
  </ui-item>
</ui-toolbar>
  • <ui-item> gains a title part and a badge slot and part
  • no existing item's markup changes
