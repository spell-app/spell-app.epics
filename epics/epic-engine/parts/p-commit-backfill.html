An epic page's commit lists go wrong once its phases are renumbered or its work spans several repos: epic commits --backfill files old commits under the wrong phases, and a child repo's commits can't be listed at all (outline-spell P25-P28).

  • Backfill matches a commit to a phase by the epic's name in its subject and by the phase itself (its name, kept by its stable id), never by the bare number alone.
  • Backfill and epic commit look in the kit and every child repo (epic config repos), and each listed commit links to its own repo on GitHub.
