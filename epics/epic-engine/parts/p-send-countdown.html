Owen, 2026-10-29, looking at an epic page's header:

I'm thinking about the 'send page comments' paradigm again. Here's what I want: Send works on a 2-minute no interaction timer. This is integrated into the single send button in the epic doc toolbar with the countdown ring. Pressing it sends immediately. Turning it off could be from a gear menu in the epic page header which replaces this [the round git button in the header]. BTW: that [git button] doesn't seem to do anything for now?
  • Send is ONE button, the paper plane, with the countdown ring drawn round it.
    • What you've marked or typed goes by itself 2 minutes after you last touched the page.
    • Pressing the plane sends it at once.
    • No wand comes back: the ring is the plane's own.
  • A GEAR menu in the page header takes the git button's place.
    • It turns the countdown on or off.
    • It shows or hides the commits in phases and items, as the git button did.
    • Anything else in the header that is a setting rather than an action goes in it too.
  • The git button's "does nothing" is explained and fixed: showing commits says where they are when every phase is folded.

Send sends by itself 2 minutes after you stop working on the page, a ring round the plane counting down; a press still sends at once.

A gear menu replaces the git button: the countdown's on / off and the commits' show / hide.

  1. What was there, and why it went.
    • P20 · Countdown Send (2026-10-22) built the countdown: a wand beside the plane turned it on and off, and a ring round the wand drained over the 2 minutes.
    • On 2026-10-25 Owen took the wand off every page: "remove the wand EVERYWHERE ... We may bring the wand back later". That was the day the rows' Do Now wands went too; the header kept Send alone, and only a press sends since.
    • The code stayed: the page's countdown (SendCountdown) and the ring, behind a wand attribute nothing sets.
    • So this phase brings back the countdown, NOT the wand: the ring goes round the plane itself, and its switch lives in the gear.
  2. Why the git button seems to do nothing (checked headless on this worktree's page, 2026-10-29).
    • The button works: pressed, it shows every commit, and its tooltip turns to "Hide the commits".
    • But no commit is on the page until you open something: all of them are in the phases' and items' own files, loaded only when one opens. On epic-engine's page, 0 commits were in the page with the button on; opening P49 · One Repo Tooling loaded 7, and all 7 showed.
    • The button shows because the folded phases are flagged as having commits. With the Phases section folded too, pressing it changes nothing you can see.
  3. Send with its ring (<epic-send-buttons>).
    • The wand goes from the element, its wand attribute and its words with it. A new countdown attribute asks for the ring; the epic page's header sets it.
    • The ring is drawn round the round plane: a pale track, a blue arc draining clockwise, as P20's was round the wand.
    • Hovering the plane says how long is left ("Sends by itself in 1:42; click to send now").
    • Countdown off, or nobody listening (the question below, Q59): the plane filled blue, waiting for a press, as today.
    • The build plans' ring (2px #666, while plans wait for Accept) stays the pill's own border, outside the countdown's ring, so the two never meet.
    • During a stage the ring goes round the Accept button's check; the countdown only sends marks, never accepts (J143).
