The top bar of every docs page (<spell-site-header>, spell-app's packages/server/src/site/SiteHeader.ts) has four icon buttons: the logo (Spell docs), the pencil (Edit sections in place), <> (Open the source in VS Code) and the theme (light / dark). In VS Code's side bar none of them shows its tooltip.

  • Why: each title is on the link or button, but the pointer lands on the icon inside it (its <svg>). Electron 38 and up, on macOS, shows a title only on the element under the pointer itself, not on a parent (electron/electron#49843), as spell-epic's src/AGENTS.md "TOOLTIPS" says.
  • Fix: the icons take no pointer (.brand svg, .tool svg { pointer-events: none; }), so the pointer lands on the titled element.
  • Check: in headless Chromium, the element under each button's centre (shadowRoot.elementFromPoint()) was the icon (svg / path) before, and is the titled button after.
