The notebook pane's header is the last one still built by hand, so it may not read like the others.

  • The notebook's title, lock and save note sit in the same bar as every other pane (the hand-built header call).
  • It stays readable in a narrow pane.

Look at it beside the other panes, then move it onto the shared bar.

  1. Look first: pictures of a notebook beside the editor's other panes, in Glass and Fruity, open and narrow, with what differs named. The header holds a title that's a menu of notebooks, a lock, and a small "Saved" note.
  2. Move it to the shared bar, <ui-panel appearance="pane">, with the title in its title and the lock and note in its actions, as the other panes do. No new slots.
  3. Narrow: the note gives way first, then the name shortens, and the lock stays.
  4. What goes: most of NotebookHeader's CSS. What it does stays the same.

Checked by: the bar measured at 40px, level with the Inspector's first row; its centres lined up; the notebook tests; the designer review in Glass and Fruity; yarn review in app.
