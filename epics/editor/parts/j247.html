Card.samples.spell, beside Card.spell, holds a type with one member made new per sample, as an app's "stock" is a new stock pile does: 

card samples is a thing
	"queen of spades" is a new card whose rank is queen, suit is spades and direction is down

No parser rule was added. The file is never compiled, as Card.editor.spell isn't (SP.isSamplesName()); Design reads it as text. Ask for a shorter form (no card samples is a thing line) and it becomes a question with options.
