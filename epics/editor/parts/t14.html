A blur on an ancestor can trap a position:fixed popup. Glass And Fruity gave top-level panels one, and nobody opened a dropdown from inside one to see.

Checked headless in Glass, 2026-10-31: nothing is trapped. Popups are in the browser's top layer, which a blur on an ancestor can't contain.

  • A <ui-dropdown> in a top-level <ui-panel> whose blur is on (blur(28px) saturate(1.7)): the panel ends at y 236, and its list runs from 225 to 422, whole and on top.
  • The Inspector's Events menu, in the real editor, runs past its card and over the Views card.
