On the Views card, the capsule behind the tabs is gone, so the bar itself is the track and only the chosen tab has a pill. This is what makes it read as one thing, but it goes beyond moving the old look into the widget.

  • A theme can bring a track back with one setting (--ui-menu-segmented-header-background).
  • The pictures show both, for your call on the details page.
  • Every header that follows gets no inner track, unless you say otherwise.
