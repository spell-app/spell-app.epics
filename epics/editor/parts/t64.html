Proposed fix (built): when the code's cursor rests on a word, the editor asks the language service what that word names.

  • A property or a variable (the stock, la reserva): it finds the line that declared it, e.g. <stock-pile "stock"/>, and inspects what that line draws, the same way a click on that line would.
  • Anything else (a keyword, an action's name, a word naming nothing drawn, like the loop's card): the cursor's line is followed, as before.
  • It stays quiet: focus stays in the code, the code doesn't scroll, and nothing happens while you're typing.

Built: declaredThingAt() in the language service, and followCodeCursor() in the follow-along.
