How T75's 'accept each bit' works in the Published since your draft began dialog:

  • The right side, Your Changes, is now the result: editable, and what Publish sends out.
  • Each change has an arrow between the sides (Monaco's own revert arrow, shown on hover): a click copies the published version's block into yours.
  • Keeping yours needs nothing: it's already there. So there's no 'keep mine' button per change.
  • ⌘Z in the right side puts your block back, one change at a time; you can also type in it.
  • Buttons: Cancel · Start Again from Theirs · Publish (was 'Publish Mine Anyway').
  • Monaco's own menu in the gutter ('Revert Block') is turned off, so there's one way to do it.
  • Not done: the arrow's tooltip is Monaco's English 'Revert Change', not our wording.
Publish's clash dialog takes the published version change by change: "Current Published Version" and "Your Changes" over the two sides, an arrow on each change copies theirs into yours (⌘Z puts yours back), Publish publishes the right side; both sides drawn bubbly
