Choosing a tag from the list writes its closing tag as </>, the way the examples close every element ("Writing an example"), not </content>.

  • <content>, <toolbar>, <context-menu>: the caret a line a level in, </> under it
  • a tag whose quoted word is its contents (button, header, label, item ...): <button>|</> on its line, as a button is usually written; the list is the tokenizer's, so it follows "A tag's quoted word" in PARSING.md
  • a tag a quoted word names (tab, panel, example, tree-node): <tab "|">, a line in, </>
