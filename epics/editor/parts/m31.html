1. In the editor (spell server's App tab), open @spell/examples/Solitaire in Edit.
  2. Click the stacked-layers icon right of the crosshairs in the top bar (or View › Anatomy, or ⌥⌘A). The game should tilt back, with its piles, cards and card pictures on layers above it, each tagged with its type.
  3. Drag on the app to turn it, scroll to zoom, then click Front. Move the Spacing and Depth sliders.
  4. Click a card's layer: it should be selected in the Views tree and the Inspector, and the code should go to Card.spell. Double-click a column's layer: only that column and its cards should show. Press Esc to go back to the whole app.
  5. Hold ⌥ and drag a card to another pile: it should move, on its layer.
  6. Try Parts, Outlines and the full-window button. Click the icon again: the game should be flat and look exactly as it did before.
