Owen, 2026-11-18, with a screenshot (the app's tag says “Solitaire in one piece”): “header bar doesn't split, even with parts on. We should see the label, buttons/spacers/etc, no?”

Likely cause: the app clips its insides, so P34 lifts it whole (“in one piece”), and the header's title, button group, buttons and score never get layers of their own.

Anatomy gives every part the Views tree shows its own layer, Spell UI's among them, and turns off what draws a part's insides flat while it shows.

  • A layer per row of the Views tree: the program's views, and the Spell UI parts and tags it draws (the header's segment, toolbar, title, each button, the spacer, the score).
  • A part whose own tag draws no box (display: contents) is lifted by its layer box: the first box drawn in its shadow root. One rule, in one place, so T119 can move it into UIComponent's base as each part's contract.
  • The glass blur, a hidden or scrolling overflow and isolation, on the boxes between a layer and the app, are turned off while Anatomy shows, and put back exactly on close. Fading, filters, masks and clip paths still keep what's inside flat ("in one piece").
  • A Spell UI part's own insides (its shadow root) aren't layers of their own; a view's are, so the card backs still show.
