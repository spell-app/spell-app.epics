The window bar is built into Spell UI as <ui-menu appearance="header">: alone it is a whole bar across a window; with segmented it is the row of a pane's bar, from One Header Row.

  • The first stage of One Header Row put a pane bar's height and hairline on the pane panel, so the top bar had nothing to borrow for its own box.
  • The two share a word and 13px text. The heights are separate today, 38px and 40px, until Glass And Fruity adds the height setting.
  • Changing the pictures' answer can change the pills without touching this bar.
