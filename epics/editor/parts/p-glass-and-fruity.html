Glass and Fruity are close to macOS, but the look partly lives in the editor and several details are off.
  • Each gap in the Overview's table is checked against Apple's own pictures before a value changes.
  • Panels and side bars are glass by themselves, not through the editor's CSS.
  • Selection becomes a solid violet accent in both themes, with white text at regular weight.
  • Controls have a fixed height for each size, and the editor gets 13px text without changing the docs pages' size.
  • Toolbar actions group into capsules, edges get a bright inner rim, and the focus ring hugs its shape.
  • Reduced transparency and higher contrast both work.
  • The theme editor and theme bench wear the same header row, and the bench's panels are checked (T1).
Both themes get tuned toward modern macOS. A pane's glass moves into the widgets and themes.
  • The current look: translucent capsules, floating side bars, grouped toolbar actions.
  • Our pills already lean this way, and it reads as new.
  • Flatter: small-radius segmented controls, side bars flush to the edge.
  • Calmer and denser, but it may look dated at launch.
  1. First, two checks: look at Apple's own pictures and correct the gap table, and see whether the theme bench's panels really load (T1).
  2. Glass by its own settings: panels and side bars get the material from the widgets and theme rules. The editor's copy of it goes.
  3. Sizes: a new control-height setting, listed in the theme's tree so the theme editor shows it, and the widgets use it. App pages get 13px text without changing docs pages.
  4. Selection: a solid violet accent in both themes, with white text at regular weight.
  5. Depth and edges: a translucent pill track, a bright inner rim, and a closer focus ring.
  6. Toolbar actions group into a capsule, through the toolbar's right slot.
  7. Access: solid surfaces when reduced transparency is asked for, and stronger edges for more contrast. Neither is supported anywhere in Spell UI's CSS today.
  8. The theme files: values go in Glass's and Fruity's theme sources, never in the sheets generated from them. The theme editor and bench wear the new header row.

How it's checked: before and after pictures beside Apple's, in both themes, light and dark. A quick look that the other themes still draw properly, then the designer review.

Left out: tuning other themes, and glass that truly bends light, which CSS can't do. This phase gives the look, not a copy.
