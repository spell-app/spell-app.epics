Each to draw ... line in the view's block of its file is a row. A two sided view's sides read Front and Back, a plain to draw it reads Its drawing; the Spell line is the row's tooltip. Spanish files list no drawings yet, as the palette lists no Spanish views.

Read a view's drawings in its file's own language, with the words the language already has, not a second list:

  • the type's line by the language's kindDeclaration (una "carta" es una ...)
  • a drawing's line by its drawing words (para dibujar eso), and Spanish's para dibujarla, its it fused on
  • a side by its possessive (su), its row named as written (Frente, Dorso), turned by the name it compiles by (front, back)
