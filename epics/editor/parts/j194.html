• The Inspector cards are <ui-panel appearance="pane">s, so the shades are the pane's own: two strips in its shadow DOM over its body, shown by :state(more-above) / :state(more-below).
  • Only panes: a boxed panel's body never scrolls by itself, so it draws none.
  • The body is the first child in the pane's content that scrolls by itself (ScrollShades, beside the panel): a pane's content box doesn't scroll, what's put in it does.
  • A script, not the scroll-driven animation: Chrome doesn't move a scroll timeline when the content under it shrinks with no scroll, so the top shade stayed after a long card was swapped for a short one (the cause of I227).
  • Tokens on the panel: --ui-panel-scroll-shade (now 0.1 light / 0.3 dark, I201), -size 16px, -top (Views sets it to its path line's height).
  • The kit's scrollEnds() takes an axis now, block for up and down, rather than a second copy.
  • Other panes (the runner, compiled code, notebook header, Publish's clash) get shades only if their body scrolls by itself.
