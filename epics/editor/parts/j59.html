With icons in the editor's menus (I22), a menu of things on or off (View) keeps both: the tick's gutter first, then the icon column, then the words, as a Mac's menus put a checkmark before an item's image. The other way, the tick replacing the icon while on, would hide what the row is just when it's on.

Every title in a menu starts at the same place; View's ticks sit at the far left.
