The epic pages' Spell UI is one classic script that inlines every import(). Without care it grew from 4.0 MB to 9.3 MB with Mermaid inside. Now Mermaid and ELK load as lazy scripts beside it, like highlight.js and marked: _assets/lazy/mermaid-engine.js (5.5 MB, 1.6 MB zipped) and elk-engine.js (1.6 MB), and spell-ui.js grew 22 KB.

They're committed, as the other lazy engines are, so each Mermaid upgrade adds about 7 MB to the repo's history. The Mermaid script holds its own ELK copy: classic scripts can't share one. Another way: draw no diagrams on epic pages at all.
