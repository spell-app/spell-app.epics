In the editor, open Solitaire (File › More Examples › Solitaire), then press Edit. Turn Inspect app on (the crosshair between Edit and the code icon), and click a card. In the Inspector's header, click All Cards.

Rank and Suit show their values as pills; Value, Color, Name and the others show their Spell in the code editor; Events, Actions and Tests are cards of their own under it, each resizable. Take King off Rank: Card.spellml's rank line loses king, and ⌘Z puts it back. The Tests card's ▶ marks card setup with a green check.
