This is about one notice in the editor. It shows when someone tries to add a part, like a button or a label, to an app that doesn't draw anything on screen yet. Without a "to draw" section in the app's code, there's no place on screen to put the new part.

When it shows:

  • a click or a drag from the parts list on the right, when the app has nowhere to put it
  • Paste, too: the Edit menu greys Paste out, and gives this notice as the reason

P35 · Placing And Isolation reworded it. Today it says, in short lines:

// English
There's nowhere to put this part yet:  your app doesn't show anything on screen.
In the code on the left, add a "to draw" section under your app, saying what it shows.
Then pick the part again.

// Spanish
Aún no hay dónde poner esta parte:  tu aplicación no muestra nada en la pantalla.
En el código de la izquierda, añade una sección «para dibujar» bajo tu aplicación, que diga lo que muestra.
Luego vuelve a elegir la parte.

// before P35
This program has no drawing to add to:
add a <draw> to it first.

The novice-voice check, a reader with no context, failed it on one point. It found the wording short and clear, with a next step. But it called "to draw" an unexplained insider term: a newcomer isn't told what to type.

  • Its rewrite: "type a new line starting with "to draw" under your app's name, then describe what it shows, like "show a title"".
  • It also suggested naming the part ("Your {part} needs a place on the screen ..."), and saying where to pick it again ("from the parts list").

Why it was kept as it is:

  • The check's example isn't Spell. "show a title" is no line Spell reads, so it would teach the wrong thing.
  • "to draw" is Spell's own word for this section, the one the guides teach, so the notice uses it.
  • The other notice P35 wrote, the one for a part added but empty, passed the check.

One more thing, found reading it again: "Then pick the part again" doesn't fit Paste, where nothing was picked from the parts list.

In Spell, the line is nearly always to draw it (177 of the examples' files), with the parts it shows under it, one step in.

  • No change. "to draw" stays unexplained for someone who has never seen one.
  • Take the check's point, with a real Spell line: "In the code on the left, add a line under your app's name: to draw it. Then, one step in under it, write what it shows."
  • "Then add the part again", so it fits Paste too.
  • The Spanish to match, with «para dibujarla»; checked with the novice check again.
  • A button on the notice writes an empty "to draw it" section into the app, then adds the part there.
  • Nothing to type, but it's new behaviour: a small todo of its own, not a wording change.
  • Someone adding a part to an app that draws nothing is shown the exact line to type, and where.
  • The notice reads right after a Paste, too.
  • Two texts change, English and Spanish; no code.
