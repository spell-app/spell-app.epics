Several of one type share one switch, and the choice is remembered for their type.

  • Of several types there's no All, as there's no one type to change: each change goes on each one's own line where it has one.
  • A field's dot and tooltip speak for the last one clicked.
  • Events and Actions hide with several selected: they belong to one thing.
  • Fields that differ say Mixed; a change to all of them is one undo step.
