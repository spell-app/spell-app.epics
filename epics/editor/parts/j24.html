The plan said undo finds the home again from the current parse. Each step instead keeps the text before and after, a tag's head or the whole line, and finds the one nearest where it was. It's simpler and works in any language and file, and it does what the plan asked: typing elsewhere doesn't break it, and a deleted tag says so. Editing inside that same line by hand also counts as gone.
