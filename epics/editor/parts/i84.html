Owen, 2026-11-14: "Feels like <number> and <stock-pile> should be rounded rather than <>?" Asked which tags: "things that are implicitly making variables" (the lavender ones in his screenshot: <number "score"/>, <stock-pile "stock"/>, <discard-pile ...>; not <action "redo"/> or <text>).

  • Which tags: the ones the parse says DECLARE a member where they're drawn, <number "score"/>, <stock-pile "stock"/>, <column "C1"/>: the compiler's own test, not the tag's name or colour.
    • <action "redo"/>, <text>, <container>, and a view whose member is said on a line of its own, keep their points.
  • The parser's bubbly layout lists where each of those tags starts; the editor's marks give its pill, and its <, / and >, one class, bubbly-tag-declares.
  • ONE rule in bubbly.css: that pill's ends round, and the drawn ⟨ / ⟩ hidden.
    • The file's own characters stay, see-through, so the caret, clicks, selection and copy land as before.
  • The page (<spell-code>) draws a drawing's tags as coloured words, not pills: nothing to round there.
