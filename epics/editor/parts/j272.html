The plan said to follow Spell UI's own tracking (Browser.isDark, the theme menu's preference) and add no new watcher. The theme menu only tells its own listeners; the site header's sun / moon and the editor's own switch write <html>'s class without telling anyone.

So the element listens to ColorScheme.watch() (the OS, other tabs), UIT.THEME_APPLIED_EVENT (a theme worn), and one small observer on <html>'s class and style, where every switch shows (lint allow-listed). It reads the outcome with light-dark() on a probe, whatever switched it.
