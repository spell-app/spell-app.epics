In the editor, Solitaire, in Edit: right-click a card with a real mouse. On a Mac, also Control-click. On Windows, also press the Menu key and Shift+F10. Then in Run, right-click the board.

The menu opens at the pointer, with Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate, Delete, a line, Show in Code, Inspect. Hovering a greyed row shows why. In Run, the game's own menu shows.
