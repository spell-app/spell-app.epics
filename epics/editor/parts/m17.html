1. In VS Code's ⎇ editor window, open the editor from the epic page's link (or the Spell editor tab you use) and go Home.
  2. Under The editor, open Editor bar. The left pane shows a second editor, in a frame.
  3. In EditorBar.spellml, swap two lines inside the first <ui-menu position="left">: {draw its project menu} and {draw its app menus}. Press ⌘S, then ⌘Enter.
  4. Look at the frame, then the editor around it.
  5. File › Publish.
  • After ⌘Enter, the frame's bar shows File Edit View Run Help before the project's name; your own bar doesn't change.
  • After Publish, your own bar changes too.
  • Swap them back and publish again to undo.
