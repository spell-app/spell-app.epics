Once merged (and, for VS Code, after spell app vscode from the main checkout): in a drawing of a .spell file of your own, put the caret at the end of a line ending <content> and press Enter. Then type <x></x>, put the caret between the two tags, press Enter.After <content> the new line starts one tab further in. Between <x> and </x>: the caret on a new line one tab in, </x> on the line under it at <x>'s indent. The same in the web editor and in VS Code.
