Once merged: in Chrome, open the editor (test.spell.app) on a project of your own (not an example: the editor saves). In a .spell file, copy two lines of a body with ⌘C. Put the caret on a blank line inside another body, a level deeper, right-click in the code and pick Paste. Then press ⌘Z once.

The pasted lines land at the caret's indent, the second kept a level under the first if it was, exactly as ⌘V puts them. One ⌘Z takes the whole paste back.
