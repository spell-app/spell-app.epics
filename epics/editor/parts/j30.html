The app's own element fills its background, so a press there may be a band.

  • A press that never moves is still a click on the app, as before.
  • A band never selects the app, only the innermost parts it touches: cards, not the pile they're in.
  • A band touching nothing lets go of what's selected.
  • You can drag from any empty spot, and a click on it still inspects the app.
