For P38 · Mermaid Drawings: you asked for a tag that "draws in various styles". Which styles should <ui-mermaid-diagram> offer?

Mermaid has two separate dials. The LOOK is how lines are drawn: classic (plain lines), handDrawn (sketchy, like a whiteboard) and neo (flatter and softer, new in 2025). The THEME is the colours: eleven presets (default, forest, neutral, dark, neo, the newer redux family ...) and base, the one whose colours can be set.

Spell UI's own colours go through base, read from the page's theme, light and dark. Either way, settings written at the top of the diagram's own text still win, as they do on GitHub.

  • A look attribute: classic (the default), hand-drawn, neo.
  • Every diagram matches the page around it, and follows light and dark with no setting.
  • Mermaid's own colour themes are still there for anyone who writes one into the text.
  • A look attribute as in A, and a theme attribute: spell (the default), neutral (good for printing), forest, redux-color ...
  • Cost: Mermaid's presets don't follow Spell's light and dark, so each needs its dark partner picked, and some have none (forest).
  • Cost: more to translate, and more to keep up with as Mermaid renames its themes (it added four in 2026).
  • The two styles people know; neo left out until it settles.
  • Cost: Mermaid already makes neo the default look for flowcharts and five more types since 12.0, so leaving it out goes against where Mermaid is heading.
  • <ui-mermaid-diagram look="classic | hand-drawn | neo">, classic by default
  • colours always Spell UI's theme, read from the page, redrawn on a switch to dark
  • no theme attribute; a theme written into the diagram's own text still works
