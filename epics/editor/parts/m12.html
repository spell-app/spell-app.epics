1. In the ⎇ editor window's browser tab of the editor, open Solitaire (Home › Examples › Solitaire), in Edit.
  2. Click the + in the top bar: the first column is This app. Click Pile.
  3. In the Inspector's Views card, click the sort icon left of the magnifying glass, and pick By name, then By type.
  4. Open Not drawn at the bottom of Views, and click Deck.
  5. View › Program Panel, then its Reference tab. Type shuffle in its search, and click shuffle (a list).
  6. File › Discard Changes, so the example is as it was.
  • An empty pile is added under the board.
  • The board's piles reorder, and stay so after a reload.
  • The Inspector shows the deck's 52 cards.
  • The Reference shows This app and Spell, and shuffle's description.
