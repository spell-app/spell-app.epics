I165 makes the Inspector column's last card reach the column's bottom. When that card is folded (Views folded to its bar), a folded card is only its bar, so it can't stretch.

  • Chosen: the last open card stretches instead. With Views folded and nothing selected, the Inspector card (the crosshairs help) reaches down to the Code card's bar, and Views' bar ends the column.
  • Not chosen: nothing stretching, the folded bars sitting under the cards with the page showing below them, as before I165.
  • Why: the column always ends with a card, whichever cards are folded, and a folded bar always sits at the bottom where its card would end.
