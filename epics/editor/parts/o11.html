Two long aims shape the work now, even though neither is finished in these two weeks.

"Change this": select things on the screen, say what you want, and it happens. The epics' comments are the model: a place you can find again, a note, and a send.

  1. The selection supplies the place: project, source position, a short excerpt.
  2. A small box on the selection takes the words. It's a popup, not a panel.
  3. It reaches a Claude session, which edits the files.
  4. The program compiles again, keeps its state, and shows the result.
  • The app has no agent plumbing today.
  • The public preview has no server, so this works on your own machine first (Q10).

Turtles all the way down: the editor edited in the editor. It's far off in code, since the editor is compiled from Solid and isn't a Spell project. The steps (Q11):

  1. Inspect the editor's own <ui-*> elements, read only.
  2. Move panes to .spellml, one by one.
  3. Edit them with everything above.

Meanwhile, every widget the editor grows in this epic is a widget a Spell app can use, which is the real start of it.
