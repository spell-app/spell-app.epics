Fixed: selecting a part in the app with Inspect on opens the Views rows around it, then scrolls its row into sight, to the middle when it was out of view.

  • The cause: the rows were opened inside the update that changed the selection. That fold's write, made in the same update, redrew the app's pane through the editor's layout piece, so the Inspector went on watching the old, gone pane, and Views showed only what was left in it.
  • The fix: the rows open once that update is done, in one change.
  • Not fixed underneath: a pref written while the selection settles can still redraw <AppPane>, which its docstring says is drawn once. That's in the editor's layout files, P20's, so it's left for them.
  • scrollIntoSight() in spell/util/dom does the scrolling, and <ui-tree-item> uses it for any selected row.
