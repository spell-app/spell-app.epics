Owen, 2026-11-18: “Each SpellUI component should work as a layer, really expect to see the toolbar animate. How can we do this?”

Why it doesn't today:

  • a part's own tag draws no box (display: contents): its box is inside its shadow root
  • 3D flattens at any frosted blur, clip, filter, mask, opacity below 1 or isolation: the toolbar's glass blur is one

Proposed: a contract in UIComponent's base, so Anatomy never guesses:

  • each part names its layer box (the first drawn box in its shadow root)
  • an :state(anatomy) each part's CSS answers by dropping what flattens 3D (glass becomes a solid colour, clips open), put back on close
  • Anatomy sets the 3D context down the whole chain, across shadow roots and slots
  • layers fly apart from flat when it opens, so a toolbar visibly comes apart into its buttons

Its first case: I261, Solitaire's header. WIP on branch editor-i261-wip.
