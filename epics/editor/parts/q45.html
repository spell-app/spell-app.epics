For P35 · Placing And Isolation: you asked for a way to work on one view by itself, e.g. Solitaire's card, inside its own project. Where should that view-on-its-own show?

Today, opening Card.spell still shows the whole game in the app pane. The editor already draws views one by one for its own Spell (each view in a zone of its own), so drawing one view alone is not new.

  • With a view's file open, the app pane's bar gets a switch: App | Card alone.
  • Card alone shows one card in the middle of the pane, its property editor beside it.
  • The code, the palette, the Views tree and the Inspector stay where they are, and work on it.
  • Remembered per file: open Card.spell again and it's still alone.
  • Why: nothing new to learn; the pane you edit the app in is the one you edit a view in.
  • A new page in the editor for the view, reached from the file's tab or the Views tree's right-click menu ("Open Card Alone").
  • Its own address, so it can be bookmarked or shared.
  • Cost: a second page to keep in step with the editor: its palette, tree and Inspector have to be wired again.
  • A board of the project's views, each in a zone, as the editor's own Spell shows them: card, pile, deck.
  • Good for seeing them all at once; each still editable.
  • Cost: a busy pane for a project with many views, and it's further from "this one view".
  • the app pane's bar gets an App | (view) alone switch, shown while a view's file is open
  • no new page or route
