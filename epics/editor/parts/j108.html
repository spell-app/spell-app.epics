Spell's own <divider/> and <children/> put the caret after them, as you asked. A Spell UI tag that holds nothing (its vocabulary has no default slot) is written <icon |/> instead, its attributes listed at once: an icon or an input with no attributes says nothing.

  • 31 tags hold nothing by their vocabularies, among them icon, input, image, slider, textarea, rating.
  • Also code and markdown: they read their text, but through no slot, so they come out closed too. A slot in their vocabularies would change that.
