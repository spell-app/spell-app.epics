The plan said a step's text sits in a <ui-popup>. Built instead: the <ui-tour> element is itself a popover over the whole page (above dialogs opened before it), holding the ring, the masks and the step's box, a <ui-segment> placed beside the ring by CSS anchor positioning.

Why: a manual <ui-popup> is announced as a tooltip (its Back, Next and ✕ need a dialog), and its outside-click and Escape handling closes it rather than the tour. Same look for a person: a box beside the target, on the other side when there's no room.
