Looking for the "VSCode popup which shows when you are editing", two candidates turned up. Popups on hover and suggestions were already made quieter on 2026-10-29, but not these two.

A: what shows by itself as you type. A space after a word like put opens it over your code.

B: what shows while holding ⌥ over a word. It carries the compiler's words and the compiled JavaScript.

  • It's the only one that interrupts unasked, so it best fits "distracting".
  • It's the ugliest, and the least fit for a newcomer.
  • Both get the same treatment.
  • The first turns off by default, and both get plain words and the app's look. This is what gets built unless you say otherwise, since both need it.
