Proposed fix:

  • In the Code card, clicking an action's name ("cheat" in "to cheat") turns it into a small text field holding the name. Enter renames it, and Esc or clicking away cancels.
  • The rename goes through the language service, as a new renameActionAt(). It changes the action's head (keeping its quotes, title and icon), every call (if anything changed auto play) and every <action "…"/> button and menu row, in every file of the project. That's one step of the editor's history, then a compile.
  • Only for the program's own actions that take nothing. An action that takes something is called in its own words around what it takes, which a rename can't follow, and events and hooks (on click, when the score changes) have no name of their own to rename.
  • Refused, with a message: an empty name, one another action already has, one that isn't words, or a call written in other words than the name.
  • The editor's own rename (F2) on an action's head now renames actions too, the same way.
