Your comment C1 on P37 · Mermaid Drawings, on "Icons": "How hard to switch out to FA?"

Spell UI's icons are already Font Awesome 7 Free, drawn as SVG. Mermaid's fa:fa-car looks up a registered fa pack first and draws SVG, falling back to Font Awesome's font only when none is registered. So the tag registers just the icons a diagram names, fetched through UI.icons, before each draw: about a dozen lines.
