Violet with white text goes on plain and vertical menus, the docs list and the code suggestions. It stays off text menus, tabs, secondary, inverted and coloured menus, and segmented controls keep their white key.

  • Apple describes this highlight for lists; segmented controls are drawn differently.
  • Dropdown rows are unchanged.
  • Glass and Fruity docs pages show it too, once their bundles rebuild on main.

Replaced in part, after you approved this: you asked of the View menu, "Why are these purple?" In a menu of things on or off (<ui-menu ticks>), what's on is now a tick in plain text, and the solid fill is only for the row under the pointer or keyboard. Other plain menus keep the solid selection.
