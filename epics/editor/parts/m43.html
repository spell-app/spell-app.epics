Open Spell UI's Mermaid diagram page from the page server (/components/ui/ui-mermaid-diagram.html), Examples tab, Themes, Colours menu. Rest the pointer on the drawing; then Tab to it. Pick forest, then Dark, then reload; then Put back.

The small palette button shows in the drawing's top right after the usual hover wait, and at once by Tab. Forest and Dark redraw that one drawing, and are still there after the reload; Put back returns it to Spell's colours, like the page.
