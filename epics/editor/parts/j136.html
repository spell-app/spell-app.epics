Besides each card's ✕ and the Inspector card's crosshairs, the dropdown arrow beside the top bar's Inspector button (a menu ticking each card to show or hide it) is gone: it was the other way to hide one card. The button now opens or closes the whole column, every card in it; View › Inspector does the same, without starting Inspect app.

A card can still fold to its header with its chevron. An old remembered list of hidden cards is no longer read, so nothing stays hidden.
