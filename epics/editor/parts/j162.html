A select's menu was capped at 16em (about 7 choices), and never checked against the window.

  • Now every select's menu fits the room above or below it, whichever is bigger, and scrolls only past that, as <ui-dropdown>'s menu does.
  • Outside a compact editor the 16em cap stays (--ui-select-picker-max-height); a compact editor lifts it, so a segment's 10 looks show at once.
