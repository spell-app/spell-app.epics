1. In this worktree's editor (⎇ editor window, Spell › Home), open the example Solitaire.
  2. Click Edit in the top bar.
  3. Right-click a card on the table (Control-click on a Mac trackpad works too).
  4. Click Run in the top bar, and right-click a card again.
  5. Back in Edit, open the Views tree (View › Inspector) and right-click the DiscardPile row.
  • Step 3: Auto Play, Cheat, Restart, a line, Undo, Redo, then a THICKER line, then Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate, Delete, a line, Show in Code, Inspect, a line, Change This...
  • Choosing Cheat there turns a card up, and the menu goes.
  • Step 4: only the game's menu (Auto Play ... Redo), no Cut / Copy.
  • Step 5: the same menu as step 3.
  • Shift + right click anywhere in Edit: the browser's own menu.
