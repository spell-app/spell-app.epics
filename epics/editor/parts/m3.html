1. In macOS, open System Settings > Accessibility > Display, and turn on Reduce transparency.
  2. Look at the editor in Chrome, in Glass and in Fruity.
  3. Turn it off, turn on Increase contrast, and look again.
  4. Turn that off.

Reduce transparency: solid panels, no blur. Increase contrast: stronger edges and darker quiet text.
