Yes, and parent is the word we settled on. It's now in the Layout In Spell plan.

  • A pane written anywhere on the page says parent="the split panel's id", and joins that panel as if it were nested there.
  • <ui-section>, <ui-tab> and <ui-step> already work this way, through one shared piece of code, ParentLink.
  • The panes will be <ui-panel>s, which already take parent, so <ui-split-panel> only needs an id. 
<ui-split-panel id="editor-panes"></ui-split-panel>

<ui-panel parent="editor-panes">the code</ui-panel>
<ui-panel parent="editor-panes">the app</ui-panel>

One thing to watch: joining moves the element on the page. The running app's pane must not restart when it moves, and the plan already stops and comes back to you if it can't stay running.
