Which icon says 'won't be saved'?

Which mark should a field in the Inspector show when a change to it won't be kept? Today's crossed-out floppy disk doesn't read as “can't save”.

agent editor-icons of epic editor, after your note on the crossed-out floppy 2026-11-16
editor -- the Spell editor: here, the Inspector's property fields. Some fields can be changed while the program runs, but the change isn't kept: the program gives them no starting value, so the next run starts over. Hovering such a field shows a mark at its top right (the floppy under a red circle-slash, I226), and a line under it says “A change here won't be saved.” (I244). You sent a crop of the mark: “That doesn't read as can't save either. What other icons can we use?” The one mark every such field shows, in light and dark, English and Spanish. The note under the field and the tip on the mark stay as they are.
  • Each picture below is a “Score” field drawn as the Inspector draws it, hovered, so the mark shows: Fruity light, Fruity dark, then the mark alone, four times its size.
  • The mark sits at the label row's end, 1em, faded as the info icon beside it is; its tip says why.
  • Every icon is from Font Awesome 7's free set, which the editor already loads (fa7-free).
  • What a change there really does: it works now, and goes back to the program's value on the next run.
