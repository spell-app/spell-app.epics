Spell words to drive the editor

Plain Spell sentences that make the editor do things: switch modes, inspect a view, show a panel, open a file, start a tour. A button, a help page or a tour could then say switch the editor to edit mode instead of needing TypeScript. This page lists what we'd want, with wording for you to pick.

  • Every phrase here is NEW language, so each one needs your OK, with its ambiguity shown first.
  • Once you've decided, the editor session sends the words you picked to epic language, with /send language. It hasn't been sent.

The editor already has names for most of what it does, in its own Spell. Nothing outside the editor can use them yet.

  • The editor's menus are written in Spell, in the editor's own program (SpellEditor.spell). Each menu item is an action with a name:
  • The one list of those names is EDITOR_NAMES: about 40 actions (save, publish, show code, stop inspecting ...), plus the values that tick and grey the menus (inspecting, anatomy showing ...).
  • Some names read as statements already (stop inspecting, show inspector); others are nouns, menu labels more than sentences (run mode, edit mode, site view).
  • In Spell, calling an action is writing its words. Solitaire's to "deal the cards" is called as deal the cards. So "driving the editor" can mean: other Spell may call the editor's actions by their words.
  • Spell's events today read like this, from Solitaire: when creating solitaire, after moving a card to a pile, when the score changes, when clicked. The tours' events below follow that shape.
  • Everything else on this page (Anatomy's focus, the debugger's steps, Explain This, the Keyboard Shortcuts dialog, the Inspector's cards) is reached only from TypeScript today.

The editor's own help and tours will use these words. May an app you're working on use them too? A running app mustn't switch your modes, or throw your changes away, on its own.

An app's tour or button may switch modes, inspect, show panels and open files, only when the person clicked or pressed a key. Save, discard, publish and delete stay the editor's own.

  • Two kinds of words:
    • showing: modes, inspecting, panels, opening a file, Anatomy, help, tours. Nothing of yours changes.
    • changing: save, discard, publish, delete, rename, reset to saved, new file. Only the editor's own Spell may use these.
  • "On a click": as the browser allows a popup only right after a click. A timer, or when creating solitaire, can't switch your mode.
  • A tour that starts by itself is the exception: the person said yes to it (tours Q10), so its steps count as asked for.
  • Outside the editor (a published app, the Run tab): the words do nothing, quietly.

Help and tours that ship with Spell, nothing else. Safest; an app's own tour can't show its code.
