How should Inspect Card show the other parts here?

The right-click menu's "Inspect Card" row has a little arrow at its end that opens a list of every part under the mouse. You said nobody can tell the arrow is separate from the row. Pick how it should look instead.

editor -- Spell's editor: the app beside its code, with the Inspector. "Select Under Mouse" (editor's P31) built the split row: click "Inspect Card" to inspect the top part, or click its arrow for a list of every part under the mouse. You tried it: "the arrow in the menu doesn't work ... it's not clear that it is separate from the item itself" (Q37). How that row looks and behaves. Clicking it to inspect the top part stays the same either way; only the way to reach the other parts changes.
  • Today: one row, "Inspect Card", with a faint line and a › at its end.
    • Click the words: the Inspector shows the card you right-clicked.
    • Click the ›: a side menu lists every part under the mouse, the app down to the card's text, the card itself ticked.
  • The trouble: the line is faint, and a › on a menu row means "this whole row opens a side menu" everywhere else. So the row reads as one thing.
  • How other apps do this:
    • Figma: its own row, "Select layer ›", listing the layers under the cursor. The plain click on the canvas picks the top one.
    • macOS and VS Code: no split rows in menus at all. A row with › opens on hover, and clicking it does nothing more.
    • Microsoft Office: has split rows, but draws the arrow end as a separate button with its own hover.
  • Fitts's law, in short: a big target close by is quick to hit. A full-width row is a big target; a 30px arrow cell is a small one, and the mouse has to stop on it exactly.
