The header look

Is this one bar the look to take to every header in the editor? It's built on the Views card only, so far.

session editor, while building One Header Row (P2) 2026-10-31
editor -- polishing the editor for a public preview on the web. You asked that the tabs look good as a collapsible header over a pane, since they "feel like several concepts bolted together". The Views card now does this, and the plan said to show you before moving the rest. Whether the file tabs, the Program panel, Run | Edit and the properties card follow this look. Nothing else moves until you say.

The Views card's header was three things side by side: a caret, a capsule holding the tabs, and loose icons. Now it's one bar.

  • The bar is the track. The inner capsule is gone, and only the chosen tab's raised pill remains.
  • One line. The chevron, tab icons, tab text and action icons all sit within half a pixel of one centre line.
  • Closed, the bar is left. It still shows which view is chosen, and choosing a tab opens the card.
  • The actions reach the far edge, lined up with the properties card's eye above.
  • Narrow, the tabs become one dropdown, "Views ▾", at 300px and under (measured at 300, 230 and 170).

It's made of plain widgets, and the editor's own tuning for this card is deleted:

  • a <ui-panel appearance="pane">: one flat bar over the rest of the box
  • a <ui-toolbar appearance="segmented link header"> in its bar

Later headers would use the same two, folding or not.
