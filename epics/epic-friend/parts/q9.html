Names On Everything (P2) needs two things: a word for who wrote a reply, and a way to mark Claude's replies that isn't a name (O3). The coherence review found two words in use, and the first idea for the marker taken.

  • The page's replies say who wrote them in from (<epic-reply from="Owen">).
  • The comments' data, and the docs pages' note replies, say by.
  • role, the first idea for Claude's marker, is the browser's accessibility attribute: a reply with role="claude" would confuse screen readers.
  • from in the page's markup, by in data: both kept, and recorded as one idea in the word list.
  • Claude's replies carry a bare attribute: <epic-reply from="Ana" claude>, "claude": true in data.
  • No page changes beyond adding the marker.
  • One word: a migration renames every page's from to by.
