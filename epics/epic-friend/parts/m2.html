On GitHub, make the content repo private: Settings > General > Danger Zone > Change visibility. Or in a terminal: 

gh repo edit spell-app/spell-app.epics --visibility private --accept-visibility-change-consequences

Then check it:

gh repo view spell-app/spell-app.epics --json visibility

It prints PRIVATE. Only then merge epic-friend: its first turn after the merge pushes all 3,673 unpushed commits of spell.epics (every epic page, guide and comment).

Tell Claude before merging: it will take contentRemote back out of the kit's settings.
