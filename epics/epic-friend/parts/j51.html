The new epic-on-windows job (windows-latest) runs Processes' tests, epic's node tests and the window script's, with continue-on-error.

  • Nobody has run them on Windows: epic's tests write paths with / in many places, and what the job finds is epic-windows' to fix.
  • Linux needs no job of its own: the main checks job runs everything on ubuntu-latest.
  • A red Windows job shows in a PR without blocking it, until continue-on-error comes out.
