spell epic <args> sits with the kit's own commands (src/cli/commands.ts), beside runEpic() in tools.ts, not in the epic folder's manifest: the published spell has no epic bin of its own, so the kit is what has to find one.

  • It runs the checkout's epic/bin/epic, else the installed spell-epic's, else npx -y spell-epic@<spell's version>.
  • Never the epic on the PATH: that may be another checkout's, at another version.
  • npx spell@next epic init always runs an epic of the same version as the spell it came with.
