A comment or mark reaches the rest of the team only once its own Claude has answered it, since the review inboxes stay on each machine.

  • Comments and marks are shared through git before they're answered: the inbox split into a shared part by person, and a local part (listening, heartbeat, working). (Q4, its later step)
  • Each person's Claude answers that person's comments; others see them, and the answers, after a sync.
  • Old inboxes are read as before, and moved over once.

The inbox splits in two: a shared part, the comments and marks, kept by person and synced with the plans; and a local part, who is listening and the heartbeat. A rule says whose Claude answers what.

  1. Split the inbox. Each epic page's inbox becomes two files: <epic>.comments.<person>.json5, committed and synced, holding that person's comments, marks and their threads; and the local inbox, never committed, holding who is listening, the heartbeat and what's being worked on. ReviewInbox, CommentList and GuideInbox read both.
  2. One writer per file. Each person writes only their own comments file, so a pull never conflicts there. Claude's answers go into the comments file of the person it answers.
  3. Whose Claude answers. A person's Claude takes only that person's comments and marks; everyone else's show on the page, read only, with their name.
  4. Committed with the plans. Shared's ignore list keeps the local part out and lets the comments files in; the sync carries them.
  5. Old inboxes are read as before, and moved over once, into the person's file.
