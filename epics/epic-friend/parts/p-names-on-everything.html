About a dozen places decide "is this Claude's?" by testing the author against the literal "Owen", and every writer stamps "Owen" on what a person writes.

  • Claude's replies and status cards name whose session wrote them, "Claude (Ana)"; just "Claude" when it's your own. (Q7)
  • A person who calls themselves "Claude" stays a person: Claude's replies are marked by a role attribute, never by the name (O3).
  • Every reply, comment, mark, pick, test result and log line written for a person names them ("passed in Ana's run").
  • Your own words say "You" on the page; everyone else's show their name. All people share the ivory colour, Claude stays violet.
  • Old pages and inboxes still read: from="Owen" stays a person's reply, and a migration step adds the role to Claude's.

One check, isClaudes(), tells Claude's words by a role of their own, not by a name. Comments, marks, picks, test results and log lines carry the person's name; the page says "You" for your own, and "Claude (Ana)" for another person's Claude. A migration brings older pages along.

  1. One check. isClaudes(reply) in ThreadFacts: true when the reply carries Claude's role (role="claude" on a page reply, role: "claude" in an inbox). An old reply with no role: Claude's unless its name is "Owen", so nothing old changes meaning.
  2. The dozen tests of "Owen" call it: CommentList, EpicThread, PlanDoc (three), InboxCommands (two), the comments tool (two), GuideChanges, AnswerThread, EpicReply, MoreRequestsFirst. Whose turn it is becomes "person" | "claude" | "done" (the Turn type).
  3. Writers stamp the person. The server stamps a comment's by as it saves it, from the name of Phase 1; the CLI stamps replies, picks, test results and log lines from the same name. Claude's own replies get role="claude" and by = whose session wrote them.
  4. On the page. A person's reply shows "You" when it's yours, else their name, in the ivory colour; Claude's shows "Claude", or "Claude (Ana)" when it's another person's session. Status cards the same.
  5. Migration. A new step in Migrations adds role="claude" to Claude's old replies, so the fallback in step 1 can go one day.
  6. Bundles rebuilt, never hand-edited: the comments script, Spell UI's, the epics pack.
  7. Checked: tests with a person named "Claude" and one named "Ana"; the migration on a page with old replies; the demo checks check-review-epics and check-test-run; a page drawn with two names. yarn review in epic/.
  8. Left out: the wording "Owen" in page text, help and skills (the next phase).
