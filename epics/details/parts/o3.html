The pieces, and what each one does.

  • The template, details.html: the page, its context area and question markup.
  • details.js, and its CSS:
    • turns the question markup into Spell UI cards
    • posts the answer
    • shows the answered state on load, through the route's GET
  • The route module, detailsRoutes.ts: POST and GET on /api/details/answer.
    • listed before the app's routes (appRoutes.ts): their /api ends in a catch-all 404
    • it maps the page's URL, on main or under /worktrees/<w>/, to its file
    • ONLY inside a details/ folder of the docs
  • The script, details.js: yarn details new | show [--wait] | wait | answer | list | sweep.
    • built as planned; the durable doc is Details Pages
  • The skill, SKILL.md: when to reach for it, how to write a good page, and the flow above.
  • A line in the root CLAUDE.md, and the "modal questions" memory: more than 4 options, or a picture needed, means /details.
