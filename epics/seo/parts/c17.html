A few examples put their parts inside test-only stand-in owners, instead of a real ui-* family. Their static render can't match the live one.

  • the stand-ins: stub-accordion and stub-search
  • an example, the title part's (title.html): 
    <stub-accordion><ui-title>What is a dog?</ui-title></stub-accordion>
  • As I read it: the static render handles only the families it knows, and a stub-* owner isn't one. Not checked further.
  • A duplicate of this caveat, made when a script ran twice, was closed as C18.
  • nothing changes: those examples stay out of static parity
