D54 · P5: per-page stylesheets clear StaticRender.sheetUsage before each page; --css lets it add up

sheetUsage grows over every render; a per-page sheet should scope only to what THAT page adopted, so StaticDocument.render() clears its two maps first. With --css (one sheet for many pages) it isn't cleared, and the sheet is built once after the last page from the union of their tags.
