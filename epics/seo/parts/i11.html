In a shadow root, a ::slotted() rule LOSES to any page rule (outer context wins, whatever the layers), and reset.css never reaches light children. Statically both are plain rules in ui.* layers: (1) rewritten ::slotted() rules now beat the page's .ui-typography (ui.base), e.g. a slotted <ul> in <ui-message> gets the message's 8px top margin and 16px indent instead of typography's 0 / 24px, and .header + p spacing applies where the page's p margins won; (2) reset.css is @scope ([data-ui]) with no limit, so author ul / p / h* inside any component lose their margins and padding. Seen in ui-message/types (11%), variations (16-18%), content (4.7%). Suggested: emit ::slotted()-derived rules in a layer before page (e.g. @layer ui-slotted, page, ui;), and give reset.css the components' limit plus the slotted elements themselves (to ([data-ui-slotted]:not([data-ui]))).
