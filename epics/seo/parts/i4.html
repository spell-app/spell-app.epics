UIItem adopts its OWNER's sheets in its shadow root (ui-list.css holds the item rules, e.g. the image shorthand's avatar size at ui-list.css:449, :host(:state(in-list)) > .item > img.avatar.image). StaticStylesheet scopes ui-list.css to [data-ui="list"] only, and the limit [data-ui-slotted] > * stops at the slotted item root, so nothing inside an item gets list rules. Result: every <img class="ui avatar image"> renders at full width; ui-list/variations is 55575px tall statically vs 2736px as elements (98.7% different), ui-list/content 3791 vs 978px.
