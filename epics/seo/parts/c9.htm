Some static list items are wrappers, <li data-ui-li> with display: contents, around a card or a button. Older Safari tells screen readers they aren't list items.

  • Older WebKit dropped the list-item role of an element with display: contents.
  • Current browsers keep it.
  • The role=list on the <ul> stays either way.

Net effect:

  • nothing changes: only screen-reader users on older Safari are affected
