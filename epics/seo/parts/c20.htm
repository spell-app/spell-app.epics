A list, table, form, modal, accordion or card written inside a <p> works live, but on the static page it closes the paragraph early, so its content drops out of the paragraph.

  • Replaces the two earlier caveats on paragraphs (C12, C14).
  • What's fixed: the flattener turns every <div> root under a <p> into a <span> (StaticFlattener.inParagraphs(); the decision on roots in running text, D43).
  • What isn't: a root that is another block element still closes an open <p> in a browser's parser:
    • <ul> / <ol>: list, steps
    • <table>
    • <form>
    • <dialog>: modal, flyout
    • <details>: accordion
    • <article>: card
  • Authors don't see it until the static page: as elements it's valid, since a host is phrasing content.

Net effect:

  • authors wrap such elements in a <div>, not a <p>
