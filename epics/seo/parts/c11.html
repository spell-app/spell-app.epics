Popups, modals, flyouts, page dimmers and modal sidebars always start closed on a static page, even when the author wrote them open.

  • Why: their open state needs JS (showModal(), showPopover()), so the static render writes the closed starting state:
    • a closed <dialog>
    • a hidden popover
    • the content still in the HTML
  • Example: <ui-popup on=manual open> renders closed; that's the 1% parity difference in the ui-popup/variations example.
  • Similar: a shape shows its active-index side; tabs show the selected pane.
  • nothing changes: a reader opens them with their trigger
