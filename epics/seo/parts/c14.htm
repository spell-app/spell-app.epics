The static HTML is re-parsed: a <div> (or <hr>, <ul> ...) start tag closes an open <p>. ui-dropdown/types' "Show me posts by <ui-dropdown inline>" sits in a <p>, so statically the dropdown drops to its own line (4.7%, most of that example's diff). Any family with a <div> root inside phrasing content does the same. Authors: a <div> / <span> around inline elements, not <p>.
