The terminal study's live board, in a real terminal (VS Code's is fine), run from spell_core's folder.

It was only ever clicked headless, by its test.

cd spell-lang/src/py/core
uv run --group study python experiments/terminal/textual_board.py
  • a Solitaire board fills the terminal: the stock, the discards, four foundations, seven tableau piles
  • hovering a card lights it up yellow
  • clicking a face-up card that can move (an ace, or one lower and the other colour than a tableau's last card) moves it, and the board is drawn again
  • clicking the stock turns its top card onto the discards
  • q quits
