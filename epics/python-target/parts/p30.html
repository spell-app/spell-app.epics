A test plays a game by the page's messages, a click or a call, and checks the patch each one sends back.

  • The player is spell_core's own module, spell_core.playing (judgement call J82).
    • Each message is run by the program's page session, as the server runs it, and answered by its patch.
    • After each message, the patches so far must add up to the program's whole state; when they don't, it raises PatchMismatchError.
    • Things are named by their @id until references land in P27 (judgement call J83).
  • Solitaire's test plays seed 1 patch by patch:
    • the whole deal: 52 cards, 23 in the stock, tableaus 1 to 7
    • a click on the stock patches exactly three things: the card, the stock and the discards
    • a function call, by name
    • five moves, each patching only the cards that changed
    • a click on a face-down card patches nothing
    • a change left out of a patch fails the check
