The printed-thing decision (Q17) sends a dump of a thing or a list to stderr. Python has no things yet (P10), so chose a rule that already covers them over one that names their classes: a line goes to stderr when any of its values isn't plain data.

  • Plain data, on stdout: text, numbers, yes / no, nothing, plain lists (lists and tuples) and dicts.
  • Anything else, on stderr: a thing, a spell list (a class of its own in P10), a function, a set.
  • The whole line goes there, words and all: print "the card:", card is one line on stderr.
  • Warnings and errors go to stderr as before, as in node.
  • P10's things and lists go to stderr with no change to the console
