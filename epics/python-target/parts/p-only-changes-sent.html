After P17, the only way to bring a reader up to date is the whole state again: every thing, after every click and at every pause, though a click changes a card or two.

spell_core notes each change to a thing or a list: which thing, by id, and which property or which items (Q21, B).

It hands over a patch on request: every change since the last patch, then it forgets them.

A check, run by the tests: the patches, applied in turn to the first full state, give the full state of now.
