Where the page has no terminal equal, the prototype chose the nearest thing, not a general answer. Each is in What doesn't carry over.

  • A class starting with a digit (a card's rank, 8) becomes _8: Textual refuses it as it is.
  • Emoji suits lose their "draw me as a picture" mark (♠, not ♠️): terminals disagree on how wide the mark draws, so columns slip.
  • A stacked pile's cards overlap by docking them all to the top (Textual), or by showing the last only (Rich, Ink).
  • An icon draws as a pattern of text, ░░░░.
  • A span across two columns becomes two equal shares of the row (Textual), or a cell then an empty one (Rich).
