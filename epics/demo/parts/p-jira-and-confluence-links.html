Tickets and pages that need you are easy to miss first thing.

  • One sentence: how many tickets and pages, and the most important one by name.
  • Links sent to your phone, never read aloud.

The links part finds the tickets assigned to you and the pages you were tagged on, and says how many there are.

The links themselves go to your phone, so you can open them later.

  1. Ask Jira for your open tickets, and Confluence for pages that tag you since yesterday.
  2. Name the most urgent one; count the rest.
  3. Send the links to your phone.
