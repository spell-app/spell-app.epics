Mornings are serious enough. The briefing should end with a smile.

  • One joke at the end of every briefing, under 10 seconds out loud.
  • No repeats within 30 days.
  • Works on its own, before the other parts are built.

The joke part picks one short, clean joke a day, and never the same one twice in a month.

  1. A list of 60 short jokes, checked to be clean and kind.
  2. Pick today's from the ones not told in 30 days, and remember it.
  3. Call the briefing from the good morning handler, with the joke as its only part for now.
