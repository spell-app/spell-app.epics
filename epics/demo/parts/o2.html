Three small steps, each one easy to test on its own:

  1. Gather. Ask the calendar, mail and weather at the same time, and stop waiting after 3 seconds.
  2. Pick. Keep only what matters: the next few meetings, the mail you'd answer first, today's weather.
  3. Say it. Turn the picks into one natural paragraph, and speak it.
