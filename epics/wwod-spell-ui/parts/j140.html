The ariaLabel getters added in step 1 are gone: the raw attribute is read inline, where it's used.

  • The table's form: this.attributes[UIT.ARIA_LABEL].
  • Gone from:
    • UIReveal, UISidebar, UITabs, UIDimmer, UIBreadcrumb
    • UIDocsNav, UITable, UIMenu, UIRoot
    • UIItem, which also lost its active-attribute and ariaExpanded getters
  • Kept where JSX needs undefined, not null, in several places:
    • UIButton (private)
    • UILabel
    • DialogElement: its onNameChanged watches it. An onChange can't name a raw attribute.
  • controller.ariaLabel no longer exists on those; nothing read it.
