Where disabled only changes how a component looks, its getter is named looksDisabled. It never overrides isDisabled, which also swallows clicks.

  • The table asked for @cssState("disabled") get isDisabled() on families whose disabled is only a look.
    • But overriding isDisabled makes UIHost swallow every click on the host.
    • None of them did that.
  • Groups 4, 6, 7, 9 and 10 each kept the behaviour, under a new name. Step 3 made it ONE name.
  • @E.cssState("disabled") get looksDisabled() is on:
    • UIIcon, UIText, UIForm, UIField, UIFields, UIEmoji
    • UISegment, UILoader and UIProgress: group 9's isMarkedDisabled, renamed
  • It's documented once, on UIElement.isDisabled, and in ui's AGENTS.md.
  • Families that DO disable keep the isDisabled override, with @cssState on it. That's UIImage, UIReveal, UIDropdown and the form controls.
  • :state(disabled) works as before, and clicks are unchanged.
  • To make one swallow clicks, rename its getter to isDisabled.
