Swapping <ui-popup on="click"> for on={UIT.CLICK} in <ui-docs-themes> broke every render: Failed to execute addEventListener ... parameter 2 is not of type Object. Solid treats an on-prefixed attribute with an EXPRESSION as an event binding (event name "", handler "click"); a string literal stays an attribute.

  • Kept the literal, with a JSX comment saying why (UIDocsThemes.palette())
  • Any family passing on (popup, dimmer ...) to a COMPOSED ui-* tag must do the same; attr:on={...} may be the way to keep a constant (not tried)
