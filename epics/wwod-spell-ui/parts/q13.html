Revised in P1 (2026-10-05), after reading docs/theming.md "Component tokens": the two private name shapes mean different things, on purpose:

  • --_ui-button-radius: the private ALIAS of a public token (--ui-button-radius), the public name with _, mechanical and grep-able
  • --_button-*, --_card-*: variation plumbing no page ever sets; renaming them --_ui-button-* would make them read as aliases of public tokens that don't exist
