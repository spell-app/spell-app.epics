Each component's sections are now grouped by property, not by runtime phase, as the brief asked. The diffs are big, but mostly moves.

  • A property's section holds its state, getter, derived values, handlers and @onChange together.
  • For example:
    • UICalendar: Dates, The value, The popup, Views and pages, Range partners, Form
    • UIDropdown: Options, Value, The form, Search, The menu rows, Open
    • UIDocsNav: The list, The current page, Search, Favourites ...
    • UITable: Rendering, The managed table, Data mode, Sorting
  • Also one interface line per concrete class: export interface X extends E.AttributeValues<...> {}.
  • Reading order only.
  • The diffs of UIDocsNav (~660 lines) and UITable (~480) are mostly moves.
