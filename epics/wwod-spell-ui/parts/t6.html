A form with an off-value toggle should submit panel=closed, then panel=open.

  • Open the checkbox page's Examples tab, "Two Values". Or yarn dev, then ui-checkbox, values.
  • Wrap the toggle in a <form> that submits to a page showing its query. Or log [...new FormData(form)] in the console.

Expect:

  • The toggle: panel=closed while unchosen, panel=open when chosen.
  • The email box starts chosen: reply=yes, then reply=no.
  • A ui-change listener sees detail.value as "open" or "closed".
  • form.reset() brings back the starting values.
