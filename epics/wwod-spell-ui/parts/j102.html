Runs of 3 or more checks on one DOM subject became one toMatchObject() on the element itself, reading ARIA through its properties.

  • For example: 
    expect(element).toMatchObject({ localName, className, role, ariaLabel ... })
  • The same on a computed style, on host.internals, and on events.
  • Chosen over building a plain object out of getAttribute() calls.
  • Checked first: a mismatch does fail, on elements, on computed styles, and on the element's internals.
  • ARIA attributes are read through their properties (ariaLabel, ariaCurrent ...). An absent one reads ariaHidden: null.
  • Attributes with no property, or whose URL the browser resolves, keep their own getAttribute() check: part, href and others.
  • An event's target stays toBe(host). toMatchObject() compares DOM nodes as equal-looking, not as the same node.
  • The static-render test files (*.ssr.test.tsx) keep their per-attribute checks. Their nodes are linkedom's, which have no ARIA properties.
  • No test checks anything different.
  • ProgressValues.test.ts uses toEqual() on the instance. Lint's no-misused-spread refuses WWOD §20's spread copy, { ...value }.
