A page with its own component pack: demo.html. Its pack.json and hello.js sit beside it.

  • It's shared content, served by this checkout's page server at /ui/examples/component-pack/demo.html.
  1. Open it from THIS worktree's page server: spell dev server url, or /spell-serve.
    • Expect the site layout, and a card: "Hello, Owen! This element came from a component pack."
    • And a gold "new" badge, with no console warnings.
  2. DevTools, Network:
    • pack.json once, and hello.js once: two tags, one module.
    • On any site page, docs.components.json once.
  3. DevTools, Console: document.querySelector("main ui-components").matches(":state(loaded)")
    • Expect: true.
  4. Edit pack.json: change "skeleton": "inline 4 x 1.5" to "inline 4 by 1.5", and reload.
    • Expect a console warning: 
      [@spell-app/ui] <ui-components>: the pack pack.json didn't load (render): ... can't read "inline 4 by 1.5" ...
    • The card and the badge stay empty. No root reports them: they aren't ui-*, and no pack names them now.
    • Put it back after.
  5. Skeletons, which flash by on a fast load: in DevTools, Network, throttle to "Slow 3G" and reload.
    • Expect: while hello.js loads, a placeholder 18em wide, with a header and two lines, where the card goes.
