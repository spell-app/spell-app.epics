<ui-components> lives in <ui-root>'s family. The root's barrel defines it BEFORE the root. Rejected: a family of its own (ui-components/).

  • Its files, in the root's family folder:
    • UIComponents.ts
    • ComponentPack.ts
    • ui-components.vocabulary.en.ts
    • ui-components.fallback.ts
  • Its own family (rejected): it needs RootLoader, which lives in the root's folder.
    • Two library entries importing it would make Rolldown split RootLoader and the catalog into a hashed chunk both load.
    • And RootLoader's own glob would list ui-components' index.ts as a lazy family.
  • In the root's family (chosen): a pack only means something to roots.
    • Defining it in the same barrel, first, lets a root find every pack on its way before it looks at its tags.
    • That works because defining a tag upgrades its elements in place, in order.
  • ui's root entry (@spell-app/ui/ui-root) defines four tags:
    • <ui-components>
    • <ui-root>
    • <ui-loader>
    • <ui-placeholder>
  • The catalog says "ui-components": { folder: "ui-root" }.
  • No new library entry.
    • The root family's chunk grows from 11.24 to 12.25 kB gzipped, with the skeleton parser (yarn measure).
    • core.js is unchanged: 113,028 B, 33,299 gzipped.
  • It adopts the family sheet, ui-root.css, for :host { display: contents } alone.
