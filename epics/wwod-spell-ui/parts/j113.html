Chose dropping none from SkeletonText's grammar over keeping it for component packs, because a pack never needed it: an entry without skeleton already draws nothing of its own, and its word still wins over the catalog's (RootLoader.skeletonFor(), pinned by RootLoader.test's 'wins over the catalog'). Keeping it would leave two spellings of one thing, against the left-out rule.

  • SkeletonText.parse() returns a SkeletonSpec (was | false); format() takes one
  • a pack or vocabulary writing "none" now fails loudly: can't read "none" (a pack: a render SourceError naming the entry)
  • no shipped pack used it (docs.components.json, ui/examples/component-pack/pack.json); the test fixture's x-pack-legend lost it
