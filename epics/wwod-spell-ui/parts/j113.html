Skeleton text no longer has a none: a pack entry with no skeleton just leaves the skeleton key out.

  • Why: a pack never needed it.
    • An entry without a skeleton already draws nothing of its own.
    • And its word still wins over the catalog's (RootLoader.skeletonFor()).
    • RootLoader's test "wins over the catalog" pins that.
  • Keeping it would leave two spellings of one thing, against the rule that optional things are left out.
  • SkeletonText.parse() returns a SkeletonSpec; before, it could also return false. Formatting takes one, too.
  • A pack or vocabulary that writes "none" now fails loudly: can't read "none". In a pack, that's a "render" SourceError naming the entry.
  • No shipped pack used it: not the docs pack, nor the example pack (pack.json). The test fixture's x-pack-legend lost it.
