Renames in the form, button, dimmer, label, statistic and breadcrumb components. isValid() stays a method, and Owen's two notes are answered.

  • isValid() stays a METHOD on the form, its host, and FormController. A getter would change the element's API, and validate on every read.
  • The four form states are one getter each, on form, field and fields.
    • They're isError, isInfo, isSuccess and isWarning.
    • StateFlags is removed.
  • UIForm.rules() became get formRules(), over the rules getter. Reading after a write is fresh now.
  • UIField.shownErrors() is gone: the field's host reads untrack(() => controller.errors).
    • Brand's field and its host collapsed the same way in step 3, with @state formErrors.
  • Owen's "WTF? shortcut keys?" note, on the button: its invoker effect is the Invoker Commands API, not keys.
    • That's the commandfor and command attributes.
    • It's now one @onChange, with a section comment: 
      @onChange("commandfor", "hasNativeInvokers") onCommandTargetChanged()
  • Owen's "horrible, impossible to read" note, on the token block of ui-button.css:
    • The names are KEPT: --_ui-button-* is the package-wide alias convention (Q13). componentTokens.test.ts enforces it.
    • The block is grouped under headers: Box, Type, Colours ...
    • A line says how to read an alias.
  • The renames: 
    UIDimmer      isActive()  -> isShowing
              activeState -> isActive
UIBreadcrumb  tokens()    -> dividerTokens
  • Element APIs are unchanged: el.values, el.isValid() and el.errors.
  • A token rename, if wanted, is its own sweep.
