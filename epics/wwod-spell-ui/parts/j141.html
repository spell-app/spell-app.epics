The helpers that give an attribute its default stay as getters, though the table wanted them gone.

  • Why: the converter turns an EMPTY or unknown value into undefined.
    • For example show="", or type="weekly".
    • The vocabulary's default applies only when the attribute is absent.
  • The getters:
    • UICalendar.calendarType: this.type ?? DEFAULT_TYPE
    • UIDocsThemes.shownControls
    • UIDocsToc.menuSize
  • No behaviour change; three small getters.
