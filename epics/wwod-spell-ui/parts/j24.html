Chose to keep ServerRuntime.STYLES (src/static/ServerRuntime.server.ts) a private static readonly over moving it to a module const, because its closures call ServerRuntime.registered, a PRIVATE registry: a module const below the class can't reach a private member (TypeScript refuses). The other way out, making registered public, trades away the registry's privacy for no bundle gain (src/static/ is node-only, never bundled).

  • 61 of the 63 ALL-CAPS statics are module constants now; the two left are ServerRuntime.STYLES (this) and UIElement.ISOLATE_ERRORS (a debug switch, as Q18 says).
  • STYLES' docstring says why it stays.
  • No constant went ABOVE a class: none is read while its class loads.
