Effects that used to live in render() now also name isReady, and return early until it's true. That keeps their old timing.

  • Why: an @onChange starts in onMount(), before an element that isn't ready yet renders.
    • The effects it replaced were created inside render().
    • That is, once the runtime and sheets were in, and the box existed.
  • The effects, by group: 
    UITransition.onVisibleChanged, UIVisibility.onWatchSettingsChanged   (group 1)
UISticky's watch                                                     (3)
UIDropdown.onOpenChanged + its highlight                             (4)
UISidebar's two                                                      (6)
UIDimmer's two                                                       (7)
DialogElement.onOpenChanged
UIPopup.onPopoverModeChanged     isReady + trigger:  popoverMode reads UI.browser, which exists only once loaded
UIProgress's ARIA                its ariaValues getter returns undefined until ready
  • Step 3 added UIShape's two; group 8 had flagged a change.
    • onSidesChanged(isReady, sides)
    • onActiveSideIndexChanged(isReady, index)
    • So an active-index change before the sheets load flips, animated, once the stage is drawn. It no longer swaps at once.
