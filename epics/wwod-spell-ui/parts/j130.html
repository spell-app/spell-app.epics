An @onChange starts in onMount(), before a not-yet-ready element renders; the effects it replaced were created inside render(), i.e. once the runtime and sheets were in and the box existed. Each such effect names isReady and returns early until it's true:

  • UITransition.onVisibleChanged, UIVisibility.onWatchSettingsChanged (group 1), UISticky's watch (3), UIDropdown.onOpenChanged + its highlight (4), UISidebar's two (6), UIDimmer's two (7), DialogElement.onOpenChanged, UIPopup.onPopoverModeChanged (isReady + trigger: popoverMode reads UI.browser, which exists only once loaded), UIProgress's ARIA (its ariaValues getter returns undefined until ready).
  • Step 3 added UIShape's two (group 8 had flagged a change): onSidesChanged(isReady, sides) and onActiveSideIndexChanged(isReady, index), so an activeIndex change before the sheets load flips (animated) once the stage is drawn, instead of swapping at once.
