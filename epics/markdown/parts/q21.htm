D16 · Case-insensitive literals: /i after a keyword or choice, e.g. (note|tip)/i

Owen's idea, {xxxx}/i, regex-style. Scope: a keyword (note/i) or a choice of literals ((note|tip)/i). Not a {subrule}: what it matches is that rule's business, so {x}/i is an error. / is only a flag directly followed by i; spell's (operator:/|divided by) is untouched.
