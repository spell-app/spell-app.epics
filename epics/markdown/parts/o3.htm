Dogfooding finds spell-isms in $/parser. Fixed in the parser, simplest change that works, each through the review gate (D5, D7). Known before starting:

  1. Tokenizer: tokenize, matchWord, matchText, matchComment, matchJSXElement ... are arrow-function FIELDS, so a subclass method of the same name is silently shadowed. No subclass existed until now.
  2. Default tokens misfire on markdown: don't ... won't one quoted string; http://x and --- start comments; # a comment; <b> JSX (unclosed <br> swallows the rest); 1. a number; café splits; ¬ / ∆ rewritten.
  3. Literals.matchAtStart ignores whitespace: ** also matches * *, though Symbols' docs say "no space in-between".
  4. No HTML-string serializer: P.render.toText strips tags; toHTML ~20 lines.
  5. Choice tries every alternative, keeps the longest: may be slow on inline text.
  6. The P barrel loads all of it (2.7k-line JS-codegen AST.ts, scopes, incremental): matters for the ui bundle (1.5).
