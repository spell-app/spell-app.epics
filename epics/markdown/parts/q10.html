After P2.3 a rule knows its spacing, but printing it back as syntax drops it: isn't prints as isn ' t, -{spaces}{x} as - {x}. Re-read, those are LOOSER (spaced parts may space), never wrong. Fixing it is ~40 lines across the four printers. Used by spell's SPELL: DECLARES comments and LSP descriptions.

  • Each container prints the gap before a part from its spacing (P.rulexSpacing()): none touching, one / some as {space} / {spaces}, unset one space. Sequence, Literals and spell's SpellStatement share P.joinRulex(); literalSeparator is gone.
  • Repeat: - {3,} vs -{3,}, [{item},] vs [{item} ,], {name:rule}+ as written, and a sequence list item keeps its parens (it printed unreadable before: [sources:its {property} and]).
  • Printed as spell writes them: the? not (the)?; (=|is|of) not (=|(is|of)).
  • Spell's 472 syntaxes: 452 print back exactly, 20 (5 syntaxes) read back as the same rule but lose redundant parens or a space inside {value: expression}; 0 unreadable (4 before). docs/rulex/experiments/round-trip.mts.
  • Speed unchanged (printing isn't on the parse path): SP.spellParser.speedTest() 72-74 ms on the diff, 69-73 ms on HEAD.
