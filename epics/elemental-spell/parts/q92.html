Owen asked: "Are we saving the AST, so writing back from the editor's sidebar is easy?"

  • Not to disk, and it needn't be.
  • While a file is open, every node of its parse knows its exact span in the file's text.
  • Writing a change back is replacing that span's text and reparsing it: P11's cells already save that way, and P16's do too.
  • So a sidebar edit is easy, in .spell and .spellx alike.
