Each spellx tag line is rewritten into its code-spell twin's tokens (SpellxTwin), then parsed by the ordinary rules: the same match, AST and code by construction, and no rule copied.

  • The brief said a rule per tag: instead the twin words are each language's data, in its words file (spellx.twins), and the only new rule is spellx_closer.
  • A slot keeps the file's own tokens (name, values, phrase, an inline body); the twin's words are new tokens at the tag's start, marked synthetic.
  • A description becomes the line's trailing comments, so it's the docstring.
