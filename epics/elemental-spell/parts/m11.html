1. In the elemental-spell worktree's editor (its own port, in .spell-server.editor.json), open @spell/examples/Solitaire and press Run.
  2. Check the cards look and turn over exactly as before: click the stock, drag a card to a pile.
  3. Open Solitaire.spellx. In the board's drawing, after {draw its T7}, add a line: 
    <card id="queen-of-spades"><text slot="front">Q</text><text slot="back">Hello</text></card>
  4. Press Run again.
  • Every card in the piles draws as before, the queen of spades too.
  • The new card after the seventh tableau shows Q and Hello in place of its two pictures (outside a pile both its sides show).
  • The browser's inspector shows it as <solitaire-card> holding the two <ui-text slot=...>, and in its shadow root <slot name="front"> and <slot name="back">, each with the card's own drawing inside, unused.
