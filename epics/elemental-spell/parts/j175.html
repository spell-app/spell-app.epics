• Each slot placed in a drawing becomes the web's <slot name="front">, holding the slot's own drawing.
  • Nothing put in it: the browser shows that drawing, as today.
  • Something put in it (slot="front"): that, for that one card; every other card draws its own.
  • A subclass that draws the slot again changes what's inside the <slot>: its drawing shows when unfilled, and a filling still wins.
  • CSS: the slot's own drawing keeps part="front", so ::part(front) still styles it; a filling is the page's own element, styled by the page's CSS ([slot=front]), which ::part can't reach.
  • A <slot> takes no box of its own, so nothing moves on the page.
  • Where the drawing isn't in its element's shadow root (an app's page, drawn in its children), the slot's own drawing shows, and slot= does nothing.
