The words a person reads and writes change; the base parser's own words don't (Q23).

  • A Template declares a kind of thing (was "type"); a Thing is one of them.
  • A View is a template that draws itself (was "drawable thing"); a viewable list was "drawable list" (Q58); a two sided view is a view with a front and a back (Q1).
  • A Page is a view with its own address; an App is a view you publish (was "project").
  • An Action is something a template does; an alias is the words it answers to (Q57); a part is one of a view's named drawings (Q56).
  • The old words are dropped, every file migrated (Q61): P1.
