Outside a paragraph, a <br> lays its neighbours out one per line (Rating's ratings, Icon's sizes). The converter writes those lines as a <stack spacing="none">: a line of one element is the element, a line of several (or of text) a <text> around them, one line of text. spacing="xs" drew the rows about 5px further apart than the docs page's; none is within a few pixels. A <br> anywhere else (inside a table cell's text, a checkbox's label) stays in its <html> block.
