• width="20" is 20 pixels, as font="14" and <image width> read a bare number.
  • xs, sm, md, lg, xl (and none) are the theme's space steps, the same words <stack spacing> takes.
  • Anything else is a CSS length as written: 2em, 50%.
  • Not the column grammar of a field's width (4 of 16): a space isn't in a grid.
