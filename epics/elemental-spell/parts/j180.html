Every converted example is <example "Name" description="...">: its name the page's heading, or its section's title. The description paragraph's <code> becomes backticks and its <a> a Markdown link, [words](link), which <ui-example>'s description now draws as a link. A message or other markup with slot="description" stays a bare spellx child, and the example now draws it AFTER the attribute's sentence (before, the slot replaced the sentence).
