Owen, 2026-10-24, on Q45: "<draw "front"> shortcut".

  • With a body, <draw "front">…</> DEFINES the front drawing: short for <draw section="front">, by Q39's bare names.
  • Self-closing, <draw "front"/> PLACES it (Q48).

		<otherwise>

		</>

</>
