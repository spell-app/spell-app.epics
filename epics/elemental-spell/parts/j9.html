• vista for view, lista visible for viewable list, página / pagina for page.
  • vista reversible for two sided view, not vista de dos caras: de is the word between a list and what it holds (lista de cartas), so a built-in name holding it would be read as a list.
  • The old words (cosa dibujable, lista dibujable, dibujable) are errors naming the new ones, as in English.
