A <br> converts to a <stack spacing="none"> around the lines: a wrapper for what is just a gap.A new <ui-space> (spellx <space>, Spanish <es-espacio>): a line break by default; inline, width, height; fluid (grows and shrinks), grow and shrink (a switch, or a number). The converter writes <br> as <space/>, and the 20 br stacks are undone.
  1. The <ui-space> element
    • Symptom: Spell UI has no gap or break of its own, so a drawing (which has no <br>) wraps lines in a stack instead.
    • Changes: a new element, <ui-space>, with no look: an empty box.
    • Bare, it is a line break: what follows starts on a new line (J188).
    • inline: a gap inside the line instead, a space's width unless width says.
    • width / height: a number is pixels, xs ... xl the theme's space steps, anything else a CSS length (J186).
    • fluid: grows and shrinks with its row or column; grow and shrink, a switch or a number, for each half on its own (J189).
    • Spanish: <es-espacio> (J187).
    • Its docs page, with examples of each; its tests; in the root catalog, so <ui-root> loads it.
  2. <space> in spellx
    • Symptom: a drawing can't name a gap.
    • Changes: none of its own: a drawing's bare names come from Spell UI's catalog, so <space/> is <ui-space> once the catalog lists it, and <espacio/> in a Spanish file.
    • Code spell's drawing already says <ui-space> by its full name: nothing more to add.
    • A spellx test that <space/> compiles to <ui-space>.
  3. The notebook converter writes <br> as <space/>
    • Symptom: P26 wraps lines between <br>s in a <stack spacing="none">.
    • Changes: each <br> outside a paragraph becomes a <space/> where it was; the stack wrapping is removed (J190, reversing J183).
    • A paragraph's <br> stays a blank line in its text.
    • Its tests say so.
