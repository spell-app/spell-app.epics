Owen, 2026-10-24, on Q17 (M6): "Internally to the parser, we should probably keep Type and Method rather than Action so the base parser is not too" (specific to Spell); (M8): "template should not bleed back into the underlying parser".

  • Template, View, Page, App, Thing, Action and Phrase are the words in spell syntax, its docs, the editor app and Spell's runtime.
  • spell-parser's generic parser (the one the markdown engine and others are built on) keeps Type, TypeScope, Method: it parses any language, not just Spell.
  • How that squares with his Q17 pick B ("everything, in this epic"): everything a person reads or writes is renamed here; the base parser's internal names are not.
