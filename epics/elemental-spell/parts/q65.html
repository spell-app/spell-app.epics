Round 3 of the event names: a fold that keeps its header (it has an open attribute) has two sets of names from you.

  • opening / opened, closing / closed: <ui-accordion>, <ui-section> (round 1), <ui-docs-example>'s code pane (round 2)
  • showing / shown AND hiding / hidden: <epic-page-note-head>, <epic-thread> (round 2, with "folding is a programmer concept")

All five open and close, as their open attribute and the platform's <details open> say; show / hide is for things that appear or vanish whole (a tab's pane, a transition, the site's edit panel, a note box).

All five show and hide, accordion and section too; open / close stays for overlays (modals, flyouts, popups ...).
