1. In a terminal at the kit's root, ~/www/spell: 
    spell app serve
    and open the address it prints.
  2. In the editor, open project @spell/examples/Solitaire, then the file Card.spellx in its file list.
  3. Press Notebook on the code editor's toolbar, left of Save.
  4. Open the suit property's card (click its name); change an alias, then press Enter.
  • The card template is one card with a heavier border, its prose drawn as text, its members indented cards that fold.
  • The suit card shows a form: Name, One of, Aliases.
  • After Enter, pressing Text shows the changed alias in the file's text.
