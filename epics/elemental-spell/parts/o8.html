A notebook is a page written in spellx, not a view of a file: <spell-notebook "...">, a subclass of page, its prose markdown, its <example>s drawn live, each edit saved and redrawn by its own cell (P16).

  • The first notebook, P11's cards over any .spellx file with a form per property, was built and then removed, with its editor button (P20).
  • Every Spell UI component page is a project of notebooks, in @spell/notebooks (P24).
  • The converter that made them is retired: notebooks are edited by hand now (epic content's P1).
