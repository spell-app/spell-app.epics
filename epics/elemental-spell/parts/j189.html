• Owen's ask: grow, as a boolean or a number. So bare fluid is flex-grow: 1, fluid="2" grows twice as fast as a fluid beside it.
  • Elsewhere in Spell UI fluid means "takes the full width of its container" (button, menu, stack ...). A space's is the same idea along its owner's direction: it fills what's left. So it keeps the word.
  • Outside a flex box (plain page flow) it does nothing.
