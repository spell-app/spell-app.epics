Owen, 2026-10-24: "the clicked thing -- I think original target is sufficient for this and isn't click specific."

  • Inside a handler, target is the thing whose handler runs, and the original target is the thing the event first happened to. 
    - on mouse down:
	// target is the pile;  the original target is the card pressed
	if the original target is a card:
		turn the original target over
  • No the clicked thing: for a mouse event, the thing under the mouse IS the original target, and the input state can say what's under the mouse at any other time.
