Spellx has no tag that adds members to a template of another file (plain Spell's a card:). Writing them in Card.spellx made the card depend on the game, and Card, Deck and Pile stop being a library (the declarations tests import them without the game). So to play a card, the pile's drag and drop handlers and a card: - on click: play it are a chunk; the empty stock's click is an <on click> on the stock pile.
