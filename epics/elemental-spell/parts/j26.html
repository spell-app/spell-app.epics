• An <otherwise> after a </> that closed its <if>: reported, its fix deletes that </>, and the chain is read as one (Q53).
  • An unknown tag word: reported with the closest word and a fix, then read as a member with a body.
  • A template inside a template: ends the outer one there.
  • An <if> among members: read as markup; a handler in a drawing: read as a handler; a part defined in a drawing: read as an element.
  • A named closer for a tag further out closes every one inside it, each reported unclosed.
