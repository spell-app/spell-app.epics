One class, whatever language says it. TwoSidedView.es.spell is compiled for its declarations only; the generator pairs each of its statements with the English one (type, parts, property, Nth rule) and gives it the English compiled names:

  • dirección reads .direction (a property's output)
  • frente draws drawFront() (new P.TypeScope.partOutputs)
  • está boca arriba is isFaceDirection("up") (new valueOutputs on the alias rule)
  • voltear la carta is .turnOver()

A twin statement with no English partner fails the generator. The alternative, a Spanish class of its own, would have meant two classes for one template.
