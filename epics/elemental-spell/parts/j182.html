headline is titular, subhead subtítulo, all-caps mayúsculas, title-caps iniciales (for "initial capitals"). As values of letra= they keep their accents; as bare attributes on <es-texto> they're ASCII (subtitulo, mayusculas), as every Spanish attribute name is. subhead is a new theme token, --ui-font-family-subhead: the headings' face in most themes, the body's sans in Spell's and Matrix's, which draw a serif or display face for headings.
