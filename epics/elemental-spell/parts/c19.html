A root's constants are every program's: mapping the template's arriba to 'up' at the root would change a Spanish program's OWN arriba (it did: a rule test caught it). So the Spanish values stay unmapped as constants; voltearla boca arriba, voltearla and está boca arriba work, poner su dirección a arriba sets "arriba". Fix later with values that belong to their property rather than the root.
