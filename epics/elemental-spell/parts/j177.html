font="14" writes --ui-font-size: 14px on the element itself (its style), and font="10/20" also writes --ui-line-height as 2 (20 / 10). Every Spell UI component works out its sizes from --ui-font-size, so a button with font="14" is a 14px button, padding and all, not just 14px letters in a 16px box. On <text> it is simply the letters' size and line height. It can't be bare: <text font="14/20">.

The other way: setting only font-size on the element would leave most components' boxes at the page's size.
