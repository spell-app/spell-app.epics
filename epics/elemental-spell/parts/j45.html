For J30 (P3's reader retired, its model kept as a view). SX.SpellxView.read(text) walks the tokens spell's parser reads a .spellx file as: a match holds its TWIN's code-spell tokens, while a tag's own head, closers and prose are tokens of their own (SpellxTagToken ...), with every name, attribute and span the tree needs.

  • No rules run, nothing compiles: cheap enough for an editor to rebuild per keystroke.
  • Its diagnostics are what the tokenizer noted: spellx's own mistakes, as messages with spans. P3's problem codes and quick-fix edits are gone (nothing read them; P8's quick fixes can come back to them).
  • The five o1 fixtures read to EXACTLY P3's outline snapshots, unchanged.
