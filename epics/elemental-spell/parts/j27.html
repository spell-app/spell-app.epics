• A template's and a declaration's: checked against the language's attribute words, an unknown one an error naming the closest.
  • An event's: never -- they're its parameters (<event "flipped" side="direction" />).
  • A component's in a drawing (part=, color=): never -- which components exist, and what they take, is P6's (the packs).
  • In a drawing, a declaration word other than <draw> (<menu>, <action>) is a component, as Q50's context rule says.
