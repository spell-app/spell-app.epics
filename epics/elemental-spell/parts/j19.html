For Q62 (<solitaire-app>). The project's .css files are installed where the app mounts (inside <spell-viewer>'s shadow root); drawn inside <solitaire-app>'s own shadow root, the board (.Board, [layout] ...) would lose them.

  • So App.drawsInItsChildren: its element's shadow root is just a <slot>, and core.drawThing() hands the app's drawing over as the element's children, as a viewable list's items are.
  • Every example draws exactly as before, one element deeper.
