Owen's model has App as a subclass of View. Making App extend View in P1 isn't safe:

  • every view class defines its own element as it's declared (static elementTag = core.defineElement(...)), so every app would get a tag like <solitaire-solitaire>
  • an app draws through App.start() and core.mountApp(), or a layout's markup, not as an element; the runner and layouts don't expect one

Revisit with P2 (<spell-viewer>), where the App template's tag is decided.
