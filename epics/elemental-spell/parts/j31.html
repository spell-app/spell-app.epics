P5, the code-spell twin of <action shortcut="meta+T" enabled-when="..."> (Q33, Q38).

  • - to turn it over: then - shortcut meta+T (once per key), - enabled when it is face down, - selected when it is face up, - undone as "Turn Card", - icon "rotate", before its steps.
  • Spanish: atajo, activa cuando, seleccionada cuando, se deshace como, icono.
  • Not chosen: - to turn it over, on meta+T: on the heading line (Q38's note) -- one line per key reads better, and repeats as spellx's shortcut= does; keys, checked when, only when.
  • Compiles to a getter per condition (turnOverIsEnabled) and core.describeAction(Card, "turnOver", {...}) after the class.
