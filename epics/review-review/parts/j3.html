Below 400px wide, the contents rail shrinks to just its bars button, floating at the top right of the page header; above it, the 36px strip of section icons shows.

  • Chosen: 400px. A phone (390px) and VS Code's side bar at its usual width (about 250-380px) get the full page width with only the bars; a side bar dragged wider than 400px gets the strip back, with hover-to-widen labels.
  • Option: 480px. The bars-only look up to a wider side bar; you'd almost never see the strip in the side bar.
  • Option: 360px. The strip stays in most side bars, costing 36px of a 300px column (12%).
  • One number in spell-doc.css (the @media (max-width: 400px) block under "Rail"), so it's a one-line change.
