Picking an option card marks the whole item (one mark per item), so its ellipsis button changes too: an orange circle showing the letter, instead of a fifth color.

  • Orange matches the picked card's frame (the contract's "picked, not yet sent" orange).
  • Revisit is orange too: the letter, and the tooltip ("Picked B" vs "Revisit soon"), tell them apart.
  • Example: choose B on Q7, and Q7's line ends in an orange B instead of the grey ellipsis.
  • picks read at a glance on the item line, even with the card folded
  • if you'd rather picks had their own color (teal), it's one CSS line
