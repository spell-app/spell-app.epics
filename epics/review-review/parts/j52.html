Before this change an answered question had no Choose pills at all: you could Revisit it with a note, but not pick another option on the page. Now its Choices panels get Choose pills while you revisit it.

  • When: its Revisit box is open, or it carries a revisit or pick mark. Never on the option already chosen.
  • Picking B with a note ("B after all?") is a revisit carrying the pick: talked over in the review, never applied by itself (as on open questions).
  • A plain pick (no note), once sent and applied, answers the question again with B: the old answer card moves into its Original Discussion.
  • Checked in a browser against a fake inbox, 350px light and 900px dark: Revisit, Choose B, save, Clear.
  • You can change your mind on an answered question from the page; the picked title turns orange.
