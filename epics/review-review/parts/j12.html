When an item could be two colors at once, the page shows the more urgent one. You'd notice it after a /bedtime run: a judgement call made overnight is red, not green.

  • The order, strongest first:
    1. closed (struck or answered): green if recent, else grey
    2. work under way (data-queued, data-working): orange
    3. waiting on you (an open question; an open judgement call or issue you haven't reviewed): red
    4. reviewed recently, or touched by a /bedtime run: green
    5. everything else open: blue
  • Chose this over "bedtime changes are always green" (D2's wording) because red is the one color that asks you to act: hiding a new overnight judgement call in green would let it slip by.
  • A just-added todo or caveat stays blue (not green): green means decided, reviewed or closed recently, and adding isn't any of those.
  • A reviewed caveat or todo goes green, then back to blue (not grey) once the review is no longer recent: it's still open.
  • Closed wins over queued: an issue closed while still on the to-do list is green or grey, not orange.

Example: overnight, Claude adds J12 (a call) and T9 (a todo). In the morning J12 is red and T9 green; after /epic review marks J12 reviewed, it turns green.
