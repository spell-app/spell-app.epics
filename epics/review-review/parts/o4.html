Each item's id badge (Q1, J4 ...) gets a color for where it stands:

  • Red: open and needs you, e.g. a judgement call nobody has reviewed.
  • Blue: open, but not urgent, e.g. a todo for later.
  • Orange: in progress: Claude is working on it, or it's waiting in your batch.
  • Green: changed in this or the last commit, or during a /bedtime run (D2).
  • Grey: decided or reviewed a while ago.
  • Each section's title gets a round filter button that steps through these (all, then orange, red ...), colored like the status it shows.
  • Today orange means "not reviewed" and blue means "queued" in a few places (the review picker, open question chips); those follow the new meanings.
  • Each phase ends with a To review line, e.g. To review: I3, T4, J1, J2, linking the items found during that phase that you haven't reviewed, in page order. It replaces the hand-written "Judgement calls" line.
