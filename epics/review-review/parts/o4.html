Each item's id badge (Q1, J4 ...) has a color for where it stands:

  • Red: open and needs you, e.g. a judgement call nobody has reviewed.
  • Blue: open, but not urgent, e.g. a todo for later.
  • Orange: in progress: Claude is working on it, or it's waiting in your batch.
  • Green: changed in this or the last commit, or during a /bedtime run (D2).
  • Grey: decided or reviewed a while ago.
  • Red beats green: an item waiting on you stays red, even when it just changed.
  • Done items aren't struck through: the color says it. Only canceled items, made moot by another decision, are.
  • Each section's title has one round filter button, instead of "Open | All": it steps through "all", then each color the section has, colored like the status it shows, and remembers your choice per page.
  • Each phase ends with a To review line, e.g. To review: I3, T4, J1, J2, linking the items found during that phase that you haven't reviewed, in page order. Old pages keep their hand-written "Judgement calls" lines beside it (J14).
