• Where the terse style comes from: the plan-doc rules and the /epic skill both say "caveman lite" (drop articles, fragments OK). The details-page rules already say the opposite ("Not caveman"). The plan-doc rules switch to the details-page rules.
  • Every phase: a bulleted Goal, then, once done, a bulleted Done (what was built, with the things you tend to ask about first: where to see it, what changed in how you work, what's still rough), then Commits.
  • Commits: short hash (a link to GitHub) and one or two sentences on what it changed for this phase or item. Phase commits can be filled in for past epics from git history; for items it's a new habit (Fix I3: in the commit message). A "Git" button in the page header shows or hides them.
  • Files and Verify: hidden until you click the folder or flask icon in a phase's header.
  • Every item: a lead sentence in plain words, then bullets; a tricky one gets a concrete example (real code, a table, before / after).
  • Old docs: their open items are rewritten in P4, the rest in P8 (D4).
