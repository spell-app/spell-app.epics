• The writing rules are written for you coming back cold.
    • The old "caveman lite" style (drop articles, fragments OK) is gone from the rules and the /epic skill.
    • Every item: a plain lead sentence, then bullets, ids explained, an example for anything tricky.
    • Every question, issue, judgement call and decision ends with a Net effect list.
  • Every phase: a bulleted Goal, then, once done, a bulleted Done and its Commits.
    • Done says what was built, with the things you tend to ask about first: where to see it, what changed in how you work, what's still rough.
  • Commits: a short hash (a link to GitHub), and one or two sentences on what it changed for this phase or item.
    • Past epics' phase commits are filled in from git history.
    • A commit naming an item (Fix I3:) shows on that item.
    • A Git button in the page header shows or hides them.
  • Files and Verify are hidden until you click the folder or flask button on the Phases title, which remembers your choice per page.
  • Rewriting an item never loses its earlier text: it moves into a folded Original Discussion.
  • Answered questions read in order: the question, a folded Choices list, then the answer card.
  • Old pages: their open items are rewritten in the new style (P4); their closed and decided items still read the old way, until P8 (D4).
