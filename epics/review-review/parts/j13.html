Each phase's "To review" line (last in its body) links the items that phase raised that you still need to look at. The plan said "red or blue, and not reviewed"; the script lists a bit more.

  • Listed: items added while the phase was active (data-phase), still open, not reviewed (no data-reviewed), and not on the to-do list or being worked on.
  • The difference: a todo or caveat a /bedtime run added is GREEN (D2), yet you haven't reviewed it. The narrower rule would leave it off the line; this one keeps it.
  • Deferred items stay listed: deferring isn't reviewing.
  • Page order: Questions first, then Judgement calls, Caveats, Todos, Issues, To test.

Example: To review: Q4, J12, C5. Review J12 and it drops off; review all three and the line goes.

  • updateToReview() filters on open + not reviewed + not under way, not on the color
  • overnight todos and caveats show up in the morning's To review line
