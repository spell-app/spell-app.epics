Everything that reads an item now looks only at its current text. The old text is history: you can read it, but nothing acts on it.

  • plan-doc: optionCards(), chooseOption(), inferOption(), the recommendation, links from decisions (review state) and check()'s links all skip it.
  • items --json: details and detailsHtml are the current text; a new original field holds the old, as text (or null). The review picker page shows the current text only.
  • Page: no "Choose" pill on old cards; the contents sidebar leaves out headings inside it.
  • Kept: a click on an old card's label still unfolds it, and an old chosen card keeps its green frame. That is what was chosen then.

Net effect

  • Approving a question whose cards were rewritten away says "needs talk" instead of picking a card from history.
