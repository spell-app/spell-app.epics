Everything that reads an item now looks only at its current text. The old text is history: you can read it, but nothing acts on it.

  • The plan-doc tool skips it when it:
    • finds option cards, chooses one, or guesses one (optionCards(), chooseOption(), inferOption())
    • reads the recommendation
    • follows links from decisions, for the review state
    • checks links (check())
  • items --json:
    • details and detailsHtml are the current text
    • a new original field holds the old, as text (or null)
    • the review picker page shows the current text only
  • The page: no "Choose" pill on old cards, and the contents sidebar leaves out headings inside the old text.
  • Kept: a click on an old card's label still unfolds it, and an old chosen card keeps its green frame. That is what was chosen then.
  • Approving a question whose cards were rewritten away says "needs talk", instead of picking a card from history.
