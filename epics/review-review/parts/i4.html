The page tells you whether a Claude session is listening from the inbox's listening field. A session that stops without running inbox unlisten (closed tab, crash, context reset) leaves it set.

  • Example: you close the review session's tab; later you click "revisit now" and the page doesn't warn you, but nothing will answer.
  • The /epic review skill unlistens when it finishes, but can't when it's killed.
  • Fix: inbox wait stamps a heartbeat (e.g. listening.seen) every minute while it waits; the page treats a heartbeat older than two minutes as nobody listening.

Net effect (once fixed):

  • the "nobody is reviewing" warning is right even after a crash
