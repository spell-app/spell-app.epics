  • Chosen from the decision title: a letter first (B: real URLs), option B, or every word of one option title (Merge at the end picks B. Merge at the end).

  • Per doc (chosen / not): commands 2/1, epic-review 1/4, review-review 1/0, solid-migration 4/0, ui-component-creation 5/4, ui-docs-rework 4/0.
