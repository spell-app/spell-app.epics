When you reload a plan doc (or VS Code restarts), the page goes back to the section you were reading: the address holds it, e.g. #p3. Today it scrolls there but leaves the section folded, as you left it (J9).

  • Example: you were reading P3's Goal, folded P3, scrolled on to Questions, and reloaded. The address says #decisions, so you land on Questions; P3 stays folded.
  • The catch: the address follows whatever is at the top of the screen, so after a fold it may name a section you only scrolled past.
  • no change: a reload keeps your folds
  • Your folds stay exactly as you left them.
  • If the remembered section is folded, you see its title at the top.
  • You always see the content you were reading.
  • But a reload can open a section you had folded on purpose.
