After installing the extension (yarn vscode) and reloading the window:

  • The right (secondary) side bar shows two tabs: "Spell Docs" with the spell hat, and "Review" with a circle-check outline. Both icons should be crisp and the same colour as VS Code's other side bar icons.
  • yarn docs:open --vs shows the docs index in Spell Docs. yarn docs:open epics/review-review/review-review.plan.html --review shows the plan doc in Review.
  • Switching between the two tabs keeps each page as it was, scroll position included; neither reloads.
  • Opening the Review tab by hand in a fresh window, before anything was sent to it, shows a line saying how to fill it.
  • Each tab's title-bar buttons act on its own page: back / forward, reload, restart the page server, open in the browser.
