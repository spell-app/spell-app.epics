Each item in the review inbox holds a single mark, and the latest wins. So on a question you can either choose an option card or write a revisit note, not both.

  • Example: on Q7 you click "Choose" on B, then open Revisit and write "B, but only for plan docs?". The revisit replaces the pick: Claude sees the note, not that you chose B.
  • Where: packages/epics/tools/inbox.js (marks: { [id]: mark }) and the page's menu, which shows one color per item.
  • Fix options: let a pick and a note live together on one item (a mark with both pick and note), or treat a revisit on a picked question as "pick, with a remark".

Net effect (once fixed):

  • a question can carry your pick and your question about it in one send
