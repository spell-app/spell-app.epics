T3 took away the search across checkouts, which is where list's checkout came from. I kept the field, and its meaning: where the epic runs. /epics reads it.

  • Before: the first checkout holding the epic, its own worktree first, then main. With shared content, that was already "its worktree if it has one, else main".
  • Now: epicCheckout() asks only whether .claude/worktrees/<name> exists.
  • Over: dropping the column. /epics and /epic's cheat sheet name it.

Example: spell dev plan-doc list still prints review-review 45 / 88 (.claude/worktrees/review-review).

Net effect

  • list and list --json print what they printed before.
  • checkouts(), epicFile(), hereDoc(), epicsDirOf() and realOrSelf() are gone.
