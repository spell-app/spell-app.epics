The new original command puts text recovered from git into an item's Original Discussion. It does not stamp the item or flag it UPDATE.

  • Why: restoring many items at once would turn every one green ("recent") and orange-flagged, though nothing about them changed.
  • It still logs one line per item: I7 original discussion restored (as of 2026-10-04 20:49).
  • It drops a commits block from the given HTML (the item keeps its own) and an answer card that says what the item's says.

Usage: spell dev plan-doc original <name> <id> --file old.html [--as-of "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM"], printing added, unchanged or empty.

  • The restore after the inventory can run through the tool, item by item, in date order.
