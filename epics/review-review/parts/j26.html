In a collapsing group, an OPEN section sits flush under the title above it and keeps its usual space (1.5em, --ui-section-margin) only BELOW its content.

  • Folded next to folded: no space at all, so a run of folded titles reads as one accordion.
  • Open after folded: no space above its title, so a title never jumps down under the pointer when it is clicked open; only what is below it moves.
  • After an open one (folded or open): the usual 1.5em, so its content ends with breathing room.
  • A section that can't fold (collapsible="false") keeps its normal space on both sides.
  • Boxes (bordered, styled) overlap by the border width, so two stacked boxes draw one line, not two. Each box keeps its own rounded corners and shadow: stacked styled sections look like touching cards, not one accordion box with square inner corners.
  • The other way: space on BOTH sides of an open section (symmetric, but the clicked title moves down 1.5em); or no space at all, open or folded (a pure accordion). 
<ui-sections collapsing>
  <ui-section header="Overview" collapsed>...</ui-section>   <!-- folded:  touching -->
  <ui-section header="Install" collapsed>...</ui-section>
  <ui-section header="Configure">...</ui-section>            <!-- open:  flush above, 1.5em below -->
  <ui-section header="Deploy" collapsed>...</ui-section>
</ui-sections>
  • Opening a section never moves its own title.
  • An open section's content is separated from the next title, not from its own.
  • Stacked styled sections are touching cards with one shared edge.
