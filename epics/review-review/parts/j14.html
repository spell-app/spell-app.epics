migrate removes the hand-written "Judgement calls: J4 J6" lines that end some phases in older docs. The new "To review" line replaces them.

  • It removes the line with any icon, not just the compass.
    • The commands and details docs have the same line with a gavel, linking T items (judgement calls were todos there).
  • The items the line linked get that phase (data-phase), unless they already have one.
    • Old items have none, so without this they'd vanish from every To review line.
  • Tried in memory, nothing written, and each check clean:
    • spell-ui-pages: 4 phases get lines, J1-J8
    • commands: 6 lines removed
    • details: 2 removed

An example, spell-ui-pages's P2:

  • before: Judgement calls: J4 J6
  • after: To review: J4, J6, while they're open and not reviewed; then nothing
  • P4's migrate run removes these lines from every doc.
  • An accepted (closed) or reviewed call no longer shows under its phase.
