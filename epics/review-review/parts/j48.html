The old top-level commands are kept as aliases: bin/spell.mjs routes them to the same lean entry as spell dev, so they're as fast.

  • spell --help lists them as deprecated: spell dev plan-doc / spell dev goals.
  • No warning on stderr when used: skills and hooks still calling them (until the callers switch) would get noise in their output.
  • Nothing breaks while callers move over; drop the aliases once nothing calls them.
