What a review looks like now, step by step:

  1. You type /epic review wwod (D3).
  2. The wwod epic page opens in the Review tab of VS Code's right side bar, at the first thing that needs you, and the session waits on the page in the background.
  3. You read down the page. Every item's line ends in a round ellipsis button, its action menu:
    • Approve: fine as it is. The button turns green.
    • Add to todo: follow it up later, as a todo (violet).
    • Add Details: a background agent writes a fuller explanation into the item, which spins until it's done. You keep reading.
    • Revisit: a note box opens under the item, with a grey check (revisit soon, talked over with the next send) over a blue send (revisit now: an agent answers at once, in a reply card on the item).
    • Clear: takes the mark off.
  4. An open question's option cards (A / B / C) each have a Choose pill: the pick frames the card orange, and its letter shows on the item's button.
  5. While marks wait, the page header's round paper plane is blue. Clicking it hands them to the session: approvals and picks go straight into the page, todos are filed, and the "revisit soon" notes are talked over in the chat, one at a time.

Why this shape: a review used to be a string of modals in the chat, each showing one item without the doc around it. On the page you have the whole context, and you decide at your own pace.
