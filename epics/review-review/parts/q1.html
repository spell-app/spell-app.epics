  • Example: you approve J2, add T4 to the todos, and write "why not reuse the details route?" on I3. Nothing has gone to Claude yet.
  • Those three marks must survive a reload of the page (a background agent writing into the doc reloads it).
  • epics/wwod/wwod.inbox.json holds the marks and notes.
  • The page draws them over the doc (green menu button, your note in its box).
  • Claude applies them to the plan doc itself when you send, through yarn plan-doc, so the doc's history stays Claude's edits.
