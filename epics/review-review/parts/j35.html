yarn plan-doc inbox review-review apply edits the plan doc from the marks Owen sent, then clears them. Three edges, and what it does:

  • Marks not sent yet are left alone, even when named (apply j9 prints "no sent mark"): Owen may still change them, and the send button is how he says he is done.
  • If Owen changes a mark on the page while apply runs, his newer mark stays in the inbox for the next round. apply clears a mark only while it is still the exact one it applied (same time stamp).
  • A mark on an item the doc no longer has is dropped, with "no such item".
  • It tries the marks on a copy first, and writes the doc only when something applies: a batch of revisits alone leaves the doc untouched.

Net effect:

  • no edit Owen did not send; no mark of his lost to a race
  • revisits and unanswerable approvals stay in the inbox, printed under "left:"
