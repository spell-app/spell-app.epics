Whether a listening session is gone is decided in one place, inbox.js: the routes answer listening: null once its heartbeat is over 90 seconds old, so the page needs no clock rule of its own.

  • The two times are named once each: LISTEN_HEARTBEAT_MS (30s) and LISTEN_STALE_MS (90s), in scripts/inbox.js; the stale listening stays in the file until the next listen or unlisten.
  • Beyond the ask: besides wait (every 30s) and listen, the session's inbox apply, done, clear and working also stamp the heartbeat, so a session busy between waits still counts.
  • Still a gap: a session talking a revisit over with you for more than 90s, running none of those, shows "nobody is reviewing" until it runs wait again. Marks you make then do wait for that wait, so the warning is roughly true.
  • Example: you close the review tab mid-wait. 90s later the send button's tooltip says "No Claude session is reviewing this doc", "revisit now" shows the notice, and yarn plan-doc inbox review-review prints "listening: nobody (session ... last seen ..., over 90s ago: gone without unlisten)".

Net effect:

  • after a crash the warning comes back within 90 seconds, with no page change needed
  • a long chat with no inbox command can show the warning while the session is still there
