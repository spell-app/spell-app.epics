For issue I9 (answered questions read upside down), the chosen option in an answered question's new Choices accordion is marked by a small green check in front of its title and the title in green text. The heavy green frame around the chosen card is gone.

  • Chose this over a "chosen" tag at the right of the title, or a tinted row: the check reads at a glance, even in VS Code's 350px side bar, and leaves the right of the row free for the Choose pill when you revisit.
  • The Choices accordion opens on the chosen panel when you unfold it (open="2"), the others folded; several panels can be open at once (exclusive="no") so you can compare.
  • Example, Q16: unfold Choices and you see A · Push main first (recommended), B · Branch only, then ✓ C · No PR while main is ahead open, in green.

Net effect

  • No green frames; the chosen option is a check and green text inside a folded Choices section.
