When Owen approves an item on the page and sends, yarn plan-doc inbox review-review apply turns the approval into a doc edit. What it does depends on the kind of item:

  • An open question: answered with the option card marked "(recommended)", that card chosen, and the question marked reviewed. With no recommended card, nothing is guessed: the mark stays and apply prints "needs talk", for Claude to ask in the chat.
  • An open judgement call: closed (accepted) and reviewed.
  • An open test: closed (it passed) and reviewed.
  • Anything else (an open caveat, issue or todo, or an item already closed or answered): only marked reviewed. Approving a caveat accepts the risk; it does not make it go away.
  • Every applied mark also writes one log line, e.g. J9 approved: closed (accepted).

Chosen over closing every approved item: an approved issue or todo still needs the work done, so it stays open.

Net effect:

  • approving Q7 (recommended A) answers it "Land without unfolding", card A chosen
  • approving J9 closes it; approving C1 only marks it reviewed
  • a question with no recommended option waits for a talk
