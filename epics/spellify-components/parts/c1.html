A drawable type may pick its own element tag, instead of the project-name one (from epic drawable-thing, T1; Owen, 2026-10-11: "do it, fold in to another epic").

  • For example: a card is a drawable thing, drawn as <my-card>.
  • Unnamed, the tag stays the singular project or library name, then the type: <game-card>.
  • Needs a little more grammar, and a check that the name has a dash and isn't taken already.
