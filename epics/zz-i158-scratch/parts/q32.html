The icons-only stage needs every item to have an icon. This decides what a row does when one item has only a title.

  • The titles cut down to the minimum, then the row goes to its overflow button, with no icons-only stage.
  • A development warning names the item with no icon, so the page can give it one.
  • Cost: none; the overflow stage exists anyway.
  • As the docs' letter marks do: "Questions" shows "Q".
  • Cost: a drawn letter mark in <ui-item>, and two items starting with the same letter look alike.
  • a row holding an item with no icon never shows icons only; it goes from cut titles to the overflow button
