The countdown starts again whenever you work on the page, as P20's did.

  • Working on the page: a key, a click or tap, the mouse wheel, a scroll, or typing into a box.
  • NOT a mouse simply moving over the page: you can be reading with the pointer still, or have left it there.
  • The page hidden (another tab, VS Code's side bar closed, the window behind another) counts as not working on it: you've gone to type in the chat, which is when you want your marks sent.
  • The 2 minutes are measured on the clock, not by counting ticks. A browser slows a hidden page's timers, so a hidden page sends on its next tick, or the moment you come back, never late by minutes.
  • the activity list stays P20's (ACTIVITY_EVENTS)
  • no pause while the page is hidden
