The state filter's chips and the "3 hidden · show all" line are now ONE pair of elements in the epics pack, family epic-state-filter: <epic-state-filter counts="attention:2 open:5" shown="open"> and <epic-hidden-note count="3">. An item section, an epic page's toolbar and the Epics page (the docs runtime's wireItemFilters()) all draw them, where each had its own copy before.

  • They filter nothing themselves: a chip's click says what was picked and what should show next, by the one rule (StateFilter.nextShown()); the owner filters and sets shown.
  • On the Epics page the chips now come in the epic pages' order (blue, red, orange, yellow, green, grey) and say the epic pages' words ("1 needs attention: show only these" for "1 needs you"). Their colours stay the page's (--spell-state-*).
  • Not Spell UI's <ui-button color basic>: a 22x18 pill in the item states' colours, solid or outlined, would be the component restyled back through ::part(), the "fighting the component" of J16.
  • An epic page's chips now live one shadow root deeper: the section and page tests and check-header-epics.mjs reach them through the element.
