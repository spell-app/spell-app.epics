• Drive-by: a status card taken (its at) before Owen's FIRST reply in the thread. Its line goes first in the item's details, above the Choices, with 'your earlier comment'. An item with no reply of Owen's has no drive-bys.
  • One line per task: a card continues the one before it when it was taken the minute that one finished (at equals its done-at). The tool writes a Noted then its Done that way. The line shows each step's state and time, then the last card's summary, else its reading. The earlier cards' readings are hidden.
  • The thread is the run of replies that ENDS the item's details. A reply before another card (Owen's note above More Details) is a card of its own.
