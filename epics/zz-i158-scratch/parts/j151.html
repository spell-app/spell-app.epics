For P52 · Commit Log: a migration step, log-commits, gives every epic page, open or merged, its change entries at once.

  • It reads each epic's history across the repos, as the backfill does, and adds an entry per commit not already in its Log.
  • Run again, it adds nothing: an entry is found by its sha.
  • The review lines stay where they are; nothing is deleted.
  • An old epic whose subjects named no epic (P3: Name only) gets entries only where the phase name matches, as its Commits lists did.
  • epic migrate --all --step log-commits once, after the merge
