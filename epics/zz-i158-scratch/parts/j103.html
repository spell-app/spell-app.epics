The inbox's listening gets a pid: the monitor's poller. The listener is live exactly while that process runs.

  • So the monitor writes the inbox only when it takes the place, not every 30 seconds.
  • A monitor that dies frees the place at once, and a Send wakes the session the old way.
  • A wait's heartbeat keeps the pid; /epic review's inbox watch replaces it, and the monitor stands back until that session's heartbeat goes stale (about 90 seconds after its last wait), then takes the place back.
