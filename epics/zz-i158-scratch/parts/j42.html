Passed, Skip and Not now (or P / S / N) mark the test and move on a third of a second later, so the press shows first. Failed (F) stays on the card and opens the note box and the screenshot's area. Pressing an answer that's already chosen keeps it (no clearing, unlike the review buttons): to change it, press another; a failure's note and screenshot go when another answer replaces it. The alternative, a press clearing the answer, would drop a typed note by accident.
