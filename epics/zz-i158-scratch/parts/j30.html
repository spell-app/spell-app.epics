Every page's section toolbar, a guide's and an epic page's alike, is now Spell UI's menu: <ui-menu class="spell-toolbar" secondary>, one <ui-item> link per section, its badges <ui-label>s. One builder draws both (toolbarMenu() in spell-doc-runtime.js).

  • Why the menu and not a new <epic-page-toolbar>: it fits exactly (a <nav> of links, the current one selected and announced as the current page, an icon box, a slot for the title and badges, tokens for every colour and size). Spell UI is in the page's bundle, so the toolbar draws at once; an epics-pack element would pop in after the first paint (the pack loads after it) and hide its buttons in a shadow root, where scroll-follow, check-spell.js and a details page's <epic-ask> look for them (data-rail).
  • The comments' toolbar bullhorn is an item of the same menu (<ui-item link>), its orange count a <ui-label>; the spell syntax guide's Expand all and Collapse all are appended to it, and kept when the toolbar is built again.
  • Icons are centred by the menu: measured 0px off on every page, at 1200 and 390px, light and dark; the old <ui-icon> cells put every glyph 1.8px left of centre.
  • Two small looks moved with it: an epic page's badges now overlap a third of a badge on the CENTRED icon, so they sit 3px further left than before (closer to Owen's 2026-10-10 "1/3 way overlap"); a guide's icon and title are 10px apart, its icons-only buttons all 34px wide, as before.
  • <epic-page>'s own tools (its state filter, expand and collapse all) stay its own, at the row's right.
