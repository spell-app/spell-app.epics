The outlines are reviewed HERE, on this epic page: one Overview sub-section per repo, each listing its READMEs with their sections, pictures, expands and stubs. Not as skeleton files in the repos.

  • The Review tab is where comments, bullhorns and Do Now already work; a dozen skeleton Markdown files would have to be read one by one in the editor.
  • An Opus writer later reads its repo's sub-section as its brief, plus the writers' brief (1.2) and the research reports in this epic's research/ folder.
  • Once an outline is approved, the writing phase for that repo can start without waiting for the others.
  • P1 · Map and Outlines ends when every repo's sub-section is written and this page is in the Review tab.
  • The writing phases P2-P7 each take one sub-section as their brief.
