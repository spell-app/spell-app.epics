  • Every picture is the real editor's own drawing of Solitaire.spell, copied from the running editor with its stylesheet. Only the feet are moved.
  • A foot is the short bar that turns out of the bottom of a block's rail, round a corner: it says "this block ends here".
  • Three pictures of the same lines, side by side:
    • Today: each foot has a strip of its own under the block's last line, 5px. Feet that end on one line stack, each a tab lower than the one inside it.
    • A: every foot in the top of the blank line, each at its own indent, overlapping.
    • B: the feet stepped down inside the blank line, innermost highest, about 8px apart.
  • Where there's no blank line under a block, A and B both give its feet one full line, 25px (the recommended answer to question 3). Today's strip there is 5px.
  • The height over each picture is the whole stretch: A and B are always a whole number of lines.
