Found by I7's page tests (sideBarEdits), not the CodeMirror swap: the side bar can't change a Solitaire foundation's suit any more.

  • Solitaire's foundations were made by lines like a new foundation with name = "clubs", suit = clubs, and the side bar wrote a change of suit into that line.
  • Since language's commit 417e06bec, a member made new takes its name as its id, drawn by <foundation "clubs" suit=clubs/>.
    • So the tag in the app's drawing is what makes each one, and its suit=clubs sets its suit.
  • The side bar doesn't see that: a change of suit says The app sets this as it runs, so this change won't be saved, and nothing is written.

Tests it fails, in sideBarEdits:

  • the two suit edits, with and without the code editor
  • 'this one: typed in a real field' (its name and suit on the tag)
  • 'a change to several is one undo step', and the foundations' selection tests that follow, which still look for a new foundation with name = ...

The fix: the side bar writes a tag-made thing's value into its tag's attribute; the tests then read the tag's text.
