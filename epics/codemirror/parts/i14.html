Found by I7's page tests (sideBarEdits), not the CodeMirror swap. Solitaire's foundations were made by lines like a new foundation with name = "clubs", suit = clubs, and the side bar wrote a change of suit into that line. Since language's commit 417e06bec (a member made new takes its name as its id, drawn by <foundation "clubs" suit=clubs/>), the tag in the app's drawing is what makes each one, and its suit=clubs is where its suit is set. The side bar doesn't see that: a change of suit says The app sets this as it runs, so this change won't be saved, and nothing is written.

Tests it fails: sideBarEdits' two suit edits (with and without the code editor), 'this one: typed in a real field' (its name and suit on the tag), 'a change to several is one undo step' and the foundations' selection tests that follow, which still look for a new foundation with name = .... The fix is the side bar writing a tag-made thing's value into its tag's attribute; the tests then read the tag's text.
