Built: a peek box under the code, as Monaco's peek view was.

  • Peek Definition (⌥F12) and Peek References (⇧⌥F12), in the code's right-click menu under Go to Definition and Find All References, each with its key.
  • A box opens under the caret's line, showing the place's file, read only, scrolled to it, the place marked. It's a slice of that file (the same one the Inspector's Code card shows), so it's drawn bubbly when the look is on.
  • Peek References lists every use on the right: a click shows it, ↑ ↓ move through them.
  • Enter, or a double-click on the code or a use, goes there. Esc or ✕ closes it, with the caret back in the code.
  • <spell-editor> has it too: the box needs nothing of the app.
  • Its words are in English and Spanish ("Ver la definición", "Ver los usos").
  • Checked headlessly on Solitaire: ⌥F12 on pile opens Pile.spell; Esc closes it; Enter goes there.
