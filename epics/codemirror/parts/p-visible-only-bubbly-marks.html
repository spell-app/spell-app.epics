Every relayout rebuilds the bubbly look's marks for the WHOLE file, though CodeMirror only draws what's on screen.

  • A relayout costs about a screenful, not the whole file: measured on Solitaire.spell (207 lines) and a big one, SpellUI's Table examples (2,667 lines), before and after.
  • Nothing looks or behaves differently: bands, pills, rails, feet, descriptions, folding, find, peek, clicks and typing on a blank line all as now.
  • Lines scrolled into view arrive drawn, never plain for a moment.
  • No new scroll jumps: the line heights CodeMirror estimates stay what they are now (I21).
  • From Owen's question, "does codemirror have the concept of only rendering what's visible on the page?": it does, and the bubbly look now uses it.

The height-changing parts (feet, rows of feet, tags joined over lines) stay worked out for the whole file, in the editor's state.

The colouring and pill marks move to a view plugin that builds them only for the lines CodeMirror draws, again as the view scrolls.
