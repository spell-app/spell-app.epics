⌘F now opens the find bar with its Replace row showing (I8 C1). Folding it with the chevron is remembered in the editor's preferences, the same place as its mode and panel sizes: spellEditor:findShowsReplace in the browser's storage.

  • So it stays folded in every editor, in other files, and after a reload, until the chevron or ⌥⌘F shows it again.
  • ⌥⌘F unfolds it for good too, as the chevron would.
  • <spell-editor> on another site keeps its own, in that site's storage.
