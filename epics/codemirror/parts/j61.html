Every popup that shows by itself while typing now waits for the typing to pause HOVER_DELAY, 0.8 s, the same constant as the hover (the page's --ui-hover-delay); each key starts the wait again.

  • Suggestion list: CodeMirror's activateOnTypingDelay, 100 ms (its default) => 800 ms.
  • The list after a space after a tag (<action ): at once => 800 ms, dropped if a key comes first.
  • Signature help after a space (off by default): at once => 800 ms after the last key.
  • Unchanged:
    • the words strip under an attribute's value, 1 s (T70)
    • hovers, 800 ms
    • Ctrl+Space, Shift+Cmd+Space and Alt+Down, at once
    • the list again after choosing a tag or attribute (a choice, not typing), at once
