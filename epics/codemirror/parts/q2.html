The app has two undo histories today: the editor's own (editor.history, moves in the Inspector, the side bar, the running app) and Monaco's, for what was typed in the code. Epic editor I167 just settled their order: ⌘Z in the running app undoes the program's move first, then the editor's, then what was typed in the code.

  • CodeMirror's own history (@codemirror/commands) replaces Monaco's, one per file; undoInEdit() asks it as it asks Monaco now.
  • Nothing you do today changes.
  • Every pause in typing becomes a step of editor.history, so one ⌘Z order covers everything.
  • A bigger change, outside this epic's ask: a step per pause, its own undo grouping, every edit path rethought.
  • ⌘Z works exactly as it does today, with CodeMirror under it.
