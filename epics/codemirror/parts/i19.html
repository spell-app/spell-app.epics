Fixed: a click in a head's comment puts the caret where you clicked.

It was the wrapping, as you guessed, but only one kind of it.

  • A head's comment (-- e.g. "the card is a face card") sits at its band's right end. When the pane is too narrow for it there, it moves to a row of its own under the band.
  • The editor works out which letter is under the pointer from where each piece of the line is drawn. That comment is drawn outside the band's box, so the editor never found it. It took the band as the nearest piece and put the caret in the head's words above your click.
  • Comments in a body that wrap onto more rows were fine already: clicks there landed where they should, before and after.

Now a press in that row is placed by the comment's own letters:

  • one click: the caret on the letter clicked
  • past its end: the line's end
  • a drag from it: selects its words
  • two clicks pick a word, three the line

Not covered yet: a drag that STARTS somewhere else and ends in that row still ends in the head's words above, as before.
