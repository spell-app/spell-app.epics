owenoak: “So typing in solitaire.spell is horrible -- the scroll jumps around, the auto-help pops up waayy too fast. I know the machine is loaded, but this is really bad.”

Every popup that shows by itself as you type now waits for the typing to pause 0.8 seconds, the same wait as the hover (--ui-hover-delay). Each key starts the wait again, so nothing shows between quick keys.

Measured in Solitaire.spell, headless, real keys 80 ms apart:

  • The suggestion list: was 18-32 ms after a key, mid-word (after <a, after <action ", after icon="p, after its ). Now: nothing while typing; 818-875 ms after the last key once you stop.
  • The list after a space after a tag (<action => <action "|" />): was at once. Now 0.8 s, and dropped if you keep typing.
  • Signature help (off unless Settings… turns it on): was at once after a space. Now 0.8 s after the last key.
  • Unchanged: the words strip under an attribute's value (1 s of rest, T70); hovers and error popups (0.8 s, mouse only); accent fixes (they change the text, they're not a popup).
  • Still at once: Ctrl+Space (89 ms), ⇧⌘Space, ⌥↓, and the list again after you choose a tag or attribute from it.
