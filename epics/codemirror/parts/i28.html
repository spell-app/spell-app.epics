The fix, all in the layout, from the text alone only:

  • In a body of steps, a line with steps under it whose first word starts a control line (para, while) is that control line: a declaration can't be there.
  • A top-level line starting with an article that doesn't read: read again with its first words a stand-in type (square), taken when it reads whole as a declaration.
  • A type's member starting with a quoted name, then a verb ("card" is): a property's band.
  • I26's second read tries two more places: the line's own scope, which by then knows the types declared below it; then a list of what its body names (the piece).
