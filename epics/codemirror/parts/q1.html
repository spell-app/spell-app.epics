A change bar is the thin coloured mark at a line's left edge saying "this line differs from something": green for added or changed, a red tick where lines were deleted. You asked for one; today's Monaco in the app has none, so it's new, and it needs a baseline to compare with.

CodeMirror's merge package draws it (a 3px bar, unifiedMergeView with its diff drawing hidden), and the baseline can be moved on each save or publish.

  • The bar shows what Publish would send: the same baseline Publish's diff uses.
  • Saving doesn't clear it; publishing does.
  • Why recommended: saves are automatic (2 s after typing), so a bar against the save would vanish as you watch.
  • What you changed this session; reopening or reloading clears it.
  • Cheapest: no published copy to fetch.
  • As VS Code's dirty marks, but the app saves by itself 2 s after typing, so the bar would flash and go.
  • A green bar beside every line that differs from the published copy, red ticks for deleted lines; publishing clears them.
