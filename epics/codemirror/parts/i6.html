Found and fixed in Spell UI's <ui-property>.

  • Why Spanish only: a Spanish schema gives its choices words (valueLabels: down shows as abajo). With words, each read of a property's choices made a NEW list; without them (English) it handed back the same one.
  • Each keystroke in the Code card makes the Inspector read its schema again. The property saw a "new" list of choices, made its select again, and noted its value may have changed, from inside that effect: Solid's EFFECT_RELAY_TEAR, 3 times (Solitario's card, its direction).
  • Now it keeps the list it made while its values and words are the same, so nothing is made again. The cause has nothing to do with the look: the dark look is likely just where it was first seen.
