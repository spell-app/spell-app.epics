CodeMirror shows its own words by asking a table keyed by its English ("Find", "Replace", "Fold line", "$ unchanged lines" ...): 33 of them. The test keeps that table in its own file, CodeMirrorTexts, Spanish always bundled. WWOD §6 says the app's words are keys in AppTexts.en.ts, each other language a lazy AppTexts.<lang>.ts.

  • Each CodeMirror word becomes an AppTexts key (cmFind: "Find"), its Spanish in AppTexts.es.ts, lazy with the rest; a small map turns CodeMirror's English into those keys.
  • One home for every word the app shows; the Spanish reviewer finds them all in one file.
  • A CodeMirrorPhrases.es.ts beside AppTexts, keyed by CodeMirror's English, loaded with the Spanish chunk.
  • Easier to compare with CodeMirror's list after an upgrade; a second place to look.
  • Every word in the editor, CodeMirror's and ours, is in AppTexts; Spanish loads only on a Spanish page.
