The bubbly look draws a comment's -- as one dash, —, but the two characters are still there: a click on the dash's right half puts the caret between them (column 24 of Card.spell's group "Names" line, found for I24).

Enter there is handled since I24, but a letter typed there splits the --, and the caret is drawn in the dash's middle. Fix: make the dash one step for the caret (CodeMirror's atomicRanges over the BubblyHtml.DASH marks, in CodeMirrorBubbly.ts, which text-feet held during I24).

The comment's dash is one step for the caret now, as it's drawn.

  • A click on the dash's left half puts the caret before it; on its right half, after it.
  • Left and Right step over it in one step.
  • Backspace right after it, or Delete right before it, takes both - (J64).
  • A letter typed after a click on the dash goes after the --: the comment stays whole.
  • A drag that starts on the dash's left half selects from before it.

How:

  • The mark that draws the dash is one step for the caret (CodeMirrorDecorations.build()).
  • CodeMirror alone would have sent a click on the left half to after the dash. It counts characters, and the place between the two - is a tie. So a click there now goes to whichever end is nearer on screen (CodeMirrorSelection.atomicPresses).
  • // isn't drawn as one mark, so it's left as it was.
