The bubbly look draws a comment's -- as one dash, now one step for the caret (I25).

  • Backspace right after it deletes both -, and Delete right before it does too: CodeMirror's own handling of a range that's one step, so the dash goes as it's drawn, one mark.
  • The other way, deleting one - at a time, would leave a single - drawn as text, no longer a comment.
