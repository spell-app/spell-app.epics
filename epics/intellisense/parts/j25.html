Compiled Spell still writes prop: on <ui-*> tags, so every prop:<name> stays typed in spell-ui/jsx. To stop people writing them by hand, each one ends its docstring with:

  • @deprecated write `options` -- `prop:` stays only for compiled spell
  • WWOD forbids JSDoc tags (@param ...) in our docstrings.
  • @deprecated is the exception here: it is the editor's own signal, drawn struck through, with the hint on hover.
  • The generator says why, on deprecatedProp() in the element manifests.
  • Cost: jsx.ts grows by two lines per prop: member (1,525 of them).
