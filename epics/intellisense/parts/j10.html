Every program that writes <ui-*> JSX now uses Spell UI's generated types:

  • Spell UI's own: the loose DocsJSXTags keeps only <ui-docs-search>
  • the app: its catch-all index, [tag: `ui-${string}`], is gone
  • spell-epic: its per-family ui-* lines are gone
  • spell-lang's check of compiled spell

spell-brand writes no <ui-*> JSX.

They caught 21 errors, in 15 kinds of place:

  • 5 real mistakes in our JSX, fixed there
  • 6 types of ours looser than the values they hold: narrowed (one, a hue read from JSON, typed where it's used)
  • 3 gaps in the generated types, filled in the generator
  • 1 left as a typed cast with a HACK: comment, in 3 files (issue filed)
