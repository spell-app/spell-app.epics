The Solid 2 examples on Spell UI's docs pages taught the old workarounds. Now:

  • options={...}, visible={open()} ...: no prop: (19 pages, the getting-started table among them)
  • on-ui-change={(event) => ...} in place of ref={(el) => el.addEventListener("ui-change", ...)} (33 examples)
  • Their comment: "events and rich data are plain props"
  • <ui-tree-diagram>'s "From script" text likewise.
  • Left alone: the plain-JS examples' addEventListener(), React's onui-hide, and the Solid 1.9 compatibility page (1.9 still needs prop:).
