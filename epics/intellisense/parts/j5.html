Each rich (json) property of a tag is typed twice: by its plain property name (options?: unknown), and as "prop:options"?: unknown.

  • Why: epic solid-17 drops prop: (objects, arrays and functions go to the property); until it lands our JSX still writes prop:options.
  • The prop: one's docstring says it goes once solid-17 lands.
  • Typed unknown: the vocabulary names no type for them.
