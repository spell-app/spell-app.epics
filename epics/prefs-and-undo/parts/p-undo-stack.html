Nothing in the repos has undo.

A GENERIC UndoStack in util's persist folder, on the kit's reactive engine, with TRANSACTIONS; Spell's runtime and spell-app build on it.

  • Nothing Spell-specific in it: a step is a list of records that can undo and redo; what a record holds is up to whoever builds on it (Spell's runtime, spell-app).
  • A step has a title in a person's words ("Change Margin"), and optionally the thing it changed.
  • Transactions:
    • one stays open until its work finishes, waits and promise chains included
    • undoable work started while it's open (nested, or begun while it waits) folds into it, never a step of its own
    • the records stay separate inside; a person sees one step
  • Undo and redo apply what was recorded, at once: nothing re-runs and nothing waits, so an async action's redo is instant.
  • Two stacks; a new step empties redo; a limit; clearing.
  • Folding: a step with the same title and the same thing as the newest folds into it, so typing "hello" is one step.
  • Live, so a menu shows them and updates by itself:
    • canUndo and canRedo
    • undoTitle ("Undo Change Margin", or just "Undo") and redoTitle
  • Where to see it: UndoStack, in the kit's persist folder; README-util's new section, "UndoStack: one step for everything an action does". Nothing uses it yet: P6 wires it into the runtime.
  • Transactions, as Owen asked (Q6):
    • ONE step for everything recorded while its function runs, or until its promise settles, chains included.
    • Undoable work nested in it, or begun while it waits, joins it: never a step of its own.
    • A throw still records what changed, and passes the error on; nothing recorded: no step.
  • Undo and redo put the records back at once: nothing is run again, nothing waited on, so an async action's redo is instant (tested: the action ran once).
  • The stacks move before the records run, so what they set off sees the new history; nothing records while it undoes or redoes.
  • Folding: the same title AND target fold into one step, so typing "hello" undoes to "" and redoes to "hello"; a step with no target never folds.
    • fold() is the hook a recorder overrides to merge records: P6 keeps the minimum there.
  • A limit, and clear() for when another document opens.
  • Live: its four answers are spell cells.
    • canUndo and canRedo
    • undoTitle and redoTitle
    • An observe() re-runs once per stack written, so an undo shows a plain observer one in-between state; Solid batches.
  • Generic: records say how to undo themselves; titles are English by default, and titleFor words them whole in another language (tested in Spanish).
  • The persist folder's one import from outside it: util's reactive engine. The docs bundle came out byte-identical: tree-shaken.
  • Checks: kit 823 passed / 46 skipped / 72 files (+14 undo tests).
  • Merged into main.
