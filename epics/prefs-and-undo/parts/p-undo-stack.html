Nothing in the repos has undo.

A GENERIC UndoStack in util's persist folder, on the kit's reactive engine, with TRANSACTIONS; Spell's runtime and spell-app build on it.

  • Nothing Spell-specific in it: a step is a list of records that can undo and redo; what a record holds is up to whoever builds on it (Spell's runtime, spell-app).
  • A step has a title in a person's words ("Change Margin"), and optionally the thing it changed.
  • Transactions:
    • one stays open until its work finishes, waits and promise chains included
    • undoable work started while it's open (nested, or begun while it waits) folds into it, never a step of its own
    • the records stay separate inside; a person sees one step
  • Undo and redo apply what was recorded, at once: nothing re-runs and nothing waits, so an async action's redo is instant.
  • Two stacks; a new step empties redo; a limit; clearing.
  • Folding: a step with the same title and the same thing as the newest folds into it, so typing "hello" is one step.
  • Live canUndo, canRedo, undoTitle ("Undo Change Margin", or just "Undo") and redoTitle: a menu shows them and updates by itself.
