Nothing in the repos had undo.

Added a GENERIC UndoStack to util's persist folder, on the kit's reactive engine, with TRANSACTIONS; nothing Spell-specific in it: Spell's runtime builds on it.

  • A step is a list of records that undo and redo themselves, with a title in a person's words ("Change Margin") and optionally the thing it changed.
  • Transactions: ONE step for everything recorded while its function runs or until its promise settles, chains included; undoable work nested in it, or begun while it waits, joins it. A throw still records, and passes the error on.
  • Undo and redo put the records back at once: nothing runs again, nothing waits, so an async action's redo is instant.
  • Two stacks, moved before the records run; a new step empties redo; a limit; clear().
  • Folding: the same title AND target fold into one step, so typing "hello" is one step; fold() is the hook a recorder overrides.
  • Live, on spell cells: canUndo, canRedo, undoTitle and redoTitle, worded whole in another language through titleFor.
  • README-util's new section, "UndoStack: one step for everything an action does".
  • Where to see it: UndoStack, in the kit's persist folder; README-util's new section, "UndoStack: one step for everything an action does". Nothing uses it yet: P6 wires it into the runtime.
