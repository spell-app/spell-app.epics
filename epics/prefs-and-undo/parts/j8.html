⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z undo and redo while the app has the focus, as planned, and ALSO while nothing on the page has it (the page's body).

  • Why: clicking a card doesn't move the focus, because a card isn't something you tab to.
    • So after playing a few cards the focus is still on the page's body.
    • With the app's element alone, ⌘Z would do nothing right after a move.
  • A text field keeps its own ⌘Z, and an open modal keeps its own keys, as planned (Q19).
  • The catch: with two apps on one page and nothing focused, the one whose keys were registered last wins.
  • ⌘Z works right after clicking a card
  • a page with two apps and nothing focused undoes the newest app
