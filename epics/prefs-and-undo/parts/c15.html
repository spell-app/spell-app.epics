A change made outside any undoable action isn't recorded, and undoing an older step can put its values back over it.

  • Only an action declared undoable is recorded (Q2): a timer, or a plain method, records nothing.
  • e.g. a card turned by an action, then turned again by a timer, then the action undone: the card goes back to how the action found it, and the timer's turn is lost.
  • The same as most apps' undo: the history assumes the actions are what changed things.
  • a program that changes things outside its actions can see undo overwrite those changes
