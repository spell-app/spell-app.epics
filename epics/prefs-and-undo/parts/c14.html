A recompile that keeps the game in progress starts its undo history empty.

  • Editing a program and recompiling it carries its things over (the game stays as it was), but it's a new run, and each run has its own history.
  • So the moves made before the edit can't be undone after it. Restart clears everything anyway.
  • after each recompile, Undo starts with nothing to undo
