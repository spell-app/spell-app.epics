1. In VS Code's main Spell window, open a terminal, then build and serve the public preview:
cd ~/www/spell/spell-app
spell app preview build
spell app preview serve
  1. Open the address it prints in a browser, open the Solitaire example, and change a line of Solitaire.spell.
  2. Wait a second, then reload the tab (⌘R).
  3. Press Reset Example.
  • After the reload, your change is still there.
  • DevTools' Application tab lists the edit under IndexedDB: in the database spell-prefs, a spell-static entry for the file.
  • After Reset Example, the file is the example's own again.
