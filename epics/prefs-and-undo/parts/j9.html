Undo and redo do nothing while an undoable action is still running: they answer false, and the key passes on.

  • e.g. ⌘Z while a deal still waits between cards does nothing: the deal finishes as one step, and the next ⌘Z undoes it.
  • Why: putting the step before it back in the middle of a deal would tangle the two.
    • The deal would go on moving cards out of piles that had just been put back under it.
  • Not in the plan: found while building, and tested.
  • ⌘Z during a waiting action is ignored, not queued: press it again once the action is done
