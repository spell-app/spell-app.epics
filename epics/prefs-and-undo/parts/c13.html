An app drawn by its page's markup gets no ⌘Z.

  • A <spell-viewer> with markup inside it draws the app itself: the app never mounts its own drawing, and the undo keys are listened for when an app mounts.
  • The app's undo() and redo() still work, e.g. from a button in that markup.
  • The fix: listen for the keys where the viewer lays the app out too.
  • an app laid out in markup undoes only through its own buttons, for now
