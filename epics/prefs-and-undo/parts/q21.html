P6 · Undo in the Runtime keeps its own way to mark an undoable action, declareAction("turnOver", { undoneAs: "Turn Card" }) on a thing's class. Epic 's P10 (actions and menus) may take it over later.

  • Why it came up: asked whether P6 could start (2026-10-24), elemental-spell's session said go, but asked for no public declareAction().
    • Its P10 makes "an action" a described command (a title, an icon, shortcuts, when it's enabled), every run going through one place, with undo and redo as built-in app actions.
    • It suggested a plain runtime call instead, e.g. core.undoStep("Turn Card", fn).
  • Owen's answer (in the modal, 2026-10-24): "As planned".
  • So P10 can take it over:
    • declareAction() is a thin wrapper over a runtime recorder: the history on the running program, and one undo step around a function
    • the tracking the while handlers use grows into that recorder, its calls kept stable, for elemental-spell's P9
    • the app's undo and redo, their live titles, and ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z are ready for P10 to wrap
  • P6 builds declareAction() as planned, plus the runtime recorder under it
  • elemental-spell's P10 may replace declareAction() with its own actions, calling the same recorder
  • any events P6 sends are named in pairs:
    • undoing / undone
    • redoing / redone
