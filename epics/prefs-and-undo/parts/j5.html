The plan said a remembered property on a thing with no chosen id is an error at its declaration (TypeError), naming the fix. Built instead: a warning on the program's console, once per class and property, and the property just isn't remembered.

  • Why not at the declaration: nothing knows then whether a thing will get a chosen id.
    • new Settings({ id: "main" }) sets its id after the class is declared, and after the thing is made.
    • A made-up id (settings-k3x9q2mf) is new each run, so nothing kept under it would ever be found again.
  • Why not at its first read: a throw there would break a running app over a preference.
  • The warning names the fix: Settings.zoom can't be remembered: this Settings has no id the program chose. Give it one, e.g. `new Settings({ id: "main" })`.
  • An app needs no id: a program has one app, so its remembered properties are kept by name alone (@owen/solitaire:theme).
  • a thing with no chosen id keeps working, and forgets its remembered property, saying so once
  • an app's and a chosen-id thing's are remembered as planned
