Owen's comment on the plan, 2026-10-24, on transactions: Spell-app may have its own "subclass" or implementation of this, but the base mechanism shouldn't be spell-specific.

  • UndoStack holds steps, transactions, folding, the limit and the live titles: nothing about Spell's things or properties.
  • It's built on the kit's reactive engine directly, not on Spell's Observable.
  • A step is a list of records that can undo and redo; what a record holds is up to whoever builds on it.
  • Spell's runtime adds the recorder of property and list changes (P6); spell-app may add its own.
  • P5 · Undo Stack: the generic stack, in util's persist folder
  • P6 · Undo in the Runtime: Spell's recorder on it
