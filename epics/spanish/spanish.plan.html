Spanish is the second language Spell reads: a file named Carta.es.spell is parsed, compiled, run and edited in Spanish, and one project may mix languages. Built: language packs (English the first, with its own inflector; Spanish for the whole grammar, on the same rule classes), a CLI that checks and fixes them, article agreement warnings in both languages, the parser's messages and a test's run-time lines in Spanish, Spell UI as <es-*> tags, the editor and VS Code, and Solitario, Solitaire written again in Spanish. Then a translator, spell translate <project> --to es: names first (a shared glossary, then Claude), each line written by its own rule in Spanish, then Claude fixes what fails lint, a test or the shape check; Solitaire comes out lint clean and passing its tests in under a minute, spell translate speedtest times it, and --review lets a person check the names first. The docs, Spell Epic and the spell command's own words stay English.
