• Our texts register with Spell UI's UI.i18n scoped as spell-app/app, as a component's are under its tag.
    • so a key of ours, e.g. save, never rewords Spell UI's shared one
  • appTexts.text() reads APP_TEXTS itself before the runtime loads, and under node. So editor.ts stays free of spell-ui (it reaches it by dynamic import only).
  • A notice that names a project group (Created example Solitaire.) gets the group's words as params.
    • the params: {type} / {Type} / {theType}, from appTexts.groupWords()
    • Spanish says Se creó el ejemplo Solitario., Se borró la guía.
    • so the noun's gender never has to agree with a word around it
  • The group words themselves (Projects, Examples ... and their descriptions) are app texts, keyed by the group's type. Spaces' PROJECT_GROUPS keeps its English, as the fallback for a group the texts don't know.
