The kit's startPass() stamped every pass with the page's effort. With --names-effort best and the page at balanced, the status card would have said the names ran at balanced. So startPass(name, { effort }) now takes the effort a pass runs at, when the command chose it (RunPassStart in the kit's runs types, one test). The translator always passes the tier it really asks at, and adds a progress line when its flag beats the page's pick: names: at best, by --names-effort, not the page's balanced. The page's picker starts at --effort, never a pass's own flag.
