Tags, attribute, event, slot and part names are ASCII (tamano, sin-anios, es-boton). Values keep their accents (tamano="pequeño", "móvil", "página"), and canonicalize() also finds them typed without (pequeno), so the old smoke page's spellings still work.

  • ñ in a name became n where it reads fine (tamano), ni where n would read as another word (sin-anios for disable-year).
  • Tabs are es-solapa / es-solapas, not pestanas (eyelashes) -- a native speaker may prefer another word.
