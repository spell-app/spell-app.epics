Owen, 2026-10-23: "why is the type name being created camelCase at all?" Claude had answered gestorTareas for todos-app.

  • The names prompt says so, and the check refuses a name whose form changed, and asks again: words (plain or dashed) can't come back camelCase; a camelCase name can't come back as words (newTaskName => nombreNuevaTarea is right).
  • A dashed name with de inside reads as one name: un gestor-de-tareas tiene ... compiles to GestorDeTareas. The small-word check splits names at spaces only.
  • The two grammar gaps are fixed too, as a person may write them (J170, J171).
