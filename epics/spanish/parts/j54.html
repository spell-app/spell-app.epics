The grammar's two keyword lists, for both languages in one grammar, are written by scripts/vscode-grammar.ts, and a test fails when they're out of date.

  • Statement words: what the pack's statement rules start with (multi-word phrases too, like de lo contrario), but not the articles.
  • Little words: what can follow an expression (is not, no es, y), plus the articles.
  • The old hand list's words that aren't keywords in any pack are gone: while, until, put, when, either, one of, of, in, with, then.
  • It's a plain script (yarn tsx scripts/vscode-grammar.ts), not a yarn script or a spell command, so the commands page needn't change.
