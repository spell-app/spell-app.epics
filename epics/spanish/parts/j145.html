Optional and choice words are matched to English's by place (a symbol to the same symbol); where the two syntaxes don't have as many, the word is left out, never guessed.

  • So esperar jugar solo (hand: esperar a jugar solo), pausar 50 mseg, para fila de 1 a 7: all read the same.
  • Why not include them: some change meaning, e.g. (hacia abajo)? in a counting loop.
  • An optional article is written when English said one there (la imagen de ...).
