The pack's words are checked against English's, but only where the tables are shaped alike:

  • every table English has, e.g. blacklist, memberStopWords ...
  • every declarations pattern
  • typeWords: a word for every built-in type English has a word for (the words themselves differ)
  • builtInMembers: only that each key names a real built-in member, since English has none and Spanish translates five
