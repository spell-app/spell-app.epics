esperar que X (sea Y)?: que is REQUIRED, unlike English's optional that. A bare esperar X is async's wait.

A check of a fact stays indicative, esperar que it es una cosa: the word rule for "is a" has no subjunctive sea yet.

  • should: X (debería|deberia) ser Y
  • echo: eco X
  • a test's start and end, with no tests in English either:
    • (iniciar|empezar) (la)? prueba (silenciosa)? "..."
    • (terminar|finalizar) (la)? prueba

What core.expect() reports is the Spanish text as written, e.g. el rank de it.
