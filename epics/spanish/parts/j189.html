A typed or picked answer goes through the names pass's readability check (NamesPass.problemWith()), plus a check that no other type or name already has that word. One that fails comes back once, as a new row (id <kind>:<words>~2, its reason in From and its details), and the run waits again with a fresh timeout. Still unreadable after that: the proposal stays, and the report notes it. A typed type's gender: naipe (m) / (f) says it, else the proposal's or the picked alternative's, else Spanish's guess by the ending.
