Each pack has a parserMessages.ts beside its messages.ts: {name} templates by key, as P12's CORE_MESSAGES are, read with SP.Language.of(scope).parserMessage(key, params); English's table is every key and the fallback.

  • A separate table from the core's: those are copied into a project's words file for the runner, these never leave the parser.
  • P8's agreement warning moved in as the did-you-mean key: ArticleAgreement.warning() is gone, one design instead of two. The pack keeps an article(kind, noun, type) for examples.
  • English params and Spanish params differ where the languages do: English writes a {type}, Spanish {aType} (un/una by gender).
  • spell lang packs check reports a key a pack lacks, as the parserMessages table.
