spell lang packs check finds each <parser>.addRule(...) call in <module>.en.ts and <module>.<lang>.ts by reading their text, not by loading the parsers.

  • Why: the fix needs each registration's place in its file, and the loaded parsers merge a rule's registrations into one group, losing their order.
  • A loop that registers a rule counts once, as written.
  • Rules are matched by class, plus ruleName for a specialized word rule: Wording(pause_units).
