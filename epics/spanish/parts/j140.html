New word rule event_name_link. In Spanish it is (en|de|del|sobre): al clic en carta con una carta is event clic-en-carta, and its name ends at con.

  • A linking word must sit between two plain words, never at either end.
  • Not a / al: al starts the handler line.
  • English registers the rule as just on. English names already take on as a plain word (card click on table), so English is unchanged and the language packs stay aligned.
  • An event name is still at most 4 tokens, linking words included.
