A phrase like - it "is a (suit)" builds its syntax from the language's word rules now, so a project's declarations change:

  • they said {operator:is} (a|an) (expression:club|...)
  • they say {operator:is_or_is_not_a} (expression:club|...)
  • Why:
    • the same phrase in Spanish, - "es un (palo)", builds from es / un the same way
    • and is a face card matches isn't a face card
      • and are not a face card too
  • Fixture declaration snapshots were reblessed; compiled code didn't change. The committed declarations in spell-app's examples and library still say {operator:is}: they load through the alias, and change on their next compile.
  • Declarations diff on the next compile of each example.
