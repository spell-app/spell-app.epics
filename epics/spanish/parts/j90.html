Solitario (option a) writes its cards in Spanish, in four .es.spell files, rather than importing the English card library.

  • So every word of it is Spanish: a person reading it never meets the English card's names.
  • The cost: a second copy of the cards to keep in step with the library's.
  • Words picked: dama for the queen (not reina), símbolo for a suit's symbol.
