In a Spanish project, a keyword typed without its written accent gets it back by itself: el numero de becomes el número de.

  • How: the language server's on-type formatting, asked after a space or a new line; the extension turns editor.formatOnType on for spell, and Monaco's editor has it on too.
  • It fixes a word only once its line parses, so in practice when you press Enter at the end of the line.
  • Not a quick fix with editor.codeActionsOnSave: that waits for a save, and Owen asked for nothing to do.
