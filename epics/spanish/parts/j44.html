P8: the article check runs in BlockLine on each committed statement (SpellWarnings.noteArticles()), finding every form of the language's indefinite_article / definite_article word rule in the line, then the noun after it.

  • Why: the rules' syntax writes its articles as plain choices, (a|an), (un|una); making each one a {indefinite_article} slot would change every rule's groups and snapshots for no change in what parses.
  • So it also warns where no rule looks at the article, e.g. if the x is an card; English needs no type, Spanish only for a noun naming a type.
  • Not checked: JSX text, quoted phrases, the noun after an adjective (una nueva carta reads nueva, no type, no warning).
