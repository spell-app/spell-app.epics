Chose (el|la|los|las)? X (de|del) Y for the X of Y, the article optional (the lead's pick).

  • so el palo del naipe and palo de la carta both read
  • its X is (su|sus) X
  • its ordinal: su tercera carta

An object literal's property takes (=|es|de), English's =|is|of.

  • e.g. rank de "queen", largo es 1
  • a list of them is joined by (,|y|e)

A native speaker might prefer the article required: a loose X de Y could swallow quitar carta de la pila's item. English's remove X from Y has no clash.
