How methods read in Spanish:

  • a method starts para: para mover (una carta) a (una pila):
  • a test, para probar ...; its call, probar ...
  • an animation: (crear)? (animación|animacion) ...
  • an argument's type: (x como (un|una)? tipo)
  • its default: (x (=|es|de|como) valor)
    • English's set to has no Spanish form
  • props: (con a, b y c)
  • a quoted phrase ends (si|es)?, as English's (if|is)?
  • a receiver left out in an outline body is (de|del) ..., after the phrase

Risk: para is also for-each's first word, as in para cada carta en la pila.

  • the two tie on length, and the earlier-registered module (statements) wins the tie
  • the declarations' method-line pattern, ^para, also stubs every para cada ... line as a method call ahead
