Spell UI's translation mechanism was smoke-tested and empty: no component had a Spanish dictionary, the page-wide call wasn't built, and a Spanish program drew with English tags.

Built all four parts: a Spanish vocabulary per component, the page-wide runtime, Spanish texts, and Spanish drawables and JSX in Spell; Spell Epic and the spell command keep their English.

  • A Spanish vocabulary sits beside every Spell UI component (104 files), and the shared one once; names are ASCII (es-boton, tamano), values keep their accents and are found without them.
  • <ui-root lang="es">, or the page's lang, defines every component in Spanish, as es-*, loaded lazily, its texts Spanish; the Spanish chunk is 10.3 kB gzipped, on Spanish pages only.
  • By hand: vocabulary.defineComponents({ prefix, dictionary }).
  • A Spanish program's JSX writes <es-*> tags as they are; its drawables are named from Spanish (cartas-carta), and its runner turns Spanish on before it draws.
