Gaps found writing Solitario, each worked around:

  1. No Spanish constructor hook: para crearlo isn't create(), so Solitario calls para preparar by hand.
  2. An outline method name with a fused pronoun (-la) doesn't match its calls: the sentence style is used.
  3. al vs a in method names don't match each other.
  4. The plural están vacíos isn't a word yet.
  5. A plain es needs its subject written.
  6. A multi-word quoted type name is singularized (pila de descarte).
  7. Spell UI's colours are English words: "red" si su color es rojo sino "black".
  8. Number words can't be names.
