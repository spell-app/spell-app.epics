Owen's comment on 1.3 (M4): "build this into the tokenizer?" Yes: the number pattern is the language's.

  • 1,5 with no space after the comma is one number
  • 1, 5, with a space, stays two numbers in a list, so como uno de 1, 2, 3 keeps working
  • the tokenizer takes its number pattern from the pack (P3); Spanish sets the comma (P5)
