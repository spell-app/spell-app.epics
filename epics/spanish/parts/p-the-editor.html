A Spanish file was uncoloured, completed English words, and warned in English with English examples.

Made the highlighter, the language server, Monaco and VS Code follow the project's language, and put back a keyword's missing accent as you type.

  • spell/es fences colour with Spanish's parser.
  • A Spanish keyword typed without its accent is accepted, and fixed as you type a space or Enter: numero becomes número, esta está; never si, and never a name a person chose.
  • VS Code's and Monaco's keyword lists are written from the packs, English and Spanish in one grammar; si no: and de lo contrario go back out a level.
  • In a Spanish project, completion offers Spanish words (si / para / devolver), and the Type Explorer's built-in types are Spanish's.
