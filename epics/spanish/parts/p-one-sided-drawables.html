A drawable thing has two draw sides (front, back), and a drawable list draws its items with no hook of its own.A drawable thing draws with one 'to draw it'; a drawable list's 'to draw it' is a wrapper around its items, and 'to draw each item' wraps each one.
  • Owen, 2026-10-22: "drawable thing should NOT be two-sided -- single draw it command"
  • A drawable list: to draw it is the wrapper around the list's items; by default a hidden (or error) wrapper, then the items' elements in its slot
  • to draw each item is the wrapper each item is drawn in; by default it just draws that item's own drawing, when the item is drawable
  • The library's cards are drawable: a card is a drawable thing, a pile is a drawable list of cards; a game adds only its layout and CSS
  • The front / back sides (DrawSide, side_name, frente / dorso) go; their callers move to draw it
