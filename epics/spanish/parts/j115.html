Translating Owen's rewritten Card.spell into Carta.es.spell:

  • number card: carta numérica (over carta de número)
  • card names (a variable in the deck's state): nombres, as a variable name can't hold de
  • specific cards: cartas concretas; card rank / suit headings: rango de la carta, palo de la carta
  • await turn it face down: esperar a voltear eso boca abajo; item column of [...]: el elemento columna de [...]
  • the expected strings follow the words: "dama de espadas", "comodín rojo", "D♠↑"
