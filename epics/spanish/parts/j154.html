• A <ui-*> tag, its attributes and values: Spell UI's Spanish names (its components.es.ts, through its own Vocabulary, handed in by spell-app: spell-lang never imports Spell UI).
  • A text in a Spell UI attribute that takes a set of values: its word there, color={... "blue"} => "azul".
  • Texts people read (aria-label, title, content, label, alt, placeholder ...): through the dictionary's texts. A fixed list, as no vocabulary marks an attribute as text.
  • part: through the dictionary's names (part="picture" => "imagen"). class, name (an icon's), events (onClick) and plain HTML tags stay.
  • CSS: only selectors. An attribute selector with no tag ([layout="vertical"]) is taken as the project's own things', through its names; comments and custom properties stay English (the hand Solitario reworded two comments).
  • Spanish to English doesn't turn <es-*> back into <ui-*> yet: the vocabulary is built from English, the canonical names.
