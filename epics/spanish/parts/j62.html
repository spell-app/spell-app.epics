The brief said the Spanish texts load only when Spanish is in use; the language server's are a static import instead.

  • the editor's are lazy: AppTexts.es.ts, a chunk only a Spanish page fetches
  • the language server's Spanish labels are not: LspTexts.es.ts, about 40 short strings, well under 1 kB gzipped

Why:

  • Hover and completion answer synchronously: a lazy pack would show English labels on the first hover of a Spanish project, then Spanish.
  • The labels follow the PROJECT's language, not the page's, so an English editor can open a Spanish project at any time.
  • The Spanish language pack they label (SP.spanish: its parser, words, messages) is in the same bundle statically already, far bigger than these strings.

Making it lazy: load it when a Spanish project opens (SpellModels.use()), with English until then.
