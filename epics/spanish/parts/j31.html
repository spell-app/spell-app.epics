Cards' top and bottom of the deck became the way Spanish says a pile's ends:

  • the top card of the deck: la carta de arriba del mazo
  • the bottom card of the deck: la carta de abajo del mazo
  • over la primera / última carta del mazo, which also works
  • It needed a second Spanish registration of ordinal_position_expression:
    • (el|la) {arg} de {ordinal} (en|de|del) {expression}
    • specialized with Priority.specific, so it beats the loose property read the same words also are (mazo.arriba.carta)
  • English's output is unchanged; the Spanish compiles to the same mazo.firstItem / mazo.lastItem.
