The names pass is one claude -p call for every type, name, phrase and text (and their genders), so the terms agree. Its comments go in calls of their own, one per file, started at the same time.

  • Why: the comments are most of what claude writes (11.5k of 16k characters on Solitaire), and no code reads them. One call for everything took 122 s; the names alone, 83-88 s.
  • Effort, measured on Solitaire's names prompt: default 85-120 s; --effort medium 83 s; low 45 s but worse names (escalera for tableau, numérica for seven, fused dibujarlo against the prompt); Sonnet 40 s, but talón, tablero, pila descarte. So medium, the default model.
  • Every call runs with no tools, from a temp folder: it only answers, and reads no project's CLAUDE.md.
  • A second, short call (6-11 s) re-asks for the names Spanish can't read: on Solitaire, clic en carta, rango de carta each time.
