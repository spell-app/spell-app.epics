Two skill calls bracket the flight. In between, Owen works on the pages alone, and everything he does lands in files on disk.

  1. /airplane, before the flight (online):
    • a pre-flight check: page server up, extension current, no page loading anything remote, no session mid-work
    • airplane mode ON: the pages stop saying "start /epic review", and say "queued for when you land" instead
  2. On the plane (no Claude):
    • open any plan doc or guide in the side bar
    • mark items, write notes on any section or phase, add new todos and questions
    • all of it saves through the local page server
    • a plan doc's marks go to its inbox file; a note on a guide or other page is written into the page itself, the way goals thoughts are, and travels with it in the shared content repo
  3. /airplane land, after (online again):
    • one list of everything left, across every epic and page: marks (sent or not), drafts, Do Now requests, page notes, new items, details answers, goals thoughts
    • works through it in background agents, the way /bedtime does: each answer written INTO its item or page, nothing merged
    • then /epic review, for Owen to go through what came back
  • What Owen leaves is only files: the epic inboxes (epics/<name>/<name>.inbox.json) and the notes written into the pages.
  • /airplane land (P5) only reads them. It is built before the flight anyway, in bedtime mode with the rest (Q8), but it doesn't have to be merged before Owen flies.
