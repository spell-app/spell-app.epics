Your example (red solid, orange and green off, click orange: orange + green) contradicts "keep what's on"; I read it as: clicking a state that's off turns it on and keeps the others on (red + orange). The whole rule, the same for a section's chips and the toolbar's:

  • everything showing: a click shows only that state
  • a hidden state: it shows too
  • a shown state, others showing: it hides
  • the only one showing: everything again
  • one rule in code: StateFilter.nextShown() (spell-epic), restated as the runtime's nextShown() for the Epics index
  • each chip's tooltip says what its click will do ("2 open: show only these")
