Folding an open item from its stuck line threw the page's scroll away. Fixed.

The cause, reproduced in Chrome on epic-components' plan doc:

  • An open item's line sticks below the section titles while its details scroll under it.
  • Folding it there (a click on the stuck line) hid every pixel of details ABOVE the window.
  • But the page kept its scroll position, and the browser's scroll anchoring had nothing to hold on to: its anchor was inside the hidden details.
  • So the item vanished off the top, and the page showed whatever came that far below: as far down as Owen had read into the item.
    • 1500px in the test; a few screens on a long item.
  • Sections were fine: the runtime already scrolls a section's stuck title into place as it folds (keepTitlePut()). Items had no counterpart.

Ruled out:

  • scroll-follow's address update (replaceState): it fires no hash change
  • the folds' followHash(): it runs only on a hash change, or on mount
  • focus: a click on body text moves none

The fix: EpicItem.keepLinePut().

  • It runs inside the item's toggle, once the close isn't cancelled (ui-close).
  • When the item's top is above its stuck line, it scrolls at once, so the line stays where it is on screen and the details fold away below it.
  • The runtime is unchanged.
  • Folding an item from its stuck line keeps the line where it was, the next item right under it.
    • Checked on the real page: the line at 177px, before and after.
  • A browser test: folding keeps its STUCK line where it is on screen. Without the fix it fails by 1500px.
