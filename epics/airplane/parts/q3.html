  • Sent or not, every mark counts. Owen can still change a mark on the page until the landing.
  • Drafts are listed separately, as "you started writing this", and each one is asked about, never acted on.
  • While airplane mode is on, each mark counts as sent the moment it's made, so the Send button isn't needed.
  • But Owen loses the chance to change his mind before landing.
  • As today: marks that weren't Sent wait for the next /epic review of that doc.
