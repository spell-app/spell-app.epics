Claude Code's extension opens a session's tab with a prompt typed in (claude-vscode.primaryEditor.open, the bridge's open-session), but has no command to SUBMIT it. So the click brings the session's tab up with /epic review <epic> in its input, and the page's notice says to press Enter there. The click also still copies the command.

  • One click plus one Enter, instead of copy, find the tab, paste, Enter.
  • A session busy with a turn gets it typed too; the notice says it starts once that turn ends.
  • A session with no window bridge (a terminal one, or no spell extension): nothing sent, the notice says to type it there.
