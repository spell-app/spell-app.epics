  • Epics page (and anywhere an epic's state shows): four states instead of 'sleeping': in progress; errors (every phase done, urgent items left); done (every phase done, nothing urgent); paused (open phases, untouched for a long time, like shared-content). The sleeping icon is gone everywhere.
  • Plan doc: the right-edge contents rail becomes a sticky toolbar across the top; each section in it has a badge counting the items that wait on Owen (urgent, or Claude replied and waits on his action).
  • Clicking a header no longer loses the page's scroll.
  • Choosing an item's action (its toolbar, or the button by its reply box) folds the item.
  • A picked option keeps its Chosen marker through Send and after Claude applies it, so Owen can see what he has addressed.
  • Claude's green Done cards only when something was done; a card that only says Claude heard him reads as that, not as Done.
