Owen asked to lose the box's bottom bar (Save, Cancel, Delete) and have it save as he types, as a plan doc's note box does. With no Delete button, a saved comment goes when its box is closed empty (open it from the card's pen, clear the text, × or Escape).

  • The first pause in typing adds the comment; later pauses edit it. The floppy in the header shows it saved, or turns red.
  • No Cancel: what's typed is kept. To take a comment back, empty it and close the box.
