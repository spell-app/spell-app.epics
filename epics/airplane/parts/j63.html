Owen (2026-10-10): "The buttons don't have browser tooltips -- unclear what they do." J42's pills (✓ ⟲ list wand) showed nothing in VS Code's side bar.

Why they didn't show.

  • VS Code's side bar shows a title only while the element right under the pointer carries it itself, not a child of it. Electron 38 and later, on macOS: electron/electron#49843 (open; Chrome itself still shows them). VS Code 1.141 runs Electron 43.
  • Nearly every control had its title on the button, and the pointer lands on what's inside: the icon, the chevron, the words. A <ui-button>'s title is on the host, and the pointer lands on its inner <button>.
  • Some had no title at all: each card's empty title="" (which hides the doc's own title data) stopped even Chrome's walk up to an ancestor.
  • And most review pills' titles only repeated their name ("Approve"); what they do was only in the screen-reader label.

The fix, one pattern for all.

  • A shared sheet, Tooltip.css, adopted by every family: everything inside a titled control lets the pointer through to it (pointer-events: none). A titled <ui-button>'s inner button does too, so the host is the hit; its hover and pressed fill are put back on the host.
  • The section titles' words and icon let the pointer through to <ui-section>'s fold button.
  • Every control's title says what it does, verb first, from its element's vocabulary.

Measured on a copy of this doc (1138 controls, headless Chromium, the pointer at each control's centre): before, 1053 had no title on the element under the pointer (100 even by Chrome's own walk); after, 0. Left: 36 controls Spell UI draws itself (below).
