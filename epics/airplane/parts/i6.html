A picked option's Chosen marker went missing after Send, in three places. All three are fixed.

How it was found:

  • On a scratch epic with a question, judgement calls, and a reply with options.
  • Pick, Send, then inbox apply as a session would, watching the pill at each step.
  • Picked and sent were right: dashed, then outlined.

Where it went missing:

  • Folded away. Once applied, the option cards fold under a closed Choices heading.
    • So the solid Chosen pill is out of sight, and the item looks unanswered.
    • Fixed: the folded heading names the pick, in green: ✓ Chosen: B · Bananas.
  • Between the inbox and the doc. apply clears the mark before the doc's answer reaches the page.
    • The page polls the inbox, and sees the mark gone.
    • The doc's pick arrives later: the live update re-fetches the item's part, and waits while the view is hidden.
    • Meanwhile the pill dropped back to Choose.
    • Fixed: the page remembers a sent pick Claude took (ReviewClient.takenPickOf()). The pill stays Chosen · sent, and the chip outlined, until the doc's pick lands.
  • A pick riding on a revisit ("B, but ..."). apply leaves those to talk over.
    • Then inbox clear or done dropped the mark, and the pick with it, unless the session had answered with decide --option.
    • Fixed: clear and done keep the pick as that option set's choice, with a Noted card (PlanDoc.keepPick()).
    • Unless an answer already chose in that set.

Also: the pill now says in words where the pick stands.

  • Chosen · not sent
  • Chosen · sent
  • Chosen, once applied
  • With no Claude listening, and with Review Now, it stays Chosen · sent until a session applies it.
  • Once Owen picks, the card says Chosen from then on: not sent, sent, applied.
  • On open or closed items, folded or not.
