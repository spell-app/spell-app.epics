Fixed (2026-10-10). Three causes, each found by sampling the line every frame, headless:

  1. A click on an item's line scrolled the page to its top (Owen: "clicking item headers jumps to top of page, seems maybe related to auto-closing after I reply").
    • <epic-page> delegated focus, as every Spell UI element does unless it says not to.
    • So a mouse-down on any plain text in the doc focused the page header's first link (the breadcrumb). Focusing that sticky link scrolled the page to the top, and the click then missed the line: nothing folded.
    • After a reply, the item folds and its focused button goes with it: focus is back on the page, so the next click on a line did it. Without a reply, any click while nothing had focus.
    • Fix: delegatesFocus: false on <epic-page>, as <epic-item> already had; also on <epic-update> and <epic-commit>, the other text holders with a button or link inside.
  2. The line dropped 20-80px when the fold's hold ended, for an item near the page's end.
    • The runtime keeps the page's height while a fold closes, so the browser can't pull the reader back.
    • To let go, it took that height off for a moment to measure the page. That one layout was enough: the browser clamped the scroll to the shorter page.
    • Fix (releaseHeight()): the held height only shrinks to the window's bottom, never below, and goes once the page is taller than that. It never measures by taking the hold off.
  3. A link's landing kept scrolling for a second.
    • A link into a section or phase follows its target until it stays put (folds open above it).
    • A target near the page's end can't reach its line, so it never "stayed put": it was scrolled back every frame for the whole second, fighting a fold or a scroll in that time.
    • Fix (EpicFold's land()): "put" also when the scroll went nowhere, and it stops at once on the reader's click, wheel, touch or key.
