A plan doc's comments don't wake a waiting /epic review by themselves: the next wake hands them over.

  • A saved comment isn't a mark.
    • It isn't sent, and plan-doc inbox wait doesn't exit for one.
  • On every wake, /epic review checks the inbox for comments (section 7.3, new step 3b).
    • A wake is Send, Review Now, or any Do Now.
    • It answers each comment like a revisit's note.
    • Then it marks the comment done (plan-doc inbox <name> done cm3), and its card turns solid, "Answered".
    • clear cm3 drops one instead.
  • /airplane land answers them with the epic's other work.
  • Making comments wake wait is a small follow-up, if Owen wants it: a "handed over" flag per comment.
  • Comment, then press Send (the paper plane) to have a listening session take it now.
