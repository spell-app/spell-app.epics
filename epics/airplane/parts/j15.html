Urgent means an item whose chip is red (state attention: an open question, or an unreviewed judgement call or issue) or orange (state replied: Claude's options are waiting on Owen's pick). Both wait on Owen. Todos, tests and caveats never count. A session is running when its record in ~/.claude/sessions has a live pid, and either its cwd is the epic's worktree or its title, minus the icon, is the epic's name. The docs index writes each card's facts (data-phases, data-updated, data-urgent, data-future), and RunningEpics.render() marks every card again on each request, so paused and in progress follow the clock and the sessions. In epic-page itself, running means a session is listening to its review. Planning (no phases yet) is now in progress or paused, with no yellow bubble.
