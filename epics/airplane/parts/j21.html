Owen's five groups come from the one state rule (EpicState).

  • errors: Urgent
  • done: Done
  • future: Planning
  • in progress or paused: Active while it has phases left; Planning while it has none yet
  • a starred epic shows only under Favorites

Inside each group:

  • Alphabetical, by title.
  • An empty group is written hidden, so the page can move a card into it.
  • The group headings are small eyebrow lines, not h3 headings: an h3 would join the page's outline and rail. (They're div role=heading.)
  • Paused epics sit with the active ones, told apart by their grey pause mark.
  • An epic still being planned (no phases) shows under Planning, beside the future ones.
  • The docs home's Epics count reads by the same groups: 5 active · 2 planning · 17 urgent · 7 done.
