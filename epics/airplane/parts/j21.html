Owen's five groups (Favorites, Active, Planning, Urgent, Done) map from the one state rule (EpicState): errors is Urgent, done is Done, future is Planning; in progress or paused is Active with phases left, Planning with none yet. A starred epic shows only under Favorites. Alphabetical by title within each group; an empty group is written hidden, so the page can move a card into it.

The group headings are small eyebrow lines (div role=heading), not h3s: an h3 would join the page's outline and rail.

  • Paused epics sit with the active ones, told apart by their grey pause mark.
  • An epic still being planned (no phases) shows under Planning, beside the future ones.
  • The docs home's Epics count reads by the same groups: 5 active · 2 planning · 17 urgent · 7 done.
