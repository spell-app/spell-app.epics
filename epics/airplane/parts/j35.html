A docs section's comments go first in its body, right under its title (the end of a long section is far from it); any other block's right after the block; a plan item's right after the item, outside it, so they show while it's folded. Only the OUTERMOST block counts (a table inside an aside is the aside's). A bullhorn sits over a block's top right corner, but on a text block (a phase field, Overview prose, a list, an item) it floats right and the text wraps round it. Text selected in a paragraph that's in no block is a comment on its section, the quote kept. Plan docs' blocks: items, phase fields, the summary, Overview prose, tables, asides, code; never the log, commits or status cards.

  • A comment's quote is highlighted softly on the page (the CSS Highlight API: the page itself never changes).
