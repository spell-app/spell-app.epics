Two new plan-doc commands find the docs, and a new skill, /docs-check, judges and fixes them.

  • The finder and the digest are verbs on the plan-doc noun, since both read an epic's plan doc.
    • spell dev plan-doc docs <name> finds the docs.
    • spell dev plan-doc decisions <name> digests what the plan doc settled.
    • Both take --json; they live in EpicDocs.
    • Not under spell dev docs: that one works on pages, not epics.
  • The judging and the fixing are a new skill, /docs-check <epic...>.
    • Not a form of /fussbudget: that one fixes how text reads, this one what it says.
  • Owen runs /docs-check airplane skillz ..., or /docs-check alone to pick epics in a modal.
  • decisions was not asked for. It gives the skill's agents what the plan doc settled, in one call, instead of reading every part file:
    • the Done lists and Updated notes
    • each item's status, chosen option and answer
  • A row in the commands page (commands.json), and two rows in the plan-doc rules' commands table (plan-doc.md).
