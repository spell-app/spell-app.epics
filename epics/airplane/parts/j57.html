Once Claude starts on a comment (working cm3 on, the thread's "Claude: thinking…"), the words you'd written before count as read. So the reply box under the thread is empty again, and what you type goes after them as a NEW reply, waiting for Claude in turn. The pen on your first words goes too.

The other way, your last reply stays in the box to change while Claude is reading it. Then Claude could answer words you've since rewritten, and never know.

Built in CommentList (heldSince(): taken or working, whichever is later) and the page's pendingReplyOf().
