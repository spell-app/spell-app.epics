Open a plan doc in the side bar's Review tab while its epic's session (titled "🚧 <epic>") is open and idle, and nobody is listening. Click the orange pill in the send bar.

  • The epic's session gets /epic review <epic>: sent, or typed in with its tab in front for one Enter.
  • Within a few seconds the pill goes: the session is listening.
  • Try it again with no session for the epic, or with two: the page says which sessions it found, and nothing is typed anywhere.
