Built from the mockup and your notes. Where they left a gap, I picked the quiet option:

  • Turn: a thread is your turn once Claude spoke last; a plan doc's comment answered in the doc (no words on the thread, cm1 on J52) counts as Claude's turn spoken, so it opens with the pills.
  • Pills: the item's grouped pill (check, history, x), on the page's white ground as on an item's line; tooltips just the name, as the item's. A closed thread shows one Reopen pill instead; the toast after that's good / skip it has Undo.
  • Reply box: saved as you type, as ONE pending reply until Claude takes it (a second save changes it, not adds one). Emptied, it goes.
  • Pen: while your last words wait unread, a pen in the header brings them back into the box (your first words too, while the comment is new). Emptying your first words deletes the comment only when the box closes, with Undo.
  • Times: the first message carries the day, the rest the time, unless the day changed; a hidden copy floats at the first line's end so text never runs under the time.
  • Folds: remembered for the visit only; a reload opens your-turn threads again. The old read-news tracking (localStorage) is gone: the bullhorn's orange dot now means your turn.
  • Old servers: the page works out whose turn it is itself when the server sends none: main's page server shows threads at once, but reply / that's good / skip it need it restarted on the merged code (the next opener does that).
  • Every comment is one ivory thread under its paragraph, open while it's your turn.
  • Your reply on a thread is new work for Claude, as a new comment is.
  • Nothing for you to do unless one of these surprises you.
