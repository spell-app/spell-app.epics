A new todo or question from the page waits in the inbox as an ordinary mark, under its own key: new1, new2 ...

  • It's kept with the other marks (marks).
  • The page server picks the key, under the inbox's lock.

Chose it over a separate list of its own, because a mark already has everything a new item needs:

  • unsent / sent, and Send
  • Review Now's count
  • wait handing it over
  • inbox apply clearing it only while it's unchanged
  • A new item goes to Claude with the next Send, like Make Todo.
  • inbox apply makes it, as plan-doc add does, with a Done card: Made from the page: Owen's new todo, written ...
  • Until then it shows at the end of Todos or Questions, dashed (outlined once sent), with Edit and Remove.
  • inbox clear new1 drops one without making it; its note is never kept as a reply.
