The toolbar doesn't fit everywhere, so it tightens in steps.

  • Nine buttons with labels need about 900px; a plan doc's column is 880px at most.
  • It's narrower still in VS Code's side bar, or on a phone.

The steps, re-fitted as the window resizes:

  1. every label
  2. else compact: tighter, a size smaller
  3. else crowded:
    • icons and badges only; the current button keeps its label
    • the names move to tooltips
    • the row scrolls sideways if it must, never the page

Rejected:

  • wrapping to a second row: a taller sticky header on every scroll
  • always scrolling, with labels: hidden buttons on a desktop
  • Desktop: one row, every label.
  • Phone or side bar: a row of icons with their badges, the section you are reading named.
