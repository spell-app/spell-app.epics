Nine buttons with labels need about 900px; a plan doc's column is 880px at most, less in VS Code's side bar or on a phone. So the runtime fits the row in steps: every label; else compact (tighter, a size smaller); else crowded: icons and badges only, the current button keeps its label, the names in tooltips, and the row scrolls sideways if it must (never the page). Re-fitted as the window resizes. Rejected: wrapping to a second row (a taller sticky header on every scroll) and always scrolling with labels (hidden buttons on a desktop).

  • Desktop: one row, every label.
  • Phone or side bar: a row of icons with their badges, the section you are reading named.
