  • The note is written INTO the page's HTML, the way goals thoughts are (Q2).
  • So it's committed with the shared content repo.
  • Claude's answer goes under it.
  • A note saves with no Claude, through a new page-server route.
    • The route: POST /api/notes, like the details and goals routes (detailsRoutes, goalsRoutes).
    • It writes the note at the end of its section, or after the page's h1, inside a group for that section. 
      <spell-notes for="<section id>">
  <spell-note id="n3" status="new">...</spell-note>
    • It takes any served page under the shared folders.
    • It needs the server's token and the same origin, and writes under a file lock.
    • It reuses the goals code's insert, page.js.
  • The note shows on the page as a folded yellow card ("Owen · 10/10").
    • Claude's reply goes under it once answered.
    • Its status: new, answered or done.
  • On the page, the docs runtime (spell-doc-runtime.js) draws:
    • a bubble on each section header, on hover
    • a "Note" pill in the page header
    • a count of new notes
    • edit and delete, on a note still new
  • Generated pages keep their notes when they're regenerated, or the bubble is off there: a judgement call, recorded.
    • They are the docs index, the epics list (index.html) and the guides list (index.html).
  • Claude finds and answers them with spell dev notes:

spell dev notes done <page> <id>

    • A bubble on each section's title: on hover, or always, with a count, once the section has notes.
    • A Note pill in the page header, for the whole page, with an "N new" link.
