Owen's five card asks, built as one change ("cards only when news", "under the paragraph", no quote, the summary in the header, the bullhorn's count and look). The calls made on the way:

  • READ means: the card unfolded, or the comment opened in the pane. An unfolded card stays until Owen leaves the page, so it doesn't vanish while he reads it; one opened in the pane goes at once. Kept per page in localStorage (spell-comment-read:<path>): each comment's latest news stamp, its last answer, else when it was taken. A newer answer brings the card back.
  • Cards start folded: unfolding is the "read" gesture.
  • The bullhorn, once its block has comments, ALWAYS opens a short list (each comment's first words and state, then New comment), even with one comment. Opening that one comment directly would leave no way to add a second comment on the same block.
  • The bullhorn's look: a pill "📢 2" (no longer a circle once it has a count), outlined while every comment waits, solid once Claude has one, an orange dot (orange: changed since you looked) while one has unread news. Its tooltip says how many have news.
  • Where a card goes: the smallest paragraph, list item, cell, pre, dt / dd, blockquote or h4-h6 holding the quote. Inside a list item or cell it goes at the end, so the list or table stays valid. Never inside an element whose shadow root would hide it: then under the block, as before.
  • The card no longer has Edit: only comments Claude has show a card, and those can't be edited.
  • No card while a comment just waits for Claude; a card only for news (taken, or answered), until read.
  • The card sits right under the paragraph holding the quote; its header is the pane's summary, with no quote block and no "Owen".
  • The bullhorn shows "📢 N", outlined or solid by state, with an orange dot for unread news; a click lists the block's comments and New comment.
