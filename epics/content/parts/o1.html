Everything people see as Spell's own samples: the built-in projects in the @spell space, in app's spaces/@spell/.

  • Examples (@spell/examples, 11): small apps -- Solitaire, Task List, Calculator, Tic-Tac-Toe, FizzBuzz ...; two have Spanish twins (Solitario, Lista de tareas).
  • Notebooks (@spell/notebooks, 93): one per Spell UI component, each a few pages (Usage, Examples, API, Theming).
  • Guides (@spell/guides, 12): "A first spell", "A tour of Spell", and a "how it works" walk through each example.
  • Library (@spell/library): people, the shared people library.
  • NOT here: the frozen test projects (@spell/test): they're the parser's tests, and change only with it.
