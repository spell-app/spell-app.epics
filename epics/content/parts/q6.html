Two ways to pick what to upgrade (Overview 1.4):

  • Now: each spell content update makes a phase "Content update 2026-10-29 14:05" whose plan holds a choices question: you tick features, Accept Phases, it's built.
  • Later phase here: the standing list (B), once epic-engine has the pieces.
  • One standing item, "Upgrades on offer", whose choices set GROWS: Claude adds each new feature as a tick box when it spots one.
  • You tick some and press Update now: the session makes the phase from your ticks and builds it; the ticked ones leave the list, the rest stay.
  • Content's side: about 1h more. Finding features and remembering picks are the same for both; B adds "add new features to the list" and "ticks become a phase".
  • epic-engine's side, on top of its P50 (ticking several, adding your own):
    • a set that stays open after you pick: your ticks applied, the rest left to pick later, new options appended over time
    • an "Update now" on it, which sends the ticks at once (or Send does, as today)
    Built: epic-engine's P50 · Choices Questions (on main, commit f3f554068) has standing sets that stay open after a pick, so nothing is sent to epic-engine.
  • Content's side is about 30 minutes now, so the standing list is built in P3 (J3), not a later phase.
  • P1 builds spell content update and the remembering file; P2 is the first update.
  • A later phase, "Standing Upgrade List", builds B, with a request to epic-engine for the standing set.
