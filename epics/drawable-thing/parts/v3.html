Play Drawable Solitaire by hand, in the editor app and in the <spell-app> web component, and check each card is its own element and the game still plays.

  1. In the editor app, open @examples/Drawable Solitaire and run it.
    • In devtools, every card is a <solitaire-card>, its drawing inside a shadow root.
    • Cards look like cards: white, bordered, red or black text.
    • A face-down card shows a striped back.
    • Clicking the stock deals a card to the discards.
    • Dragging or clicking cards plays them.
    • AutoPlay works.
  2. The same in the demo page, /demo/spell-app.html, with project="@examples/Drawable Solitaire".
  3. The original Solitaire still looks and plays as before.
