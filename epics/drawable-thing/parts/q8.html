A drawable thing's element gets its styles exactly the way Spell UI's own ui-* elements do (Owen, 2026-10-10).

  • "These elements are just like Spell UI's": they reach into the shared runtime for the theme and the base sheets.
  • So a <game-card> next to a <ui-button> follows the same theme, and changes with it.
  • On top of those, the type's own sheet, from a <css> block in its spell (P4).
  • No styles of its own invented by the bridge: nothing a ui-* element wouldn't have.
  • the bridge (P3) adopts the shared runtime's sheets in each element's shadow root, as Spell UI's elements do
  • a theme change restyles spell's elements with Spell UI's
  • P4 adds the type's own sheet after them
