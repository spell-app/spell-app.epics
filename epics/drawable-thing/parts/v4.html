The demo page shows Drawable Solitaire's cards and a pile, written by hand on a plain page, and two buttons that call a card's method and set its property.

  1. In app, build the element bundle: cd spell-app/app && yarn build:element.
  2. Start the page server: spell server.
  3. Open /demo/drawable-elements.html.

What you should see:

  • Three cards:
    • the ace of spades and the queen of hearts, face up
    • the 7 of clubs face down, striped green
  • A pile holding the king of diamonds.
  • The first button, ace.turnOver(), turns the ace face down, and back up on the next click.
  • The second, ace.rank = "jack", shows the jack of spades.
