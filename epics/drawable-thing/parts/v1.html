Check the editor side of P4 · CSS In Spell by hand, in the spell editor app.

  1. Open a project, and add this to a type's outline: 
    	<css>
		.red { colr: red }
	</css>
  2. The block's lines colour as CSS; the <css> lines as tags.
  3. Typing col inside the braces offers color.
  4. colr is underlined, "Unknown property".
  5. Hovering color shows its docs.
  6. The block folds.
