A card written by hand inside a pile written by hand shows in the pile, but the pile doesn't know it holds the card: its list is empty.

  • Each hand-written tag makes its own thing: the pile a new, empty pile; the card a new card.
  • The pile draws its outside around its <slot>, so the card's element shows inside it.
  • But anything the pile works out from its cards (its top card, its count, its state) sees none.
  • Making the page's markup the pile's members would be a later step: each child card added to the pile's list when it's mounted. 
<solitaire-pile>
  <solitaire-card rank="king" suit="diamonds" direction="up"></solitaire-card>
</solitaire-pile>    ... the king shows;  the pile's own list of cards is empty
  • P8's goal, "an ace shows on a pile", is met for what you see.
  • A pile on a plain page can't yet be filled from markup: a script adds cards to its thing.
