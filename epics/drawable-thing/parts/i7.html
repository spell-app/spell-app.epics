Spell code that writes a drawable list's element by hand, e.g. <test-project-pile><h3 in="header">...</h3></test-project-pile>, compiles and draws in javascript, but its TypeScript fails tsc.

  • The error: Property 'test-project-pile' does not exist on type 'JSX.IntrinsicElements'.
  • Why: the TypeScript the target writes declares no JSX tags for the program's own drawable types; Spell UI's <ui-*> tags are declared by the app's types.
  • It shows in the new fixture P15 added, CardTable: two lines in its tsc snapshot.
  • A fix: the TypeScript writer declares each drawable class's tag (its project's and its type's words) as an intrinsic element, typed by its declared properties.
