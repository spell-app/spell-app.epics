A drawable list's to draw must include <children/>, or its items are on the page but never show.

  • <children/> is spell's word for the web's <slot/> (P15).
    • Spell writes a real slot on the page.
    • The web's own word still works too.
  • The items are the element's children (J18), and a shadow root shows children only where it has a slot.
  • No error, no warning: the pile just looks empty.
  • A drawable list with no to draw at all is fine: the default draw is just the slot.
  • A named place, <children name="header"/>, doesn't show the items: they always go in the unnamed one. It shows only children written in="header". 
to draw a tableau:
	return <div className="pile tableau"/>            // its cards don't show

to draw a tableau:
	return <div className="pile tableau"><children/></div>   // they do
  • Writing a drawable list's frame: put <children/> where its items go.
  • A later warning for a frame without one could catch it; nothing does today.
