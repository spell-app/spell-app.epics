What can the code in a <spell> block see and change?

It's for P9 · Spell Inside Markup (O6).

 
<solitaire-stock-pile>
  <spell>
    when clicked
      - deal three cards to the discards
  </spell>
</solitaire-stock-pile>
  • when clicked is the stock pile's click handler.
  • "the discards" is a pile the PROGRAM made, not the stock pile's own.
  • So the block needs the stock pile ("it") and the program's things, both.
  • "it" and "its" are the tag's thing (the stock pile)
  • any other name is looked up in the program, as anywhere in it
  • methods and variables the block makes belong to that one element's thing
  • the block sees the stock pile alone; the program only through a name like "the app"
  • cost: Owen's example needs "the discards of the app" instead
  • the block is just more of the program; "it" means the tag's thing
  • cost: a method the block makes is everyone's, not that element's
  • a <spell> block compiles into the tag's thing's scope, inside the program's
  • its handlers (when clicked) listen on that element, through spell's events
