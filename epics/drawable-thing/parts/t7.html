Check, by hand, that a drawable element and its thing agree both ways (P13).

  1. In app, build the element bundle: yarn build:element.
  2. Open /demo/drawable-elements.html on the page server.
  3. Open the browser's inspector on the ace of spades.
  4. Press ace.turnOver(), then the setAttribute("rank", "2") button.
  • The ace's tag, shown live under the buttons and in the inspector, changes direction="up" to direction="down" as the card turns, and back on the next press.
  • The setAttribute button makes it the 2 of spades: the card redraws, and the tag says rank="2".
  • Nothing shows an error, and the page doesn't flicker or loop.
