A recompile carries over the program's THINGS only.

  • A top-level variable holding a plain value, e.g. the turn is 3, is whatever the new run's start set it to: a module's variables can't be set from outside it. A fix would have the compiler keep such values on a thing, or hand them over.
  • The Thing Explorer groups things under the heading they were made under: the cards carried over were made during the carry-over, so they show under none.
