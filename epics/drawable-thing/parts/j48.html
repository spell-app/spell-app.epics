A place in spell means the order the program made the things of that type, as Tableau#1 does (J42). I chose that over the order they're drawn in.

  • the first tableau compiles to spellCore.thingAtPlace(Tableau, 1).
  • the last tableau compiles to place -1, counting from the end.
  • Every ordinal word spell has works: "second" to "tenth", "penultimate" and "last".
  • Only tableaus count, not a sub-type's things: the first pile finds no tableau (T10).
  • A program has no page: what it draws, and where, can change; the order it made things in can't.
  • New meaning: the first card with no of after it was a parse error, and is now the first card made. the first card of the deck is still an item of the deck.
  • As built; the same as Tableau#1.
  • Needs the page: compiled spell would ask the runner, and a program with no page would have no places.
  • the first tableau is T1 in Drawable Solitaire, made first.
