What may the spell inside a layout's curly brackets hold: in the text, and in an action attribute? For P9 · Spell Inside Markup (O6). 

<ui-text>Score {score of the game}</ui-text>        <!-- a value, shown and kept up to date -->
<ui-button action={autoplay}>AutoPlay</ui-button>   <!-- something to do, on a press -->
  • A hole in text shows a value, and redraws when it changes.
  • An action runs something when the button is pressed.
  • a hole holds any expression: {the number of cards in the stock}
  • an action holds any statement: action={deal three cards to the discards}
  • cost: whole programs creep into attributes, hard to read and to report errors in
  • a hole holds a name or a property: {score of the game}
  • an action names a method: action={autoplay}
  • cost: "the number of cards in the stock" needs a property made for it first
  • holes read any spell expression, which can't change anything
  • an action names a method of the program, or of the tag's thing
  • longer actions go in a <spell> block, where there's room (Q12)
  • the layout's holes are parsed as spell expressions, read in the program's scope
  • an action names one method; anything longer is a <spell> block
