The Draw As Element phase (P5) planned to switch Solitaire and the cards library to drawable things. Built instead: a NEW example, Drawable Solitaire, beside the original, which stays as it was.

  • Why: Solitaire's look comes from the page's CSS, and a card's element cuts that off.
    • Its cards use Semantic UI's ui button classes, styled by the page.
    • Its styles.css reaches into each card from outside: .Pile.stacked .Card.
    • A drawable card draws in its own shadow root, which page CSS doesn't reach (C9).
    • So switching Solitaire would have broken a working example.
  • Drawable Solitaire is a copy of Solitaire, with these changes:
    • Its card is a card is a drawable thing:, with a <css> block: its own look.
    • That look: a white, bordered card; red or black text; a striped back when face down.
    • The ui button classes and the bicycle icon are gone from the card.
    • Its styles.css targets the card's tag instead: .Pile.stacked drawable-solitaire-card.
  • Open it as @examples/Drawable Solitaire; its compiled files are committed.
// Solitaire:  a thing, styled by the page
a card is a thing
// Drawable Solitaire:  its own element, its own styles
a card is a drawable thing:
	<css> ... </css>
  • Chosen and built.
  • The original keeps working, untouched.
  • The cost: two copies of the game to keep in step.
  • One example, as the plan said.
  • Its whole look moves into the card's <css>; no plain-thing Solitaire left to compare with.
  • Every project that imports the library gets drawable cards.
  • But the Solitaire that imports it (Solitaire-import) would lose its page-CSS look too.
  • Solitaire stays as it was.
  • The cards library is not drawable yet.
  • A new example, @examples/Drawable Solitaire, shows drawable cards.
  • P5's Files list is out of date: the library and Solitaire weren't switched.
