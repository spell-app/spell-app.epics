What is the element's tag called? A custom element's name needs a dash, and one name can be defined only once per page.

  • Not ui-: Spell UI's loader tries to fetch any unknown ui-* tag from its own catalog, and fails.
  • spell- is spell's own, as in <spell-app> and the spell editor's tag.
