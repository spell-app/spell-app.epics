The small rules of references, chosen so a written reference always finds the same thing again.

  • Tableau#3 is always the third tableau: a thing NAMED "3" can't be found by its name.
  • A thing whose name an earlier thing of its type has, or with no name, is written as its place: Hand#2.
  • A thing with no name, kept in a top-level variable, goes by that variable: Game#game (J23's "top-level fallback").
  • Two things with the same name: the first made wins (P19 warns where spell names one).
  • A thing that isn't the program's (a scratch list, a test's) has no reference, and its attribute is left off.
  • An id on a layout's tag now finds a sub-type's thing too, as Pile#stock does: <solitaire-pile id="stock"> is the stock pile.
    • With no id, a tag still takes its one thing of its EXACT type.
  • referenceFor() and thingForReference().
  • Tableau#3 would mean a tableau named "3" if there is one: two meanings for one text.
  • Every reference the page shows finds its thing again.
