Owen's Klondike sketch, laid out in plain HTML around Drawable Solitaire, plays in the browser.

  1. Build the element: cd packages/app && yarn build:element.
  2. Start the page server (/spell-serve), if it isn't running.
  3. Open /demo/klondike-layout.html on it.
  • The page lays out the board, not the game's own drawing:
    • a header row: the title, the score and the AutoPlay button
    • a row of piles: the discards, the stock, a gap, the four foundations
    • a row of the seven tableaus
  • Score shows 0, and rises as cards go up to the foundations.
  • AutoPlay plays every card it can.
  • Double-clicking the stock deals.
  • The line under the board, "N cards left in the stock", counts down as it deals.
  • Nothing shows ⚠, and the program's console has no layout errors.
