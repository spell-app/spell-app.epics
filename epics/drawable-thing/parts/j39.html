Chose: a {...} in a <markdown> block is always a hole, except in code.

  • Over: keeping any one that doesn't read as spell as plain text.
  • Why a choice: Q23 made holes live, and P14 also asked that braces reach the page as written.
  • Code keeps its braces: a fenced code block, or a `code` span, e.g. `{ color: red }`.
  • \{ is a real brace: it's kept as written, and markdown shows it as {.
  • A hole is read as a text fill-in is: {rank} means {its rank} in a card's drawing.
    • Otherwise it's spell exactly, as a layout's hole is: one that leaves out a needed "the" isn't read again with it in front (J26, B).
    • This call first said such a hole was tried again with "the" in front: the code has no such retry.
  • A hole that still doesn't read is a parse error, reported with its line, as a {...} in JSX is.
  • Built.
  • A typo in a hole is an error you see, not text that quietly shows on the page.
  • Braces in prose would just work.
  • But a mistyped hole would show as {the scroe of the game} on the page, with no error.
  • Score: **{the score of the game}** follows the score; \{braces\} show as braces.
