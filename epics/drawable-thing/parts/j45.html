A card's pile isn't declared: it's worked out from a card belongs to one pile (J23's "worked-out properties" cost). It's shown, and setting it MOVES the card, as move the card to ... does. I chose that over showing it only.

  • Core finds it from the program's classes: an exclusive list (Pile.exclusive) whose items are cards, and the card's getter pile. See elementApiOf().
  • card.setAttribute("pile", "Tableau#T1"), or card.pile = tableau, asks spellCore.move(): the tableau's can take rule decides.
  • A move the piles refuse: the card stays, and the attribute shows its pile again.
  • A reference that finds nothing (yet): nothing changes, and the attribute stays as written.
  • Removing the attribute leaves the card where it is: taking a card out of every pile is a script's job.
  • A property DECLARED as a thing (a chip has a home as a stack) is simply set to the thing found; no attribute sets it to nothing.
  • Reading card.pile on the element gives the reference text, "Tableau#T1".
  • As built.
  • The attribute is written, but changing it does nothing.
  • Every Drawable Solitaire card shows its pile, e.g. pile="Tableau#T3", and follows it as cards move.
