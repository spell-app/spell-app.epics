An HTML formatter folds a <spell> block's lines into one, and spell needs its lines and indents.

  • oxfmt and Prettier both see the block as ordinary text, and reflow it.
  • A <!-- prettier-ignore --> comment just above the block's element keeps it as written; the demo page does this. 
<solitaire-stock-pile>
  <spell>
    when double-clicked
      play from the stock pile
  </spell>
</solitaire-stock-pile>

... after formatting, one line:  no handler reads
<spell> when double-clicked play from the stock pile </spell>
  • A layout with a <spell> block puts <!-- prettier-ignore --> above its element.
