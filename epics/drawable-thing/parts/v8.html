Check, by hand, that a recompile carries the program's things over (P16). Only the runner tests have played it so far.

  1. In the editor app, open @examples/Drawable Solitaire and run it.
  2. Play a few moves: deal from the stock, move a card or two.
  3. Edit a card's <css>, e.g. the face-down stripes' colour, or its to draw.
  4. Then press Restart.
  • After the edit: every card stays in its pile, face up or down as it was, drawn the new way. No new deal.
  • The console says what was kept: Kept allPiles, deck, ... from the last run: Restart starts afresh.
  • The Thing Explorer shows 52 cards, not 104.
  • Restart deals a new game.
  • Known (J36): whatever the start does before its first pause, e.g. its tests' output, prints again on each edit.
