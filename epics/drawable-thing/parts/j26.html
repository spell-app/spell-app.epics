A curly-bracket hole that doesn't read as written is read a second time with the in front, so Owen's Klondike sketch works as he wrote it.

  • Why: the sketch writes {score of the game}, but spell reads only the score of the game.
  • Only holes get the second try: they're values. Actions and <spell> blocks are statements, and don't.
  • A hole must still use ALL its text, either way.
    • {no such thing at all} isn't read as just its first word, no.
    • It shows ⚠, and the program's console says why.
  • Where: LayoutSpell.compile(). 
Score {score of the game}        ... doesn't read;  read again as {the score of the game}:  "Score 3"
Score {the score of the game}    ... reads as written:  "Score 3"
{no such thing at all}           ... reads neither way:  "⚠", and a line on the program's console
