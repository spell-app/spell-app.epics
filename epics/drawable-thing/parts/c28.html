P18 renamed Drawable Solitaire's tags and added thing references; the durable doc, Drawable Things, is shared content this phase didn't edit.

  • Its examples' tags lose the word drawable:
    • <drawable-solitaire-card> is now <solitaire-card>
    • <drawable-solitaire-tableau> is now <solitaire-tableau>
  • A section on references:
    • by name or place: Tableau#T1, Tableau#1
    • a sub-type's thing: Pile#T1
    • in attributes: thing="Pile#stock", pile="Tableau#T1"
