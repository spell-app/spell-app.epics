A future epic: these are notes from epic (2026-11-05), not a plan. Read from the services' help pages; nothing here has been tried.

  1. Every domain at Squarespace. spell.app and spell.social are registered there, and their DNS is there too.
    • A record goes in at account.squarespace.com/domains › the domain › DNS › DNS Settings › Custom Records › Add record: Name is only the part before the domain (@ for the domain itself), MX has a Priority, TXT values go in without quotation marks.
    • Before adding: delete any old MX record and any Google Workspace or email preset (presets can't be edited, only deleted); leave the website's "Squarespace Defaults" alone.
    • Squarespace has a one-click Fastmail preset: the domain › DNS › Add preset (top right) › Fastmail.
  2. Owen's mail off IONOS, into one service. Fastmail fits (one account holds every domain, about $5-6 a person a month; check its pricing page).
    • Old mail: Fastmail's Settings › Migration › Import › Other (IMAP), from imap.ionos.com, port 993. It copies every folder once, and can keep fetching new mail until the switch.
    • Aliases and forwards at IONOS: list them first, then make each one in Fastmail (Settings › Aliases).
    • The order matters: Fastmail set up and the MX records pointed at it BEFORE a domain leaves IONOS, so no mail is lost while the domain moves.
  3. The IONOS domains moved to Squarespace.
    • At IONOS: Domains & SSL › the domain's gear › Renewal & Transfer: turn off the transfer lock (and private registration), then Show Authorization Code; the code also goes to the domain's contact address, so check that address first.
    • At Squarespace: transfer the domain in with that code. A domain must be 60 days old; most take about 5 days.
    • Then cancel the IONOS plan, once nothing is left on it.
  4. Spell's sign-in mail, from epic atmosphere's Real Sign-In Mail phase: Postmark sends from sign-in@spell.app. Each domain gets:
    • Postmark's signature (a TXT named like 20261105pm._domainkey) and a pm-bounces CNAME to pm.mtasv.net
    • ONE SPF record, v=spf1 include:spf.messagingengine.com ?all: Postmark needs no include, its bounces carry their own
    • ONE DMARC record, _dmarc: v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:owen@spell.app, then p=quarantine after a few clean weeks
  5. A public spell.app on a rented host. Owen said "Railyard"; Railway (railway.com) is a guess at what he meant. It replaces atmosphere's Q8 B, a machine Owen already runs.
    • A volume for the server's disk: the save queue (atmosphere Q19) and the spaces' data live there, and a deploy must never wipe them. Railway's Hobby plan: $5 a month with $5 of use included, volumes 5 GB to start.
    • spell.app pointed at it with a CNAME (and Railway's check record) at Squarespace; Railway issues the certificate. A bare domain like spell.app can't take a CNAME, so it needs Squarespace's forwarding to www or Railway's own answer for the bare domain: an open question.
    • The Spaces test host (atmosphere Q17's option D) could run there too, beside Spell's server, instead of on Owen's machine.

Open choices for its planning: Fastmail or another mail service; Railway or another host (Fly, Render); whether spell.app is the bare domain or www; backups of the volume.
