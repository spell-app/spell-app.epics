Every ui-accordion title gets an expand arrow, even when its panel is empty. You'd notice it in a contents sidebar: a section with no sub-sections offers to open into nothing.

  • Cosmetic: nothing breaks.
  • Recorded as finding 17 in spell-ui-findings.md, with the repro: <ui-accordion><ui-title>Leaf</ui-title><ui-content></ui-content></ui-accordion>
  • No workaround yet; the old static sidebars had the same arrow.
  • Suggested fix there: hide the arrow and skip toggling when the content is empty, or a leaf attribute on ui-title.
  • once fixed in @spell-app/ui: a title with an empty panel shows no arrow
