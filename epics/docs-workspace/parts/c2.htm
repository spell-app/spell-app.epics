Claude Code's own EnterWorktree made the new worktree's branch from origin/main, not your local main. Anything you'd committed but not pushed was missing from the plan's worktree, and you'd notice when a fix you'd just made wasn't there.

  • Looks done or obsolete: the WorktreeCreate hook, .claude/hooks/worktree.mjs, now makes every worktree from local main ("unpushed work included, not origin/main", its header says) (checked 2026-10-04).

Net effect:

  • a new epic's worktree starts from what's on your local main, pushed or not
