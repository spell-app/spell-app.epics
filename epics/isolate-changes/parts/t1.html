Checks that /isolate moves you into the new window at once.

in a package window, type /isolate tmp-x.
  1. The tab is renamed tmp-x at once.
  2. The turn ends within seconds.
  3. A new, tinted window opens, with the session in a tab and continue typed in.
  4. The old tab closes: the log, ~/.spell/windows/handoffs/<id>.log, doesn't say "left its old tab open".
  5. Press Enter on continue: it runs yarn install, then says "ready in tmp-x".
