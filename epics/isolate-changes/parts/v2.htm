Checks that you can't start a worktree from inside another one, and that your text isn't lost.

Step 1: in a new session in tmp-x's window (from the test above, V1), type /isolate tmp-y do a thing.

What should happen:

  • It's blocked, with "You're in worktree tmp-x".
  • Your text is quoted back, and saved in ~/.spell/prompts/tmp-y.md.

Step 2: from a package window, type /isolate tmp-y.

What should happen:

  • After the move, it carries on with "do a thing".
  • It deletes ~/.spell/prompts/tmp-y.md.
