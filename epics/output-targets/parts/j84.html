  • JSWriter now holds what both writers say the same way: kindOf() and the value-kind helpers (givenKind(), definitionsKind(), propertyKind() ...), coreCall() / listCall() / typeTest() / eventCall(), comparison(), templateText(), loop(), nameOf(), module()'s imports, forProject().
  • TSWriter keeps only what's TypeScript's: types, interfaces and declare module, decorators, JSX and Solid's shapes, on<{ card: Card }>(...)'s type argument, ! where ?. can't go, and its tidier parentheses. 1970 lines down to about 1380.
  • TSProject is renamed WriterProject (file too): both writers read it now. TSGivenValue / TSWhere became GivenValue / ProgramPlace; TSList and TSUndeclared keep their names, as they're TypeScript's.
