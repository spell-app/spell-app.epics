In the app's editor (yarn start in packages/app), open Solitaire. The right column's third pane, where the Match inspector was, is now "Spell Tree".

  • Click on a line: the pane draws that line's tree
  • Move with the arrow keys: it follows
  • A blank line or a comment line: the hint "Put the cursor on a line to see its spell tree."
  • The Match inspector, and its "show rule names" eye toggle, are gone
