Solid's error net, <Errored>, does more work than it needs to in Solid 2 rc.13, so drawThing() doesn't rely on it alone. A reader of packages/core/src/drawing.ts will see two extra layers, and should know why they're there.

  • What <Errored> does in rc.13, measured while building P10:
    • it re-reads its children whenever ANYTHING under it changes: a pile with one broken card drew three times for that one card
    • it makes its stand-in again each time: a card added to the pile made a new stand-in for the broken one
  • What drawThing() does about it:
    • draw() runs in a memo inside the net, so it runs again only when what IT read changes, never because the net re-read its children
    • a try / catch around draw() makes the stand-in once, as the memo's value
  • What still reaches <Errored>: an error a live value throws LATER (() => this.x throwing after the first draw). That one may still redraw more than its own node.
  • Worth trying again on each Solid release candidate bump: if <Errored> stops re-reading, the try / catch can go.
  • one broken card shows ONE stand-in, made once, and doesn't redraw its pile
  • two layers in drawThing() where one net should do, until Solid's net settles
