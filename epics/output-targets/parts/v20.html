Run Solitaire in the editor, open the Thing Explorer, and pick a card. Press ▶ on its turn over action.

  • A card's computed properties show by their camelCase names: isFaceUp, shortRank ...
  • Its stored ones keep spell's names: rank, is_set_up.
  • Its actions read "turn over", "move to pile": no "(pile)" slot any more (J87).
  • ▶ on turn over still turns the card.
