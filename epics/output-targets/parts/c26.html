A spell object's JSON writes each thing out wherever it's held, so fromJSON() makes a separate copy for each place.

  • A board holding its piles in all_piles AND in stock reads back with two stock piles, each owning its own cards.
  • A thing that holds itself, directly or not, can't be written at all: JSON.stringify() throws on the cycle.
  • Fine for a debug view, or saving one list; saving a whole game would need ids and references (JSON-LD's "@id"), a later step.
  • Read a board back from the JSON of its parts that hold each card ONCE, e.g. its list of piles, not the board with every pile named twice.
