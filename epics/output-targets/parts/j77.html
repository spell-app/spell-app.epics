The methods module's shared file holds only TYPES, the ones two or more of its rule files read:

  • the signature data: MethodSignatureData (built by method_signature, read by MethodDefinition and quoted_type_expression), MethodArgData (every method_arg rule and method_keyword), and the two types inside them, MethodTypeInfo, MethodExtraVar
  • the call-site rules': MethodRuleDeclared (all three), MethodOperatorRuleProps and OperatorOperands (postfix and infix)

They were private to methods.ts; now exported from the folder, so the rule files can share them.

  • OperatorOperands is still a COPY of expressions' private type of the same name (its docstring says so). Left as is, over importing expressions' one: that would mean exporting it from a module another agent is splitting at the same time. Worth folding into one once both are split.
  • Single-use helpers stayed with their rule, e.g. DANGLING_WORDS and isTopLevel() in MethodDefinition.ts.
