Fixing the six spell-ui/* lint errors in ui-form, two uses stay hand-written. Each has a disable comment, and an entry in the lint patterns test's allow-list.

  1. FormBinding keeps its createEffect.
    • It is a helper class, not a component, so no @E.onChange can reach it.
    • It makes one effect per bound control, each in a root of its own that is disposed when the control goes.
    • That's a per-item effect, which the Solid authoring rules leave explicit.
    • Same reason as SlottedItems.
  2. UIRepeat's template watch is @E.whileConnected, plus its own MutationObserver, not @E.watches.
    • It watches only while connected, as before, and as UIForm and UIRoot do.
    • @E.watches would watch for the element's whole life.
    • And a watched method is not called at the start, so it would need a second hook to take the template on connect.

The other three errors were fixed as the rules say:

  • queueMicrotask() became E.afterSolidUpdate(): the same microtask.
  • FormBinding's two untrack() calls became @E.untracked on writeBack(). That decorator works on any class.
