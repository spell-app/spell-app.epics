  • A callback's parameter: TypeScript types it from the collection, since core's helpers take a CollectionOf<T> (J14, T7).
    • For example in spellCore.map(list, (x) => ...), filter(), forEachSequential().
    • 27 places:
      • 13 counting loops: for each number from 1 to 7, as getRange() is number[]
      • 14 loops over a plain array: for each rank in card ranks
  • A local set from getItemOf() or randomItemOf() that spell can't type: TypeScript gives Card | undefined, from the list's own type.
  • The marker stays where TypeScript can't help: a method's own parameter, a property.
