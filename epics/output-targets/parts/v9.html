In the editor (spell dev server, then the app), run Solitaire. Flip a card, move a few cards between piles, then press Restart. Keep DevTools' Elements panel open while you play.

  • It looks and plays exactly as before P10.
  • Only the cards that change flash in the Elements panel: flipping a card redraws that card, moving one touches only the two piles; the rest of the board doesn't flash.
  • Restart deals a fresh board, with no errors on the program's console.
