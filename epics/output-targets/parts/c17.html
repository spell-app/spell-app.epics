The form's rows of fields drop their bottom margin by :host(:last-child) (Fomantic's .field:last-child): a wrapper per row would make every row's last field :last-child, packing the rows tight. Instead each row is its copied nodes straight in the repeat, its top-level elements registered as holding the item (FormBinding.hold()), and the template is kept as a <template> LAST child, so no row is ever :last-child. Moves use moveBefore() where the browser has it (Chromium does), so a moved row keeps focus; elsewhere a moved row's focus is lost.
