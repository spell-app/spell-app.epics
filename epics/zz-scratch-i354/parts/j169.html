With a card folded (Code, say), the bar above it and the bar below it both resize the open cards on either side of it, across the folded one, which stays its header's height. A bar with no open card on one side (every card above it folded) doesn't move.

No card can be dragged smaller than its header (40px). A card dragged under its header plus a little of its body still folds when you let go, as before.

In Spell UI: any <ui-split-panel> pane's own min/max height or width now bound a drag, and a pane whose min and max are the same is skipped. min-size in px now holds for every pane, not only fixed ones.
