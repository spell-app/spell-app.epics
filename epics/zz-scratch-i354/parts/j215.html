The debugging build makes every action (to ...) and handler (on click, when a card is dropped, after moving ...) able to wait, so each of their lines can stop.

  • A getter (its "shorthand" is ...), a drawing, a question (it can take a card if ...), when creating and a test answer at once, as they must: the page and the core use their answer right there. Their lines never stop, and Step Into passes over them.
  • Why: making them wait too would change what the program does, not just how fast (a drawing would show half-done, core.canTake() would get a promise instead of yes or no).
  • Other option: a dot on such a line could be drawn as one that won't stop.
