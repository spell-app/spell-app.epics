Today every key is fixed, written in the editor's code. The menus' keys are also written a second time, in the editor's own Spell file, and a test checks the two agree.

  • Keeps this phase small (3-5 hours).
  • Click a row's keys, press the new ones; a key another row has already is refused, saying which.
  • Kept per person, on this device; Reset puts the editor's own keys back.
  • The menus, the code editor and the tooltips all follow your keys.
