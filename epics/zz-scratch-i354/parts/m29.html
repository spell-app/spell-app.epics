Open Solitaire in the editor, show Pile.spell, and click in the code's left margin beside cancel if the card's pile cannot give it up. Then drag a face-up card onto a column. While it's paused, press F10 twice, F11 on a line that runs another action, then ⇧F11 and F5. Scroll and open other files while it's paused. Last, click the square (Stop Debugging).

A red dot shows, and a bar above the code says it's debugging. The drag pauses the game on that line, lit, with the card (The thing) and the pile (What it was given) beside the code. Each F10 moves the light a line; F11 opens the other action on its first line, in its own file; ⇧F11 and F5 finish the move, and the game plays on. The editor answers while paused. Stop Debugging takes the bar away, the game as it was, the dots kept.
