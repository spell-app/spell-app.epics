Five examples ship with the editor today: Solitaire, Solitario (in Spanish), Calculator, FizzBuzz and "Todos - Form Based". They live in spell-app's spaces/@spell/examples folder.

  • Todos - Form Based becomes Todo List: its title is one line in its project file. The "live data" box is the form's debug attribute, which prints the whole list as JSON under the form; dropping it hides the box. It also has a grey "Test:" menu and a print the app line to tidy away.
  • Calculator opens with an "IDEAS FOR EXTENDING THIS" comment, has a TODO, and draws with the old Semantic UI class names rather than Spell UI.
  • Errors isn't an example: it's a seven-line broken project in YOUR Projects folder (on your disk, not in git). It's moved off your disk, set aside rather than erased (Q4).
  • Four new examples (Q3): Tic-Tac-Toe, Bill Splitter, Flash Cards and Habit Tracker.
