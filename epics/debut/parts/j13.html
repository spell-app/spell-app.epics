Owen asked for <ui-toolbar> slots icon, title, center and right, and the docs' top bar now uses them: the menu button, the brand, the site's links, then search and the theme picker at the right edge at every width. The brand is a plain link styled as a heading: a <ui-header> inside a toolbar draws as a menu's header (small, sitting high). A slot with nothing in it draws no box, and a toolbar with no items of its own gives its menu box no room.
