Where do the public pages (front page, guides) live?

  • They have live editors in them, so they need the editor's bundle, and they ship with the public build.
  • A site folder in spell-app's app, built into the same public build.
  • The page server shows them locally too, under the App tab.
  • Beside the guides, edited from any checkout, never conflicting.
  • But the public build must then copy them out of a folder no repo tracks.
  • The pages are tracked in spell-app, reviewed in its diffs, and published with the editor.
