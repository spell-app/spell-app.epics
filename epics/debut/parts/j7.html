Owen, 2026-10-23: 'Sidebar -- click on the page should make the sidebar hide.' So <ui-flyout modeless> now light-dismisses: a click on the page lands where it went AND closes the flyout; a click inside it, or on its opener, doesn't. This replaces J6's 'Escape only'. closedby="closerequest" still keeps it open on a page click. It's done by Spell UI's own outside-click handling, never the browser's (that needs a backdrop). Open 220ms easing out, close 160ms easing in (--ui-flyout-duration, new --ui-flyout-close-duration); 500ms before.
