Owen asked for Apple's sliding selection on the segmented tab bars. The thumb is ONE box under the items (the track's ::before), placed from the chosen item's measured box and slid with a transition (SegmentedThumb, used by <ui-menu> and <ui-tabs>).

  • Not anchor positioning: a CSS transition doesn't run when the anchor moves (the value anchor(left) never changes), and a menu's items draw their boxes in their own shadow roots.
  • The chosen item's own fill fades out once the thumb is drawn; the first placement doesn't slide, only later choices; no motion with reduced motion.
  • every segmented menu and tab bar slides, in every theme; --ui-menu-thumb-duration (200ms) tunes it
