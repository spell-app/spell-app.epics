Spell now sets --ui-bar-background (its page, --ui-background) and --ui-bar-color (its text colour).

  • Without them, an epic page's toolbar fell back to the ivory (--ui-brown-background), beige bands on Spell's cool page.
  • The ink too: the toolbar mixes its hover and selected fills from it, and would still have mixed them from the brown text.
  • Spell UI's pages already fell back to the page colour, so they look the same; the editor's Spell bar draws no box.
