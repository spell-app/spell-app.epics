How Spell writes clicks, drags and drops, in the outline (.spell) and in tags (.spellml): Owen settled it on the details page by typing over its examples, 2026-10-29, in two rounds: the second (sent 22:51) closed the one-tag-per-ability question and made every form below work for every event, condition and while. It builds on Q42 (when for conditions), Q43 (an on drag runs as the drag starts) and Q44 (an event implies its ability).

  1. Every form, for everything: every event, every condition and every while, in the outline and in tags, takes all of the forms below. Owen, round 2: "on/when, optional colon, optional (a)...(is), single tag and nested form for all -- this and other sections".
    • on or when to start it (rule 10)
    • an optional colon (rule 9)
    • optional small words (rule 4)
    • in tags, both the one-line self-closing tag and code nested between tags (rule 7)
  2. Short forms beside the long ones: "it is" and the colon may be left out.
    • - clickable when it is face down = - it is clickable when: it is face down
    • - its cards are draggable when the card is face up (a pile's) = - it can give up a card when: the card is face up
  3. A one-line event needs no colon: - on click play it.
  4. Optional words: articles and "is" may be left out anywhere. The docs show them in brackets: - while (a) card (is) dragged over:.
    • The small words: (a), (is), (the), (its), (it), (of it).
    • In tags too: <when (its) score changes>, <when (a) card (is) dropped>, <while (a) card (is) dragged over(:) ... />.
  5. Shorter event names, "on it" understood:
    • on card dropped (was "a card dropped on it")
    • on card dragged out (was "a card dragged out of it")
    • on a card dragged over
  6. Tags take bare words, no quotes: a condition or an event name is plain words inside the tag.
    • <clickable when it is face down />
    • <droppable when the card is one lower ... />
    • <cards draggable when the card is face up />
    • <when its score changes>...</when>
  7. A one-line event in a tag closes itself: <on click play it />. Longer code goes between tags.
    • Both forms, for every event, condition and while (round 2, rule 1): <on click(:) play it/> or <on click>play it</>; <while (a) card (is) dragged over(:) ... /> or <while card dragged over>...</while>.
    • Code on new lines inside a self-closing tag is NOT allowed (one of Owen's boxes tried it, for on drag): it goes between an opening and a closing tag instead.
  8. Three closing tags, all the same: </>, </on> and </on click>.
    • </> is the one to prefer; the explicit forms where they help a reader find the start.
  9. The colon is optional, in the outline and in a tag: <while card dragged over: ... /> or the same without it.
    • In a tag it may follow the event or the condition: <clickable when(:) it is face down/>, <on click(:) play it/>, <draggable when(:) it is face up/>.
  10. on and when mean the same for an event, since English reads better one way or the other: - on card dropped: = - when a card is dropped on it:.
    • An event's on / when STARTS the line (or the tag); a condition's when FOLLOWS an ability or a list's rule (clickable when ...). Position tells them apart.
    • This answers the details page's second question (on or when for a pile's drops): both.
    • A when with a passive verb is an event too, about the thing itself: - when clicked play it, - when double clicked play it, - when dragged ..., <when dragged>...</>, <when double clicked(:) play it/>; and - when (the) drag is cancelled ... (round 2).
  11. A new event, drag cancelled: - on drag cancelled: (or when drag is cancelled) runs on the dragged thing when its drag ends with no drop taken.
    • Dropped where nothing takes it, or Escape pressed.
    • The thing goes back where it came from, as today; the event runs then.
  12. its cards are draggable when is the pile's say over which of its cards may be picked up at all (today's can give up). It is not "drag cancelled".
    • It pairs with droppable when on the same pile, the short form of it can take a card when, as it is of it can give up a card when.
    • A card no longer says - it is draggable when: its pile lets it go: the pile's rule makes its cards draggable. A card can still say draggable when ... for a rule of its own.
    • Changed 2026-10-29, in the modal: it was lets go when, a verb among adjectives (clickable, draggable, droppable, selectable). Owen: "I'm concerned that lets go when doesn't match anything else in the semantics".
  13. One tag per ability, standing alone: an ability's tag never holds its events. Owen picked A on the details page's first question, round 2: "Each stands alone".
    • <draggable when it is face up/> beside <on drag ... />, two tags side by side, never <draggable><on drag>...</></draggable>.
  14. The attribute on a view's tag stays, beside the ability tags. Owen, round 2: "Make sure we keep the <spell view "card" draggable> etc form as well".
    • A bare ability word on the view's own tag (draggable, clickable, selectable, droppable) says the same as its tag, <draggable/>.
    • The outline's - it is draggable stays too, and - it can be dragged, which the runtime reads today, still works.

The examples below now show round 2's forms too: the passive when clicked / when dragged, the small words in brackets, the colon as (:) where it's optional in a tag, both tag forms for one event, and the view's draggable attribute beside the ability tags. 

a card:
	- clickable when it is face down
	- on click play it
	- when double clicked play it
	- when dragged set the hint of the solitaire to ""
	- when (the) drag is cancelled show "Not there"
a pile:
	- its cards are draggable when the card is face up
	- droppable when the card is one lower ...
	- while (a) card (is) dragged over (it): set the hint of the solitaire to ...
	- on card dropped: put the card on it
	- when a card is dragged out of it:
		...
the game:
	- when (its) score changes: if its score is 520 show "You won!"
 
<spell-view "card" template="two sided view" draggable>
<clickable when(:) it is face down/>
<on click(:) play it/>
<when double clicked(:) play it/>
<draggable when(:) it is face up/>
<when dragged>
	set the hint of the solitaire to ""
</>
<on drag cancelled(:) show "Not there"/>

<cards draggable when(:) the card is face up/>
<droppable when(:) the card is one lower and the other colour/>
<while (a) card (is) dragged over(:) set the hint of the solitaire to "Drop it here"/>
<on card dropped(:) put the card on it/>
<when (a) card (is) dropped>put the card on it</>
<on card dragged out>...</on card dragged out>

<when (its) score changes>
	if its score is 520 show "You won!"
</>
