  • The two looks not picked can still ship as themes people choose: P2 makes the editor themeable either way.
  • Lavender canvas, white cards, Palatino headings with an italic lede, pill buttons, the hat mark; aubergine in dark.
  • Boards: Main (editor) and BrandHome.
  • Dark all through, built on Shades of Purple: the code, panels and menus share one deep purple; a pale lilac light variant.
  • Boards: StudioEditor and StudioHome.
  • The fruity macOS look: frosted panels over a soft wallpaper, the system font; smoky glass in dark.
  • Boards: GlassEditor and GlassHome.
