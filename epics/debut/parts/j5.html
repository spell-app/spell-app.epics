Your note on V1 asked to merge the day / night button into the theme picker. The picker is now one palette button. Its panel opens with a three-way control, Light | Dark | Auto, then the theme list. Auto follows the device, as the old "Match system" switch did.

  • On top so it's always in sight: the theme list scrolls.
  • Keyboard: focus lands on the chosen theme as it opens, as before; Shift+Tab reaches the scheme control, and arrows move and choose.
  • The sun / moon button alone (show="scheme") stays: only its own example uses it, but it costs nothing to keep.
  • Light keeps the outline sun icon: at this size it can still read as a gear, but the word Light sits beside it.
  • One round button in every docs page header, not two
  • the button's tooltip: "Theme and light / dark: Spell, Auto"
