You can see the running app, but can't look inside one of its parts, or change it, without editing the code.

  • Click any part of the running app and see what it is, in a tall Inspector column at the far right.
  • Change a property there, and the app changes at once.
  • The Inspector looks like the Theme Creator's, in Spell Brand's theme, in light and dark.

Owen, 2026-10-24: "yes on editor phase. The editor goes in a tall column to the far right. Use the styling/sizing from Theme Creator, but in the brand's theme".

  • The column
    • A tall Inspector column at the editor page's far right, beside the app, full height.
    • An icon in the app's header shows or hides it, and the editor remembers which.
  • The look: the Theme Creator mockup's inspector, its styling and sizes.
    • A 380px column, with 16px gaps.
    • Nested panels that fold, each with an info tip.
    • Fields with a label and a description.
    • Segmented menus, tinted to their panel.
    • All of it in the Spell Brand theme, whatever theme the rest of the editor wears: a nested <ui-root theme="spell-brand">.
  • Picking
    • While the Inspector is open, clicking a part of the running app selects it, outlined.
    • Hovering shows the outline first.
    • Esc stops picking.
  • What it shows: a <ui-property-editor> for what's picked.
    • A drawn Spell thing (a drawable thing's element, a card say): its type's properties, from the running program's class, bound to the thing (PropertyEditorFiles.schemaForType()).
    • A Spell UI element (<ui-input>, <ui-button> ...): its tag's attributes, from its vocabulary.
    • Its header says what it is: "Card · the queen of spades", or "ui-input".
  • Edits change the running app at once.
    • Writing them back into the code is a later phase, a todo for now.
