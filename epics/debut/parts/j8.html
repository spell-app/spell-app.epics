Your redirect: the toolbar's letter marks become ACCESS LETTERS. Each item gets one, unique on the page: its title's first letter, else the next free letter in its title (Types t, Groups g, Content c, States s, Variations v, Group Variations r). The letter is drawn bold and underlined in the title; the separate mark shows only when the titles don't fit (icons only). Pressing the letter jumps there as a click does, except with a modifier, while typing, with a dialog open, or while a control that takes keys (a menu, a dropdown, a live example) has the focus. Epic pages' icon-only toolbars have no titles, so no letters.
