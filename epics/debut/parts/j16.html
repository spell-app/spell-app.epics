  • The panel is one box drawn behind the toolbar, reaching up behind the tabs: 50% white glass, blurred. While it's stuck, a strip in the stuck titles' colour (90%) fills round it, out to the column's edges and over the gaps, so nothing scrolling under shows. In place there's no strip, so the panel reads as glass on the page.
  • Section titles: 50% white in place, 90% stuck; the fill is now set as a colour in both states from the site's sheet, so the change eases (measured 0.5, 0.73, 0.9 over 200ms). A section's first nested title sits 0.5em below its own.
  • A click on a section button glides: the browser's own jump to the anchor is stopped and the site lands with a smooth scroll (a jump under reduced motion). A page arriving at an address still jumps.
