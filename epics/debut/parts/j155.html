The theme editor's previews and the brand index's thumbnails frame pages on their own origin and dress them themselves. Following the space there would put the person's theme back over the theme being previewed, so a same-origin frame keeps its choice in the browser (ThemePreference.keptIn). VS Code's side bar frames are cross-origin, and follow it. And the theme editor no longer writes a previewed frame's theme key (PreviewPage.themeKey is gone): with one key for every place, it would have changed the person's own browser copy.
