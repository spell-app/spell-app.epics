J66's corner maths, in UIButton.css:

  • The track's inner radius is its corner, less its border and inset; the buttons' corners take it.
  • Two new tokens, as the segmented control's: --ui-button-group-padding (the inset, 0.125em) and --ui-button-group-item-radius (where buttons meet; by default the inner radius, so every tile is rounded all round).
  • The buttons give up the inset from their own padding: a group is still exactly as tall as a lone button (40px).
  • In a plain grey group, a hovered tile sits on the track's grey, so it reads a little darker than a lone button's hover.
  • A basic group's dividers are straight lines on each button's edge, not a shadow that would bend round a rounded tile.
  • An inverted group draws one outline, on the track, instead of one per button.
