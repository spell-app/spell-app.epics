For P17-P20: which word for which idea? The theme work uses four words, two of them for more than one thing.

  • theme: a whole look (Spell, Fruity ...). But the kit's site header calls its light / dark button "the theme" (flipTheme(), its tooltip), right where P17 puts the theme menu.
  • scheme: light / dark, everywhere else (ColorScheme, the menu's flipScheme()).
  • look: three things: the editor's chrome per theme (<html data-spell-look="glass">), a theme and a scheme together (the docs' remembered look), the facelift boards. After P17 every theme is a look under its own name.
  • accent: Spell UI's accent colour (--ui-accent, Spell's ivory, which P18 keeps), and in the plans the primary colour ("a tint REPLACES the theme's accent: its primary colour").
  • The coherence review found them; the word list (agents/coherence/words.md) settles none yet.

  • look retired: the editor marks the theme by its name (data-spell-theme); the remembered pair is a theme and a scheme.
  • primary for what a tint replaces; accent only for Spell UI's accent colour. P20 named "Tints".
