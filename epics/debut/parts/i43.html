Finding 5's guide toolbar (Glass / Fruity: the chosen key 10px, round on the left only, in a 9px track; the track full width) is spell-epic's, not a theme's.

  • toolbarMenu() (spell-doc-toolbar.js) gives the titled toolbar secondary: true; an old name does nothing on a page since ui-gaps P13, so it draws as a plain bordered menu, its first item the menu's left corners.
  • Fix there: appearance: "secondary", and flex: 0 1 auto beside the track's width on ::part(menu) (ui-toolbar's own sheet lets its menu grow).
  • Spell UI's side is in: Glass's keys round to the track less its padding (7px), Fruity's track and keys are pills, a segmented ui-toolbar's track hugs its items.
