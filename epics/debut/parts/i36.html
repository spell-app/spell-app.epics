Button's Inverted example, in dark: the segment is rgb(34, 23, 80), the page rgb(26, 16, 64).

  • Where it comes from: the theme's inverted segment, not the page. An inverted segment fills with --ui-segment-inverted-background, which defaults to the theme's surface; the Spell theme's dark surface is violet 925, the page's background darker still.
  • So in dark, every inverted segment on any page reads as a raised card, not as a darker band.
  • Not changed here: a theme call (its own default, or the Spell theme setting the token).
