Owen suggested <ui-toolbar>, a subclass of <ui-menu>. It takes every menu attribute; its items never wrap, its box scrolls sideways with the scrollbar hidden, and a ‹ / › button shows at each end while there's more that way.

  • Each click scrolls 80% of the width, smooth (instant with reduced motion).
  • The buttons are out of the Tab order: every item is keyboard-reachable, and focusing one scrolls it in.
  • A button at its end keeps its room (hidden), so items don't jump; when everything fits, neither shows.
  • Left-to-right only for now.
  • Not yet used by the docs site's tab bar or section toolbar: that's the next step.
  • a new family, spell-ui/ui-toolbar, with its docs page
