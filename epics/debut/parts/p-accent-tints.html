Plan adjusted for P18 · Themes as Data:

  • A tint is also theme data, in the theme file's shape: the primary and page values it sets, which the builder and saved themes carry as they are.
  • Its sheet still comes from the theme editor's, as planned. Nothing else changed; the plan stays accepted.
  1. The tints: every one of Spell UI's colours, by its own name, the same list as the theme editor's swatches (UIT.ThemeHues): Red, Orange, Yellow, Olive, Green, Teal, Blue, Violet, Purple, Pink, Brown, Grey and Black, thirteen.
    • Read from that list, not written again: a colour added to Spell UI becomes a tint by itself.
    • A tint is the same colour as the theme editor's swatch of that name.
    • A colour that can't reach AA on a theme, light or dark, is left out of that theme's row (below, Checked). The likeliest: Yellow and Olive as link colours on a light page.
  2. What a tint does: the theme editor's own colour settings for that colour, as Primary and as Background (ThemeSheet), laid over the theme.
    • Primary: buttons, links, selection, focus, the active menu item. Solid colours, as today.
    • Background: the page behind everything, as a soft GRADIENT of the tint, light into deep: a pale wash of the colour at the top, a deeper one at the foot (in dark, deep into a little lighter).
      • Drawn once behind the page, fixed, the way Fruity's wallpaper is drawn today; panels and cards keep the theme's own colour on top of it.
      • On Fruity, its three colour washes take the tint's shades.
      • The page's text is checked at both ends of the gradient.
    • (coherence) No second writer of those settings: the tint is a theme-editor sheet with two choices made; the gradient is that sheet's Background, written where it writes the solid one today. A tint is also theme data, in the theme file's shape (P18): the primary and page values it sets, which the builder (P22) and a saved theme (P23) carry as they are.
    • A theme kept in the theme editor (Use this theme) still wins where it chose a colour.
    • Put on by the theme list itself (UI.themes.apply()), in a slot of its own after the theme's, so a theme and a tint picked in a row land in order; never in the theme editor's sheet slot, which the kept theme uses.
  3. In the theme menu (<ui-theme-menu>, the element P17 makes of today's docs-only menu), in every place:
    • the palette's panel: under the themes, a row of tint swatches, None first, for the theme chosen; fourteen swatches wrap to two rows where the panel is narrow
    • the row is the theme editor's colour row, lifted out of it into a piece both use
    • the theme dropdown (show="theme", the components pages): the same row at its foot
    • the menu's label says both: "Glass - Pink"; no tint, "Glass"
    • arrow keys move along the row; each swatch's name is its tooltip and its accessible name, the same name the theme editor shows
  4. Remembered as one name (ThemePreference): "Glass - Pink" on screen.

    • Each place remembers its own, as P17 left it (J87).
    • A tint no longer in the list: the theme alone.
