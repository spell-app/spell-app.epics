No stuck-title element any more, nor a marker: SiteSections.followTitle() checks the title's top on every scroll and sets site-title-away on html. Below 1200px the bar's brand gives way to the page's title and its bug / original-page buttons. At 1200px and wider, where there's no bar at rest, the bar comes in fixed over the page column only (it would push the page down in the flow). On phones the bar's site tabs make way while the title is there.
