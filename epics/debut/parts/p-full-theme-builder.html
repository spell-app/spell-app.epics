The theme editor offers a few choices, and previews one made-up app.

  • Its choices: primary, secondary and background colours, two fonts, text size, corners, depth and glass.
  • A theme sets far more colours than that: surfaces, text, borders, links, the feedback colours, focus, shadows, the palette.
  • Its preview is a sample Habit Tracker, not a page anyone will see the theme on.
