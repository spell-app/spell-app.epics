  • Coherence, found before building:
    • ThemeSheet vs ThemeFile: two writers of a theme's CSS. ThemeSheet's recipes (the primary's text, header and background, the focus border) become theme-file values, and ThemeFile writes them.
    • The token tables' groups (J125): FoundationTokens lists its groups itself. Its groups from the theme tree take the tree's order; their rules and titles stay, saying what each token is. The tool's folds read the tree.
    • BenchColor vs the kit's colour helpers: the maths is already the kit's (rgbToHex()). What's twice is painting a colour on a 1px canvas to read it back: the bench's and the docs' token tables' (ColorProbe). TokenColor keeps the one canvas, and ColorProbe paints on it.
