A card says it can be dragged in a spell block, not as an attribute, and its click and drag don't show as its events.

  • ONE word per ability, written two ways that mean the same:
    • draggable, clickable (new), selectable, droppable
    • as the template's attribute, <spell-view "card" draggable>
    • or as a line in the outline, - it is draggable
  • An optional condition says when: - it is draggable when: it is face up, the outline twin of an attribute that holds one (draggable="when: it is face up") (Q42).
  • Clicks and drags are events: - on click: and - on drag:, shown in the Inspector as the thing's (when on drag runs: Q43; whether an event implies its word: Q44).
  • can be dragged and can be selected go: kept one release as aliases, with a lint warning "say is draggable".

  • Lists say when: too: - it can take a card when: ..., - it can give up a card when: ...; if: kept one release, with a lint warning "say when:". A thing still drags only when its list lets it go.
