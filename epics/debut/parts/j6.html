Your note asked for the page to stay usable while the narrow screens' side column shows. <ui-flyout modeless> does it: shown with show() rather than showModal(), so no dimmer, no scroll lock and no focus trap. Fomantic calls the same thing dimPage: false; modeless says what changes for a person, not only the look.

  • A click on the page leaves a modeless flyout open (its closedby of any acts as closerequest); Escape, its close icon and following one of its links close it.
  • In Spanish: sin-bloqueo.
