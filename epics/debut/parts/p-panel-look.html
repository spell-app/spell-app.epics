The property panels (the Inspector, the theme editor) are loose: one field a row, tall gaps, plain labels, so little fits on screen (Owen: "Note how tight that is compared to our current").

Spell UI's panel look (<ui-panel> around <ui-property-editor>) becomes the tight one in Owen's reference panels, built into the components so every panel gets it:

  • tight: small type and gaps, section bands (Defaults, Frame, Appearance, Layout ...)
  • two columns for fields with short inputs and selects
  • checkboxes on one line with their label, stacked tight
  • icons in select menus and segmented buttons (shapes, colours, alignments)
  • sides fields with their letters inside (T, L, R, B) and laid out north / west-east / south, for margin and padding
  • a field's (i) tooltip, and a light hint at its label's right (LaTeX, LaTeX or a #)
  • a value that isn't its field's default shows bold: what's changed stands out
