Spell UI's panel look (<ui-panel> around <ui-property-editor>) becomes the tight one in Owen's reference panels, built into the components so every panel gets it:

  • tight: small type and gaps, section bands (Defaults, Frame, Appearance, Layout ...)
  • two columns for fields with short inputs and selects
  • checkboxes on one line with their label, stacked tight
  • icons in select menus and segmented buttons (shapes, colours, alignments)
  • sides fields with their letters inside (T, L, R, B) and laid out north / west-east / south, for margin and padding
  • a field's (i) tooltip, and a light hint at its label's right (LaTeX, LaTeX or a #)
  • a value that isn't its field's default shows bold, so what's changed stands out: an editor's setting, show-defaults, which the Inspector and the theme editor turn on

The tight look moves INTO Spell UI's property editor and its properties, as a compact modifier, the word Spell UI's other compact looks use: <ui-property-editor compact>, and <ui-panel compact> for the panel's tight bands. The Inspector and the theme editor set it; a property editor without it keeps today's look. Most of it the Inspector does today by hand (its Compact class and about 80 lines of its CSS): that goes, and the components do it.

compact brings the layout: steps 1-3 and the hint in the label row (step 6). Changed values in bold (step 7) are a setting of their own, show-defaults, which the Inspector and the theme editor set too. The rest works in any property editor: icons on choices, the segmented menu, <ui-sides>, the (i) tip, :state(set).

Today, measured headless (fields per 600px of the card): a picked <ui-item> 8.6, a <ui-menu> 15.5, a Solitaire card 8.5. Your reference fits about 17. Inspector, an item · a menu · a card · the theme editor's Basics.

  1. Tight type and gaps, section bands: a new compact attribute on <ui-property-editor> (a modifier, as Fomantic's compact words are), and on <ui-panel> for its bands; shown as :state(compact), which its groups and properties read from the editor.
    • 13px text, labels in regular weight (so bold can mean "changed", step 7), 3px from label to control, 8px between rows.
    • The controls it makes are drawn size="small": the editor's size, passed down, small when compact unless it says otherwise.
    • New tokens, read through private aliases: --ui-property-editor-font-size, -row-gap, -column-gap, -label-distance.
    • A group's band (<ui-property-group>, already a panel band): tighter, 4px above and below its title.
    • Its look is the theme's (): tokens each theme may set, --ui-property-editor-band-transform (case), -band-weight, -band-size, -band-background.
      • Spell UI's default theme: your reference's look, Title Case as written, 13px bold, on the panel's surface a step darker
      • a theme that wants today's small capitals sets uppercase and a smaller size
    • Inspector: its property editors say compact; its own sizes (14px, 10px rows, the 6px corners) leave its sheet; it keeps its card padding and the brand theme.
    • Theme editor: its Basics are a property editor in each fold, each made compact, so they tighten too; its folds keep the token tree's look (your "one editor").
  2. Two columns for short fields: a compact editor (and each group) lays its properties on a two-column grid; a property says how wide it is.
    • By its control's kind: each kind in UI.propertyControls gains width: "half" | "full". Half: number, select, colour, date, a segmented row of up to four, sides. Full: text, textarea, slider, icon picker, a nested editor.
    • Or by its own width, the field's attribute it already has (width="1/2", width="16"): that wins.
    • Shown as :state(half) on the property; its own sheet places it in the grid.
    • A panel narrower than about 18em goes to one column (a container query).
    • Inspector: its .half class and size / colour list go; only the "icon beside icon-position" pair stays its own, as width="1/2".
    • Theme editor: its fields hold their own controls, so they're full unless they say; Name beside Builds on, and Headings beside Body, get width="1/2".
  3. Checkboxes inline with their label, stacked tight
    • The checkbox and toggle kinds gain inline: true: in a compact editor the property draws inline (its inline, the field's), the box then its label then its (i).
    • A lone yes-or-no field, or two together: one a row, about 22px apart.
    • A run of them shows as ONE multiple-choice select, its chosen ones as pills (): Spell UI's <ui-dropdown multiple>, each field an option, its (i) text the option's tooltip.
      • when: three or more yes-or-no fields in a row in one group, by themselves; a group says otherwise with flags="pills" or flags="checkboxes" (Spanish indicadores, etiquetas / casillas)
      • its label: the schema's name for the run, else "Options" (Spanish "Opciones"); a whole row, in any editor, compact or not
      • each field stays its own property: picking a pill sets it true, removing it false; the form's values don't change, and :state(set) marks each pill on its own
      • a picked <ui-menu>'s eight yes-or-no fields: one row of pills, not 176px
    • Inspector: Compact and its watcher go (it set fluid and inline on each made control).
  4. Icons in selects and segmented buttons
    • A choice can carry an icon: { value, text, icon }; the property puts it on each <ui-item> it makes. The select already draws an item's icon and image, open and closed.
    • Where icons come from: a schema attribute's new icons (value => icon name), and the shared value sets' own (ValueSets): text and vertical alignments, positions, directions, floated, attached.
    • Colours: a hue's select shows a round chip of the hue before its name, in Spell UI (the Inspector's HueSelect moves there).
    • Segmented: the buttons kind draws a segmented <ui-menu>, not a row of radios. So the segmented menu becomes a form control: name, a value (the picked item's), ui-changed, read by the form like a select.
    • Icon-only segments when every choice has an icon, each one's text its tooltip; else icon and text.
    • A choice of up to four values, all with icons, is drawn segmented by itself; anything else a select.
  5. Sides fields, for margin and padding: a new form control, <ui-sides>.
    • Four number boxes, each side's letter inside it (T, L, R, B, faded), laid top / left-right / bottom; two sides in one row.
    • Its value takes either form (, A and B):
      • CSS's text, one to four numbers, top right bottom left: value="10 20 10 20", written in markup like any attribute
      • an object, { top: 10, left: 20, right: 20, bottom: 10 }: set as the property, or as JSON text in the attribute
      • how it tells them apart: an object, or text starting with {, is read by side names (a side left out is empty); any other text is CSS's numbers
      • it gives back the form it was given (text in, text out; an object in, an object out), and the form reads its value as that text, or that object
    • sides="top bottom" shows only those boxes, in one row; none: all four, in a cross.
    • A new value type, sides, and a kind drawing it, half a row.
    • As every new tag: vocabulary, Spanish file, a native fallback (four number inputs), docs page, root catalog, site data; it leaves the planned list if it's on it.
    • No Spell UI attribute holds sides today, so no Inspector field shows one until a program's property is a sides; the docs' example does.
  6. The (i) tooltip and a light hint at the label's right
    • In a compact editor, a property's hint sits in its label row, at the end, before the (i): 11px, muted, cut short with "…", the whole text its tooltip. A plain <ui-field> and a property editor without compact keep the hint under the control.
    • Where hints come from: the property's hint (the field's), else the schema's new hint ("LaTeX", "LaTeX or a #").
    • The (i) keeps its tip and hover delay; it shrinks to 12px.
  7. A value other than its default shows bold, when the editor says show-defaults
    • A setting of <ui-property-editor>, off unless given: show-defaults (Spanish mostrar-predeterminados). The Inspector's editors and the theme editor's set it; the docs' compact example too.
    • A property is :state(set) while its value isn't its default: the schema's default, else its own new default attribute; no default: any value at all. Never while things disagree (mixed) or read only.
    • It reads its control's value the way the form does (FormFields), as it changes.
    • Bold only with show-defaults, compact or not; :state(set) in any editor. Bold reaches the control through an owner token, as a field's width does (InputOwnerTokens gains weight): inputs, selects, dropdowns and the segmented menu's picked item read it.
    • Theme editor: its choices give default the theme's own, so a choice you've made reads bold, as its "Theme's own" output already says untouched.
  8. The static render: the editor, group and property aren't drawn statically (they need a schema and a live form), so nothing changes there. <ui-sides> is a form control, so it joins the static families; the form family's new owner token is drawn statically too.
