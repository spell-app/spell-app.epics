No parser change, so the words file records no parameter types: put a card on a pile names its parameter by its words (a pile => the program's Pile). A card's own a card reads it: play it, put it on a pile. A two sided view's built-in actions have no words file, so they read by method name: turn face up, turn face down, turn over (not 'turn it over'). count the points for it on a pile shows too: it's an action in spell. Actions run directly with their arguments (core.runAction() takes none), so core.onActionRun() listeners don't hear Inspector presses.
