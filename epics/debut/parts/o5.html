The storyteller voice is the one Owen picked from the voice contest for newcomers: it lives in spell-epic's novice voice now.

  • Its rules, in short: open on a person, not a product; show the real code, then say what the reader just saw; give them something to do on the first screen; "Real today / Not yet" near the top; warm, never cute, never "simply" or "just".
  • There's no public site yet. The nearest things: the Spell Marketing brand mockup ("Write an app in a sentence.") and spell-lang's language README, which already reads like a tour.
  • The guides: "Your first spell" (a walkthrough), "A tour of Spell" (an explainer), and one short "how it works" per example. The landing page's hero is the front page.
  • They live in spell-app's unused Guides group (Q5): each guide a project, opened from the editor's Guides column. How a guide's prose shows there is decided at the start of P10 (J1).
