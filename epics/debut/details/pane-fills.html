The editor's panes: frosted or see-through?

In Glass and Fruity, the running app's pane now shows the wallpaper through, as an epic page's open drawer does. The editor's other panes are still frosted. Should any of them follow? Here's each one both ways.

session debut, after giving the running app the drawer's fill 2026-10-26
debut -- Spell's public preview: the editor, its themes and its examples, ready for anyone to try. You liked how see-through an epic page's open drawer is, "e.g. under the running app". So the running app's pane got that fill (I48). You asked to see the other panes both ways before picking. Which of the editor's other panes, in Glass and Fruity, show the wallpaper through the same way. Spell and Merlot have solid panes and don't change.

Two fills, both over a soft blur of the wallpaper:

  • Frosted, today's panel: 45% white in light, a dark grey glass in dark. The wallpaper shows as a pale wash.
  • See-through, the drawer's: 28% white in light, 2% in dark. The wallpaper's own colours show, strongly in dark.

About the pictures:

  • Real screenshots of this checkout: Solitaire in Debug, every side bar open, a card picked. The option's CSS is laid on for the shot.
  • "A" is see-through, "B" is today. Each pane is cropped close; Q1 has the whole editor.
  • Glass has no wallpaper, so there the two fills look nearly the same. Fruity is where it shows, most of all in dark.
  • The running app's pane is see-through in every picture: that's already built.

The quick answer first: make every pane see-through, like the running app? Or go pane by pane below? These are the whole editor, everything see-through next to today.

One look for every pane: the wallpaper's colours everywhere, as under the running app.

:root:is([data-spell-theme="glass"], [data-spell-theme="fruity"]) :is(.SplitPanelPane:has(> .InputRoot), .SplitPanelPane:has(> .ProgramPanel), .SplitPanelPane:has(> .InspectorCard)) {
  background: var(--spell-app-background);   /* the drawer's:  28% white in light, 2% in dark */
}

Recommended: the panes you read text in stay calmer, and the running app stands out as the one see-through window.

See-through: the sheet's cards show its wallpaper, and the sheet reads as one piece.

:root:is([data-spell-theme="glass"], [data-spell-theme="fruity"]) .InspectorPane.up .SplitPanelPane:has(> .InspectorCard)   /* on a phone */ {
  background: var(--spell-app-background);   /* the drawer's:  28% white in light, 2% in dark */
}

Keep frosted. Recommended: the same as the desktop Inspector's cards (Q2), so the two stay alike.
