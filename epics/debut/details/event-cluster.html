Clicks and drags as events

How a card says when it can be clicked or dragged, and what runs when it is: the words in an outline, and the one tag in spellml.

What Solitaire's card and piles say, in both forms, once P27 is built; every example and guide gets rewritten to match.
  • An ability: what people may do to a thing -- click it, drag it, select it, drop on it.
  • Its condition: when it may, e.g. a card drags only when its pile lets it go. No condition: always.
  • Its events: what runs as it happens, e.g. as the card lifts, as it's dropped.

Today, the card says only that it can be dragged; the pile's rules decide the rest:

<spell-view "card" template="two sided view">
	<spell>
		- it can be dragged
	</spell>
</spell-view>

<spell-view "tableau" template="pile">
	<spell>
		- it can give up a card if: the card is face up
		- it can take a card if: ...
	</spell>
</spell-view>

The proposal, in the outline form (Q42's when:):

a card:
	- it is draggable when: its pile lets it go
	- when drag starts:  set the hint of the solitaire to ""
	- when drag ends:  ...
	- it is clickable when: it is face down
	- on click:  play it

a tableau:
	- it can give up a card when: the card is face up
	- it can take a card when: ...
	- when a card is dropped on it:  put the card on it

In spellml, how does a card write an ability, its condition and its events together?

One tag per ability, its condition an attribute, its events inside. A bare tag: always.

<draggable when="its pile lets it go">
	<on start>set the hint of the solitaire to ""</on>
	<on end>...</on>
</draggable>

<clickable when="it is face down">
	<on click>play it</on>
</clickable>

<draggable/>          -- always, no events

A pile: <droppable when="..."> holding <on dropped>, <on dragged over>, <on dragged out>.

No ability tag: each event tag says it, the first one's when is the condition. Shorter, but a drag's condition sits on one of two events.

<on click when="it is face down">play it</on>
<on drag-start when="its pile lets it go">...</on>
<on drag-end>...</on>

The condition on the <spell-view> tag itself; events as separate tags. Not one tag per cluster.

<spell-view "card" draggable="its pile lets it go" clickable="it is face down">
	<on click>play it</on>
	<on drag start>...</on>
</spell-view>

In the outline, what are the dragged thing's two events called?

Your words: - when drag starts: as it lifts, - when drag ends: as it's let go, dropped or sliding back.

- on drag: as it lifts, - on drop: as it lands; shorter, like on click, but "drop" is also the pile's word.

Everyday words: - when it is picked up:, - when it is put down:.
