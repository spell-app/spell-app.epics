One open question at the bottom.
  • Events start a line (or a tag) with on or when: the two mean the same, so write whichever reads better.
    • - on card dropped: ~== - when a card is dropped on it:
  • Conditions: a when AFTER a word is a condition, never an event.
    • clickable when it is face down, a list's when:
  • Abilities: clickable, draggable, droppable, selectable.
    • Writing an event gives its thing the ability: on click makes it clickable (Q44).
    • The ability's own line only adds when: draggable when it is face up. No line: always.
    • A pile says it for its cards: its cards are draggable when ..., beside its own droppable when ....
  • Short and loose: small words are optional ((a) card (is) dragged over), and so is the colon.
  • Tags: bare words, no quotes.
    • One line closes itself: <on click play it/>
    • More lines go between tags, closed by </> (preferred), </on> or </on click>
  • while stays as it is: set something for as long as it lasts, put back after.

  • Events: a bolt and the event AS YOU WROTE IT (when clicked stays that; on click if you wrote that), its code under it, in a box that grows as you type.
  • Conditions sit with them: a hand and the ability with its when (draggable when, clickable when), its condition under it. A pile shows its cards are draggable when and droppable when.
  • The + adds an event, as the old card's did.
  • Actions (P29): a button per action the card's type has; a press does it, on the running app.
    • An action that takes something gets a field after it: play on pile a menu of the piles, a number a number field, text a text field.
    • One that can't run now (its own condition says no) is greyed, its reason the tooltip.
  • Nothing picked, or nothing to show: no band.

What you wrote: <draggable when ...> and <on drag ...> side by side. One way to write it, matching the outline line for line.

<draggable when it is face up/>
<on drag set the hint of the solitaire to ""/>

The events nest inside their ability's tag: grouped, but a second shape for the same thing.

<draggable when it is face up>
	<on drag set the hint of the solitaire to ""/>
</>

Either shape reads; two ways to write one thing.
