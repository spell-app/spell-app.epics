A background helper can't open a question modal: Owen isn't watching it. What does it do when it hits a choice only Owen should make, e.g. "rename positionFor() everywhere, or only in this file"?

A · Decide, record, ask only if stuck (recommended)
  • A choice with a sensible default: take it, and record it as a judgement call (in the plan doc when the session has an epic, else in its report).
  • A choice it can't make safely (would delete work, publish, or change the task's meaning): stop there and report the question; the session asks it in a modal.
  • Why recommended: the same rule as /bedtime, so Owen reviews both the same way.
 B · Always stop and ask
  • Any choice: stop, report it, wait for Owen.
  • Cost: most tasks come back half done, and the point of /bg is not waiting.

Net effect (A):

  • a helper's choices show up as judgement calls (gavel icon, red until reviewed) or in its report
  • a blocking question comes back to the session, which asks it in the modal
