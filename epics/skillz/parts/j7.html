  • Why: prose may mean the phase as it was numbered then (a log line, an old decision), and rewriting it would change history.
  • Cost: an unlinked mention can go stale after an insert.
  • Habit: link phases when naming them, and they stay right.
  • Net effect: links always follow the phase; plain text is left as written.
