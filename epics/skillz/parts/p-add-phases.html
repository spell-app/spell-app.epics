Adding a phase to a running epic, or working a handful of its items together, meant spelling it out in prose each time.

Two new /epic forms on the session's own epic: /epic phase [ids] [name] adds a phase, /epic start works a phase or a block of items.

  • A background <epic>-plan-doc agent drafts the phase and places it before Doc Review.
  • A block of items is worked together, each closed when done, in one commit <epic> J1 + J2: ....
  • phase and start are reserved names, let through by the prompt hook.
  • add-phase ... --before N inserts the phase as PN, the later to-do phases moving down with their links; refused once one of them has started (J7).
