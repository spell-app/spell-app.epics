/bg hands a task to a helper agent that works in the background, so the Claude panel is free again at once: Owen can keep talking to the session (or send more /bg tasks) while the helper works.

  • /bg [n] <task>: start the task.
    • n (optional, default 1) is the most agents the task may use IN ALL, the helper itself included. /bg 4 <task>: the helper plus up to 3 of its own.
    • A hard cap: unlike the root rule (up to 5 agents, unasked), the helper never goes over n.
  • /bg list: the background tasks this session started that are still running, each with its name and age.
  • /bg stop <name>: stop one.

What happens, in order:

  1. The session starts ONE background helper with the task word for word, plus what the helper can't know by itself:
    • which checkout or worktree it's in
    • the epic and plan doc, if the session has one
    • its agent budget, n
    • the house rules that matter: WWOD; stage, never commit unless told; shared files edited at their real path; judgement calls recorded in the plan doc; plain shell commands in a worktree
  2. The session replies ONE line, started: <short name> (<n> agents), and ends its turn.
  3. When the helper finishes, its notice wakes the session, which writes a short report: what changed, where to look, anything that needs Owen, with links as the root rules say (side bar + browser; plan docs in the Review tab).

Several /bg tasks at once are fine. Two that would touch the same files: the session says so, and starts the second only once the first is done.
