Footers, Tiny Pills

Owen's idea for HTML footers: each block's end tag a tiny pill in its block's colour, at the rail's turn, its words tiny inside it. The same drawing three ways: as it is today, then A (footers only), then B (footers, and end tags in a line too). Not built into the look: pick one, or say what to change.
