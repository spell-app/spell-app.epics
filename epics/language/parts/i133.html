When we go into multi-line-tag mode, indent all of the attributes the same as prettier would format them. backspace with cursor here should move `appearance` back to the line above, with a space between

A long tag now goes over several lines the way Prettier lays it out, and draws as one tag.

  1. Enter between a tag's attributes puts each attribute on its own line, one level in from the tag (Solitaire's <toolbar: 4 tabs, where before they were lined up 11 tabs in). The > or /> goes on its own line, back at the tag's level.
    • If the tag is already split, fully or partly (like your draft's line 11), Enter lays it out again the same way.
    • The caret stays where it was in its attribute, or goes to the start of the next one. After the last attribute, it goes on a new line of its own, ready for another.
    • One undo puts it all back. Format Document keeps a tag laid out this way as it is.
  2. Backspace at the start of an attribute's line moves it up to the line above, with one space between (no space before a >). Once every attribute is back on the tag's line, a > on its own line comes up too, so the tag is one line again.
  3. The drawing:
    • The tag's first line is its band, rounded at its right end.
    • Each attribute line is a pill in the tag's colour, round that line's words only, hanging on the tag's rail the way its children do. Before, the band covered the line's indent, from the margin across the outer rails.
    • A > on its own line ends the band, starting at the band's left edge.
    • The rail runs down past the attribute lines into the children.
  4. An attribute's opening quote mark now has a space's room after its =, as it has after a space.
