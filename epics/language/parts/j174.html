English which, that and who all start a condition with no subject. who isn't kept for people only. Each rule spells the words out ((where:where|which|that|who)) so it still turns away lines that have none of them, and a list that read too far gives the word back. A where reading of the same words is unchanged, so it still wins. Spanish que adds no subject, because Spanish leaves it out after donde too (implied_it). So que tiene ... doesn't read, just as donde tiene ... doesn't.
