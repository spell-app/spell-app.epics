  • A finger held still about half a second on a view opens its right-click menu where the finger is, as a right click does: on iPhone too, where Safari never sends one (LongPress, listened for once per page by SpellElement.defineFor()).
  • Android Chrome sends its own for a long press: whichever comes first opens the menu, the other is swallowed, so it shows once.
  • No menu for a tap, a finger that moves more than 8px, a second finger, a mouse or pen, a press in a text field, or once a drag has started; once it opens, a card held under it never drags, its lift makes no click, and the press selects no text.
