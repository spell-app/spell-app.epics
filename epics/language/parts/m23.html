cd ~/www/spell.worktrees/language
spell server start
spell app create upgrade-test
cd ~/www/spell.spaces/@spell/owen@/upgrade-test
printf '{ imports: [{ path: "/upgrade-test.spell", active: true }] }\n' > __project.json5
printf 'a card is a view where\n\tit has parts "front"\n\tto deal it\n\t\twait for: show it\n' > upgrade-test.spell
cat ~/www/spell.worktrees/language/.spell-server.editor.json

In Chrome, open that address followed by /edit/owen@spell/upgrade-test/upgrade-test.spell. Close the Upgrade notice if it shows (its x). Rest the mouse on the word parts (yellow squiggle) until its popup shows, then press its Fix it button. Then press Cmd+Z.

The popup shows the warning with a small blue Fix it button under it. Pressed, the line reads it has slots "front", the squiggle and the popup go, and the caret stays where it was. Cmd+Z puts parts back.
