1. In the ⎇ language window, run Developer: Reload Window (Cmd+Shift+P), so the new Spell extension loads.
  2. Open app/spaces/@spell/examples/Solitaire/Card.spell (Cmd+P, Card.spell).
  3. Wait a moment after it opens (the look arrives with the language server, about a second).
  4. Click into a pill's word ("card" on the first declaration line), type a letter, Backspace it; then Cmd+F and find rank; then arrow left and right across "card".
  5. Open Settings (Cmd+,), search spell.bubblyLook, untick it; then tick it again.
  • step 3: the text is near monochrome in the look's ink; references (its rank, the deck) semibold; marks and comments faint, comments in italics; "card", "rank" and each value of its rank (ace, jack ...) on a soft tinted pill (purple for the card, amber for its properties, green for its actions), with a little space either side, the file's own quote marks faint outside the pill
  • step 4: typing, Backspace, find and the caret work as ever; the caret sits on the letters, never in the space beside a pill; the pill follows the edit
  • step 5: unticked, the file goes back to the theme's colours with no pills, at once; ticked, the look comes back
  • a pill over several words (to "deal the cards") is one pill, not one per word (T115)
