Some tag attributes use other words than the plain Spell line they stand for (template= for is a, address= for at), and some hold Spell inside quotes (enabled-when="the draft has changes"), where quotes elsewhere mean text.

  • Owen's words (2026-11-10): “what are other forms we might consider changing this way”, then “make this a phase”.
  • The rule: an attribute is the plain line's own words; braces hold Spell, quotes hold text.
  • The candidates, each its own question on this page, with counts from the built-in projects:
    • template="two sided view" (27) becomes is-a={two sided view}, as in a card is a two sided view.
    • A place's address= (21) and a site page's address= (409) become at=, as in x is a web server at "..." and it has a home page at "/"; a site already says at=.
    • A one-line alias, <alias "is a face card">if its rank is jack, queen or king</> (9), becomes <alias "is a face card" if={its rank is jack, queen or king} />.
    • enabled-when="..." (5) and selected-when="..." (10) take braces, as they hold Spell.
  • Left alone, because they don't fit the rule:
    • shortcut="meta+T" (37): text, a key, not Spell.
    • A property's alias="is (color)" (5): a phrase pattern, not Spell to run.
    • One-line action bodies (18), <action "count up">set the count to the count + 1</>: steps stay a body.
    • Handler heads, <on fizz with number as a number>: already the plain line's words.
    • title=, icon=, description=, tab=, tags=: text. for-each={...} already takes braces. aka= already the plain word.

Each attribute takes the plain line's own words, and braces hold Spell while quotes hold text, as P42 does for a property's is={...}.

Which attributes change is Owen's call, one question each: Q42 (template=), Q43 (address=), Q44 (one-line alias), Q45 (enabled-when=, selected-when=).
