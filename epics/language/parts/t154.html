P62 already sends a long press as a right click (so on right click and <context-menu> open); this adds on long press and makes sure the translation holds only where there's no mouse.

`on long press` (Spanish `cuando se mantiene pulsada`) hears a finger held still on a view, JavaScript and Python; on a phone or tablet with no mouse a long press nothing takes is also a right click (`on right click`, the right-click menu); with a mouse it's only a long press (`LONG_PRESS_EVENT`, `LongPress.hasNoMouse()`)
