Owen asked for the pill under a computed value (color={its color}) to be inverted. Two ways to do it:

  • Picked: a deep shade of the tag band's own colour (62% of the hue over near-black; in dark mode 45% of the hue over white), with paper-coloured words. It stays in the tag's colour family, so it reads as part of that tag. The words on it measure 5.1 (amber) to 7.8 (purple) contrast in light mode, and 9.1 to 11.3 in dark mode.
  • Not picked: the ink colour itself (near-black, with white words). It stands out more, but it looks heavy, like a different kind of thing dropped onto the band.

It's one setting, --bubbly-expression-pill in bubbly.css. Changing it back to var(--bubbly-ink) gives the ink version.
