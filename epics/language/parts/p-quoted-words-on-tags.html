A quoted word after a tag name only works on a few tags: <header "Hardware" size="large"/> doesn't work, so a one-word header needs its closing tag.

  • Owen, from epic mockup (r13): "<header "Hardware" size="large"/> means the same as <header size="large">Hardware</header>, right? ... Have it look for other tags where this would make sense."
  • Works with attributes after the word, closed by /> or by a normal end tag.
  • No Spell UI component changes: <ui-header> draws its contents through a slot and has no attribute for its text, so the word becomes its contents rather than a new attribute on each component.
  • The tags, from counting real use in the 490 .spell and .spellml files of the built-in projects, Spell UI's docs and the editor (approximate counts, by pattern):
    Tag Used Holding only short text The quoted word is
    header 1,477 502 contents
    button 1,096 922 contents
    item 3,912 2,015 contents
    label 441 312 contents
    title 137 132 contents
    link 213 117 contents
    breadcrumb 68 67 contents
    checkbox 97 62 contents
    radio 35 29 contents
    tab 116 label= on 101 label
    panel 40 label= on 30 label
  • Left out on purpose:
    • text, description: often long, or text mixed with values
    • td, th: the web's own table tags
    • segment, column, content, stack: they hold layouts; their short text is mostly in demos
    • author, date: high counts, all from Spell UI's feed and comment demos
  • Stays as it is: <cell "Name"> is still a column's name, <example "x"> still a name, <tree-node "If"> still a label. <action "...">, <property "..."> and <draw "..."> are read by another route and aren't touched.
  • A quoted word can now mean the tag's contents, as well as an attribute: <header "Hardware" size="large"/> draws exactly what <header size="large">Hardware</header> draws.
  • The tags whose contents are nearly always one short piece of text get it, in English and Spanish, as both header and ui-header.
  • tab and panel take a quoted word as their label, which is what those tags already name themselves with.
  • Tokenizer: quotedNameTags, matchJSXQuotedName()
  • SpellParser: the list of tags
  • SpellxView: reading a tag's parts back, for the editor
  • PARSING.md, the spell syntax tables if a rule's docstring changes (yarn spell:gen:syntax)
  • Tests: the parser's tokenizer tests, spell's JSX tests, and a test that every word in the list is a real component name
  • For each tag in the list, the short form and the long form compile to identical code, in English and Spanish, on the JavaScript, TypeScript and Python targets.
  • <cell "Name">, <example "x"> and <tree-node "If"> compile as before: spell content check passes with nothing blessed.
  • Open a page using the short form in the editor: the Inspector shows the tag, and changing an attribute keeps the quoted word.
  • yarn review in parser/, and spell speedtest --against HEAD.
