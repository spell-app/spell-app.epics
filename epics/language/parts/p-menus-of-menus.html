A Spell menu is one list of actions, so a menu bar of several menus, or a sub-menu, can't be written (editor T38).
  • The editor's menu bar and its More Examples › sub-menu could be written in Spell (r4, its second ask).
  • Toolbars, the app's menu bar and context menus all draw nested menus from the same core.menuOf() items.
  • A sub-menu filled from a list (the projects under More Examples) comes the way Q21 picks.
  • A menu may hold another menu: <menu "menu bar"><menu "file menu"/><menu "edit menu"/></menu>.
  • menu-bar="menu bar" names that menu, so the bar is File · Edit · View · Run · Help.
  • A menu has a title, as an action does (P18).
