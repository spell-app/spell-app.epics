When you open a file, the code now shows once, already drawn the way it ends up. Nothing gets redrawn a second later.

  • Why: the editor opens Solitaire from the compile cache, so nothing has been parsed yet. The code was first drawn from a parse of that one file on its own, which knows nothing of Card, Pile or Deck. About 1.3s later the project's parse arrived and everything was drawn again. That's the change you caught.
  • It wasn't only the feet. The tags that make members (<stock-pile "stock"/> ...) went from plain bands to round ones, from 1 to 12. The underlines went from 1 to 4. A plain-text frame came before all of it.
  • Now, while a file's project parse is on its way, its lines stay hidden on the paper. They appear in the same update that lays them out from the project's parse. A parse that never comes still shows them after 4 seconds.
  • A new parse is also laid out straight away now. Before, it waited another 150ms, showing the old layout in the meantime.
  • This covers anything drawn from the parse, in the code pane and the Inspector's Code card: the feet, the laid-out tags and their ⟩, plain-line indents, group colours.

Measured frame by frame in the real editor, opening Solitaire headless, light and dark:

  • Before (main): first drawn at 1.65s from the file's own parse (1 round tag, 1 underline). The project's drawing replaced it at 3.0s (12 round tags, 4 underlines), a 1.35s flash. In dark mode: 2.0s, then 3.5s.
  • After: hidden from 1.4s to 1.97s (light, worktree's own project) and 1.8s to 3.2s (Solitaire in dark). The first frame shown is the project's drawing: 12 round tags, 4 underlines, both feet 22.6 x 14.1px, level. Nothing changes after that.
  • Switching from Card.spell to Solitaire.spell (already parsed): shown at once, from the first frame.
