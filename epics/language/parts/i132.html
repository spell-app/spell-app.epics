Owen, 2026-11-16: "These feet look weird. What can we do?" Then, offered four ways: "I like one foot for a run. Need to see presentations for options 1 and 2, inline screenshots in chat is fine".

Option 1: plain feet, like Spell's -- a </> line draws only its rail's curl, the </> text dimmed and small where it is.

Option 2: one foot for a run of </>s, under the outermost, the inner rails ending in it (nested as I125's Spell feet).

Picked: option 2 (one foot row for a run of end tags), with HTML's own foot shape:

  • a run of end-tag lines draws its feet on ONE row, nested
  • each foot: the rail turns out at a tag's slant (the rail's thickness), not round; then about 12px of bar; then the end tag, small, in a pill pointed at both ends like an inline end tag's
Re footers -- I like the idea of collapsing the footers onto one line. Rather than the curvy line at the bottom, do an angled line with the same angle and thickness as the gutter, then 12px or so of horizontal space, then the </> or </tag> small like you have it, but in an angled pill like the inline end </>.

Built (commit 0614fdc2b, not merged yet): a run of end tags alone on their lines is now ONE row of feet.

  • Each foot: the rail turns out at a tag's slant, as thick as the rail, then 12px of bar, then the end tag, small, in a pill pointed at both ends like an inline </>.
  • Nested as Spell's feet: the outer one a step below and a tab stop left. Side by side on one level would need the outer bar to run under the inner pill, so it isn't built; say if you want to see it.
  • The pills ARE the file's end tags: the line breaks between them are hidden, one step for the caret. A click on either pill lands in it; Delete and undo work as before.
  • A single end tag (</container>) gets the same angled foot; </context-menu> above the drawing's Spell foot keeps its own row, the Spell foot round under it.
  • Room under the row before a band (T141); the row on whole pixels, so the lines under it don't drift.

Left as it was: a faint hairline where a Spell rail meets its round corner, at some heights. It's there on Spell-only feet too, not from this change.

Round 2 (commit 30e50a53f, not merged yet): a run's end tags sit side by side on one level.

  • The innermost end tag comes first, nearest its rail. Each outer one comes after it, its bar running on behind the end tags before it, so they all sit on one bar.
  • The row is one foot tall again (16px), on whole pixels.
  • A click on either end tag lands in it; the caret steps from one to the next in one keypress.
  • Even room inside each pill, from its tip to the glyphs: 4.22px before the ⟨ and 4.28px after the ⟩ on </>; 4.26px / 4.23px on </container>.
  • The feet start from wherever the rails are, so they follow I136's move.
