Q67's second half, "it = the thing just named", is built but not shipped: its census found example lines whose meaning it would change, which their authors clearly didn't mean. The first half, the error with a hint, is shipped (I106).

The rule as built (branch language-I106-named-it, one commit on top of language, not merged):

  • A line names a thing when its verb acts on one: a call of a project's or a built-in method (turn X face up, put X on Y, play X), and add, append, prepend, add X to Y before Z, move, remove.
  • Its thing: the verb's direct object, its first slot (X), never where it goes (Y); only a thing of a type the program declares (Card), never text, a number or a list.
  • From the next line on, it and its X mean that thing, in the same body and the bodies under it, until another such line, a get or a set it to; a loop's it (its item) and an if ... and it keep their own inside them. A line inside an if names it for that if's body only, as get does.
  • Compiled: a variable (the card, a loop's item, an argument) is read as itself; anything else is kept first, const it = this.stock.lastItem, then it?.turnFaceUp(), so it stays the card even after the line moved it.

The census: every frozen test project, every example and library app, every Spell guide, English and Spanish.

  • Bare it: 0 reads change, anywhere.
  • its X: 13 reads change, in 2 examples, none of them meant:
    • PublicServers' OpenMeteo.spell 12-15, when creating an open meteo page: add a new city with name "New York" ... to its cities, three times after the first, then set its city to the first item of its cities. Each its cities became the city just added (it.cities), and its city the last city's. The author meant the page's.
    • StarWars 57-58, to punch it: open landing, then set its jumping to no: its became the character landed on, not the film.
    • StarWars 273, 349, 351, 385, 388, 389, 390, the facts getters: add (a new fact with label "Produced by", value = its producer) to known, after another such line: each its producer, its terrain, its crew ... became the fact just added.
  • Compiled code: 21 projects change even where no read does, about 600 lines: each object that isn't a variable kept in an it of its own first, e.g. FizzBuzz's const it = new Result({ ... }), then this.tally.append(it).
  • One more thing found: a test's lines echo what they do (echoTestAction); a line kept as two (const it = ..., then the call) lost its echo.

Owen's draft line (turn the top card of the stock face up ... add it to the discard pile) compiles, under the rule, to const it = this.stock.lastItem, it?.turnFaceUp() ... this.discardPile.append(it). Without it, it's now the error: it is solitaire here, which isn't a card, and the discard pile holds cards: say which card, or "get" it first.

The choices:

  1. Only it follows the thing just named; its X stays the thing the action is on. Changes no example, and your draft line works. But after turn the top card face up, it is the card while its rank is still the game's.
  2. Ship it as built, its too, and rewrite the two examples' 13 lines (its cities => the cities).
  3. Don't ship it: the new error catches the mistake, and its hint says to get the card first.
