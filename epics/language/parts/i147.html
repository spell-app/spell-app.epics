owenoak: “This bar and curly should be colored by group, not app <img src="tests/i147-owen.png">”

Each group's rail and its curled foot are now the group band's own colour, not the app's.

  • Why: a group's rail sits right on top of the app's rail, in the same place. The app's was drawn over it, so you only ever saw the app's colour. The foot was given the app's colour on purpose, back in I125.
  • Now the innermost rail is drawn on top, and a group's rail and foot take its band's colour. Every other block keeps its own.
  • Measured in the real editor, light and dark, on Card.spell's four groups and Solitaire's "Dealing & the Stock Pile": the rail under each group band, and the curl, match the band exactly. Before, they were the app's darker lavender.
  • Where a group ends on the same line as the type round it, the one shared curl is the group's colour too, since the rail running into it is the group's (J223).
  • Commit 1433311a2, merged. A CodeMirrorRails browser test checks the colours, light and dark.
