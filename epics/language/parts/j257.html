chose: as a duration = its two biggest parts (23 hours, 46 minutes ago), one part alone as tomorrow/yesterday/in 2 days; as a short duration = one part rounded to the scale (yesterday, in 2 weeks); as a long duration = every part to the second (23 hours, 46 minutes, 35 seconds ago). Under 45 seconds is Intl's now / in 30 seconds. Over 26 days it always rounds. On a duration (not a date) it says how long, with no ago / in.
