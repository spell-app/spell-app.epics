Solitaire's auto play

Before:

to "auto play" with shortcut "A", icon "play"
	-- Play every card that can go somewhere, until none can
	set anything changed to no
	if the discard pile is not empty
		set test card to the last card of the discard pile
		if play test card
			set anything changed to yes
			pause for 500 msec
	-- each column's last card onto its foundation
	for pile in the columns
		if the pile is empty return
		...
	-- each column's face-up cards, all together, onto another column
	for pile in the columns
		...
	if anything changed auto play

Now:

to "auto play" with shortcut "A", icon "play"
	-- Play one card that can go somewhere, then the next, until none can:  the discard pile's top card, a column's top card onto its foundation, or a column's face-up cards onto another column
	set played to the last card in the discard pile where it can play the card
		or the first card in the columns where it is the top card of its pile and (the first pile in every pile where it can take the card) is a foundation and can play the card
		or the first card in the columns where (it is not a king or it is not the first card of its pile) and it is (the first card in its pile which is face up) and can play the card
	if played exists
		pause for 300 msec
		auto play
