T166 moves a // note at the end of ANY line of code to the first line inside it, as --: a declaration's, where it becomes its description, and a plain step's too (load its sound, then -- so Hear it plays it at once indented under it, which compiles the same).

  • A -- at a line's end is left alone: it's how a drawing and a type's line are described (to draw its placeholder -- a grey ace).
  • A line holding markup is skipped: a line indented under one is more markup, never a note.
