For P55 · Solitaire's New Forms: should -- after a line's words stay as the way to describe what that line declares?

Your Solitaire rewrite puts one on several head lines:

a "column" is a pile			-- A column, built down from king to ace, red and black in turn
a card		-- play the card on click if it is a legal move
	to draw its placeholder			-- a light grey ace of its suit, face up
  • Today, on a type's declaration (the first line), it becomes the type's description: a docstring, and its hover.
  • On a re-opened type (the second) and on a drawing (the third), it's a plain comment, compiled as //-- ..., the dashes kept.
  • A // line right under a head already becomes its description (to "cheat"'s).
  • -- describes whatever its line declares: a type, a re-opened type's block, an action (its tooltip), a property, a drawing.
  • // stays a plain comment.
  • The compiled comment loses its stray dashes.
  • Only // lines under a head describe it.
  • A description on a type's line only.
