owenoak: “how can we set a header on an `alert` and/or change the text size within it (Owen picked all three: a header with `titled`, a size word, Markdown)”

A dialog's message can be small, big or huge: alert "You won!" in big text.

  • alert "You won!" titled "Solitaire" in huge text with "New game" compiles to await core.alert("You won!", { title: "Solitaire", size: "huge", okText: "New game" }). warn and confirm take it the same way.
  • Spanish: avisar "¡Ganaste!" en texto grande, en texto pequeño (or chico), en texto enorme.
  • The sizes are Spell UI's own size words, so they come out as 14, 20 and 24px against the usual 16. Only the message changes size. The title and the buttons stay as they are.
  • Spacing, measured in the dialog: the message starts 40px below the dialog's top (24px of padding plus a 16px margin) and 24px in from its left, and the button is 16px from the bottom. Those numbers are the same at every size.
    • To keep them that way, the words sit in a sized <span> inside the paragraph. If the paragraph itself were sized, its 1em margins would grow with the text (38px for small, 48px for huge).
    • The 57px from the message to the button, and the text's own line heights (22.39px at 16px), were already like that before this change.
