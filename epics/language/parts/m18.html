1. Open test.spell.app, then the Solitaire example and Card.spell (Debug, the bubbly look on).
  2. Scroll to group "Suit". Check that the card's purple rail, the group's grey rail and the bands of its "suit" / its "color" are evenly apart.
  3. Check that its has its band's usual room before it, and that it "is a (suit)" stands clear of the line to its left.
  4. Click just left of its in its "suit". The caret should blink right beside the i. Press End, then Home: it should be beside the i again.
  5. Click the blank line between "♥" if it is a heart and "♠" otherwise (add one if there isn't one). The caret should jump in to the choices' indent.
  6. Press Backspace: the caret should go out one level and stay there. Press the down arrow, then the up arrow: the line should be empty again, and snap back in.
  7. Type a letter on the snapped line, then press Cmd+Z once. Only the letter should go.
