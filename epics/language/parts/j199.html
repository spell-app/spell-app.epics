Spell UI's menus always showed an action's text: <ui-action> always drew its title, and a menu's icon keyword only centres items that have no words. So Owen's <action "undo" icon-only /> in a <context-menu> had nothing to turn on (and the compiler dropped the attribute anyway).

  • Chosen: <ui-action icon-only> shows its icon alone, its title (and keys) its tooltip and accessible name; in a vertical menu, icon-only rows next to each other share ONE row, as many context menus put Undo and Redo.
  • An icon-only row with no icon shows its title: an empty row would be no use.
  • A context-menu item's attributes are literals only (title="Restart", bare icon-only): a {...} value is refused, since the row is made when the menu shows.
  • Not chosen: each icon-only row alone on its line, its icon at the left -- it looked like a row missing its text.
Right-click Solitaire's board: Undo and Redo are two icons side by side under the divider, their names in their tooltips.
