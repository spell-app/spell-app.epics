1. Run spell server start all in this worktree. Open the editor at the address in .spell-server.editor.json (the kit's root), go to /edit/@spell/examples/Solitaire, and open Card.spell with the bubbly look on (code pane '...' menu, Settings…, Bubbly look).
  2. Click the blank line between the last line of its "symbol" is ("♠" otherwise) and group "Names". Type group : the line should read group "", drawn inside the card's white body, with the caret between the quotes.
  3. Type Foo": the " steps over the closing quote. The line ends group "Foo", indented one tab.
  4. Press Enter, then type its : you get its "". Press Cmd+Z once: it's back to its .
  5. Undo everything (Cmd+Z), since Solitaire is a built-in example.
