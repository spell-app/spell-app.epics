Python has no Intl, so spell_core's dates (P63) chose a hand-copied table of 25 regions (first day, clock, weekend, English's month-or-day order, from Intl.Locale) and written-out English and Spanish words, over adding Babel (an outside package, which decision Q33 rules out).

  • A region not in the table reads as Monday, a 24-hour clock, Saturday-Sunday, day first.
  • A program in another language says its dates in English.
  • Small Intl differences are not copied: en-AU / en-IE's unpadded short dates, en-CA's p.m., es-CL's 07-11-25, es-MX's 'vie, 7 de nov de 2025'.
  • The long time zone name comes from a short table of common zones, else the short name.
