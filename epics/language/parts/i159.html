Proposed fix (language-caret2):

  • Cause: a letter typed on a blank line takes the next line's indent (indentToAdd()), so it lands in the body the line is drawn in. After Backspace took the line out a level, that help put the level back.
  • Fix: the editor remembers the blank line Backspace (or Shift+Tab) took out a level, until the caret leaves it or something is typed there; a letter typed on it gets no indent added, so it lands where the caret is drawn. Leaving and coming back snaps it into the body again, as before.
