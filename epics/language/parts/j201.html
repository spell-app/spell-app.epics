T139 (d6c74e0da): when creating a card image makes its card from its rank and suit, or takes the card's rank and suit.

  • Given a different card later: its picture and colour follow the new card, but its rank and suit keep the first card's.
  • Its rank or suit changed later: the picture doesn't change, since its card was made as it was created.
  • A face-down one makes no card: a back needs none.
Nothing in Solitaire does either today; each card's front and the placeholders draw as before.
