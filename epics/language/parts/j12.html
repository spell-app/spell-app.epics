Mouse down, up, enter and leave run at once, as drag moves do (J3): a hover must not replace a waiting click. A click nothing takes skips the queue too, so it can't replace one either. A reorder's several drops (heardPlaced) run at once, since "only the latest" would lose all but one.

In a drawing, only a handler written async holds the queue: () => core.notify("Saved!") hands back its toast's promise and shouldn't hold clicks until the toast goes.
