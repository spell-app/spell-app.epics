Task List's first try saved the checklist itself (a browser checklist is a checklist: - it lives in browser storage, tasks belonging to it): after a reload, Loader.load(): nothing is saved as "Task#task-...", and the list came back empty. So each task is saved on its own (a browser task is a task: - it lives in browser storage), and the in-memory checklist is filled from them: the order you drag them into isn't kept over a reload (they come back in the place's order).
