Owen, 2026-11-15: where a card came from is the card's last pile (Spanish la última pila de la carta).

  • Added by belongs to one pile: no Spell text in Card.spell.
  • Any move sets it (add, move, put, the deal): the list it was in before its latest move, kept until its next; nothing before its first.
  • Part of the undo step: undo puts it back.
  • Read-only, as the card's pile: shown in hover, the Inspector and the Scope Explorer.
  • Solitaire's hook reads it: if the card's last pile is a column and it is not empty / turn its last card face up.
