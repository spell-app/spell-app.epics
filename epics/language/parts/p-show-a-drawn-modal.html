<modal "you won"> names the modal and is its title too; show "you won" (and hide "you won") shows it. "show" stays open for other things, e.g. a menu. Solitaire's win becomes that modal, drawn before the context menu.

  • A modal, a menu or a popup is named by its quoted word: <modal "you won"> draws id="you-won" and the heading "you won"; <menu "game menu"> its id alone; in Spanish too (<emergente "pista">, its heading encabezado). A quoted word on any other tag is still unreadable.
  • show "you won" / hide "you won" (Spanish mostrar / ocultar): a quoted name, nothing else, compiled to core.showDrawn() / core.hideDrawn(), which find it by its id in the page or inside a view. A program's own to show (word) still wins.
  • Python: core.show_drawn() asks its page to show it, not waiting; the page runs the same drawing.
