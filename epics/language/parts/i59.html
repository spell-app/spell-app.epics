The accepted plan named a new core helper, core.randomNumberBetween(low, high). The core already had core.randomNumber(min, max): whole numbers, both ends included, and Python's random_number() twin, seeded the same. So a random number between 1 and 6 compiles to it, and it learned to swap ends given the wrong way round, rather than adding a second helper (WWOD §2 DRY). Say if you'd rather have the new name.
