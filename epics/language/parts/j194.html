Under `return`, the lines `- 10 if ...` / `- 0 otherwise` (or without their `- `) are the same value lines a property's ladder uses (`value_if`, `value_otherwise`), compiling to the same if/return chain. A property's own ladder (`its "color" is` + `red if ...`) still takes NO `- `: its bulleted lines are its members (`- it "is (color)"`), so a bullet there can't also mean a value line.

Owen's `return` + `- 10 if ...` ladder compiles; the property ladder is unchanged.
