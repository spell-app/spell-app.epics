Owen, 2026-11-16: "Can we vary line heights, e.g. to add some space between the pill blocks?"

His screenshot: Card.spell's its "color" is block, its foot touching the next block's band (its "picture" is). CodeMirror lets each line have its own height (I120's description rows already do it), so the editor can match the drawn-bubbly page's spacing between blocks.

Done: a band right under a block's foot now stands 8px further down, so the two blocks stand apart.

  • Card.spell's its "color" is, then its "picture" is: foot to band 11.0px, was 3.0px.
  • Not between a band and its own body, nor above a plain line or a blank line.
  • 8px is a blank line's height on the page at the editor's size, rounded to a whole pixel, so the lines under it don't drift. One setting: --cmb-block-gap.
  • The drawn-bubbly page has no such room yet: it measures 2.97px there, as the editor did.

Where the room is: in the foot's row, under the foot, not in the band's line.

  • So the band's fold arrow stays centred on the band.
  • The rails round them run on through it.
  • Down and Up arrows step from the line above to the band and back.
  • A click in the room puts the caret at the end of the line above, as a click on a foot always has.
