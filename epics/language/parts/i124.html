Fixed (12c03dc57). The selection now sits centred on the bands and pills. It is a see-through black, as you asked: "Can we make the selection black so it doesn't clash with the pills?"

  • Why it sat high: CodeMirror draws the selection from the letters, and in the bubbly look the letters sit at the top of the band. So the selection started at the band's top and stopped about 2px short of its bottom. On <card-image direction=down /> it covered 869.00–888.28 on a band of 869.00–890.56. Selecting a whole line pushed it lower again, because the tabs before the words aren't lifted along with them.
  • The fix: the editor now draws the selection itself, using CodeMirror's own pieces, each moved onto the band of its line. It is centred to 0.01px on Spell bands and on tag bands. When a selection spans several lines, the pieces still meet with no gaps.
  • Colour: black with no hue, so the pill colours show through it unchanged. It is 18% while the editor has focus and 10% when it doesn't.
  • Dark mode: black can't show on the dark paper, so there it's a see-through white instead (16% with focus, 10% without).
  • Also fixed: the page's own selection style (from Semantic UI's sheet) was turning all selected text near-black. On the code pills and on dark paper the words disappeared. Every letter now keeps its own colour when selected.
  • Checked with headless screenshots over white paper, purple and cream bands, white pills and code pills, light and dark, with and without focus.
