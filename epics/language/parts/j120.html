VS Code can't draw the band behind a head, so the white pill would vanish on white paper: the pill takes its band's colour (--bubbly-pill of its part of speech) and a 1px edge in its hue, at 40% (alpha 66). Its background is the text's own, so it hugs the letters; the page's 12px round ends would cut into them, so 5px. The room is a no-break space before and after it, an attachment, never padding. A quoted input's own quote marks stay, faint, outside the pill.
