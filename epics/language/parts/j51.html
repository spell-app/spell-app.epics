It was four outline-style .es.spell files with its own direction, flip and debug prints. Now Carta, Carta.editor, Comodines, Mazo, Pila and Solitario .es.spellml, each mirroring its English file:

  • the card a vista reversible (the built-in two sided view), its two sides drawn with <dibujar "frente"/> and <dibujar "dorso"/>; the joker a subclass in a file of its own
  • its own para depurarlo, imprimir lines and dirección property gone, as English has none
  • Spanish bare tags (<texto>, <contenedor>, <cuadricula>, <columna/>), so they draw as es-* as before
  • its property editor uses Spell UI's English <ui-property> tags with Spanish labels, as the Inspector's test reads one
  • its code-spell twins moved from tests/spell/__spellx__/Solitario/ to __spell__/Solitario/, and the spellx test's C44 known gap is gone: both directions compile the same now
  • READMEs: README-spanish's outputs, quotes and links follow; its two pictures (card-and-carta.png, solitario-editor.png) and README-examples' Solitario picture still show the outline version
