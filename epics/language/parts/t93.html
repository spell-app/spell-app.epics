Owen asked (2026-11-11) for this to parse:

its "color" is one of red, black
    red if it is a diamond or it is a heart
    black otherwise
  • Today, without a colon at the end of the first line, it's a STORED property, and the two lines under it are parse errors: "Don't understand ..." and "A line of a getter's body: put it under one".
  • With the colon (... red, black:) it compiles to a worked-out color: red for diamonds and hearts, else black. The leading - made no difference either way (checked with all four combinations).
  • Change: property_value_getter takes the line without its colon when value lines (unbulleted) are indented under it; with nothing under it, it stays a stored property.
  • Members under such a property still need their - to tell them from value lines (PropertyValueGetter.parseNestedBlock()).
  • PARSING.md's "A worked-out value from a list of ITS OWN" bullet changes with it, and the rule's tests get a no-colon case.
  • The colon is optional, like the dash: an indented body is enough.
