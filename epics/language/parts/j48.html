PokéSpell says <ui-defaults><ui-button appearance="pill"/></ui-defaults>: the Surprise me! and guess buttons lose their hand-written border-radius: 2em, and the kind and Play buttons become pills too. PokéAPI keeps heights in tenths of a metre and weights in tenths of a kilogram, units Spell's dimensions don't have, so the page draws <dimension value={its height * 10} dimension="length" unit="cm"/> (and kg): a US reader now sees feet and pounds, not metres. Statusphere's six emoji buttons take their size and look from a <ui-defaults> too. The Met's artwork picture is size="large" (450px) where its CSS said at most 24rem (384px).
