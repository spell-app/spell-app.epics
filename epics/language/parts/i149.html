A tag's quote marks now sit where a Spell line's do: the opening one level with the tops of the letters, the closing one low, just past their baseline.

  • Before, on columns="7": the opening mark was entirely above the letters, 6.5px over their tops. The closing one was over their middle. Both were higher than a Spell line's (group "Names").
  • Now both are lowered: the opening mark by 7.5px, the closing by 4.5px. Measured by their ink in the real editor, on 7 and GameBar, light and dark: the opening mark's top is 1–1.5px under the letters' tops, and the closing mark ends 1.5px past the baseline. That's within 0.5px of a Spell line's marks.
  • Commit a4759cb48, merged. A CodeMirrorRails browser test holds every tag mark within 1px of a Spell line's. It fails without the fix, 7.6px off.
