The two end tags under the toolbar are now one row of matching feet: the same size, on one line, each on its own rail.

  • Why: your Solitaire has both end tags on one line, </></>. A row of feet was only built for end tags on lines of their own. So the first was drawn as a lone tiny foot, and the second as an inline end tag on a rail stub.
  • Now end tags sharing a line make the same row as end tags on separate lines. A stray space before them doesn't matter.
  • Measured in the real editor, light and dark: both feet are 22.6 x 14.1px, their tops level, the row 29px. That's exactly how the same end tags draw on two lines. Before, they were 9.4px and 21.6px tall.
  • Commit 59b0512c5, merged. The CodeMirrorRails browser test compares the two layouts, foot by foot.
