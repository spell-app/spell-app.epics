For P66 · Choose From a List. You wrote "for list of views, show the views (?)": the question mark says you weren't sure, so this asks.

A view is something the program draws, like a card drawn on the table. When a program writes choose a card from the cards and each card has a drawing, the modal can show each row as its drawing, or as its name.

  • The modal shows the cards as they look on the table, small, side by side; a click picks one.
  • Things with no drawing still show their name, as any thing does.
  • Cost: the modal draws each view a second time, smaller; about an hour more.
  • A drawn card shows its title or name, the same as a card with no drawing.
  • Simplest; drawings in the modal can come later.
  • Built as B in this phase; a new todo for A.
  • A list of drawn things shows their drawings in the modal, each one a button to pick
  • Things with no drawing show their title, else their name, else themselves as text
