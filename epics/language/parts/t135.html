Owen's Solitaire draft splits a foundation's droppable rule over several lines. A line indented under a condition that STARTS with and / or (Spanish y / o) continues the expression above it, any number of them; a trailing and over 3+ lines joins too; and a lone the card is ... line in a yes-or-no rule's body asks, rather than setting the card.

a line starting `and` / `or` goes on from the condition above it, any number of them (`JoinedLines`, Spanish `y` / `o` too); a line ending in `and` goes on over 3+ lines at one indent (it stopped at the second); in a body answering yes or no (`it is droppable when`, `it "is X" if`), a last line `the card is ...` asks, it doesn't set the card (`answersYesOrNo()`, `readsAsQuestion`); the bubbly look draws a control line's continued lines in its head, not its body
