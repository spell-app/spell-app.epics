Found by the I157 agent (blank-line caret), 2026-11-17; Owen: "Can we fix the caret definitively?"

Proposed fix (language-caret2):

  • Cause: the caret's snap writes the line's tabs at once, but the editor's drawing only moves what was already drawn with the text. The empty line had nothing drawn on it, so the room after its tabs (and the caret's place past it) came only with the next layout, after the typing paused and the file was parsed again.
  • Fix: every edit that leaves a blank line in a body (the snap, Enter) draws that line at once, as the next layout will: the room after its tabs and the caret past it, or a group's own line, or nothing between a drawing's tags. Its look is read from the lines round it. A letter typed with its indent on a blank line gets its room at once too.
  • Also: Enter at the end of a line ending in a quoted name (to "reset the stock") drew the new line inside the name's white pill till the layout came; those marks now end at the line's end.
  • A test Enters a blank line after every line of a sample file and checks the caret is drawn at once where the layout puts it.
