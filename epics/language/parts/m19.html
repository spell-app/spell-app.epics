In a Terminal, from the language worktree:

cd ~/www/spell.worktrees/language
node bin/spell.mjs run @spell/examples/Solitaire --target py

It opens the game in your browser. Note the toolbar's Undo and Redo arrows (right of Restart): both greyed out. Click the stock (top left) to turn a card. Then press the Undo arrow, then Redo. Also right-click the board and look at the menu's Undo and Redo.

After the stock click, Undo turns dark and its tooltip says Undo Turn Card (Ctrl+Z or ⌘Z). Undo puts the card back on the stock face down, Undo greys out and Redo lights up (Redo Turn Card); Redo turns it over again. The right-click menu's Undo and Redo say the same. ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z do the same while the board has the focus.
