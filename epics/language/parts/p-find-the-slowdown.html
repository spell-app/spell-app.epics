The causes, each a fix for P22:

  1. More words, tried everywhere. Each new rule is one more alternative tried at every place its group is: a statement on every line, an operand inside every expression. data's places rules alone are tried 6,549 times each on these tests (total_of, people_here, their_field ...), P16's possessives about 7,000, P19's define_menu 15,000 more. Nearly every try is turned away by its quick test, but each costs its walk: a few small arrays a try. Fix: a sequence that starts with a word checks just that word first, with nothing made.
  2. Every new scope rebuilds the language's rule map. Each rule test makes a scope; its parser's first look at its rules copied every rule group of the language, and a rule the test declares (a type, a property) copied them all again. The more rules, the longer. Fix: a scope with no rules of its own shares the language's map, and groups are shared until one changes.
  3. The waiting pass reads every program in full, several times. P9's waiting pass and data's read gathering walk each compiled program's whole tree 3 to 4 times, making an array at every node. Fix: walk without making the arrays.
