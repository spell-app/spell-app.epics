owenoak: “I'd prefer if `it lives` lined up where `it's name` does”

A plain body line's first word now lines up with the words on the bands at its level: it lives starts where its does. The bands didn't move.

  • Measured on StarWars' character in the CodeMirror demo, light and dark: it lives starts at 48.69px, the same as its (0.00px apart). Before, it started at 40.25px, a tab further left.
  • The same holds for every plain line in a body, at every level: comments (// face down at first beside its "rank") and steps (turn it face down under if it is face up).
  • Only the words moved, not the line. Column 0 is still on the rail (I140), and the caret before the first word is drawn at the word (I141).
  • The trade-off: a body's plain lines no longer line up under their head's words. They're now a tab further in, where a band line in that body already was.
  • Not changed: a line outside any block, a group's own notes (already lined up with its members, I134), and a drawing's tags.
  • Commit 480f888fd. The CodeMirrorRails browser test checks a plain line against a band line at three levels.
