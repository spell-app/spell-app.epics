An action's default title names each blank by the kind it takes, with no article: to put (a card) on (a pile) is Put Card on Pile.

  • A blank of a built-in kind (text, number) says its own name instead, as its kind says less: to append (digit as text) to (a calculator) is Append Digit to Calculator, not Append Text to Calculator; to double (n as a number) is Double N.
  • A (with ...) clause, the call's optional extras, says nothing: to notify (with message = "nope") is Notify.
  • A bare a card blank, no parentheses, is a blank too: to play a card is Play Card.
