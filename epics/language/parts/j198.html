T138's `card image` (Card.spell, Carta.es.spell) is the one place a card's look lives:

  • the unicode letter list, `picture`, moved off the card into it (its tests with it): real pictures would change only it
  • given a card it draws that card's rank and suit, but its OWN direction, face up unless `direction=down`: a card is two sided and turns itself, so each side is one image (`<card-image card={it} />`, `<card-image direction=down />`), not the card as it lies
  • `its face` is its card, else a new card of its rank and suit
  • the placeholder kept its pale look by styles.css (`--ui-red-text` / black / blue as `currentColor` on `::part(placeholder)`): the image's own ink would have drawn a black or red ace there
The cards and placeholders look exactly as before; swapping in real card pictures is one view's change.
