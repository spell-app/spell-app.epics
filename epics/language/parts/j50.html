Before, the menu bar was hand-written items: a header, Moves, Score, then three icon-only <item onClick>s repeating the game menu's actions. Now each action says its title and icon once (Restart / redo, Auto play / fast forward, Cheat / eye), and <toolbar menu="game menu" shrinkable> draws them, the right-click menu the same.

  • What you'll see: each button shows its icon, its title and its key cap (Restart N), where before an icon alone with a tooltip.
  • The score sits at the bar's right (slot="right"): in its center slot it ran into the title (the slots are 0.25em apart).
  • The game menu lost its - divider (I26).
  • At phone width the buttons fold into … (I26); a Python page has none (I22).
  • Solitario the same, in Spanish: <barra-herramientas menu="menú del juego" encogible>.
