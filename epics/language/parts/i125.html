Owen, 2026-11-16: "bottom footers overlap".

His screenshot: under the color of seven should be red (Card.spell's to test card setup, its last line, the file's thing ending there too), a green foot and a purple one curl round at the same place, one over the other, instead of nesting as Monaco drew them.

Why they overlapped: when several bodies ended on one line, the editor gave each a foot row 5px tall, one under the other.

  • In Card.spell, three bodies end at the color of seven should be red: the test, the group "Drawing" round it, and the card.
  • The group hangs on the card's rail, so its foot and the card's sat at the same place across, 5px apart down: one grey foot with a purple one curled over it.
  • And 5px is less than a body's corner, so an outer foot's corner reached over the inner one.

Now, as Monaco and the drawn-bubbly page do it:

  • Each foot goes under the one inside it, as far down as their rails are apart across: 8.44px a level, measured (the page: 7.97px, its own tab).
  • A group ending its type's body ends in ONE foot, where the type's would be; the type's rail ends beside it.
  • A group's foot is the colour of the rail it turns out of, its type's (it was grey).
  • A stack of feet still ends on a whole pixel, so the lines under it don't drift: 13px under the test, 22px under Solitaire's for row / for column / if.
