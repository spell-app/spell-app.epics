  • Weights, italics and colours come from token classes, as colours do today.
  • The input pills are decorations, which take any CSS.
  • Curly quotes are drawn over the real ": its own text transparent, the curly mark drawn on top.
  • NEVER padding or margin on real text: it moves the caret off the letters. Room around a pill is content injected before or after it, with a set width.
  • The block shapes (the gutter bar, the white body, the rounded corners, the foot) are an overlay layer under the text, laid out from each line's top (getTopForLineNumber()) and each column's left (getOffsetForColumn()), redrawn on scroll and on every edit.
  • The gaps between blocks are view zones, so lines don't overlap the shapes.
  • It stays fast on Solitaire's longest file; a setting turns it off.
