Defaults could apply page-wide, or per root: each <ui-root> (an app, a view drawn as its own app, the editor's run pane) gives its subtree its own, the nearest root winning, as icon packs do today.

  • Per root: the editor's own buttons stay as they are while the running app's are pills; two apps on one page can differ.
  • Page-wide: simpler, but an app's theme would restyle the editor around it.

A: per root, nested roots inheriting what they don't set.
