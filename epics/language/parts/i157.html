Proposed fix (agent language-caret):

  • What's wrong, measured on the demo page: a blank line of tabs in a body already has the room after its tabs that a plain line has (I142). But CodeMirror draws the caret at the end of the tabs themselves, before that room: nothing comes after it on the line to draw the caret against.
    • In the for each body (Owen's picture): the caret at 65.56px, the words of the line above at 74.00px, a tab (8.44px) apart. A letter typed there lands at 74.00px.
  • The fix: a blank body line gets a zero-width marker right after that room, at its end. CodeMirror draws the caret against it, so the caret is where the line's words will go.
    • The same rule as a plain line's (one room, cmb-plain-indent), plus the marker. So a plain line, a head band and a blank line at one level put their words at one x.
    • A letter typed there goes after the marker, where the caret was: nothing jumps.
    • Clicked, arrowed onto, End, Home back to the indent's end, Backspace out a level: all drawn there.
  • Not changed:
    • Blank lines in a drawing, between its tags or right above them: a tag typed there starts at its indent. So the one right above a drawing's first tag loses its room too (it had it, so a typed < showed a tab in until the layout caught up).
    • A group's own lines (I134): already drawn where its members' words are.
    • The top level, the blank lines before a group's line (I121), HTML files, the look turned off.
