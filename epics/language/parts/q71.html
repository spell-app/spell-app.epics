  • Naming: both lines work, sound "x" is at "..." and its "x" is a sound at "...".
  • video and movie mean the same. Spanish sonido, vídeo / video, película.
  • The tag says source.
  • Play: play sound "x", play the "x" sound, no quotes, and play "x" alone (a name first, then a file).
  • Where a video plays: where it is drawn, else a new window; in a new window always opens one; full screen too.
  • Every extra word: speed, looping, and wait, stop / pause / resume, stop all sounds and stop all videos|movies, is playing / is done, start partway, volume, when it ends, its length.
  • Comment: a sound or video sends events (ends, plays, pauses ...) a program can catch, for custom players.
