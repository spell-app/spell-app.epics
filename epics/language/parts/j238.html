  • Why: the last card of each column is a candidate however it lies. A 6♦ on a face-up 7♠, with a 7♣ at the end of another column, can go there; next round it's that column's last card, and can go back.
  • T146's version only took a column's last card to a foundation, and moved a run only from its first face-up card. Saying 'a foundation can take it' has no plain words today: where any foundation can take it and where a foundation can take it don't parse.
