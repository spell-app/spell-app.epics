Fixed (964a5e263). The caret now shows in all four places around a quote: before and after the opening one, and before and after the closing one. That holds on a Spell band (it "is a number card") and inside a tag (<action "auto play" />), whether you arrow in either direction or click.

  • Why it was missing: since I115, each curly quote is a small box that stands in for the ", and the quote mark itself is drawn outside that box. CodeMirror measured the caret from the box, and so did the empty spacers beside it. All of them are 0px tall, so the caret was drawn 0px tall. You saw it most right after a closing quote.
  • The fix: the quote and the spacer now tell CodeMirror where a caret beside them goes. It is as tall as the letters next to it. Outside a quote it sits at the mark's outer edge. Inside a pill it sits at the edge of the words.
  • Measured headless in this worktree's editor: every stop draws an 18px caret, each arrow press moves it further along, and it lands in the same place whichever direction you came from. Nothing else looks different.
  • One small thing left: when you step back over a tag's closing quote, the caret can sit just past the mark instead of right at its edge. That spot comes from CodeMirror's own invisible markers, which this fix can't move. The caret is still visible and full height there.
