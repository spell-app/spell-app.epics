Owen's C1 on I47 asked for percent as a dimension. It's a sixth built-in dimension, proportion, whose base unit is fraction and whose percent (aka %, Spanish por ciento) is 1/100 of it.

  • Every proportion is KEPT as its fraction: 18% compiles to 0.18, and its "tip" is a percent (or a proportion in percent) holds 0.18. So the bill * the tip is the tip, with no / 100, as a spreadsheet keeps a percent; its tip in percent gives 18.
  • The other dimensions keep a measure in the unit it's declared in (172 cm stays 172); a percent is the one exception, so arithmetic works.
  • Shown with Intl's percent style in the reader's own language (18%, Spanish 18 %), the same for US and metric readers.
  • Editing: a new <ui-input type="percent">, a number field whose value is the fraction, showing 18 with a % label.
  • A source's percent field (Open-Meteo's humidity, 65) stays a plain number, as its 65 means 65%, not 6500%.
