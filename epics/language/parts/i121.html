Owen, 2026-11-16: "When I click or move in the blank line under a group, auto-tabbing makes the text part of the group."

His screenshots: the cursor in the blank line after a group's body sits at the body's indent, inside the group's rail, not at the margin where the next group starts.

The cause: not the file, and not Enter. The blank lines in Solitaire hold nothing (no tabs or spaces).

  • One of the typing helps snaps the caret into the body a blank line is drawn in: clicked or arrowed onto, a blank line got the NEXT line's indent.
  • Under a group, the next line is the next group, one tab in: the same level as the group's members.
  • So the caret sat one tab in, in the group above's rail, and what you typed became one of its members.

The fix: a blank line before a group's line doesn't snap any more.

  • Clicked or arrowed onto, nothing is written and the caret stays at the margin.
  • Typing a letter there still indents it to the group's level, so group "X" lands in the right place. Anything else typed there still joins the group above: in Spell a group holds everything up to the next group.
  • Enter at the end of a body's last line still indents, as before.
  • The rail: checked. The group above's rail stops at its last member, so the blank line isn't drawn inside it.
  • Spanish files too (grupo).
