After a modal, prompt or confirm closes, the program can't tell what was clicked or what was typed or chosen; choose isn't built.

  • show "edit card" with the card: the modal edits the thing itself; after it closes it is that thing.
  • the result: the button clicked (ok, cancel, or its own word: <action discard title="Discard your changes" />, if the result is discard).
  • An action's return sets the result too: deal the cards, then if the result is ....
  • confirm, prompt and choose answer the same way: the result, the value in it; choose built.
  • The texts of prompt, alert / message, confirm, choose, notify are Markdown.

HyperTalk's way: the result is the button clicked (and what an action returns), it the value; a modal shown with a thing edits it; their texts are Markdown.

  • 1, HyperTalk's split: prompt puts the text typed in a NEW it (as get does; empty text when cancelled), and the result is ok or cancel; confirm leaves it alone (J260, J270).
  • 2, a button's own word: <action discard title="Discard your changes" /> answers discard: a bare word written first, on an action with no quoted word, not one of the tag's or Spell UI's own; it just closes, labelled by its title, else Discard. if the result is discard compares any bare word that isn't a variable (J261, J262).
  • 3, show ... with: show "edit card" with the card (mostrar ... con ...): inside the modal it is the card itself, so Cancel undoes nothing; after it, it is the card. Python sends it to its page by its id (J263, J265, J266).
  • 4, an action's value: the result only when it runs as a statement, on its own line or as an inline body; used as a value, or as its action's last line, it goes where it's written (Q70, J264).
  • 5, Markdown: prompt and notify messages are Markdown too, as alert / warn / confirm already were (J271).
  • Drive-by: Python's statements grouped inside a function may await (J268). Left out, red: a comma after an if's condition (J269).
  • Checkers, Solitaire and Solitario snapshots re-blessed: their play calls now keep what they give back as the result.
