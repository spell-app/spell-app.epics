A program plays a sound only by its file's address (play "cry.ogg"), and can't name a sound or a video once and play it by that name, nor play a video at all.

Owen's words, 2026-11-18: sound, movie or video names become page or app properties; play them either way round; quotes optional but preferred; video in a new window; HyperCard's and LiveCode's words looked at for more, like at 1.5x.

Owen's picks are on the questions page, Sounds and videos (Q71). What a program will be able to say, English, then Spanish:

  • Name one, on the page or the app, as a line, either way round: sound "card slide" is at "sounds/slide.mp3", video "intro" is at "https://...", its "card slide" is a sound at "...". Spanish: sonido "deslizar" está en "...", su "intro" es un vídeo en "...".
    • The name becomes a property of the page, or the app, so its card slide reads it too.
    • movie is the same word as video; Spanish vídeo, video, película.
  • Or as a tag, in a drawing: <sound "card slide" source="..." />, <video "intro" source="..." /> (and <movie>). A sound's tag draws nothing; a video's draws the video right there. <video "intro" /> with no source draws one named elsewhere.
  • Play it: play sound "card slide", play the "card slide" sound, without quotes when the words are one of the names (play the card slide sound), and play "card slide" alone, which looks for a name first, then plays a file at that address, as today. Spanish: reproducir el sonido "deslizar".
  • Where a video plays: where the page draws it, if it does; else a new window. in a new window always opens one: a see-through framed modal on a desktop, full screen on a phone, with its controls. full screen plays it full screen everywhere.
  • More words on play, in this order, each optional:
    • speed: at 1.5x, at half speed, at double speed
    • start partway: from 0:30 (minutes and seconds)
    • volume: at 50% volume, quietly, muted
    • looping: over and over, looping
    • where: in a new window, full screen
    • waiting: and wait, so the next line runs once it ends
  • Stop and go on: stop sound "x", pause video "intro", resume video "intro", stop all sounds, stop all videos / movies.
  • Ask about it: if the "intro" video is playing, ... is done; the length of the "intro" video, a duration (so as a duration works).
  • Hear it: a sound or video SENDS EVENTS a program can catch, to build its own player: when the "intro" video ends, ... starts playing, ... pauses; the same as on intro video ends.
  • Name a sound or a video on a page or the app, either way round: sound "card slide" is at "sounds/slide.mp3", its "intro" is a video at "intro.mp4"; Spanish sonido "deslizar" está en "...", su "intro" es una película en "...". The name is a property of the page or app, made with it, so it's found by its name at once.
  • Or with a tag in a drawing: <sound "boing" source="boing.mp3" /> (draws nothing), <video "intro" source="intro.mp4" /> (draws the video there); <video "intro" /> draws one named elsewhere. Spanish <sonido>, <vídeo>, <película>.
  • Play it four ways: play sound "card slide", play the "card slide" sound, play the card slide sound, play "card slide" (a name first, then a file). Spanish reproducir el sonido "deslizar", el vídeo llamado "intro". A name nobody gives won't compile.
  • After it, each optional: at 1.5x / at half speed / at double speed, from 0:30, at 50% volume / quietly / muted, over and over / looping, in a new window / full screen, and wait. Spanish a 1.5x, al doble de velocidad, desde 0:30, al 50% de volumen, bajito, en silencio, una y otra vez, en una ventana nueva, a pantalla completa, y esperar.
  • A video plays where the page draws it, else in a new window: a see-through framed window on a desktop, full screen on a phone, with its controls; closing it stops the video, the video ending closes it (J280).
  • stop sound "x", pause video "intro", resume video "intro", stop all sounds, stop all videos / movies; Spanish parar, pausar, reanudar, parar todos los sonidos.
  • if the "intro" video is playing / is done; the length of the "intro" video, a duration. Spanish está sonando, está terminado, la duración del vídeo "intro".
  • Each named one sends events as it plays, for a program's own player: when the "intro" video ends, starts playing, pauses; on intro video ends hears the same. Spanish cuando el vídeo "intro" termina.
  • The core's new media.ts runs it all (the sound part of ui.ts moved there); Python's core lists the new calls as not ported.
