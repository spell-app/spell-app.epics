For epic language's P44 · Drawing Attributes By Type. Once a yes-or-no, number, list, thing or handler attribute reads its quotes as Spell, should the built-in projects be rewritten to use quotes there?

  • A: yes, for those (about 160 of the 427 braced attributes): disabled={its count is 0} becomes disabled="its count is 0", so the examples people copy show quotes. A worked-out text keeps its braces, label={its title}.
  • B: yes, and text too: a worked-out text becomes a fill-in in quotes, label="[its title]", link="/recipes/[its id]", so the examples have almost no braces left.
  • C: no: quotes work, but the examples keep their braces.
  • About 160 attributes rewritten by the codemod; the examples still show braces on worked-out text.
  • About 230 rewritten; braces stay only where a value can't be a fill-in, such as a list's or a thing's own text.
  • Nothing rewritten; people learn quotes only from the docs.
