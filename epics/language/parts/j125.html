Owen typed group "foobar" on a blank line the bubbly look drew inside the card, but the line had no tab, so it went to the top level. Now the first letter typed on a blank line (tabs and spaces only) gets the next non-blank line's indent written before it, in the same undo step. It works in every Spell editor, look on or off, code and page files, because the indent decides what the line belongs to. Monaco's trimAutoWhitespace stays on: Enter keeps a body's indent (checked), and a line left untyped goes back to empty, which this help then fills in.

Works with the look off too. A blank line before a line that leaves a body is drawn outside it, so the two agree.

Needs the look, and the layout waits 300ms after typing.

  • To write a top-level line between two members, delete the tab after the first letter.
