A question (one underline with the thing it asks of, its words plain): a project's phrases (is on top, is face up), is a a kind, is a a list's value, is empty, is defined, exists, can take, can give up, there is a. Everything else combines two values, each underlined on its own: is (equals), is not, >, + - * /, is in, includes, starts with, is the same as. One row each in BubblyPresentation, so a change is one line.

`the card is in the stock` shows two underlines, `the card` and `the stock`, not one question.
