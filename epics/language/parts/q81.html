For P68 · Upgrading Old Spell. How loud is an old form before it's rewritten?

Today a retired word is an ERROR: the program doesn't compile until it's fixed. T166's same-line // comment still means what it did, so it could keep working while it says so.

  • A yellow underline naming the new form and the date it changed: "Same-line comments are old (2026-11-19): put it on the next line as --".
  • It reads as before; Upgrade or the quick fix rewrites it.
  • Retired words stay errors: their old meaning is gone.
  • Every old form stops the compile until fixed. One rule for all, but an old example breaks the day a form changes.
  • No underline: the editor rewrites old forms whenever it saves a file. Files nobody opens keep them.
  • An old form still works, underlined yellow with its date and its new form, until a fix rewrites it.
