Three additions to the markdown element, so a page like the Design Guide needs no script of its own for titles and headings.

  • skip-title, a boolean attribute: drops the text's leading title before rendering.
    • An ATX title (# Title) or a setext title.
    • The content, saving, the editor and the dirty flag are untouched, unlike the page's strip-and-set-content.
    • A level-2 heading first, or a paragraph before the title, is left alone.
  • reveal(id) on the element: scrolls to the rendered heading, and puts #id in the address.
    • It replaces the address (replaceState), as a #id link inside does.
    • It returns false for an unknown id.
  • The address: a #id naming one of its headings is revealed.
    • After the FIRST render.
    • And on every hashchange, listened to while connected, unless the PAGE has an element with that id.
    • So a table of contents outside can be plain <a href="#id"> links, with no script.
  • The Design Guide's title strip (on ui-load), its click handler, and its shadow-root reveal HACK can all go.
  • A markdown element now scrolls the page on load when the address names one of its headings, and on any hashchange that does.
