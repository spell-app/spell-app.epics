• skip-title (boolean attribute): drops the text's leading # title (ATX # Title or a setext title) before rendering; content, save(), the editor and dirty are untouched, unlike the page's strip-and-set-content. A ## first, or a paragraph before the title, is left alone.
  • reveal(id) on the element: scrolls to the rendered heading and puts #id in the address (replaceState), as a #id link inside does; returns false for an unknown id.
  • The address: after the FIRST render, a #id naming one of its headings is revealed; and on every hashchange (listened to while connected), unless the PAGE has an element with that id. So a table of contents outside can be plain <a href="#id">s with no script.

  • a <ui-markdown> now scrolls the page on load when the address names one of its headings, and on any hashchange that does
