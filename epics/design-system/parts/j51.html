Three small button gaps, one judgement:

  • download (string): forwarded to the inner <a> when href is set, bare or naming the saved file, as native <a download> (the fallback too).
  • --ui-button-font-family / --ui-button-font-size: public tokens read by the button box (default the page font and --ui-font-size); size still scales the size token.
  • host.click(): a click dispatched AT the host (script, <label for>, <ui-input>'s implicit submission) now presses the inner control, as click() on a native button does. The control's click stays inside the shadow root, so listeners see ONE click (the host's own, whatever order they were added in); an earlier listener's preventDefault() vetoes the press. Clicks from inside are untouched; a disabled button ignores click() natively. Chosen over special-casing <ui-input>: any caller of click() (a modal's Enter, tests, frameworks) now works.

Net effect:

  • Logo can write <ui-button href="....svg" download> and drop its click handler.
  • Spell App: --ui-button-font-family: var(--ui-font-family-heading) (or the brand's Palatino stack) and --ui-button-font-size: 15px on "+ Add habit"; Enter in the "New habit…" field submits, so the capture keydown HACK can go.
