Three small button gaps, filled with one judgement:

  • download, a string: forwarded to the inner link when href is set.
    • Bare, or naming the saved file, as a native link's download; the fallback too.
  • Two public tokens, read by the button box: --ui-button-font-family and --ui-button-font-size.
    • Their defaults: the page's font, and --ui-font-size.
    • size still scales the size token.
  • host.click() now presses the inner control, as a native button's click() does.
    • A click sent AT the host: from a script, a <label for>, or the input's implicit submission.
    • The control's click stays inside the shadow root, so listeners see ONE click: the host's own, whatever order they were added in.
    • An earlier listener's preventDefault() vetoes the press.
    • Clicks from inside are untouched; a disabled button ignores click() natively.
    • Chosen over special-casing the input: any caller of click() now works (a modal's Enter, tests, frameworks).
  • Logo can write <ui-button href="....svg" download>, and drop its click handler.
  • Spell App's "+ Add habit" can set the button's font tokens:
    • --ui-button-font-family: var(--ui-font-family-heading), or the brand's Palatino stack
    • --ui-button-font-size: 15px
  • Enter in the "New habit…" field submits, so the capture keydown HACK can go.
