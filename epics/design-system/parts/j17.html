P5's Brand Palette copy (Brand Palette.spell.html) computes its colours in the page script, with no colour range element: no ladder is the point on this page.

  • The page shows 5-chip palettes and specimens.
  • The page script does the original's arithmetic on L / C / H.
    • E.g. Aubergine: L 27%, C × 0.65 capped at 0.11, the same hue.
    • Likewise Lilac, Lavender, soft, dark background, the House of Owen tints ...
  • Each colour becomes a hex through window.Palette.oklchToHex(), so every hex equals the original's.
    • Gamut-mapped as in the original: colours outside sRGB lose chroma.
    • Updated (was: oklch() text, before the bundle put Palette on the window, I7).
  • The chips show those hexes.
  • The specimens are real elements, taking those hexes as inline --ui-* tokens:
    • a one-item <ui-menu>: the Build nav item
    • <ui-checkbox selected>
    • a circular <ui-button>
    • <ui-header>
    • <ui-label>, for the id pill
  • Each specimen pane forces its own scheme, with the candidate's colour as its background.
    • It sets ui-light or ui-dark, and the background token.
    • So the theme's tokens read right inside it, on a light or a dark page.

Other differences from the original:

  • The contrast figures (white 5.9 / 5.5 / 8.9 / 6.5:1 AA), and every hex printed under a chip or a column name, come from Palette at load, as in the original.
  • Turn 2's per-row tint is three small stacked chips, not a 3-band gradient.
  • The original House of Owen beside each id is a 12px square chip, not a 12px-tall bar: chips are always square.
  • The sections sit on the brand's surface tint (--spell-surface-tint, the original's #F2F2FE lavender wash), and the page on the theme's background, not Claude Design's canvas chrome.
  • The pill's "+ Add habit" is a plus icon and "Add habit".
  • Every colour, chip and specimen, and every printed hex and contrast figure, matches the original's exactly (checked in the browser).
  • The page works in dark mode; the original is light only.
