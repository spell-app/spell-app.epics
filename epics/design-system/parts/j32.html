The phone element (<ui-brand-phone>, the Spell App Build screen's preview) takes three attributes.

  • time: the status bar's clock; default 9:41.
  • dimmed: the original's fade to 0.45 while the app builds.
    • Animated over 0.4 seconds; not animated under reduced motion.
    • It also sets aria-busy on the region: it means the app is being rebuilt.
    • Not "loading" or "busy": Fomantic's loading draws a spinner, which the original doesn't.
    • dimmed is Fomantic's word for a faded state.
  • label: the region's name; default "App preview".
  • No size or device variant: the pages need one phone.
    • The width is a token, --ui-brand-phone-width (300px).
  • The page writes <ui-brand-phone dimmed> while building, and removes dimmed when it's ready.
  • A screen reader hears a region "App preview"; the status bar is hidden from it.
  • Another device shape later is a new attribute, not a rename.
