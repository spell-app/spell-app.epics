What: <ui-input>'s implicit submission click()s the form's first submitter; for a <ui-button type="submit"> that is the HOST, and <ui-button> only submits from its inner button's click handler, so nothing happens (the click event fires, no submit).

Where: Spell App's add-habit form (the phone's "New habit…" field). Workaround in the page: a capture keydown on the form calls requestSubmit() (HACK comment).

Fix it needs: <ui-button> acts on a click dispatched at its host (a click listener on the host, or UIInput calling the button's own submit path / form.requestSubmit() for a custom-element submitter).
