A checklist with checkable makes every check in it a tickable checkbox: the phone's habits.

  • The markup: <ui-brand-checklist checkable>; a lone check takes checkable too.
  • Not toggle: in Spell UI a toggle is the switch look of the checkbox.
  • The ticked state follows Spell UI's rule, as on the checkbox.
    • selected is canonical, and reflects.
    • checked is its alias: the property reads and writes selected.
    • A checked attribute in markup ticks it, like a native checkbox's; it doesn't reflect.
  • The check sends ui-change, which bubbles through the list.
    • Its detail: { selected, checked, originalEvent }.
  • Each check is a button with role=checkbox, so Space AND Enter tick it; a native checkbox ignores Enter.
  • Pages write <ui-brand-check checked>Drink water</ui-brand-check>, and listen for ui-change on the list.
  • Script reads el.checked or el.selected.
  • CSS keys on [selected] or :state(done), never on the checked attribute.
  • No form participation: a check submits nothing (the mockups don't need it).
