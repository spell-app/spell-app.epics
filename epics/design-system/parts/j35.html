<ui-brand-checklist checkable> makes every <ui-brand-check> a tickable checkbox (the phone's habits); a lone check takes checkable too. Not toggle: in Spell UI a toggle is the switch look of <ui-checkbox>. Ticked state follows Spell UI's rule: selected is canonical (and reflects), checked is its alias, as on <ui-checkbox>: the checked property reads and writes selected, and a checked attribute in markup ticks it, like a native checkbox's (it doesn't reflect; selected does). ui-change comes from the check, { selected, checked, originalEvent }, and bubbles through the list. Each check is a <button role=checkbox>, so Space AND Enter tick it (a native checkbox ignores Enter).

  • Pages write <ui-brand-check checked>Drink water</ui-brand-check> and listen for ui-change on the list
  • Script reads el.checked or el.selected; CSS keys on [selected] or :state(done), never [checked]
  • No form participation: a check submits nothing (not needed by the mockups)
