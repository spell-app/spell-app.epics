The colour chip's details tip has the same facts as the Color Set Chooser's hover popover, but no copy buttons.

  • The popover has a copy button per row (RGB, OKLCH).
  • The chip's tip (<ui-brand-color details>) is a CSS tooltip.
    • Anchor-positioned, flipping at the window's edges.
    • Its facts: the name, hex, OKLCH, contrast on white and on ink, and the "good for" note.
  • Why no buttons:
    • a tip that vanishes when the pointer leaves the chip can't hold controls
    • buttons inside a copy chip, itself a button, would be nested interactive content
  • Copying is the chip's own click, in the format copy picks.
