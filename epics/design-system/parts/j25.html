On the Logo and Logo Explorations copies, the grounds a logo sits on keep the brand's FIXED colours, as in the original; only the cards around them follow the page's scheme.

  • The grounds: white, aubergine, ivory, violet-950, lavender ...
  • The logo's purple and ink tones follow the scheme: lilac and near-white in dark.
    • So every LIGHT ground carries class="ui-light".
    • The Purple mark stays #6550CA on its pale ground in dark mode.
  • Neutral-50, the "don't" cards' ground, maps to --spell-brand-50.
  • The gradient "don't" swaps the original's literal colours (#ff5fa2 -> #29c6ff) for tokens:
    • --ui-pink-on-dark
    • --spell-violet-600
    • --ui-blue-on-dark
  • In dark mode the logo cards read like the downloadable files: the logo on the ground it's meant for.
