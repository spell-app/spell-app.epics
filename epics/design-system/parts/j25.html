On Logo.spell.html and Logo Explorations.spell.html, the grounds a logo sits on (white, aubergine, ivory, violet-950, lavender ...) are the brand's FIXED colours, as in the original: only the cards around them follow the page's scheme. <ui-brand-logo tone="purple"> and tone="ink" follow the scheme (lilac / near-white in dark), so every LIGHT ground carries class="ui-light": the Purple mark stays #6550CA on its pale ground in dark mode. Neutral-50 (the "don't" cards' ground) maps to --spell-brand-50; the gradient "don't" uses --ui-pink-on-dark -> --spell-violet-600 -> --ui-blue-on-dark for the original's literal #ff5fa2 / #29c6ff.

  • In dark mode the logo cards read like the downloadable files: the logo on the ground it's meant for.
  • The gradient "don't" is a little less cyan at its right end than the original.
