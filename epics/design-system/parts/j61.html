The site header's light / dark icon now shows the scheme the page shows, even when the page switches its own (I39).

  • The header (<spell-site-header>) reads, in order:
    • the dark or light class on the page's root element (forcedScheme(), in site.types.ts)
    • then the stored choice
    • then the OS
  • An observer on the root's class re-draws it when a page switches its own scheme.
  • A click flips from what the page shows.
    • The choice is stored, or forgotten when it lands on the OS's.
  • No new event or method: a page just toggles the classes, as Spell App's pill does.
  • Spell App's pill and the header icon stay in step both ways; no page change needed.
  • The docs, Spell UI site and brand bundles must be rebuilt for it to reach pages.
