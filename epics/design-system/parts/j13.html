Three choices on the colour range element (<ui-brand-color-range>):

  • A strip look, not in the brief: the Chooser's folded Variants header.
    • 17 dots of 10px, one image named for the ladder.
    • Small, and the Chooser needs it.
  • hue-shift is in degrees, passed straight to Palette.generateScale().
    • The Chooser's "Hue drift" slider is 2.5 times that (its code: hueShift: s.drift * 2.5).
    • So a page wiring that slider multiplies.
  • The range's ladder, anchor step and CSS are always up to date, even straight after a write.
    • They live on the host, BrandColorRangeHost:
      • scale
      • anchorStep
      • css()
    • Each is computed from the host's CURRENT properties, through ColorLadder, not from the element's memo.
    • Why: Solid applies writes a microtask late.
  • range.value = x; range.css() works without waiting.
  • ui-change follows colour changes only: a new name alone renames the chips and the CSS, silently.
