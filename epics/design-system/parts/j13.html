Three choices on <ui-brand-color-range>:

  • strip (not in the brief): the Chooser's folded Variants header, 17 10px dots, one image named for the ladder. Small, and the Chooser needs it.
  • hue-shift is passed straight to Palette.generateScale() as degrees; the Chooser's "Hue drift" slider value is times 2.5 of that (its code: hueShift: s.drift * 2.5), so a page wiring that slider multiplies.
  • scale, anchorStep and css() live on the host (BrandColorRangeHost) and are computed from the host's CURRENT properties through ColorLadder, not the element's memo, so a read straight after a write is right (Solid applies writes a microtask late).
  • range.value = x; range.css() works without waiting.
  • ui-change follows colour changes only: a new name alone renames the chips and css(), silently.
