The preview cards are real <ui-card>, <ui-checkbox>, <ui-input>, <ui-button>, <ui-label>, <ui-message color="accent"> and <ui-progress>. An injected <style> gives .tc-app the SAME declarations as the sheet's :root (one list builds both), plus rederive(): the tokens the foundation and spell-brand compute at :root as var() chains of the sheet's (--ui-violet, checkbox / progress / slider / button colours ...), because a custom property's var() resolves where it's DECLARED. Each card forces its scheme with ui-light / ui-dark. Chose this over an <iframe> per card (truest, but two more bundle loads and auto-height).

Also: the Live / Accent badges stay TINTED as the original's, by the labels' own --ui-label-background / --ui-label-color; the streak is a <ui-message>, so it shows the theme's message border, which the original's plain box didn't have.
