The Chooser's preview cards are real Spell UI elements, given the sheet's tokens in place, so the preview IS the theme.

  • The elements:
    • <ui-card>, <ui-checkbox>
    • <ui-input>, <ui-button>
    • <ui-label>, <ui-progress>
    • <ui-message color="accent">
  • An injected style sheet gives the preview (.tc-app) the SAME declarations as the sheet's root.
    • One list builds both.
  • Plus rederive(): the tokens the foundation and the brand theme compute at the root, as chains of the sheet's.
    • E.g. --ui-violet, the checkbox, progress, slider and button colours ...
    • Why: a custom property's var() resolves where it's DECLARED.
  • Each card forces its scheme, with ui-light / ui-dark.
  • Chosen over an iframe per card: truest, but two more bundle loads, and auto-height.
  • The Live and Accent badges stay TINTED as in the original, by the labels' own background and colour tokens.
  • The streak is a message, so it shows the theme's message border, which the original's plain box didn't have.
  • What you see is the theme on the components, light and dark, live.
  • rederive() must grow if the preview gains an element whose theme tokens chain from the sheet's.
