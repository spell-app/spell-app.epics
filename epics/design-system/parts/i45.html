.ui.secondary.menu keeps Fomantic's margin-left / margin-right: -0.35714em (ui-menu.css, Secondary), vertical too, and no public token reaches it: --ui-menu-margin is the BLOCK margin only; a text menu has --ui-menu-text-margin, a secondary one has nothing. So a vertical secondary fluid nav hangs ~5px past both sides of its column, its pills off the original's by 5px.

Brand Montage keeps .app > aside > ui-menu::part(menu) { margin-inline: 0 } (HACK) until a token (e.g. --ui-menu-secondary-margin, or --ui-menu-secondary-vertical-margin: 0) exists. Spell App's #nav and Spell Docs' .nav hang the same way.
