On the flourish page, each variant is a fluid card whose content is one focusable button box, so a click or a key reseeds it.

  • The card: <ui-card fluid>.
    • Its surface set by its background token; the aubergine one inverted.
  • Its content: one box with role=button tabindex=0, holding the flourish and the text.
    • Chosen over a plain click-anywhere box: a card is the element that fits.
    • A card's host is display: contents, so it can't take focus itself.
  • The headline is the card's header; the lede, its sub header (the theme's italic serif lede).
  • The badge: a basic label, in mono (by remapping the font family on it).
    • The inverse card's badge is forced ui-light, so it stays a white pill.
  • The original's own light / dark pill flips ui-dark / ui-light on the page's root, as Color Palette's does.
  • The eyebrow and lede name the copy's element and tokens, not the original's.
    • <ui-brand-flourish>, not <spell-flourish>
    • --spell-line-flourish, not --line-flourish ...
  • Click a card, or Tab to it and press Enter / Space, to reseed it (seed + 1), as in the original; the keyboard is new.
  • The five flourishes draw path for path what the original draws at the same seed (checked: 5 variants × 6 seeds × 2 sizes, identical path data).
