The Chooser's Base color keeps the original's layout: a swatch that opens the colour picker, with the RGB and OKLCH rows beside it.

  • The swatch: 76px, a colour chip in a button.
  • It opens the colour picker (<ui-brand-color-picker>) in a popup, on click.
  • The picker's default slot holds the 20 families, as a selectable colour set of 10 columns.
  • The RGB and OKLCH rows (inputs and copy buttons) sit beside the swatch.
  • Chosen over the swatch and hex alone, or the picker inline:
    • the rows are what reads as the original's inspector
    • the picker inline is 27em tall
  • RGB and OKLCH show twice while the popup is open: the picker has its own rows.
  • The popup has the picker's HSL square, not the original's Lightness × Colourfulness plane.
  • Typed text is read by Palette.parse() and oklchToHex(), so HSL and OKLCH text also work in the RGB field.
