Spell Marketing, the nav's Open app → button.

What: <ui-button icon> always puts the icon BEFORE the text; labeled="right" draws a separate tinted icon block. A <ui-icon> after the text in the default slot works, but <ui-icon> is display: contents, so it can't take a margin: the gap is a plain space (about 4px against the original's 6px).

Fix: an icon position on <ui-button> (e.g. icon-position="end", or Fomantic's right labeled without the block via a token), spaced by --ui-button-icon-spacing.
