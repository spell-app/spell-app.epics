Owen, after P3 (2026-10-04): "`color` attribute for Panel, including `color=accent`".

  • color (the section's own attribute) paints the whole panel from the hue's remap: the header band its background, the box and sub-head bands lighter mixes with the surface, the border and the text the hue. Sub-panels without a color follow their panel (handed down as --_brand-panel-hue-*, since the section resets --ui-color* at its slot).
  • accent is a NEW hue: Polished Ivory. The brand bundle adds the name to Spell UI's hues (packages/brand/src/hues.ts); spell-brand gives it tokens (--ui-accent-*) and the .ui.accent remap, so color="accent" tints ANY element while that theme is on.
  • No color: the ivory look, unchanged; accent names it (judgement J6).
  • the panel's docs page has a Colors example (accent, violet, grey, teal)
  • under any other theme, accent is accepted but tints nothing; a Spell UI-wide accent hue is part of moving the elements into Spell UI (T2)
