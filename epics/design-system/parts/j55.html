A section's fold chevron can now sit at the far end of its title bar, as the original inspector draws it: fold-icon="end" (I8, I15).

  • The values: start (the default) and end.
  • With end, the chevron is drawn OUTSIDE the fold button, after the badge and actions.
  • The button (the heading) stays the control.
    • The chevron is aria-hidden.
    • A click on it folds, as the button does.
  • It turns the same way as the start one: down when open, right when folded.
  • A subclass moves the default with @proto static defaultFoldIcon.
    • The brand panel sets end, so its bands keep the look they had, now with any actions before the chevron.
  • Kept the turn (down / right), not the original's up / down, so no existing panel changes.
  • A new attribute, fold-icon, on <ui-section> and every panel; the brand panel defaults to end.
  • Color Set Chooser drops its part-order rules (heading, toggle, fold icon) for fold-icon="end".
    • Its strip and copy button now sit BEFORE the chevron, as in the original.
  • Theme Creator's Preview and CSS sections can take fold-icon="end".
