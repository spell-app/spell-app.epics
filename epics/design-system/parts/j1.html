A Compare page, to judge how close each Spell copy comes to its original.

  • Why this shape:
    • judging the two needs them at the SAME layout width
    • an overlay shows small shifts that side by side hides
  • A page menu: all 13 pages.
  • Four modes:
    • Side by side
    • Overlay: the copy over the original, with a "Copy on top" opacity slider
    • Original only
    • Spell only
  • Four widths: 390, 768, 1280 (the default) and 1600.
    • Each frame renders at that width, then scales down to fit its pane.
  • Scrolling one frame scrolls the other.
  • The view lives in the address, so a reload or a pasted link lands on the same view.
    • E.g. compare.html?page=Spell%20Docs&mode=overlay&width=1280&mix=40

Other options:

  • one page with a draggable split (a swipe)
  • screenshot-only diffs: P2's brand:compare adds those anyway
  • A new page, compare.html, with its own script and stylesheet (brand-pages).
  • Each original's dark / light toggle isn't reachable from Compare: it's inside Claude Design's page.
