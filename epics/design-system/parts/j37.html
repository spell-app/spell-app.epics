One composer element covers both originals, in two sizes.

  • The app's Build box is the default, size="medium":
    • 17px / 1.5 serif text
    • a 16px radius, from --ui-radius-l
    • a small shadow, a 36px button
  • The marketing hero's "Try a spell" box is size="large":
    • 19px / 1.45 text, a 20px radius
    • the large shadow, a 38px button, roomier padding
  • Every one of those is also a --ui-brand-composer-* token.
  • The hero's "Try a spell" line is the eyebrow attribute: small mono caps in the accent.
    • Or slot="eyebrow", for rich content.
  • The text box's name is the first of:
    • label
    • the host's aria-label, or a <label for>
    • eyebrow
    • "Your spell"
  • The hint defaults to the platform's shortcut.
    • "⌘↵ to cast" on Apple devices, "Ctrl+↵ to cast" elsewhere (texts hintApple / hintOther).
    • hint="..." replaces it; hint="" hides it (the hero has none).
  • The placeholder defaults to "Describe what you want to build…".
    • placeholder="" shows none.
  • Spell App: <ui-brand-composer name="spell">, and a language chip in the tools slot.
  • Spell Marketing: <ui-brand-composer size="large" eyebrow="Try a spell" rows="2" hint="">, and idea chips in the tools slot.
