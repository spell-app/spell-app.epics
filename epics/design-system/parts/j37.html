One element covers both originals: the app's Build box is the default (size="medium": 17px/1.5 serif text, 16px radius from --ui-radius-l, small shadow, 36px button); the marketing hero's "Try a spell" box is size="large" (19px/1.45 text, 20px radius, the large shadow, 38px button, roomier padding). Every one of those is also a --ui-brand-composer-* token.

The hero's "Try a spell" line is the eyebrow attribute (small mono caps in the accent), or slot="eyebrow" for rich content; it also names the text box when nothing else does (label, then the host's aria-label / <label for>, then eyebrow, then "Your spell").

The hint defaults to the platform's shortcut: "⌘↵ to cast" on Apple devices, "Ctrl+↵ to cast" elsewhere (texts hintApple / hintOther); hint="..." replaces it, hint="" hides it (the hero has none). The placeholder defaults to "Describe what you want to build…"; placeholder="" shows none.

Net effect:

  • Spell App: <ui-brand-composer name="spell"> + a language chip in slot="tools"
  • Spell Marketing: <ui-brand-composer size="large" eyebrow="Try a spell" rows="2" hint=""> + idea chips in slot="tools"
