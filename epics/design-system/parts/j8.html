<ui-brand-color-picker> keeps the colour being edited as OKLCH (working), apart from value (#RRGGBB), as the original keeps its okEdit. Why: a grey has no hue, so setting or typing one would otherwise make the plane jump to hue 0 (red); and a drag into the hatched (out-of-sRGB) area should follow the pointer. value is always Palette.oklchToHex(working), gamut-mapped (same L and H, less C); on commit (release, Enter, a key) the marker snaps to that chroma.

The plane's out-of-gamut pixels are left TRANSPARENT and the hatch is the plane's own CSS background (repeating-linear-gradient from --ui-brand-color-picker-hatch-color / -hatch-background, defaults --spell-border-subtle over --spell-surface-tint), instead of the original's hatch drawn into the canvas in fixed greys (236 / 246). Why: it follows the theme (dark mode), stays crisp at any pixel ratio, and costs the canvas nothing. The canvas is drawn at device pixel ratio, capped at 96k pixels (measured ~4-6 ms per draw for 48k), and redrawn only when the whole-degree hue or the size changes, never per L / C move (pinned by a test).

Net effect:

  • picking a grey keeps the hue the plane shows; End on the plane brings the colour back at that hue
  • the hatch looks right in light and dark; its colours are tokens
  • dragging stays cheap: no redraw while L / C change
