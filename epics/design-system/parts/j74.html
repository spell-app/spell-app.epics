The paragraph under a docs page's h1 is the brand's italic serif lede; a lede INSIDE a section stays upright sans.

  • Under the h1: the .lede paragraph, and the cheatsheet's first header paragraph.
    • Italic serif, in the headings' ink.
  • Inside a section (a plan doc's summary, a changelog entry): an upright sans lead paragraph, 1.06rem.
    • Why: those run a whole paragraph, and five lines of italic are hard to read.
  • Every page's top reads: the title, then its italic echo, as on the brand's Spell Docs page.
  • Code and emphasis inside a lede go upright.
  • A plan doc's Overview summary looks as before, a little stronger.
