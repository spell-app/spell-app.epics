The original's popover has a title ("Choose a colour") and a close button in its head row, and its RGB / OKLCH rows sit OUTSIDE the popover, beside the 76px swatch. <ui-brand-color-picker> is the INLINE picker (the lead's brief): the head row is the chip and the hex, plus two slots so a page can rebuild the original exactly: slot="header" above the hex (styled as the original's bold serif 15px title) and slot="actions" at the far end (a close button). The RGB and OKLCH rows are inside, after the hue slider, 8px apart; the OKLCH fields are three boxes as the original's (L with a % suffix), not one joined box. The default slot comes last (the original's "Or start from a family" chips).

The plane's marker and the hue thumb are filled with the current colour and set off by a hairline, a soft drop shadow and a soft light GLOW (blurred, not a crisp ring), not the original's 2px white ring: the brand's readme ("Controls") says a custom thumb is set off by a soft shadow, never a white ring; the glow keeps the marker visible on dark colours, where a dark shadow alone disappears. The hue track's rainbow uses the colour's L clamped to 45-85% and C at least 0.1 (the original: L as is, C at least 0.06), so it stays readable for dark and grey colours.

Net effect:

  • a page puts the picker in a <ui-popup> with a title and close button and gets the original popover (the docs page's "In a popup" example)
  • no white rings anywhere; thumbs read on light and dark colours
  • no copy buttons: the original's RGB / OKLCH labels copy the value; left to pages (caveat)
