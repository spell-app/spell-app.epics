A <label for> inside the field's shadow root can't point at the control in the light DOM, so screen readers would hear an unnamed slider. Chose: the field writes aria-label="<label>" on a slotted control that has no aria-label / aria-labelledby of its own, renames it when label changes, and never touches a control that came with its own name. A click on the label focuses the control.

Other options: make the author name each control (easy to forget), or give the field role="group" with the label as its name (names the group, not the slider).

Net effect:

  • <ui-brand-field label="Vibrancy"><ui-slider> reads "Vibrancy, slider"
  • a SIDE EFFECT on light-DOM children, documented on the class
