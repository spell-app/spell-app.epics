A brand field names the control slotted inside it, so a screen reader hears "Vibrancy, slider", not an unnamed slider.

  • Why: a <label for> inside the field's shadow root can't point at a control in the light DOM.
  • What the field does:
    • writes aria-label="<label>" on a slotted control that has no name of its own (no aria-label or aria-labelledby)
    • renames it when its label changes
    • never touches a control that came with its own name
    • a click on the label focuses the control
  • Other options:
    • make the author name each control: easy to forget
    • give the field role="group", with the label as its name: that names the group, not the slider
  • <ui-brand-field label="Vibrancy"><ui-slider> reads "Vibrancy, slider".
  • A SIDE EFFECT on light-DOM children, documented on the class.
