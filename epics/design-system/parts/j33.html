The original's phone is ivory (--surface-warm) in light and the raised surface (--surface-raised, violet-900) in dark, not the theme's dark ivory (#2C2152). The element keeps that: light-dark(var(--spell-surface-warm), var(--ui-surface-muted)), since the dark --ui-surface-muted of spell-brand IS violet-900. Border, shadow and status bar text from --spell-border-subtle, --spell-shadow-md, --spell-text-strong, each falling back to a plain --ui-* token outside the theme.

Net effect:

  • Dark mode matches the original; --ui-brand-phone-background overrides both.
  • Without spell-brand: a pale grey phone (--ui-surface-muted).
