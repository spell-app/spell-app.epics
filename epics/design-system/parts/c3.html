yarn compare's score is the share of pixels whose colour differs, so use it to track a page between runs, not to judge it.

  • "Differs": R + G + B by more than 12.
    • Low enough that the faint lavender and ivory tints count.
  • One line of text wrapping differently moves everything below it.
    • So a page can be nearly right and still score high.
  • Example: Spell Docs against a blank 404 page scored only 5% at first, with a threshold of 48: mostly white against white.
    • The threshold was lowered to 12.
  • Use the number to see a page get better or worse between runs.
  • Judge the page itself by its side-by-side image (side.png), or the Compare view.
