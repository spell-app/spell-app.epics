yarn compare counts the share of pixels whose colour differs (R + G + B by more than 12, low enough that the faint lavender and ivory tints count). But one line of text wrapping differently moves everything below it, so a page can be nearly right and still score high.

  • Example: Spell Docs against a blank 404 page scored only 5% at first (threshold 48): mostly white against white. Lowered to 12.
  • Use the number to see a page get better or worse between runs; judge by side.png or the Compare view.
