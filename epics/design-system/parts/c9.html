spell-brand.css gives :where(img) a 1px hairline outline (meant for photos). On a transparent logo SVG it draws a faint box round the artwork. Logo Explorations.spell.html turns it off for its <img>s of the explored lockups (outline: none, page CSS); any page that shows an <img> of artwork has to do the same. A theme opt-out (e.g. no outline on img[src$=".svg"], or a class) would save that.
