The brand theme outlines every image with a hairline, so a page showing transparent artwork must turn it off.

  • The theme (spell-brand.css) gives :where(img) a 1px hairline outline, meant for photos.
  • On a transparent logo SVG, it draws a faint box round the artwork.
  • The Logo Explorations copy turns it off for its images of the explored lockups.
    • outline: none, in page CSS.
  • Any page that shows an image of artwork has to do the same.
  • A theme opt-out would save that.
    • E.g. no outline on img[src$=".svg"], or a class.
