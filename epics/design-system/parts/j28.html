The poster stays a 1600px square scaled down to the window (never up), as in the original, built from Spell UI and brand elements.

  • The script only sets --scale.
  • The two app panels are the same markup, one ui-light and one ui-dark.
    • So every token in each resolves in its own scheme, whatever the page's.
  • Built from:
    • the logo element: the hero tagline lockup, the nav lockups, the app icons
    • a vertical secondary menu: the nav
    • cards: the prompt, build steps, Today, Streak
    • buttons: send, Add habit
    • checkboxes: build steps and habits, in the theme's round to-do circle
    • a read-only rating of 4 circles out of 5: the streak dots
    • icons
    • colour chips: the palette, 144px with an 18px radius
    • blobs: the corners, the ivory tile's ellipse, the CTA tile's lilac mound
  • The hero lockup is tone=ink: aubergine on the light page, near-white on the dark one.
  • The ivory and app-icon tiles are forced ui-light: they're brand colours, not page surfaces.
  • The copy's "Finalizing your app" step is an empty ring (an unchecked checkbox), where the original's is a filled pale-lilac dot.
  • The steps and habits can be ticked; the original's are static pictures.
  • The wand icon is Spell UI's magic glyph (a plain wand): its icon packs have no wand-magic-sparkles.
  • In dark mode the poster's canvas and blobs go aubergine / soft lilac; the original has no dark mode.
