The poster stays a 1600px square scaled to the window (never up), as the original; the script only sets --scale. The two app panels are the same markup, one ui-light, one ui-dark, so every token in each resolves in its scheme whatever the page's. Built from: <ui-brand-logo> (hero tagline lockup, nav lockups, app icons), <ui-menu vertical secondary> (nav), <ui-card> (prompt, build steps, Today, Streak), <ui-button> (send, Add habit), <ui-checkbox> (build steps and habits: the theme's round to-do circle), <ui-rating icon=circle max-rating=5 value=4 readonly> (the streak dots), <ui-icon>, <ui-brand-color> (palette chips, 144px, 18px radius), <ui-brand-blob> (corners, the ivory tile's ellipse, the CTA tile's lilac mound). The hero lockup is tone=ink, so it's aubergine on the light page and near-white on the dark one; the ivory and app-icon tiles are forced ui-light (they're brand colours, not page surfaces).

  • the copy's "Finalizing your app" step is an empty ring (an unchecked checkbox), where the original's is a filled pale-lilac dot
  • the steps and habits can be ticked (the original's are static pictures)
  • the wand icon is Spell UI's magic glyph (a plain wand): no wand-magic-sparkles in its icon packs
  • in dark mode the poster's canvas and blobs go aubergine / soft lilac; the original has no dark mode
