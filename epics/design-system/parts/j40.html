Looked first: <ui-checkbox> is Fomantic's box (a native input under a <label>, its own sheet, form-associated), and <ui-steps> a horizontal wizard whose steps each say their own state; neither draws a round to-do mark, and restyling either would fight its sheet. So <ui-brand-checklist> is new, on their machinery: the list owns its checks as <ui-tabs> owns its panes (PartContext, ownsParts: check; each check asks the list how to show, so one step moves them all); selected is auto-controlled (Controlled, so a ui-change handler that re-sets it wins); the checked alias copies <ui-checkbox>'s CheckHost; the hidden "done" copies <ui-step>'s "Completed".

Net effect:

  • One more family for D2's later move into Spell UI: a generic checklist, brand-coloured only through --spell-* roles (plain Spell UI tokens without the theme)
