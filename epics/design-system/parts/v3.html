The Spell Brand theme is in Spell UI's theme picker, and the choice survives a reload.

  • Open the Spell UI tab, then the theme picker (the palette button).
  • "Spell Brand" is second, under Spell.
  • Pick it: the components take the brand look.
    • pill buttons, serif headings
    • the purple and ivory hues
    • an aubergine dark mode
  • Reload: the choice is still there.
