I15. <ui-section info="...">, or a rich slot="info" (bold words, line breaks), draws a tooltip under the title bar at its start edge, shown while the pointer is on the heading (the whole band) or the end chevron, or the fold button has keyboard focus, as the original's band tips. No icon: the original has none on its bands. It describes the fold button (aria-describedby), else the heading. Tokens --ui-section-tip-background / -color / -width (defaults: ink bubble, surface text, 16.25em max); spell-brand sets the brand's aubergine bubble. Placed BELOW the band (the original's sit above), as <ui-brand-field>'s tips already do: a tip above the top band would cover the page header.

  • New attribute info, slot info, part tip, three tokens on <ui-section> and so on <ui-brand-panel>.
  • Theme Creator can put back its band tips (Theme, Color, Type, Shape, Preview, CSS).
