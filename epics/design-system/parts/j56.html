A section can carry a tooltip on its title bar, as the original's band tips: info="...", or a rich info slot (I15).

  • The rich slot (slot="info") takes bold words and line breaks.
  • The tip is drawn under the title bar, at its start edge.
    • It shows while the pointer is on the heading (the whole band) or the end chevron, or while the fold button has keyboard focus.
  • No icon: the original has none on its bands.
  • It describes the fold button (aria-describedby), else the heading.
  • Three tokens, the tip's background, colour and width.
    • --ui-section-tip-background
    • --ui-section-tip-color
    • --ui-section-tip-width
    • Defaults: an ink bubble, surface-coloured text, 16.25em at most.
    • The brand theme sets the brand's aubergine bubble.
  • Placed BELOW the band (the original's sit above), as the brand field's tips already are.
    • A tip above the top band would cover the page header.
  • New on <ui-section>, and so on the brand panel:
    • the attribute info
    • the slot info
    • the part tip
    • three tokens
  • Theme Creator can put back its band tips (Theme, Color, Type, Shape, Preview, CSS).
