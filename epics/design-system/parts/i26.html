The guide's headings are the brand's serif (Palatino 700, ## 28px, ### 19px, no rule under them), its rules a 1px hairline, its bold and inline code in --spell-text-strong. <ui-markdown> draws GitHub's look: sans 600 headings, a rule under h1 / h2, a 0.25em hr; its tokens give heading COLOUR only, and ::part(body) can't reach the headings inside.

Fix it needs: tokens --ui-markdown-heading-font-family (default inherit; the brand sets --ui-font-family-heading), -heading-weight, -h1-size ... -h3-size, -heading-rule (border under h1 / h2, none to drop), -hr-height, -strong-color, -code-color; and the brand theme setting them, so every <ui-markdown> on a brand page reads as the guide does.
