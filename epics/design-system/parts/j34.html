The composer (<ui-brand-composer>, the Build box and the Try a spell box) casts the spell, and inside a form it then submits it.

  • Three ways to cast:
    • its round button
    • Cmd+Enter or Ctrl+Enter, either on every platform; plain Enter types a newline
    • the host's cast() method
  • A cast sends ui-cast, with { value }.
    • Never with blank text, nor while casting or disabled.
  • ui-cast is CANCELABLE.
    • Unless a handler calls preventDefault(), a composer inside a form then submits it (requestSubmit()).
    • The form's submit handler sees the composer's name holding the text, as the originals' forms did.
  • cast() is there for the hero's idea chips, which fill the box and cast at once: el.value = idea; el.cast().
  • Pages without a form just listen for ui-cast.
  • Pages with a form get a normal submit, which preventDefault() on ui-cast stops.
  • A page listening to both ui-cast and submit sees one cast twice.
