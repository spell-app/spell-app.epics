P5, Color Palette.spell.html. Each of the 20 sets of lib/palette.json (fetched by the page script) is a <ui-brand-color-range value=seed anchor=anchor name=set>, not 17 hand-fed chips: the ranges rebuild every step exactly (checked in the browser: all 340 hexes equal the JSON's), and the seed's ring comes from the element. The step numbers show once, in a header row, as the original: each range's own numbers are hidden with ::part(number) (an issue asks for an attribute). Chips are the elements' SQUARE chips (~62px at 1400px), not the original's 54px-tall ones: the brand readme says chips are always square. Copy: hex while hexes show, else the token, as the original; the toast is Spell UI's (UI.toast, inverted, bottom centre, 1.4s) on ui-copy. The format switch is <ui-menu appearance=segmented>; the light / dark pill flips ui-dark / ui-light on <html>.
