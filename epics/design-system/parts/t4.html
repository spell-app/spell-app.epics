The brand specimen page shows the brand look, light and dark.

  • Open the specimen page (components.spell.html) from the page server.
  • Buttons are pills.
  • The h1 is serif, with an italic lede.
  • Eyebrows are small mono capitals.
  • Eight brand-role swatches show: tint, warm, selected, inverse, accent, accent soft, blob, code.
  • Switch to dark with the sun button in the header: the canvas turns aubergine, and the swatches change.
