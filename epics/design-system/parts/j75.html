The docs' two folded boxes take the brand's two panel kinds, not the plain white accordion both were.

  • Asides (ui-accordion.spell-aside): the brand's IVORY tip panel.
  • Code (ui-accordion.spell-code): the brand's Spell / Compiled example panel.
  • An aside:
    • warm ivory (--spell-surface-warm); a warm aubergine in dark
    • 16px corners, no border or shadow
    • its title an italic serif
  • A code box:
    • a code-well frame (--spell-surface-code), with a hairline and 14px corners
    • its title a mono label
    • the code on a card inside it
  • Inline code sits on the code well, in the headings' ink.
    • In dark that's the brand's near-black well (violet-975), darker than before.
  • The kickoff prompt and the goals pages' pull quotes lose their coloured left border.
    • The brand says "never coloured left-border cards".
    • The prompt: on a white card.
    • A goals quote: an ivory panel, in italic serif.
