The brand's segmented control stays a segmented menu (<ui-menu appearance="segmented">), now themable as a track with a thumb, and it selects by itself (I6, I10, I14).

  • Where it's used:
    • the Light / Dark pill
    • Theme Creator's choosers
    • Color Palette's Hex / OKLCH / Swatch
    • Color Set Chooser's RGB / OKLCH
  • Three of the four pages already use the segmented menu; the fourth fakes it with a button group.
  • Chosen: keep it on the menu, NOT a new selectable mode on the button group.
    • The menu already IS a segmented look, and its items have selected and ui-select.

The look: enough tokens for a theme to draw a track with a thumb.

  • Each is --ui-menu-segmented-*:
    • the track: background, border, radius, padding (the inset), gap, divider
    • the options: item padding, item colour, item radius
    • the chosen one: active background, active hover background, active colour (also the focus ring), active shadow
  • The defaults keep today's bordered, joined group, filled with its colour or primary.
  • The brand theme sets them to the brand's own segmented control (.sp-seg):
    • a lavender-tint pill track
    • a 1px subtle hairline, plus a 2px inset (= the original's 3px)
    • 2px gaps, muted options
    • the chosen one: a card-coloured pill, with strong text and a small shadow (--shadow-sm)
  • Segmented tabs (the brand component pages' tabs) take the same look.

The behaviour:

  • A segmented menu now moves selected to the activated item itself; every page scripted it.
  • ui-select is cancelable: preventDefault() keeps the old choice.
  • Other menus still never move it.
  • Theme Creator, Color Palette and Spell App drop their surface and colour remaps on the menu, and their selected-toggling scripts.
  • Color Set Chooser: its button group (<ui-buttons id="css-format">) becomes a mini segmented link menu, listening to ui-select.
    • <ui-menu appearance="segmented" link size="mini">
  • Under the brand theme, every segmented menu and segmented tab set looks like the brand's pill, not a primary-filled box.
  • Accessibility unchanged: still a nav of links or buttons with aria-current.
    • Not the brand's role=group with aria-pressed.
