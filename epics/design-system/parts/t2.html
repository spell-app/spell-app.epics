The Compare view shows a page's original and Spell copy side by side, scrolling in step, in four modes and four widths.

  • From the Brand index, click Compare on Spell App.
    • The original fills the left pane, the Spell copy the right.
    • Each is scaled to fit, and they scroll in step.
  • Overlay: the copy over the original, with the "Copy on top" slider.
  • Original only / Spell only: one pane fills the width.
  • Widths: 1600 shrinks the page; 390 shows its phone layout.
  • Reload: the same page, mode and width come back; they live in the address.

From the index, click Compare on Spell App. The original fills the left pane, scaled to fit; the right says "Not built yet". Try each mode and width: Overlay shows the slider; Original or Spell alone fill the width; 1600 shrinks the page, 390 shows its phone layout. Reload: the same view comes back.
