On the Spell UI site (/ui/), in the default Spell theme, nothing should have moved; then each new token or attribute should work.

  • Menus: vertical, secondary and text menus look as before.
    • Their looks now read their own variation tokens (--ui-menu-vertical-* ...).
    • A link item fills a vertical menu's width.
  • Markdown:
    • the skip-title example drops the first heading
    • on the Design Guide's copy, a contents link, or a #id in the address, scrolls to its heading (reveal(id))
  • Unchanged at default tokens:
    • lists (bulleted, link)
    • messages (an icon message)
    • headers (a card's sub header)
  • Toasts: UI.toast({ class }) keeps the page's class.
  • Enter in an input submits a form whose submit is a Spell UI button.
    • Spell App's "New habit" field.
  • Tabs: the tab strip sits flush on its pane (--ui-tabs-menu-margin, 0).
  • The site header's sun / moon follows a page that sets its own dark or light class.
    • Spell App's Light / Dark pill.
