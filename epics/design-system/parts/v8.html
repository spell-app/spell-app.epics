The panel takes its hue from color, light and dark, and repaints when it changes.

  • Where: the panel's page on the Spell UI site (/ui/components/ui-panel.html), Examples, Variations, Colors.
  • The hues:
    • Default (primary)
    • accent: ivory, under the Spell Brand theme
    • violet, grey, teal
  • Each band, box and border is in its hue, and the Tweak sub-head band follows.
  • Dark mode: the same hues, on aubergine.
  • In DevTools, change one panel's color to red: it repaints.
