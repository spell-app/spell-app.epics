Two additions to the brand colour range and blob: hiding a range's step numbers, and a wave-shaped blob (I4, I24).

  • The range's numbers (I4): shown, the default, or none.
    • none leaves the step numbers out, with their row height.
    • As a page that numbers its 20 ladders once wants.
  • The blob's shape="wave" (I24): Brand Montage's top-left wash.
    • Its SVG path, as a mask stretched over the blob's box.
    • Mirrored per corner, so its straight edges lie on the parent's, flush in its corner.
    • Its x and y offsets default to 0 for it.
    • It matches the montage's big top-left shape exactly.
    • The right-edge bulge and the two bottom humps stay approximations: organic, and a small wave or mound in the bottom corners.
  • Color Palette drops ::part(number) { display: none }, for numbers="none".
  • Brand Montage's top-left blob becomes shape="wave" at 640 × 424, with no overhang.
