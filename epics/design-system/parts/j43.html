Behaviour choices in Spell Marketing.spell.html where the original says nothing or the element differs:

  • The page sets casting on the hero's <ui-brand-composer> while the checklist steps (the original's Cast button never changed), so the button spins and can't recast mid-build; an idea chip still restarts a running build, as the original's did (it clears casting, then calls cast()).
  • Both Start a spell buttons and Open app link to Spell App.spell.html; the original's did nothing. Sign in, See templates, Templates / Privacy / Terms stay inert (#), as the original's.
  • The call-to-action band keeps its brand colours (aubergine, lilac mound, white button) in both schemes; the hero's ink button follows the scheme (--spell-surface-inverse: aubergine in light, near-white in dark).
  • Off-token originals mapped to the nearest role: the Compiled pane's #D9D0F7 text to --spell-violet-150; the preview's ivory to --spell-accent-150 (light) / --spell-surface-warm (dark); the hero blob's violet-100 to --spell-violet-100 / --spell-lilac-soft.
  • The preview's call to action is a real <ui-button primary fluid> (serif by --ui-font-family), not the original's div; its dimming while building fades ::part(segment), because a <ui-segment> host is display: contents and can't take opacity.

Net effect:

  • Every control of the original works, plus a spinner while casting and links into the App mock-up.
  • Dark mode reads well, the CTA band identical in both.
