Behaviour choices in the Spell Marketing copy (Spell Marketing.spell.html), where the original says nothing or the element differs:

  • The page sets casting on the hero's composer while the checklist steps.
    • The original's Cast button never changed.
    • So the button spins, and can't recast mid-build.
    • An idea chip still restarts a running build, as the original's did: it clears casting, then calls cast().
  • Both Start a spell buttons, and Open app, link to the Spell App copy; the original's did nothing.
    • Sign in, See templates, and Templates / Privacy / Terms stay inert (#), as in the original.
  • The call-to-action band keeps its brand colours (aubergine, lilac mound, white button) in both schemes.
  • The hero's ink button follows the scheme (--spell-surface-inverse): aubergine in light, near-white in dark.
  • Off-token colours in the original are mapped to the nearest brand role:
    • the Compiled pane's #D9D0F7 text: --spell-violet-150
    • the preview's ivory: --spell-accent-150 in light, --spell-surface-warm in dark
    • the hero blob's violet-100: --spell-violet-100 in light, --spell-lilac-soft in dark
  • The preview's call to action is a real primary fluid button, serif by its font family, not the original's plain box.
    • Its dimming while building fades the segment's inner part (::part(segment)).
    • Why: a segment's host is display: contents, and can't take opacity.
  • Every control of the original works, plus a spinner while casting, and links into the App mock-up.
  • Dark mode reads well, the CTA band identical in both.
