Docs headings use the brand's serif: Palatino, bold, in its strongest ink (aubergine; near-white in dark).

  • For the h1, the section titles and the card titles.
  • Chosen over the system face picked on 2026-10-04: P9's goal (and the headings question, Q17) call for the brand's serif headings.
  • One token puts the system face back: --spell-heading-font, in the docs' stylesheet (spell-doc.css).
  • The sizes:
    • h1: 2.25rem (2rem in the sticky page header)
    • top-level section titles: 1.6em; nested: 1.2em
    • tight tracking
  • Plan item rows, the contents, h4s and body text stay in the system sans: they're rows to scan, not headings.
