T54's Weekend Countdown can't be written in today's Spell. Three things are missing, and each would be new language, so I stopped before building any of it.

What Spell has today:

  • now, the current moment, as a date.
  • A date's year, day (of the month) and hour.
  • pause for 1 second, inside an action.
  • No widget in Spell UI that counts down or ticks. The big numbers can be <statistic>, as Weather's temperature is.

What's missing:

  1. A date's minute and second. Proposed: the minute of now and the second of now. They're the same shape as hour: one more line each in the built-in date type.
  2. A date's day of the week. Proposed: the weekday of now, a number, Monday 1 to Sunday 7. With that numbering, "is it the weekend?" reads as the weekday of its time > 5.
    • HyperTalk numbers the days Sunday 1 to Saturday 7, which makes the weekend test "is 1 or is 7".
  3. Something that runs every second. Proposed: an every second block in a thing or an app, which runs its steps once a second while the app is open. It wouldn't add undo steps.
    • Today's only way is an action that pauses, then calls itself. That's a trick a beginner shouldn't have to learn, and every call would add an undo step.

The leading zeros need nothing new: a small action does it with if n < 10 return "0" + n.

With those three, the whole example would read like this (a draft, not built):

# Weekend Countdown
// How long until the weekend:  days, hours, minutes and seconds, ticking down every second.

weekend countdown is an app
	its "time" is a date
		// This moment:  set again every second, so the countdown redraws
	every second
		set its time to now
	its "weekend" is the weekday of its time > 5
		// Saturday is 6, Sunday 7
	its "days" is 5 - the weekday of its time
		// Whole days left after today:  4 on a Monday, 0 on a Friday
	its "hours" is 23 - the hour of its time
	its "minutes" is 59 - the minute of its time
	its "seconds" is 59 - the second of its time

	to draw it
		<container text>
			<segment padded="very">
				<text color="grey">Weekend starts in</text>
				... four <statistic>s:  {two digits its days}, labelled Days, then Hr, Min, Sec
				... or "It's the weekend!" when its weekend
			</segment>
		</container>

to two digits (n as a number)
	// 7 as "07":  each number is always two wide
	if n < 10 return "0" + n
	return n as text

The rest of T54 (its place after Counter on the home page, its tagline, picture and guide) waits on this answer.
