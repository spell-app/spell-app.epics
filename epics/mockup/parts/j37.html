Where a line leaves a box depends on who drew the box.

  • A box drawn by the diagram has field rows, and a line leaves from its field's row.
  • A box filled by a <ui-diagram-node> (a <ui-shape> that flips) has no rows the diagram knows. So its lines leave from, and land on, the middle of its first band (its whole height).
  • P11's NERD / ERD flip can give a node's rows to the diagram, if it wants lines on each field.
