The Rearrange Home Screen button sits quietly at the foot of the home page; turned on, a bar sticks along the top saying how and whose (everyone's, or only yours), with Done, and Reset to Default when you have changes of your own. Cards move only within their own section (a project lives in its group, so moving it to another section has no meaning); the Make something cards move too. Sections move by their headers, as before. Each card that can move gets a faint dashed outline (a wobble, like a phone's, made the cards hard to grab and test). A click on a title or line selects it to type over; Enter keeps, Escape puts back, emptying it brings back the original. A tagline past 56 characters gets a wavy underline and a tooltip, and is kept anyway. Section headers and Make something cards keep their words per language, as the editor's own words are; a project card's words show in every language. Left as they were: the Spanish section's small Ejemplos / Guías lines aren't editable, and your apps' ... menu is hidden while rearranging (a click there would open a project).
