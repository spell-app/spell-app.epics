With the Pages side bar gone, the board took its one extra move: a card dropped on the middle of a page's card goes into that page, as its first page, the page's card lit up. The middle is the inner 60% of the card each way; the outer 20% on each side still means the spot under it, so the card there moves along, as before.

Drag a card onto the middle of another page's card to nest it; onto its edge to put it beside.
