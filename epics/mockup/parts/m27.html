In Chrome, open test.spell.app (the main editor, which has all of this epic).

  1. Open a project of your own, change a word in two of its files (AutoSave keeps them as your draft).
  2. Project menu › Delete Draft, and say yes.
  3. Press ⌘Z.
  1. It asks first; then both files show their published text, and Your draft is gone from under the tabs.
  2. ⌘Z brings both files' changes back in one step.
  3. With no draft, Delete Draft is greyed.
