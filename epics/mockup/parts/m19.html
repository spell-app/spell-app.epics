In VS Code's ⎇ mockup window, open the editor (its address is in .spell-server.editor.json at the worktree's root). From the home page, open each of the two projects your board made under your own space, and press Site in the top bar; then Run. In the new site's section pages, press a button for a modal.

The current site's site editor shows each screen's picture on its card, and Run shows that screen with the nav beside it. The new site runs in the Mockup theme, its modals opening from section pages, and each card shows its state.
