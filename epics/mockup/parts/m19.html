1. In VS Code's ⎇ mockup window, open the editor: its address is in .spell-server.editor.json, at the worktree's root.
  2. From the home page, open each of the two projects your board made under your own space.
  3. In each, press Site in the top bar; then Run.
  4. In the new site's section pages, press a button for a modal.
  • The current site: its site editor shows each screen's picture on its card, and Run shows that screen with the nav beside it.
  • The new site: it runs in the Mockup theme, its modals opening from section pages, and each card shows its state.
