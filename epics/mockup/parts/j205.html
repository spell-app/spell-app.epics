The steps are "thumbnail": { "steps": [...] } in each project's __project.json5 (click by text or CSS, type, press a key, scroll to something), read only by yarn examples:readme --thumbnails. Every picture has 16px of room above the app's first thing; a picture that scrolls to something starts 16px above it. Where the card's 16:9 top can't hold what the app does, the picture scrolls to it: Habit Tracker's first habit with its ticks, Caja de recetas' first recipe, PokéSpell's Pikachu page at its artwork, Star Wars at its search for Luke, Weather at the weather now. Calculator: 2 × 2 = 4. Tic-Tac-Toe: four moves. Bill Splitter: the 20% tip. Flash Cards: one card known, the next turned over. Solitaire and Solitario: Auto Play for 2.5 seconds.
