A Spell program can't write column settings as data yet (spellml's attributes take no JSON), so the core names the columns of a list of things itself: each declared property in order, headed by its words (filmTitle becomes "film title"), its kind from its type (number, date, a tick box for a true or false, a choice of its values); a list, a thing and the id are left out. So an empty table of things still shows its columns, and takes its first row. Spell UI reads them as the list's tableColumns; a page's own column-defs still win. Headers are lowercase, as Spell's words are.
