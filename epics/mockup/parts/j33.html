The core names the columns of a table of things itself, from the thing's type.

  • Why: a Spell program can't write column settings as data yet (spellml's attributes take no JSON).
  • The columns: each declared property, in order.
  • Each headed by its words: filmTitle becomes "film title", lowercase, as Spell's words are.
  • Each kind from its type: number, date, a tick box for a true or false, a choice of its values.
  • Left out: a list, a thing, and the id.
  • So an empty table of things still shows its columns, and takes its first row.
  • Spell UI reads them as the list's tableColumns; a page's own column-defs still win.
