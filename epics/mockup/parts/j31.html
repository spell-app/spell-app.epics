Chose one cancelable ui-editing, its detail every change one keystroke, paste or click made ({ edits }: set, add, remove), over sending <ui-property-editor>'s change event, as the plan said. A paste or a row's delete is several changes at once, which a one-value change event can't carry as one undo step. Not cancelled, the table shows the change in new rows of its own (a docs page, plain data); a Spell program's core cancels it and writes its things, and the table follows them. The table never changes a row in place.
