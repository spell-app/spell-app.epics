Owen's rule: reusable things left, definitions middle, runs and instances right. An API description says none of that, so each collection is placed by how much it points out against how much it's pointed at: pointed at most on the left (Reference), pointing out most on the right (Records), the rest between (Core); filled before stacking, at most columns (3). One main concept (one collection pointing at all the others, which point nowhere) gets his two-lane layout instead. The Star Wars API, where every collection links to every other, comes out Planet and Vehicle | Starship and Character | Film and Species. Drag boxes to taste; where they're put is kept.
