Owen's rule for a diagram's columns: reusable things left, definitions middle, runs and instances right. An API description says none of that.

  • So each collection is placed by how much it points out, against how much it's pointed at:
    • pointed at most: on the left (Reference)
    • pointing out most: on the right (Records)
    • the rest: between (Core)
  • Columns are filled before stacking, at most columns (3).
  • One main concept gets his two-lane layout instead: one collection pointing at all the others, which point nowhere.
  • The Star Wars API, where every collection links to every other, comes out: Planet and Vehicle | Starship and Character | Film and Species.
  • Drag boxes to taste; where they're put is kept.
