Edit's white is the bubbly code's paper (white, or #1c1b22 in dark), not the theme's pane: the Inspector's cards take it only while the code is drawn bubbly (the page's spell-bubbly class). The site editor's board and form now follow the same switch, through one new token, --spell-code-paper, which the Inspector reads too instead of its hard-coded colours.

With the bubbly look turned off, every pane (code, Inspector, site editor) goes back to the theme's glass together. It's the same in every theme: the code's paper doesn't change with the theme.

The sitemap board and the page form are white in light and near-black in dark, exactly matching the code editor and Inspector, in every theme.
