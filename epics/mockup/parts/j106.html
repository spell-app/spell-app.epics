Solitaire's actions moved out of the toolbar's centre slot and into the toolbar itself, marked shrinkable, so they shrink as Owen chose: whole titles, then cut with …, then icons only (no … button), then one … button. For that, an <action "..."> placed directly in a <toolbar> now draws as one of the toolbar's items, the same way it already did in a <menu>. Before, it drew as a plain button. No other built-in project puts actions directly in a toolbar.
