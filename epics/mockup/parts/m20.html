In any VS Code window, open a terminal at the kit's root, and run this, with your company site's real address:

cd ~/www/spell
spell place sign-in work https://your-company-site
  1. A Chromium window opens: it is Spell's own, not your Chrome.
  2. Sign in as usual, through the company's sign-in.
  3. When the site shows, click back in the terminal and press Enter.
The terminal says Signed in to work, how many cookies it kept, and that they are in ~/.spell/place-sessions/work.json, in no repo.
