In VS Code's ⎇ mockup window, open the editor (its address is in .spell-server.editor.json at the worktree's root) and go to: 

/run/@spell/examples/StarWarsSite/films
  1. Click A New Hope in the table.
  2. Under "Who's in it", click Luke Skywalker, then the sidebar's ✕.
  3. Click Add a review, type a few words, click Save the review.
  4. Click Settings, then Done.
  5. Press the browser's Back.
  1. The film's page: its crawl, its people as buttons, its planets, ships and vehicles as links; the address ends /films/1.
  2. A sidebar on the right with Luke's birth year, height and home; ✕ closes it.
  3. A dialog asks for stars and words; saved, it closes and the review shows under Reviews (kept in this browser).
  4. A Settings dialog; Done closes it.
  5. Back to the films.
