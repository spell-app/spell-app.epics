There's nowhere to see a site whole, or to add, move or rename its pages, except by editing its file.

  • Open the example site's site editor (from the editor's menu, or its address): every page as a tree in the side navigation's order, and as a board of cards, each with its title, address, state and screenshot if it has one.
  • Add, delete and rename a page; drag it to another place in the tree; each one undoable.
  • Edit a page's address and its parameters in a form beside the board; a mistake (two pages at one address) shows at once.
  • Double-click a card: that page opens in Edit; a link at the top of Edit goes back to the site editor.
  • The editor itself is untouched: no new mode beside Run and Edit.

The site editor, its own page in the editor app at /site/<project>, like the theme editor: the pages as a tree and as a board of cards.

Every change is written into the site file, as any edit in the editor is; opening a page goes to it in Edit.

  1. A new route, /site/<project>, beside the theme editor's, and a SiteEditorPage built the way ThemeEditorPage is: the app's top bar, then the editor. The editor's menu gets "Site" for a project that's a site.
  2. The left side: the pages as a <ui-tree>, in the site's order. The middle: a board of cards, one per page, its title, address, state (old, new, mocked, built), its screenshot if it has one, else its title large.
  3. The right side: the picked page's form: title, address, parameters (each with its kind, from the project's types), state. A clash (two pages at one address) shows on the field at once.
  4. Add, delete, rename, and drag in the tree or on the board. Each is an edit to the site file's lines, through the editor's own source edits (PartEdits), so it saves and undoes as any edit does.
  5. Double-click a card: the page opens in Edit. Edit shows a small "Back to the site" link while it came from here.
  6. Its texts in the app's text files, English and Spanish.
  7. Checked by: app tests (each board change writes the right lines, and undoes); screenshots with /designer; a manual test for Owen.
  8. Left out: live previews of each page on its card (a screenshot or the title for now); the NERD tab (P10).
