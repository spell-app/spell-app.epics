Only a TYPE's name may be quoted (a "pile" is a thing); a one-of-a-kind's (an app, a page, a page layout, a site) is bare words, jarvis console new is a site, and its variable is those words. Allowing quotes would be a change to every one-of-a-kind, not just sites, so I left it; jarvis-new keeps jarvis console new. Say if you want quoted one-of-a-kind names.
