Chosen while building, in the shape of the words beside them.

  • A site line: , thumbnail "x.jpg"; Spanish , miniatura "x.jpg".
  • A page or view: - its thumbnail is "x.jpg"; Spanish - su miniatura es "x.jpg", and the attribute miniatura=.
  • Inside a drawing: draw its thumbnail; Spanish dibujar su miniatura. It is a slot of every view, so spellx writes <draw "thumbnail"/> too.
