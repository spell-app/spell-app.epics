A submenu's words are its label, not its title as the brief's markup wrote.

  • The brief wrote <ui-submenu title="Theme">.
  • label is <ui-item>'s own name for an item's words.
  • title is the browser's tooltip attribute: on the row it would pop a native "Theme" tooltip over the word Theme.
  • And <ui-item> already reads the page's title as its tooltip.
  • So it's <ui-submenu label="Theme" icon="paint brush">.
  • You write label= on a submenu, as on an item.
