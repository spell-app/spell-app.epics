Spell sites' groups look and act like the target sidebar; the docs site's nav is unchanged.

Reversed by Owen in I20 (2026-11-15): "The top level sections should have icons too and the icons should be indented. When collapsed, lose the indent".

  • A group's line names its icon as a page's does: group "Hardware", color yellow, icon "microchip", or group "Hardware", icon "microchip" (Spanish , icono "...").
  • Its icon shows before its label; its pages are a step in, their icons under the start of the group's label.
  • Collapsed, every icon is in one column, with no step in.

The site nav needed groups that open by themselves and have no arrow, while the docs site's own nav keeps its tinted heading bands with arrows. So a page's band takes a third value, group (Spanish grupo), on <ui-site-nav> and <ui-tree-item>, rather than a new attribute; a Spell site's group lines become it.

  • A group holding the page shown is always open; others stay as the person left them, so more than one can be open.
  • A click (or Enter) on a shut group opens it and goes to its first page; on an open group that doesn't hold the page shown, it closes it; on the one that does, it goes to its first page. The arrow keys only open and close.
  • A group left behind closes again unless the person opened it by hand.
  • Groups get no icon, and their label lines up with the pages' icons.
