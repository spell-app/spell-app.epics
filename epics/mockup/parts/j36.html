The plan said to share the tree diagram's box measuring and line drawing. Measuring is shared: TreeMeasure now measures any text (widthOf(), fontOf(), one canvas a page), and the diagram measures with it. Line drawing isn't: the tree draws straight parent-to-child lines, the diagram the NERD skill's brackets, buses, loops and curves, so there's nothing in common to move. The diagram's lines are its own, in DiagramLayout, ported from the skill's script.
