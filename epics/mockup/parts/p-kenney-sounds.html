Spell can play a sound file (epic data T145), but ships no sounds: every program has to find its own. Owen wants Kenney's audio packs in (kenney.nl, Audio): free sound packs under CC0, no credit needed -- interface clicks, casino and card sounds, impacts, jingles, digital and sci-fi effects, voice-overs.

Kenney's sounds as Spell's built-in sound library: each one by a plain name a program can play (play the "card slide" sound), the way built-in icons work, with a sound picker in the editor like the icon picker (listen, then pick).

The examples use them: Solitaire and Checkers' cards and pieces, Counter's and the calculator's clicks, a won game's jingle. A credit to Kenney in the docs, though CC0 needs none.
