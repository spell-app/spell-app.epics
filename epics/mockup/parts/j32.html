A table's row is deleted with a small × or a key, not a menu per row (the plan said "a key and the row's menu").

  • The ×: at the row's last cell, shown on hover or while the cursor's in the row.
  • The key: ⌘ / Ctrl + Delete on a cell.
  • Why no menu: one action needs no menu, and a grid's cells stay plain.
  • Delete alone clears a cell, as in a spreadsheet.
