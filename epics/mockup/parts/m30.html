In Chrome, open test.spell.app (the main editor, which has all of this epic).

  1. Open a project of your own, change a word in its main file, and wait a second (AutoSave).
  2. Reload (⌘R); press Run.
  3. Copy the app's own page address (Run's open-in-new-tab button), and open it in a private window (⇧⌘N).
  4. Back in the editor, press Discard under the file tabs.
  1. After the reload, the code and the running app both show your word, and Your draft sits under the tabs.
  2. The private window shows the published version, without your word.
  3. Discard puts the published text back everywhere.
