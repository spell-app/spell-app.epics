J34 B: a data-mode table past 200 rows draws only the rows in view, and 15 around them.

  • A spacer row above and below stands in for the rest, as tall as they'd be (one row's measured height).
  • Editable or not: the same body.
  • The cursor moving to an undrawn row draws it first.
  • Keys, edits and paste act on the data, so a paste fills rows past the window.
  • ARIA: aria-rowcount on the table, aria-rowindex on each drawn row.
