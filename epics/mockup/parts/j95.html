<ui-submenu menu="examples-menu"> shows that <ui-menu> in place, never moving it.

  • It's anchored to the row by the implicit anchor showPopover({ source }) sets, as <ui-popup> does: a menu elsewhere can't name an anchor inside the row's shadow root.
  • Its frame and place are inline styles while open (tokens, so themes reach them), put back when it closes.
  • Writing popover on it keeps it hidden until then:
    • the menu's sheet, UIMenu.css, now hides a menu written as a popover while it isn't open
    • before, its display: contents beat the browser's own display: none
  • A choice in it is stopped there and sent again from the row, once, so it bubbles through the row's menu as the inside items' choices do.
  • Known edge: inside a <ui-popup>, a click on a header or a disabled row of a by-id fly-out counts as outside the popup, so the popup closes.
  • The named menu's tree is untouched; its choices reach the row's menu.
