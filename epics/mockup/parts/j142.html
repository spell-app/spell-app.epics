Spell UI had no avatar that draws an initial: <ui-avatar> was only a picture, sized by its card, item or comment. On its own it is now a circle (2em, --ui-avatar-size), its picture or its text on the theme's primary colour, like the editor's own purple O. With link it's an <a> that Tab reaches, named by its tooltip (a new rootLabel hook on the parts' base class). No new attribute.

The no-dividers look is <ui-app-menu>'s own default, not a site-only rule: the editor's bar already hid them (EditorTheme.css zeroes their width), and its bar's pixels are unchanged before/after.

Notices and Help are <button appearance="tertiary"> icon buttons: the component's quietest look, no box.

Jarvis's top bar meets the side nav, has no lines in it, quiet bell and ? icons, and a round purple O for Me, inset like the side bar button.
