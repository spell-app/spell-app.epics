On a phone-width window, a site's nav in Run doesn't fold into a slide-out yet.

  • In Run, the site draws its nav as a plain two-column box beside the page, not in <ui-page>.
  • Why: <ui-page> takes the whole window, and Run draws inside the editor's page.
  • Fix: the site's frame turns its nav into a <ui-flyout> under a width, the way the page does, without taking the window.
