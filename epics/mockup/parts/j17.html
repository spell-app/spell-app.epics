Each change to a site is worked out from its file's text by SiteEdits, as P2's Create it already did.

  • The changes: add, delete, rename, move, a title or an address.
  • Each is made as ONE step of the editor's history, through the editor's own source edits (SourceEdits.editBetween(), editor.history).
  • A drawn part's lines are found by parsing (SourceHomes finds a value's line).
  • A site's lines are found by their own words instead, in either of Spell's forms, with no parser loaded.
