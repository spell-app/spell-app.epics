Each change (add, delete, rename, move, a title or address) is worked out from the site file's text by SiteEdits, as P2's Create it already did, then made as ONE step of the editor's history (the editor's own source edits, SourceEdits.editBetween(), editor.history). The parse-based homes (SourceHomes) find a value's line in a drawn part; a site's lines are found by their own words, in either of Spell's forms, with no parser loaded.
