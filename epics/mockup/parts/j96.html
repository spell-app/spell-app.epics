A fly-out must be role="menu", its items menuitems with arrow keys; interactive draws a menubar.

  • The row sets isAFlyout on the menu it draws round its items, or on the one it names: role=menu, roving focus, no frame of its own (the fly-out's box is its frame, as a popup is).
  • No new attribute.
  • UIMenu's item scans now follow a <slot> to what's assigned to it, since the menu a submenu draws holds only its slot; and a click on an item the menu doesn't own, or on a submenu row, sends no ui-choosing from it (no double choice).
  • Nothing for pages to write; a menu becomes a fly-out by being shown as one.
