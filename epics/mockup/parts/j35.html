Your comment on P10 asked for movable and resizable as generic attributes on most Spell UI elements (J10). Tonight they're narrower:

  • dragging a box to move it is the diagram's own (any box, lines following, remembered)
  • resizable is on <ui-diagram-node> only: bare or both, horizontally, vertically, the browser's own corner grip, limited by plain CSS (max-width: 50%)

Why not every tag: six tags already have a resizable of their own, meaning "drag its height" (<ui-segment>, <ui-table>, <ui-container>, <ui-dropdown>, <ui-search>, <ui-split-panel>). A shared attribute is added only where a tag has none, so those six would keep the old meaning while every other tag got the new one, and every tag's docs and types would change. That's a call to make awake.
