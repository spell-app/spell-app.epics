In VS Code's ⎇ mockup window, open the editor (its address is in .spell-server.editor.json at the worktree's root). Open Star Wars Site, then View › Site.

  1. Click the Film card, change Title to Movie, press Enter, then ⌘Z.
  2. Drag "settings dialog" in the tree onto the Films row.
  3. Pick Planets, press Delete, choose Delete them all, then ⌘Z.
  4. Double-click the Person card, then press Back to the site, low on the left.

Don't save: ⌘Z each change, so the example stays as it is on disk.

  1. The tree on the left, 16 cards, a pick-a-page hint on the right; the card and row say Movie, then Film again.
  2. A line marks the drop; the dialog moves first in the tree and the board.
  3. The confirm names Planet; both go; ⌘Z brings both back.
  4. Edit opens at <spell-page "person page">; Back to the site returns.
