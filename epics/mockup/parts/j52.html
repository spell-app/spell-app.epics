Dropping on the middle of a row means into only when both rows are pages with addresses.

  • Dialogs and sidebars: as you asked, they can't hold pages. I also don't let one be dropped inside a page: it opens over any page, and the language files it with the site's dialogs wherever it's written.
  • The not found and error pages: they have no address, and the language reads nothing under them, so a page dropped there would vanish from the site.
  • Those rows have just two halves, before and after.
