In a browser, open Spell UI's segment page:

http://127.0.0.1:4747/worktrees/mockup/components/ui/ui-segment.html

Open the page's dev tools console (⌥⌘J in Chrome) and paste:

const s = document.querySelector("ui-segment"); s.id = "try-me"; s.setAttribute("movable", ""); s.setAttribute("resizable", "horizontally")

Drag the segment by its middle; drag its bottom right corner; reload.

A grab cursor; it moves with the pointer, inside its container; the corner widens it, but not taller; after reload, both are as you left them (its id remembers them).
