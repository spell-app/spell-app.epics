The plan said a site would be a built-in template, reusing the place lines' shape, with no new rule module if it fit. The template carries the class (Site, an app, in the core), but a site's own lines needed rules of their own:

  • create_site: the site's line, with where it lives, gathering its parts into the class
  • site_page: a page at its address, refused when the name, address or a parameter is wrong
  • site_overlay: a dialog or a sidebar

Census of why not the classes or places modules: the places module's lines only read under a place, and the classes module would have had to import the places one (where the plain-value writer lives), a circular load. So it's a fourth small module, sites, loaded after places, three rules and two word rules, in English and Spanish.
