In Chrome, open test.spell.app (the main editor, which has all of this epic).

  1. Open any project, click the project's name in the bar (left of File).
  2. Hover Theme, near the bottom of that menu: its fly-out opens to the right.
  3. Move the mouse diagonally down-right toward a theme near the fly-out's bottom, crossing Home on the way.
  4. Narrow the window until the bar folds its menus into one, open it, and do the same with File.
  1. The fly-out opens at once, with the chosen theme ticked.
  2. Crossing Home on the way does NOT close the fly-out or open anything else; you reach the theme and can click it.
  3. Resting on Home for about half a second does switch to it.
  4. The folded menu's File, Edit, View, Run and Help fly out the same way; → and Enter open them from the keyboard.
