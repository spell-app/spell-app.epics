The fly-out is a popup surface, so its background reads --ui-popup-background first, then --ui-surface as before. Inside the editor's bar popup it inherits the popup's near-opaque surface by itself; the blur and padding come from MenuBar.css's ui-submenu::part(flyout), which now wins because the box's look moved from inline style into the submenu's own sheet.
