In a browser, open:

http://127.0.0.1:4747/worktrees/mockup/components/ui/ui-table.html#examples-content-editable
  1. Click "The Empire Strikes Back", then press the arrow keys, Tab, and End.
  2. Press F2, type a few words, press Enter; then type into a cell and press Escape.
  3. Click a Mood cell, press F2, pick another mood, press Enter.
  4. In Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets, select a block of two rows by two columns (a film's name and a number) and copy it (⌘C). Click the Film cell of "Return of the Jedi" and paste (⌘V).
  5. Add ?theme=mockup before the # and look again.
  1. A blue outline moves cell by cell; Tab runs on to the next row.
  2. The cell keeps your words; Escape leaves the cell as it was.
  3. A list of moods opens in the cell, and the cell shows the one picked.
  4. The pasted block fills from that cell, and a new row is added for the second line.
  5. The same, square, in the sketch's handwriting: no rounded corners or padding around the field being edited.
