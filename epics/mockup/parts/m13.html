In VS Code's ⎇ mockup window, open the editor (its address is in .spell-server.editor.json at the worktree's root) and go to: 

/site/@spell/examples/StarWarsSite
  1. Press Data, top middle.
  2. Click the Character box.
  3. In Character: sample rows, double-click the height cell, type 180, press Enter, then reload the page and press Data again.
  4. Click the turned Character card again.
  5. Drag the Film box somewhere else, reload, press Data.
  6. Set Columns to 2.
  7. Press Save as SVG under the diagram.
  1. A diagram of the six Star Wars collections: each box shows only its links (homeworld, films[]), dashed lines between them, a legend.
  2. It turns over like a card to every field and its kind (homeworld → Planet, url id); its sample rows show below.
  3. The height says 180 after the reload.
  4. It turns back to the NERD box, its lines from its rows again.
  5. Film stays where you dragged it.
  6. The boxes rearrange into two columns.
  7. An SVG file downloads.
