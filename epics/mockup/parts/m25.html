In Chrome, open test.spell.app (the main editor, which has all of this epic).

  1. Open a project of your own that has a picture (or any example, e.g. Star Wars Site).
  2. Click the project's name in the bar, left of File, and choose Export….
  3. In Finder, open Downloads: double-click the new .zip to look inside, then close it.
  4. Back in the editor, project menu › Import…, and pick that same .zip.
  1. The .zip is named after the project and holds a folder of its name with every file, pictures and the project file included.
  2. Import opens a NEW project of your own with the same files, and says it was imported.
  3. Its page and source files show Your draft under the tabs; Publish makes them public.
