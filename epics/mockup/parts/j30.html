Every table a Spell page drew came out as run-on text; now it's laid out as a table.

  • A table drawn from a <for ... as="table"> lays out its rows with a PAGE sheet, which never reached inside a view's own shadow root.
  • Spell UI's style registry now hands its page sheets to a root that asks (UI.styles.adoptPageSheetsInto()), and every drawn view asks.
  • Other roots are unchanged.
