A dial writes its unit right after the value: unit="%" gives 98%, unit="°" gives 250°. A unit that wants a space says so itself, unit=" px" for 16 px. No list of units, no formatting by locale.

  • A: what you write is what shows; the docs page says to lead with a space.
  • B, C: fewer surprises for word units, but a list to keep, and the Spanish and English output can differ.
