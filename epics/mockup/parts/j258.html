With no color, the arc and the handle are the theme's primary colour (--ui-primary, through --ui-dial-fill-color); the ring, disc and ticks are the ink at low strength, so they follow light and dark. Owen's picture gives each dial its own colour, which color does.

  • A: matches the slider, the progress bar and the other controls.
  • B: quieter, but an uncoloured dial would hardly show its value.
