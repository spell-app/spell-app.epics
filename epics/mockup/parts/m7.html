In VS Code's ⎇ mockup window, open the editor (its address is in .spell-server.editor.json), open Star Wars Site, then View › Site. ⌘Z after each change, and don't save.

  1. Click the Film card.
  2. Tick the first job.
  3. Press Add a story, change its words, click away, then Open in Edit.
  4. Hover a job whose details field is empty.
  1. Three jobs under the form; the middle one has its "done when" line under it.
  2. Its words struck through; the card says "1 of 3 jobs"; ⌘Z takes it back.
  3. "As a …, I want …, so that …" with "- done when: …" under it, and the site file shows it in the page's <spell-jobs>.
  4. The "Details, e.g. - done when: …" hint shows only while hovering or typing.
