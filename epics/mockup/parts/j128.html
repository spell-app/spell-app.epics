Each page under a group is written in row 1 column 2, because column 1 used to be the group's own card. With the group drawn as a line over its cards instead, the board draws a group's cards one column to the left, so they start at the section's left edge. Nothing in the file changes, and a site whose sections are pages (not groups) keeps its section card in column 1. Dragging a group's line shows only the line where it would land, not the spots: a group can only go before or after another section.

Groups' cards line up under their header lines with no empty first column; no site file is rewritten.
