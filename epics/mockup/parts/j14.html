A Star Wars page's address carries only the last part of the thing's id, /films/1, because the ids are web addresses (https://swapi.py4e.com/api/films/1/).

  • Encoded whole, the router decodes the slashes back, and the address breaks.
  • The site finds the thing again:
    1. among the program's things
    2. else by id, from its place
    3. else by reading the collection and matching that last part
