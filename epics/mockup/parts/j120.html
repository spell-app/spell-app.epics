Spell UI's other names for icons alone are a whole group's: <ui-buttons icon> and <ui-menu icon> ("Icon-only buttons / items"). On an action icon already names its icon, so icon-only is the only word left, and it matches Spell's own <action icon-only> on a button and <ui-link>'s :state(icon-only). Spanish: solo-icono, as Spell's.

Its title box is hidden, not removed, so a shrinkable bar still folds it into the … button with the rest (what Owen asked for in J107); an action with no icon shows its title.

  • Undo and Redo are icons when wide, and fold into … when narrowest.
