Tokens: `--ui-tree-column-width` (14em), `--ui-tree-column-picked-background` (the text colour at 13%), `--ui-tree-column-selected-background` / `-color` (the primary, and its text). A column tree is 18em tall unless the page sets its height. Widths a person drags are kept per level under the tree's `id` in the browser's storage, store `ui-tree-column-widths` (the same PrefStore as <ui-toolbar remember>); narrowest 80px; arrow keys move 16px, ×10 with Shift (as <ui-split-panel>'s bars).

A page can size and theme the columns without new attributes.
