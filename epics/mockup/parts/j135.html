Picking a row opens it, and closes every other open row but the ones it's inside, so the open rows are the chain of picks, one a column. A picked row with no rows is open too: it draws no column, but its `open` is what draws it grey once a deeper pick is made, and what → goes back onto. The tree's state is `columns`; its rows' is `in-columns` (as `:state(in-card)`), since both share one sheet and the tree's 18em height must not reach the rows.

No new attribute: `open` already says which row is picked in each column.
