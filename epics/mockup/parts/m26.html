In Chrome, open test.spell.app (the main editor, which has all of this epic).

  1. Open a project of your own, change a word in its code, and wait a second (AutoSave keeps it as your draft).
  2. Project menu › Versions.
  3. Click Save Current Changes, at the dialog's lower left.
  4. Pick an older version and click Restore; then open Versions again and restore your kept draft.
  1. A new entry appears at once in the list, marked as your kept draft, and the dialog stays open.
  2. Restoring the older version replaces your changes with it, as your draft.
  3. Restoring the kept entry brings your word back. Nobody else sees any of it until you Publish.
