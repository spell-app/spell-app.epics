Owen asked for a top bar on most of a site's pages, and didn't say how a site gets one. So:

  • A site opts in with a line of its own: it has a top bar (also it has an app bar; Spanish tiene una barra superior / tiene una cabecera). Sites without the line are unchanged: Star Wars keeps its page tops and its narrow menu button.
  • The bar's buttons at its end are the view named top bar, when the program declares one, found the way a dialog's view is (a "top bar" is a view).
  • A page opts out at the end of its line: , no top bar (Spanish , sin barra superior). The bar is hidden there, not redrawn, so its buttons keep their state.
  • The side bar button uses Spell UI's columns icon, as the app menu's docs example does: spell icon sidebar only finds bars.
  • Collapsed is remembered per site class and person in spell-sites:<Site>.collapsed. Only a site with a top bar collapses, since nothing else could open the side bar again.
  • Narrow (where the side bar slides over the page) is read from the side bar's own style at the click, so the container query stays the one rule.
  • Not built: extra buttons for one page only. Each page drawing its own buttons into the bar needs a slot on the page, which is more than a line. The view could show buttons by the page's address, meanwhile.
