Spell's live rooms are a space's rooms (is a room at "owen@spell/matches"): joining one is reading that space.

  • In the editor on your machine (built): two tabs join as you, this machine's person, with no sign-in typed. To make that work the editor now passes the page server's write token on a room's websocket too, as it already did for data folders (/api/rooms in the editor's proxy). The sign-in bar still shows at the top (“until you are, it takes you to be this computer's person”).
  • Anywhere else (the cloud, someone else's machine): they must sign in with a Spell account that is a member of the space, and the example names your space, owen@spell, since a room can't say “this machine's person's own space” yet.
  • Not used: the @spell org's space, which on disk is the repo's built-in folder, so a game wrote into git.
