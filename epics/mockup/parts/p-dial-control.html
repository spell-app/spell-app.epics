A new Spell UI component, <ui-dial> (<dial> in Spell): a 3/4-circle track with a coloured arc up to the value and a round handle at its end, the value big in a filled disc in the middle, its label under it; optional tick dots; dragged round, or by keys, as a slider is (min, max, step, value, a format such as percent); a form field like <ui-slider>.
