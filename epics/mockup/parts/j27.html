A site picks its theme with a property, no new words, and wears it on its own frame only.

  • The site file says <property "theme">"mockup"</property>; in code Spell, - its "theme" is "mockup".
  • The running site asks its host for the theme's tokens, and wears them inline on its frame, so the editor around it keeps its own theme.
  • The code: SiteHost.themeTokens(), and the editor's RunSite, on Spell UI's scopedTokens().
  • What comes through: the Mockup theme's colours, Patrick Hand, its 2px ink lines, its corners' sizes, no shadows
  • What doesn't: its hand-drawn uneven corners, which are rules on Spell UI's class words and go on the whole page or nowhere
  • The page's light or dark stays the page's: in a dark editor the sketch is dark
