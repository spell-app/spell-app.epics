Renaming a page renames its line in the site and its own declaration, nothing else.

  • Its declaration: <spell-page "x">, or a "x" is a page.
  • Lines elsewhere that name the page (go to the films page) are left alone: P3's check marks each as a name the site doesn't list, so nothing changes behind your back.
  • Renaming them too is a later step, if you want it.
