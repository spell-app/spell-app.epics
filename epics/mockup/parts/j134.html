A new theme surface, surfaces.side-panel, in every theme: its menu bar's colour family at its palest in light, a step lighter than the bar in dark. Named side-panel, not panel-background: --ui-panel-background is already <ui-panel>'s own box token (and <ui-field> reads it), so a theme setting it would repaint every ui-panel.

  • Spell: brand-25 #fcfdff / violet-925 #221750 (bar: brand-75 / violet-950)
  • Glass (and Fruity, which extends it): #f9f7fd (its lavender page halfway to white) / #1e1c26 (its bar's plum, solid)
  • Crisp: #ffffff (nothing paler than its white bar) / #141414 (its inset)
  • Matrix: #fafcfb (matrix-raised) / #0d1518 (matrix-panel)
  • Merlot: #fefcfd (merlot-panel) / #2b1520 (merlot-inset)
  • Midnight: #fbfcfd (midnight-panel) / #121a24 (midnight-inset)
  • Mockup: #ffffff / #2a2a2a (its inset)
  • Paper: #fffcf4 (paper-panel) / #241c15 (paper-inset)
  • Pop: #fffaf4 (its peach page halfway to white) / #24122f (pop-inset)
The editor's Inspector cards and the site editor's board and form wear each theme's own palest bar colour, instead of 5% of the primary mixed into white or near-black.
