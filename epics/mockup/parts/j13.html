A site's routing (showing the page its address names, its dialogs and sidebars, its not found and error pages) can't be said in Spell, so it's a hand-written core class, SiteBase, which the generated Site template class extends (the generator's HAND_WRITTEN_BASES). The template keeps what Spell can say, its current address, in every language.
