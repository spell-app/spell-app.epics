A site's routing can't be said in Spell, so it's a hand-written core class, SiteBase.

  • Routing: showing the page its address names, its dialogs and sidebars, its not found and error pages.
  • The generated Site template class extends it (listed in the generator's HAND_WRITTEN_BASES).
  • The template keeps what Spell can say, its current address, in every language.
  • Python's core keeps App under Site: Python runs no site yet (listed as not ported).
