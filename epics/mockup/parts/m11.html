In VS Code's ⎇ mockup window, open the editor (its address is in .spell-server.editor.json at the worktree's root), then go to: 

/run/@spell/examples/StarWarsSite/reviews
  1. Click the empty last row's "words" cell, type a few words, press Enter.
  2. Click its "stars" cell, type 4, press Enter.
  3. Reload the page.
  4. Click a cell of that row, press ⌘ + Delete.
  1. A new review row appears.
  2. Its stars say 4.
  3. The review is still there: it's kept on this device.
  4. The row goes; ⌘Z brings it back.
