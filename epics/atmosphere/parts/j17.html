A space's own folder is written by its members; a program's own data stays "only the owner".

  • A space's own folder (@family/cookbook, owen@spell/recipes) is written by its members, every thing the space's own.
  • A program's own data stays P1's "only the owner", so everyone who runs a built-in example still keeps their own rows:
    • "recipes" alone
    • personal "recipes"
    • anything under __data/
  • One rule, whoWritesFolderAt(), read by the server and the compiler alike.

So a person's own folder now refuses anyone else outright ("your role can't write"), where P1 let them add rows of their own; inviting them is adding a member (which makes the folder's __members/).
