Spell has no phone app: on iPhone, a web page can't even send an alert until it's added to the Home Screen.

  • Bo installs Spell, signs in with his emailed code, picks RS from his orgs, and sees its feed, his inbox and its threads.
  • One app for every org, with the org's name and look inside it.
  • On a plane, he reads and edits what's on the phone; his changes go up when he's back, and any the write server refuses say so.
  • More than a wrapped website: real sign-in, a list of orgs, alerts (next phase).

A phone app for iOS and Android (a thin native shell around Spell's own pages, with Capacitor: Q18 A): one Spell app holding the same pages, signing in, a list of your orgs, and a copy on the phone for working offline.

  1. Chosen: Q18 A, a thin native shell (Capacitor) around Spell's own pages.
  2. The shell: a new folder, its own build, holding the same pages the browser gets.
  3. Signing in: the emailed code typed into the app (the link opening the app is a later step).
  4. Orgs: a list of the orgs you're in, from your account, and a picker.
  5. Offline: nothing new: DeviceCopy, which keeps what's been read on the device, with the save queue for changes; long lists drawn a window at a time (drawnInWindow()), the feed through <ui-feed>.
  6. Needs you: Xcode and Android Studio on this machine (free), and a real phone to try it on.
  7. Checked by: both simulators, headless where they allow.
  8. Left out: alerts (next phase), the store (P39), a branded app per org (after the first release).
