Before sign-in, a cloud place's writes wait in this browser's save queue, and go up once you sign in.

  • The place itself (RemotePlace, identifiesBy: "a sign-in") reads no rows, and lets a write fail as no answer would.
  • So it stays pending, in this browser, through the save queue every place has, and goes up within 3 seconds of signing in.
  • No copy of its own, no sync step.
  • The bar says so: "family cookbook is kept in this browser only, until you sign in with a Spell account".
  • Kept across a reload only where the page remembers its program:
    • the editor
