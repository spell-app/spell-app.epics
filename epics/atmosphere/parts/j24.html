An account can be hidden, never deleted outright; a space can be deleted.

  • The plan says hide or delete a record, a space or an account.
  • Deleting an account would remove a person's file and every space they keep, with no undo.
  • So delete refuses an account, and says to hide it and delete its spaces one by one.
  • Deleting a space removes every thing of it but its __ names: its log, its member list (__members/).
