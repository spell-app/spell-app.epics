The abuse address is a server setting, and shows wherever something's refused or taken down.

  • "An address in the server's settings": the app's environment.abuseContact, from SPELL_ABUSE_CONTACT.
  • Its default, abuse@spell.spaces, is a guess at the domain until Q12 settles where Spell's mail comes from. Settled in P18 · Real Sign-In Mail: abuse@spell.app, and abuse@spell.space for what's hosted under spell.space.
  • It shows:
    • on the sign-in bar (signed out)
    • on the link's page
    • in the command's output
    • in every refusal of something taken down
