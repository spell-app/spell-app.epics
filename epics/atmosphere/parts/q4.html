You asked: what is "per folder" here?

A data folder is epic data's word for one program's saved rows, kept together under one address.

  • The family cookbook's recipes are one data folder, at ana@spell/family-cookbook.
  • Ana's own recipe box, which nobody else sees, is another, at ana@spell/recipes.

"One list of people per folder" means each of those carries its own list of who may read and write it.

  • The cookbook's list holds Ana, Bo and Grandma; the recipe box's holds only Ana.
  • Nothing finer than that: the list doesn't say "Bo may edit only his own recipes".
  • It matches a Space one to one: one data folder becomes one Space, and its list becomes the Space's member list.
