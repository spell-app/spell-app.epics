For the Phone App Shell phase (guide step 13). Spell needs a store app on iOS and Android: on iPhone a web page can't send alerts until it's added to the Home Screen, and the stores are how people install things.

  • Apple refuses "a repackaged website" (4.2), so it needs real sign-in, a list of your orgs and alerts.
  • Spell's pages are Solid and Spell UI: the app should run the same pages, not a second copy.
  • The same pages the browser gets, inside one app for both stores; native alerts, sign-in and storage through its plugins.
  • Its cost: one more toolchain (Xcode, Android Studio), and keeping it past Apple's 4.2 with real native parts.
  • Native screens, mature alerts and stores tooling.
  • Its cost: every page written a second time, in React, beside the Solid ones.
  • The best phone feel.
  • Its cost: two more apps to write and keep, in two more languages.
  • Phone App Shell (P40) is a Capacitor app holding Spell's pages
  • Phone Alerts (P41) uses its alert plugin
