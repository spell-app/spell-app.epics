From P38, the Diaspora comparison. Diaspora lets each person keep private lists of their contacts (aspects) and pick one when they post. Spell has no such thing in use: a person keeps friend-lists in the people library, and nothing reads them yet. This matters only when personal spaces are designed; an org already has its spaces and roles.

The person keeps a list, its name theirs alone, and a space takes its members from it. Each thing still lives in one space, with one member list that the host can enforce. Its cost: people in the space can see each other, which an aspect does not show.Closest to Diaspora. Its cost: the check happens per post, which only Spell would enforce, as a real Space has one list per space; and AT Protocol argues against it as too many lists to keep in your head.No lists. Its cost: the same group is typed in again for each new space. The friend-lists field would then be removed.

Nothing is built now. When personal spaces are planned, friend lists become what fills a personal space member list, and the guide section on Diaspora says why.
