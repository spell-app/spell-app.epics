1. In VS Code's ⎇ atmosphere window, open a terminal (Terminal > New Terminal), pick the worktree's folder, and start the cloud: 
    spell space cloud serve
  2. Open a second terminal the same way, and set up RS, with your own email as its first Admin: 
    spell space org create rs --name "RS" --admin YOUR-EMAIL --team "Events Team"
  3. In your browser, open http://127.0.0.1:4949/apps/@spell/examples/OrgAdmin, type your email in the sign-in bar, and press Email me a link.
  4. In the second terminal, read the code, and type it on the page: 
    spell space cloud mail
  5. On the Admin page: add a person, make a team's Manager until next year, and look at the log.
  6. Then open the same address with Inbox, OrgFeed and OrgChat in place of OrgAdmin: send yourself a message, post in the chat.

Each page signs in once and shows RS's data: the Admin page's change shows in its log, the message arrives in the Inbox, and the chat message shows at once in a second tab.
