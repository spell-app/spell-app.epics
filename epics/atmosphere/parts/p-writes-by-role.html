Anyone who may write a space may write anything in it: anyone could make a page, or edit someone else's post.

  • Bo, a viewer of RS's shared spaces, posts and comments there where the space allows it, and edits or deletes only his own.
  • Ana, a Lead and so her spaces' manager, makes pages and installs apps from the Admins' approved list, at the version listed.
  • Ana can delete Bo's post, and can't edit it. Cy, an editor, edits any existing page or sheet (never people's posts) and makes nothing new.
  • Every refusal says which role it needed, in English and Spanish, as P4's do.
  • An app can narrow these rules for itself, never widen them.

Spell's write server checks what each role may write: viewers post and comment where a space allows it and change only their own; editors also edit any existing page or sheet; managers (a space's Leads) also make pages, install approved apps, and delete (never edit) others' posts.

  1. Kinds of thing: a space's records say what they are (a post, a comment, a page, a file, an app); FolderPlace is handed one more check per kind, as it's handed the member check today, and knows nothing of roles.
  2. The rules: the guide's role table, as rights per kind in the one role table P20 made (ROLE_FLAGS), not a second table: viewers make posts and comments (whether they comment is a setting of each space); editors also change any existing page or sheet; managers also make pages and apps, and delete anything. Making something new is a fourth right, make (canCreate), beside read, write and manage: Spaces' one write flag can't tell editing from making, so Spell checks it where a write lands, refuses in English and Spanish, and the editor app hides "New page" from anyone without it.
  3. "Change only their own": decided by who OWNS a thing (OWNER_FIELD), as FolderPlace's publicWrite already does, not by who wrote it last (BY_FIELD), which a Lead's edit would change.
  4. Approved apps: a list in the Admin space (name, version); installing anything else is refused.
  5. An app's own rules: a program may say a sheet takes only attendees' rows; Spell checks that after the role check.
  6. Checked by: tests, a row of the table each, and one where a Lead edits Bo's post and Bo can still change it.
  7. Left out: a sheet's own "who may update" setting (the guide's "Later").
