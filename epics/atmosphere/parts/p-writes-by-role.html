Anyone who may write a space may write anything in it: a Member could make a page, or edit someone else's post.

  • Bo, a Member, posts and comments in a public space, and edits or deletes only his own.
  • Ana, a Lead, makes pages and installs apps from the Admins' approved list, at the version listed.
  • Ana can delete Bo's post, and can't edit it.
  • Every refusal says which role it needed, in English and Spanish, as P4's do.
  • An app can narrow these rules for itself, never widen them.

Spell's write server checks what each role may write: Members post and comment and change only their own; Leads make pages, install approved apps, and delete (never edit) others' posts.

  1. Kinds of thing: a space's records say what they are (a post, a comment, a page, a file, an app); FolderPlace is handed one more check per kind, as it's handed the member check today, and knows nothing of roles.
  2. The rules: the guide's role table, written once, in the app's space code: Members make posts and comments and change their own (their by); Leads make and change pages and apps, and delete anything.
  3. Approved apps: a list in the Admin space (name, version); installing anything else is refused.
  4. An app's own rules: a program may say a sheet takes only attendees' rows; Spell checks that after the role check.
  5. Checked by: tests, a row of the table each.
  6. Left out: a sheet's own "who may update" setting (the guide's "Later").
