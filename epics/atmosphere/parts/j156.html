Planning to Quiet are the organizers' moves; the team's Managers manage the team's projects too (P20), so they may make them as well, and the Board and the Admins always. Organizers must be in the org when it's proposed.

an org's events, each from someone's idea to a quiet record (OrgEvents): anyone in the org proposes one, naming its organizers; the Events Team's Managers say yes, which makes it a project under the team, its organizers its Project Managers, and RS's template project (the Sign-Up Sheet) copied into its shared space; its organizers move it through Planning, Open and Analysis to Quiet, when its spaces turn read only (SpaceFolders asks isQuietAt() before every write); the stages are the org's event program, RS's by default (RS_EVENT_PROGRAM), kept beside the events in its Admin space (@rs/__admin/events.json5) and changed by its Board and Admins (setProgram()); every move logged; the built-in program Org Events reads them through the Admin page's place at @rs/__admin/events (events, event_stages)
