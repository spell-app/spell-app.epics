One page every attendee opens (@spell/examples/PicnicLetter): the day's details, the same for everyone; their shifts, from the published sheet, only theirs; their count, from their inbox. The organizers' Send the letter adds a message to their inbox with page: "picnic@rs/PicnicLetter", to every attendee: where an org would put the page, in the picnic's shared space, which its Manager may send.

  • Its shifts are filtered on the page, the published sheet read whole: a picnic's few dozen rows (the compile warns so).
