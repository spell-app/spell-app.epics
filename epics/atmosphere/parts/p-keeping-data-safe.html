Nothing backs a space up, nobody can take their data out, and nothing stops a space growing without end.

  • Backups of every space, and a way to bring one back.
  • Ana can download her space as a folder of plain JSON, and one changed line runs her program on it: leaving Spell is built here.
  • A group's id leaves only if Spell hands over its keys (T5): the export says so in plain words, and Spell's promise to hand them over is written down.
  • Deleting a space, or an account, deletes it.
  • Limits: a save over one is refused, says why, and nothing is lost.

Backups, export as plain JSON, delete, and limits that refuse a save without losing it.

  1. Backups: a nightly copy of every space's folder, a few kept, and a command to bring one back.
  2. Export: the space's files are already plain JSON (JsonChunks), so export is a download of the folder; changing one line to is a data folder at "./my-export" runs the program on it.
  3. Leaving with the id: the export says in plain words that an org's id stays with Spell until Spell hands over its keys; that promise goes in the server's terms page (T5, a limit until real Spaces).
  4. Delete: a space or an account deleted, and gone from backups as they age out.
  5. Limits: a space's size and a row's (about 1 MB, as AT Protocol's records); a save over is refused with the existing "over the ceiling", and the page keeps the change, saying why.
  6. Checked by: tests (backup round trip, export runs the recipe box, over-limit refused and kept).
  7. Left out: handing over keys, which needs real Spaces.
