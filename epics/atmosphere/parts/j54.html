The alpha has no upload for a space: its getBlob lexicon says a space record points to a blob uploaded by com.atproto.repo.uploadBlob, and the host answers one only while a record of that account's IN THAT SPACE points to it (else BlobNotFound, never saying whether it exists).

  • AtProtoSpace follows it: upload(), putFile(), writeFile(), file(), blob(), blobs(); a file's record is app.spell.data.file: name, type, size, the blob's ref.
  • So publishing is one more record pointing to the same blob, in the public space, with no second upload. The trial falls back to uploading it again if the alpha refuses that, and logs which.
  • A record pointing to another account's blob is refused: a member's file can't be published by the org without a fresh upload.
