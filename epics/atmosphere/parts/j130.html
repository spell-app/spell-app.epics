The guide says a person's own space holds documents and approved apps, never code they wrote, since a page runs as whoever views it. Spell has no separate document kind, so P28 draws the line at:

  • refused: a program (.spell), a script (.js, .ts, .jsx, .tsx, .mjs, .py), a web page (.html) or an SVG, and a page (.spellml) with an <action> or <script> of its own
  • allowed: a page of plain markup that only shows things ({its name} reads included), its CSS, pictures and data
Mo writes his About me as a page of text and pictures; a page with a Pay button acting on its own is refused, as is any program.
