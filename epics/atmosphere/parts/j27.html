A space in Spell is one folder (@family/cookbook), so its public read and write settings are that folder's: two of an org's folders can differ (a cookbook members only, a sign-up sheet public).

  • The settings: publicRead, publicWrite.
  • The org's file is the people library's compiled Org type: a setting there means a library change and a re-compile.
  • __settings was already one of spaces' own names, unused, so it's never listed, read direct or exported.
  • Changed only by the space's admins, through the changeSettings operation.
