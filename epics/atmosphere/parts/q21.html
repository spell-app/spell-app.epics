"Member" means two things, and Paired Spaces (P20) works one out from the other.

  • In the code: an entry on a space's list, guests included (SpaceMembers); a guest is a "member" who only reads.
  • In the Spaces guide: an org role, "Member", beside Lead and Admin (Bo is an RS Member).
  • So "Bo is a member of the finance space" could mean he's on its list, or that he holds the Member role.

The words.md word list has no entry for it yet: the answer goes there.

  • An org Member gets the role "member" in the org's spaces: the two meanings meet.
  • A space's list is "who's in the space"; a guest is in it, not a member of it.
  • Cost: only words in docs and pages; the code keeps SpaceMembers.
  • SpaceMembers becomes SpaceAccess, its entries "people with access"; "member" is only the org role.
  • Cost: a rename across the spaces code and its routes.
  • The guide's Member becomes "Volunteer"; "member" stays the space list's.
  • Cost: fits RS, not every org (a company has no volunteers).
  • words.md: "member", a person holding the Member role, in the org's spaces as role "member"
  • pages say "who's in the space" for the whole list, guests included
