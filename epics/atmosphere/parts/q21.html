What you said before

  • Spaces and Spell, its roles: Members are everyone in the org; they read anything public, make posts and comments, and edit or delete only their own.
  • Leads run a team or project: they alone reach its private space, make new pages, install approved apps, change existing pages, delete (but never edit) anyone's posts, and add or remove other Leads.
  • Admins run the site technically: every space but the org's own private one. The Board is Lead of everything.
  • The guide's table: each Spaces member list has a read flag, and "what each person can write is Spell's".
  • This page: membership records with roles in __members (Q4, Q9), writes through Spell with a by field (Q6 C), anyone-may-read and anyone-may-write settings off by default (Q13), and the phases Org Roles, Writes By Role and Publish By Role built on the Member / Lead table.
  • Today's code: guest (reads), member (writes anything), admin (also manages), spell-admin (Spell's staff).

What fits

  • member = everyone in the space: fits both the guide ("Everyone in the org is a Member, whatever else they hold") and Spaces, whose member list calls a read-only person a member too.
  • manager = make pages and add people: that's the guide's Lead, in one space. So "Lead" stays the ORG role (Team Lead, Project Lead), and being a Lead of something makes you a manager of its spaces.
  • viewer = read only: today's guest, renamed.
  • Org roles and space roles split cleanly: the org roles (Board, Admins, Team Lead, Project Lead) say WHO, and the space roles (viewer, editor, manager) say WHAT they may do in one space.

What doesn't fit yet

  1. "edit anything existing" is new. No role in the guide has it: Members change only their own things, and Leads change pages but delete, never edit, other people's posts. So does an editor edit Bo's post, or only pages? I'd say pages and sheets yes, people's posts and comments no, as the guide has it for Leads.
  2. Where the guide's Member sits. In a public space every Member posts and comments and edits their own: more than "read only (maybe comments)", less than "edit anything". I'd fold it into viewer: a viewer may add comments and posts where the space allows, and change only their own (Writes By Role already plans "only their own", by who owns a thing).
  3. "maybe comments": open. With the fold above, a space chooses whether its viewers comment and post (a newsletter: no; a public space: yes).
  4. "make pages": in Spell, a page is a file in the project, and a post or comment is a record in a data folder. So "make pages" is a manager's right to add files and folders; "make a record" (a post, a sign-up row) is the per-kind right Writes By Role adds.
  5. admin: your note was cut off. My guess: the org's technical Admins, as the guide and Q20 A have it, an org role that's a manager of every space but the org's own private one. A guess: please say.
  6. The Board isn't in the ladder: it's an org role, a manager of every space.
