An org knows only guests, members and admins: there's no Board, no teams, no projects, and no Leads.

  • RS can say: the Board is Lead of everything, the Admins of everything but the org's private space, a Team Lead of their team and its projects, a Project Lead of one project.
  • Every role can end: a term with an end date, after which the person holds it no more.
  • A project names "the Events Team", not the people on it today, so a change to the team reaches every project at once.
  • An org can rename roles, add its own, and say who's eligible for one; Spell refuses anyone else.
  • Project Leads can't remove the Board, Admins or Team Leads above them.

An org's roles move into its Admin space: the Board, the Admins, standing teams and projects, each with its Leads, all temporary, always looked up and never copied.

  1. The shape: an org has teams and projects (a project under a team or the org, never under another project); a role is a person or a team holding Lead of something, from a date, until a date. Written as Spell types in People.spell, the people library, which this epic changes here (a change it causes).
  2. One role table: the table every role check reads, ROLE_FLAGS, gains the org's roles (Lead, Admin, Member, guest) beside today's, each with its rights; P20 adds a right per kind of thing, P24 the right to publish. Folded into it:
    • the list of roles, Role, made from the table
    • ProjectDrafts.mayPublish()'s stand-in, read from it (P24 finishes it)
    • SpaceMembers.keeps(), a right in the table rather than a check of its own
    The words stay as Q20 ("admin") and Q21 ("member") settle them.
  3. Where it lives: the org's Admin space, a private space folder of its own (named as Q25 settles: its recommended option, @rs/__admin), with the rules for roles and a log of every change.
    • The log is in the takedown log's shape (who, why, when; like __takedowns.json5), so P18's recoveries and P22's page read the same entries.
  4. One look-up: "who leads this space, today?" asked of the Admin space, every time, as PeopleStore's role look-up is now. Nothing else keeps a list of people.
  5. The old roles: guest, member and admin keep working for orgs with no Admin space (the family cookbook): they're rows of the same table, so SpaceMembers reads either.
  6. Rules: who may name a Lead, eligibility, and who can't be removed, checked when a role changes.
  7. Checked by: unit tests over a small RS; today's role tests unchanged.
  8. Left out: the spaces themselves (P19 · Paired Spaces), what each role may write (P20 · Writes By Role), a page for it (P22 · Admin Page).
