An org knows only guests, members and admins: there's no Board, no teams, no projects, and no Leads.

  • RS can say: the Board is Lead of everything, the Admins of everything but the org's private space, a Team Lead of their team and its projects, a Project Lead of one project.
  • Every role can end: a term with an end date, after which the person holds it no more.
  • A project names "the Events Team", not the people on it today, so a change to the team reaches every project at once.
  • An org can rename roles, add its own, and say who's eligible for one; Spell refuses anyone else.
  • Project Leads can't remove the Board, Admins or Team Leads above them.

An org's roles move into its Admin space: the Board, the Admins, standing teams and projects, each with its Leads, all temporary, always looked up and never copied.

  1. The shape: an org has teams and projects (a project under a team or the org, never under another project); a role is a person or a team holding Lead of something, from a date, until a date. Written as Spell types in People.spell, the people library, which this epic changes here (a change it causes).
  2. One role table: the table every role check reads, ROLE_FLAGS, holds a space's three roles, viewer, editor and manager (Q21 D), each with its rights; P23 adds a right per kind of thing and the right to make something new, P26 the right to publish. The org's roles (the Board, the Admins, Team Leads, Project Leads) say who someone is, and a Lead of something is its spaces' manager; everyone in a space, or in the org, is a member of it. Folded into it:
    • the list of roles, Role, split in two: SpaceRole (viewer, editor, manager), made from the table, and the org's Role (Board, Admins, Team Lead, Project Lead) beside it
    • ProjectDrafts.mayPublish()'s stand-in, read from it (P26 finishes it)
    • SpaceMembers.keeps(), a right in the table rather than a check of its own
    The words, as Q20 A settled them: a space's role "admin" is now "manager", as its flag (canManage) says; "admin" means only the org's technical Admins.
  3. Where it lives: the org's Admin space, a private space folder of its own (@rs/__admin, Q25 A: one of Spell's own names, which no project can take), with the rules for roles and a log of every change.
    • The log is in the takedown log's shape (who, why, when; like __takedowns.json5), so P21's recoveries and P25's page read the same entries.
  4. One look-up: "who leads this space, today?" asked of the Admin space, every time, as PeopleStore's role look-up is now. Nothing else keeps a list of people.
  5. The old roles: membership files saying guest, member or admin are still read, as viewer, editor (still allowed to make things, as members are today) and manager; orgs with no Admin space (the family cookbook) use the three space roles alone. A space kept in a real Space gets read for a viewer, read and write for an editor or manager; managing stays Spell's, done as the owner's account (Q6 C).
  6. Rules: who may name a Lead, eligibility, and who can't be removed, checked when a role changes.
  7. Checked by: unit tests over a small RS; today's role tests unchanged.
  8. Left out: the spaces themselves (P22 · Paired Spaces), what each role may write (P23 · Writes By Role), a page for it (P25 · Admin Page).
