Someone who leaves a space is refused at once, not when their credential runs out.

  • The read side checks the credential's signature.
  • Then it asks the space's member list whether its person still reads (read from disk, so it works while nothing's written).
  • So someone who leaves is refused at once, rather than when the 30-day credential runs out.
  • A real Space's host can't ask Spell's list; that's P11's.
