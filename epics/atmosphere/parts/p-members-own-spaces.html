A person in an org has nowhere of their own: no "About me", no "Services I offer".

  • ana@rs has an "About me" page everyone in RS can read.
  • Only Ana changes it; the Board and Admins can delete, never edit.
  • Her pages there are documents and approved apps, never code she wrote: a page runs as whoever views it.
  • When she leaves, Spell offers it to her as a file or another space of hers.

Each person in an org gets a shared space of their own (everyone in the org reads it), made when first needed, which they lead, and which goes with them when they leave.

  1. Its address: ana@rs, the org's space for Ana, beside her personal ana@spell.
  2. Made when needed: on her first page, as P22's spaces are.
  3. Its rules: she's its manager; P23's rules say a person's own space takes documents and approved apps only.
  4. Leaving: its export offered, on the account page and in the leave command.
  5. Checked by: tests.
  6. Left out: personal spaces outside an org (a separate proposal, the guide says).
