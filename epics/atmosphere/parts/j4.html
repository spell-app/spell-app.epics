RoomCopy holds one room's rows in memory and works out each change in the room's order: it never reads one place and writes another. PlaceCopy (util's places) lists every thing of a collection in one place, whole, and applies them into another in one apply(): inserts for new ids, updates for ids already there, nothing removed. The owner field is left out, and an atmosphere id's did/ part dropped, so the place it goes into says who owns it.
