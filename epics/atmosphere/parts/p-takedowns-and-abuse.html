Spell would hold groups' data with no way to take something down, and nowhere to report it.

  • An abuse contact, and a takedown tool: Spell can hide or delete a record, a space or an account, and logs why.
  • A hidden record stops being read or written, and its program says it was taken down, not that it's lost.
  • Reports, reasons and appeals become a todo for before Spell opens to everyone.

An abuse address, and a takedown command with a log of why, before the first group is in.

  1. The abuse contact: an address in the server's settings, shown on the sign-in page and in every reply to a taken-down request.
  2. Takedowns: a command for Spell's admins that hides or deletes a record, a space or an account, with a reason; each one is written to a log beside the space, kept out of exports.
  3. What a program sees: a hidden record is refused with "taken down", which the page shows as such, not as lost data.
  4. A todo: reports, reasons and appeals, before Spell opens to everyone (epic add atmosphere#T).
  5. Checked by: tests for each of the three kinds, and the log's entry.
  6. Left out: a report button, signed abuse notices.
