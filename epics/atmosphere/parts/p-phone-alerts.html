Nothing reaches someone's phone when a message or a thread they follow changes.

  • Bo's phone buzzes when Ana messages him, and when the Events Team's thread he follows has something new.
  • He turns alerts off for one thread, or a whole space.
  • A phone alert is one more choice beside email and digests (P30).

The write server sends a phone alert when it writes something a person is waiting on; each person chooses per thread or space.

  1. Who's waiting: the write server knows each write; it asks P30's one look-up who follows that inbox, thread or space, not a second one.
  2. Sending: through Apple's and Google's alert services, from the cloud, by an alert sender beside the Mailer, with the same send() shape (and a stand-in, as the mail catcher is); a failed send retried by the ONE retry queue for the server's writes, mail and alerts: the save queue, kept on the server's disk (Q19 A).
  3. Choices: per thread and per space, in the same preferences as P30's (PrefStore, SpacePrefs).
  4. Needs you: Apple's alert key (from an Apple Developer account, $99 a year) and a Firebase project for Android (free); both need your sign-up.
  5. Checked by: tests with stand-in senders; one real alert to your phone.
  6. Left out: alerts from apps outside Spell.
