From P38, the Diaspora comparison. Diaspora shipped its export in release 0.5, and the part that puts an archive back only in 0.9.0.0 (June 2024), years after the design draft of February 2016. Export alone is half of leaving.

Where Spell is today:

  • SpaceExport writes one space at a time, and spell space export has no partner that brings an export back on a server. A program can read one, which is not the same as a server taking it in.
  • An org is many spaces, and an export of one leaves out the others.
  • The org roles file, roster.json5, is in no export: an export takes only list files.
  • spell space backup restore brings back one space, from the same server backups.

The work:

  • An org-wide export: every space, its members, roles, terms and files.
  • Bringing it back on another server, with addresses unchanged.
  • One test that does the round trip and compares the spaces, roles and member lists.
