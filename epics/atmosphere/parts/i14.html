Found setting up a family test org, @willzalone (Owen, 2026-11-08). spell space org create ends by pointing at the built-in Org Admin, but that program names @rs. A copy in the org's own space answers 404 on the cloud: SERVED_GROUPS in SpellCloud lets only @spell/examples and @spell/guides through.

Worked around for the test: the cloud runs with SPELL_BUILTINS_DIR=~/.spell/willzalone-builtins, a copy of the built-ins holding WillzaloneAdmin, Inbox, Feed and Chat.

Until this is fixed, a real org has no way to get its own Admin, Inbox, Feed or Chat pages on the cloud.
