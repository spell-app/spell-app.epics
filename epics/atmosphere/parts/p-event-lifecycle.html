RS can't run an event in Spell: nothing takes one from someone's idea to a finished, quiet record.

  • Anyone in RS proposes an event in the Events Team's shared space.
  • A Team Manager says yes, which creates the event's project, its two spaces, and names its organizers as Event Managers.
  • Organizers build its pages and sheets while it's Planning; it opens; after it, Analysis (did we break even?); then Quiet: read only, and off the feed.
  • The stages and the template are a Spell program RS can change; a 501c3 application can have its own.

A Spell group app modelled on RS: an event goes Proposed, Approved, Planning, Open, Analysis, Quiet, as a program RS can change, each new event copied from a template.

  1. The org: a test RS, with its Council (Board), Admins, three standing teams, and people, as files.
  2. The program: the stages as a Spell state of the event's project; moving between them through the write server, by whoever may.
  3. The template: a project copied to make a new event.
  4. Quiet: the spaces turned read only, and left off the feed.
  5. Checked by: headless runs.
  6. Left out: invitations and the letter (next phase), the feed (P33).
