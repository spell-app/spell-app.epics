Writes by role needs to know what each thing is. A space's records already live in collections, as a Space keeps them by type (space.spell.post), so the collection says it: thingKindOf().

  • posts, comments, messages: a post, someone's own words
  • __files: a file (a page, a sheet, a picture)
  • __apps: an installed app, { name, version }
  • anything else (signups, recipes): a row of a sheet

A program that names its collection posts gets post rules: only its writer changes one.

what each role in a space may write depends on the kind of thing, by its collection (thingKindOf(): posts, comments and messages a post, __files a file, __apps an app, any other a row); the kit's FolderPlace is handed a write check (FolderSpace.changeOrDie()) and asks it of each change a member makes, its kind, made, changed or removed, and who wrote it last, knowing nothing of roles; the app's SpaceMembers.changeOrDie() answers from the ONE role table, SPACE_ROLES, now with canCreate and each kind's rights (Q21 D): a viewer posts and comments where the space lets viewers (viewersPost, on by default) and changes only their own; an editor also adds rows and files and changes any existing one; a manager also makes pages and apps, removes anything, and never changes someone's post; a refusal says the role it takes (PlaceErrorCause.needs, passed on by PlaceServer and RemotePlace), which a page names in English and Spanish (write-refused-editor, -manager, -owner); an app installs only at a version the org's Admins approved (OrgRoles.approveApp(), approvedApps(), @rs/__admin/apps.json5, logged)
