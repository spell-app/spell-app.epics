A taken-down account can't sign in again (451 at ask and invite), and every space refuses its requests. But SignInCheck.check is synchronous, so a session made before the takedown still puts that person on each request: the prefs, people and themes routes still answer them as themselves. A code or link asked for before the takedown, and used after, signs in too. Fix: end that account's sessions at takedown, which needs the command to reach the running server's SignIns (or the server to re-read its sessions file).
