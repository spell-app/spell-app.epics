A taken-down account is refused new sign-ins and every space, but a sign-in it already has keeps working.

  • It can't sign in again (451 at ask and invite), and every space refuses its requests.
  • But SignInCheck.check is synchronous, so a session made before the takedown still puts that person on each request: the prefs, people and themes routes still answer them as themselves.
  • A code or link asked for before the takedown, and used after, signs in too.
  • Fix: end that account's sessions at takedown. That needs the command to reach the running server's SignIns (or the server to re-read its sessions file).
