A group's space is owned by the group's own id, and Spell holds that id's keys. Moving the group to another host is only possible if Spell hands those keys over.

  • Spaces' proposal (0016) lets a space be copied out and back in, records and its member list both.
  • It doesn't say how a group id whose keys a host holds can leave that host.
  • So Spell must promise the handover in plain words, and build it: who in the group may ask, and how the keys reach them.
  • Until then, a group can take its data, as plain files, but not its id.
