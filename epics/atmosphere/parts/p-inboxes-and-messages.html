Nobody in an org can send anyone a message, and Spell has nowhere to tell one person something private.

  • Ana messages Bo: a copy in each inbox, so deleting hers leaves his. A failed second copy is tried again.
  • Several people: one copy each, drawn as one thread.
  • A message can't be edited once sent.
  • A trusted page sent as a pointer: "You have used 2 of your invitations", worked out when Bo opens it, its links live.
  • A message that must never change points to one saved version of its page.
  • The inbox says plainly that the Board and Admins can read it.

Each person gets an inbox (they, the Board and the Admins read it): messages land in both people's inboxes, threads are drawn from a shared id, and a trusted page can be sent as a pointer worked out when it's opened.

  1. The inbox: one more space per person, made on demand; its list is the person, the Board and the Admins. Its word is "inbox" (Q23 A): PersonInbox in the code and the space __inbox, so it's never taken for the epic engine's review inbox.
  2. Messages: a record type with its text, a thread id, who sent it; written by Spell into each inbox, a failed copy retried by the ONE retry queue for the server's writes, mail and alerts: the save queue, kept on the server's disk (Q19 A).
  3. Pointers: a record naming a trusted page (one a Manager put in the org's spaces), a title, a line, and the private facts it needs.
  4. The inbox page: a built-in Spell program; threads, newest first.
  5. Checked by: tests, and a headless walk.
  6. Left out: email and alerts (next phase); mail from outside never comes in.
  • Where to see it: the built-in example Inbox (@spell/examples/Inbox): write to anyone in the org, then every conversation; it says plainly that the Board and the Admins can read your inbox. Its picture is in the examples page, and in the epic folder's tests/inbox.png.
  • An inbox: ana@rs/__inbox, Ana's in RS (Q23 A: PersonInbox in the code), read by her, the Board and the Admins; its list is worked out, never typed.
  • Sending: a page adds a message to its own inbox's messages, and Spell writes a copy into each inbox it's to, the same id in each; deleting yours leaves theirs. A copy an inbox didn't take goes again, through the one retry queue on the server's disk (Q19 A).
  • Threads: every copy carries the conversation it's in, the first message's id unless the page names one.
  • Never edited: a change to a message is refused; only its person removes a copy.
  • Pointers: a message naming a trusted page, its title, a line and the private facts it needs, worked out when it's opened; trusted means a page in the org's paired spaces, sent by that space's Manager. A fixed one names a saved version of its page, kept for good past any cap on versions.
  • One program for everyone: @rs/__inbox is whoever's asking's own inbox, never read direct.
  • Rough: the Inbox page lists conversations oldest first, with no Reply button (J138, T48); English only (J139).
  • Checked: 11 new tests (two copies, a thread of three, no edits, refusals, a pointer, a fixed version, a retry, the address for whoever asks); a browser test where Ana writes to Bo and Cy, and Bo's delete leaves theirs.
