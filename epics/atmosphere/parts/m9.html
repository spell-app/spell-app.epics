1. In Bluesky (bsky.app), signed in as your throwaway account: Settings, Privacy and security, App passwords, Add App Password; name it "spell publish" and copy it.
  2. In a terminal, in the atmosphere worktree (the epic on your PATH is main's, which has no publish until this merges), type, with your handle and that password: 
    cd /Users/owen/www/spell.worktrees/atmosphere
ATMOSPHERE_APP_PASSWORD=xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx node epic/bin/epic pages publish guides/atmosphere/spaces-and-spell.html --site https://spell.example --as yourthrowaway.bsky.social
  3. Paste the at:// address it prints into the box at https://pdsls.dev, and open it.

It prints "published guides/atmosphere/spaces-and-spell.html", an at:// address ending site.standard.document/guides.atmosphere.spaces-and-spell, "(new)", and the page address. pdsls.dev shows the record: the guide's title, its description, site https://spell.example, path /guides/atmosphere/spaces-and-spell.html, publishedAt now. Running step 2 again says "(updated)", and the record gains an updatedAt. The guide's head now has a link rel="site.standard.document" tag.
