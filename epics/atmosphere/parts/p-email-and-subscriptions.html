A message in someone's inbox waits there unseen: nothing tells them, and nobody can follow a team they're not on.

  • Bo picks a daily digest; the next morning one email lists his new messages, each with its title, a line and a link.
  • Ana picks one email per message.
  • Cy subscribes to the Events Team from its public home page, and hears what's new there.
  • Every email has a way to stop it.

Each person chooses how they hear about their inbox: one email per message, a daily digest, or nothing (a phone alert comes with the phone app); and anyone can subscribe to a team or project.

  1. Choices: kept in the person's own preferences, through the preferences store every page already uses (PrefStore, on the server SpacePrefs): per message, digest, nothing.
  2. Who follows what: one look-up, "who follows this inbox, thread or space?", written here and shared with P38's alerts.
  3. Sending: the write server knows when it writes into an inbox, so it sends then, or adds to the digest, through today's Mailer and its send(); a failed send retried by the ONE retry queue for the server's writes, mail and alerts: the save queue, kept on the server's disk (Q19 A).
  4. The digest: once a day, on the one daily round P24 moved onto every(), not a timer of its own.
  5. Subscribing: a button on a team's or project's home page in its shared space; what's new there reaches the person's inbox as an update (Q24 A; "notice" stays the app's on-screen message).
  6. Needs you: real mail goes out through P18 · Real Sign-In Mail's sender (Postmark, Q12 A), once you've done that phase's steps. Until then, everything goes to the mail catcher on this machine.
  7. Checked by: tests against the catcher.
  8. Left out: phone alerts (P38 · Phone Alerts).
