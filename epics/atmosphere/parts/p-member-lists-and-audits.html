When someone leaves a role, nothing takes them off the spaces it let them into, and nobody can check the lists are right.

  • A Team Manager whose term ends can't read the team's private space a moment later (on Spell's own server), or within 10 minutes (on a real Space, T32).
  • An audit, spell app spaces audit @rs, lists every space whose members differ from its roles, and says how.
  • The record of who held a role stays, when they go.
  • Someone leaving the org leaves every list; what they wrote stays the org's, but their own space (P28).

Joining, leaving, a role changing or a term ending updates every space's list at once, and an audit compares each list with what the roles say.

  1. Lists that follow roles: on Spell's own server they're worked out on every read already (P22), so a change counts at once.

  3. Terms: a role's end date is checked on every look-up; the cloud's daily round also updates real Spaces' lists when a term ends.
