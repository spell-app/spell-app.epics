When someone leaves a role, nothing takes them off the spaces it let them into, and nobody can check the lists are right.

  • A Team Lead whose term ends can't read the team's private space a moment later (on Spell's own server), or within 10 minutes (on a real Space, T32).
  • An audit, spell app spaces audit @rs, lists every space whose members differ from its roles, and says how.
  • The record of who held a role stays, when they go.
  • Someone leaving the org leaves every list; what they wrote stays the org's, but their own public space.

Joining, leaving, a role changing or a term ending updates every space's list at once, and an audit compares each list with what the roles say.

  1. Lists that follow roles: on Spell's own server they're worked out on every read already (P19), so a change counts at once.
  2. Real Spaces: for a space kept in a real Space (P14 · Write Server And Proxy), each change puts or removes the person on its list.
  3. Terms: a role's end date is checked on every look-up; the cloud's daily round also updates real Spaces' lists when a term ends.
    • One daily round, not a second timer: the nightly backups' own timer (SpaceBackups.nightly(), a bare interval today) moves onto every(), and the backups, these terms and P29's digest each join it.
  4. The audit: spell app org audit, a verb of the one org command (beside host, setup and recover), comparing each space's list with its roles, printing the differences, exiting 1 when there are any.
  5. Leaving the org: every list at once; their posts stay, with their name, until the org removes them.
  6. The commands page: the new verb added to it (commands.json5), then its check.
  7. Checked by: tests and the audit's own output; the backups' tests unchanged after the move to the shared round.
  8. Left out: what someone can save as they leave (done in P7 · Keeping Data Safe's export; their own space's export is P27).
