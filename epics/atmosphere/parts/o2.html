For Ana, who has never heard of a server:

  1. She writes the line, and adds a recipe. It stays in her browser, and the page says so.
  2. She signs in with an email link, and has ana@spell.
  3. Her recipe goes up through Spell's server, which writes it into her space as her space's own account, noting that she wrote it.
  4. She opens it on her phone, signs in, and it's there: the phone reads her space directly, with a credential for that space.
  5. She starts a family cookbook with Bo. It's a space of its own, so Bo sees all of the cookbook and none of her own recipe box.
  6. She hits a limit: the save is refused, says why, and nothing's lost.
  7. She leaves: she downloads a folder of plain JSON, and one changed line runs her program on it.

What keeps this from becoming the service you don't want to own:

  • It's shaped like a Space: a group's own id owns its space, each person's rows sit under them, and a member list, made from the space's membership records and their roles, says who may read and write. So moving to Bluesky's server later changes the host, not the data.
  • Writes go through Spell, as the group's own account, so Spell's rules always hold. Reads go straight to the space, so reading still works while Spell's server is down.
  • Anyone holding a space's credential sees the whole space, so there are no private projects inside one. Something private is its own space, its own project data folder.
  • It starts invite only, and export is in the first version.
  • A person is Spell's own id, with an email or an atmosphere account attached. A project data folder's address is Spell's own too.
  • Plain files can move to a Space later, or to anywhere else.

The cost, said plainly: Spell's server holds the groups' data, and Spell is answerable for it. You chose this on 2026-11-04, as making people host their own is too complicated. What that server looks like is Q1.

  • Answerable, at first, means a takedown path and an abuse contact. A report button and appeals come before Spell opens to everyone (Q7).
  • Spell's server must be up for anyone to write, and it holds the right to write for every group on it.
