    spell space cloud invite you+ana@example.com
  2. On your laptop, in Chrome, open https://spell.example.org/apps/@spell/examples/FamilyCookbook (your address). Add a recipe while signed out.
  3. Reload the page.
  4. In the blue bar, type that email and press Email me a link. On the cloud machine, read the code (until Q12 picks a mail service): 
    spell space cloud mail you+ana@example.com
    Type the code in the bar, and press Sign in.
  5. On your phone, on cellular, open the same address, sign in the same way (a new code), and change the recipe's name.
  6. Back on the laptop, wait 5 seconds.
  • Signed out, the bar says the cookbook is kept in this browser only, and the recipe stays after the reload.
  • Signed in, the bar turns green, and the recipe is on the phone.
  • The phone's change shows on the laptop within about 5 seconds.
