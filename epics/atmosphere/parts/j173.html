Chose to check read rights on an org's program and its files, over serving a shared space's programs to anyone with the address. Someone who can't read the space (nobody signed in, or an account the org hasn't let in) gets a small sign-in page at the same address, in English, by email only.

Every name in the shared space answers a stranger the same, so nobody can find out what programs an org has; that the org itself exists does show.

The source of an org's pages is seen only by the people the org let in; Bluesky sign-in isn't offered on that page.
