What a Lexicon can say that a Spell type can't, both ways.

  • Left out of a Spell type read in, and said in its comment:
    • a string's length in bytes (maxLength)
    • blobs
    • references to other records (ref, at-uri)
    • objects of their own
    • unions
  • Written out:
    • a thing held by a property is unknown
    • a pattern or must be an email has no Lexicon of its own
