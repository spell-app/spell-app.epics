• A term (someone holding a role): named or ended by whoever leads what that role leads, or anything above it. So a Project Lead names and removes Project Leads of their project, never a Team Lead, an Admin or the Board.
  • A Board term: only the Board. An Admins term: the Board and the Admins (the guide's Admins name other Admins).
  • Teams and the org's roles (names, eligibility, its own roles): the Board and the Admins.
  • Projects: the Board and the Admins anywhere; a team's Leads under their team (the guide's Team Leads create projects under their team).
  • A role of the org's own leads nothing by default, a title (Treasurer); the four built-in ones never change what they lead.
