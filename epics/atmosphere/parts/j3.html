One word rule, each_persons_own, says "each person's own folder", in both languages:

  • English: family cookbook is a data folder at personal "recipes", your words.
  • Spanish: recetario es una carpeta de datos personal en "recetas", where Spanish puts the adjective.
  • personal with anything but a data folder isn't read as one: so Spanish atmosphere personal still means the personal atmosphere account.
  • personal before a full address is refused, saying to write a name alone.
