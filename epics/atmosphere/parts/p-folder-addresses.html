A full address, such as owen@spell/recipes, names a real folder in a space; personal gives each person their own; a folder's owner is a membership.

  1. The address: a data folder at a space's address (owen@spell/recipes) becomes the page server's places route for that space's folder.
    • It's recognised by the one test Spell already has for addresses (SpellLocation.isAddress()), not a new one.
    • Today it falls through to a plain web folder.
  2. The server side: no new server: PlaceServer serving a FolderPlace on the app's space storage, mounted in the app's route module the way the Airtable routes are.
  3. Shared or personal: one new word in the place rules, in English and Spanish.
    • "recipes" alone is the program's own folder, under its project's __data/.
    • personal "recipes" is under each person's space.
    • The word sits beside the existing personal atmosphere account.
  4. Who is owen@spell: until P3's sign-in, this machine's person, and the side bar says "same on every machine until you sign in".
  5. Slowest polling: a place states its minimum in what it can do, beside its rate limit; check it every below it is raised to it, with a note at the line.
  6. Versions from content: CSV's row hash (csvRowVersion()) moves into one shared helper, used by FolderPlace too.
  7. The owner: each row's owner in the field the atmosphere account already uses (owner), refused to anyone else with the existing "only the owner". Who that owner is follows Q9: the person or org whose space it is; a folder MAY have its own __members, read when it's there, and without one its people are the org's members, through the people store's rolesOf().
  8. Moving the recipe box: a copy from a project data folder to an atmosphere account and back, using the rooms' copy (RoomCopy) if it fits, else a small one, recorded why.
  9. Checked by: tests of each step (parser, the kit's places tests, the app's route tests); the recipe box headless, saving to owen@spell/recipes, reloaded, and moved both ways.
  10. Left out: sign-in, hosting, sharing with named people.
