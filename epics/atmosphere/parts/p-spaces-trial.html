What T31's three posts change (Oct 14, 22, 29):

  • Still no beta: "breaking changes ... on a weekly basis", "just about finished with the major churn", launch "this year".
  • A GROUP is "a DID with a bunch of permissioned spaces under it": a meta space (profile, rules), a members space (the list, roles, an index of the rest), and one space per app. Spell's "one space per org" (Q5) becomes several under the org's DID, once it moves.
  • A GROUP HOST holds the group's id and runs roles (flat role-based access: roles, each space's access record naming the roles that read it), and is the OAuth server for the group's DID, so admins sign in as themselves. So Spell's admins and its canManage, which a member space has no flag for, have a home there, not in putMember.
  • Membership both ways: the group writes a membership, the member an acceptance. Spell's invites (T6) can take that shape.
  • In those groups each member writes their OWN repo, not the group's: the opposite of Q6's "writes through Spell, as the group". Two edits to one recipe are two records again: T2 and T7 come back if Spell ever writes as each person.
  • Our shape still matches: a group's own id owning its spaces (Q1), per-person repos, a read and a write flag (memberspace), and the writer set for building a view.
