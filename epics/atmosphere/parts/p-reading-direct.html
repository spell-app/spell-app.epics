Every read would go through Spell's server, so reading stops whenever it does.

  • A program reads a space directly, with a credential for the whole space, not through Spell's server: Ana's phone opens her recipes even while Spell's server can't write.
  • Spell's server hands out that credential to each member who may read, and takes it back when they leave.
  • Something private is its own space, its own project data folder: Bo sees all of the family cookbook and none of Ana's own recipe box.
  • The page keeps the credential where other pages can't reach it.

Members get a credential for the whole space, and programs read it with that, straight from where it's kept.
