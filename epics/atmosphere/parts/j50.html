A Space's type is an NSID that should resolve to a space type declaration (its name on consent screens, its collections).

  • The trial makes spaces of app.spell.data.space (SPELL_SPACE_TYPE), under Spell's corner, with no declaration published.
  • If the alpha refuses it, SPACES_TRIAL_TYPE names another, and the log says what it said.
  • A declaration can go out through spell app lexicon publish later, beside the record Lexicons.
