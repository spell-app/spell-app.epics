Owen, 2026-11-01: "eventual phase: build the statusphere app in spell", from Bluesky's example app.

Its README lists what it covers, and each maps to something Spell needs:

  • Sign-in through OAuth: the sign-in atmosphere accounts already have.
  • Fetching profiles.
  • Listening to the network for new data: their app does this through a server tool, Tap; Spell's atmosphere account has its own live stream.
  • Publishing to an account under a custom schema, xyz.statusphere.status: the real write that has never been tried (M3 of data), and a test of reading someone else's schema.

It holds public records only, so it needs no Spaces. Becomes a phase when the phases are written, after the Overview is accepted.
