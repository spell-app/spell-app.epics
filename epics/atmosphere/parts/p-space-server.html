There is no server that holds a group's space, or knows who in a group may write.

  • Spell's own server, shaped like an AT Protocol Space: a group's own id owns its space, its rows sit under it, and a member list says who may read and write.
  • Every write goes through Spell's server, which writes the record as the group's own account, with a "written by" field naming the person. So a recipe Ana and Bo both edit is one record.
  • The member list is made from the membership records and roles in the people library, under __members, and handed to the kit's server by the app: each role gives a read and a write flag.
  • Joining, leaving and admins follow the shape of AT Protocol's lists (T6): a membership added, removed, or given the admin role.
  • A refused write says why, and reaches the page, as "only the owner" already does.

Spell's own server, shaped like a Space, writes every record as the group's account, and checks each write against the group's memberships.

  1. The server: still PlaceServer and FolderPlace from P1, on the app's space storage: no new server or route module. A space is its person's or org's folder, as today, so moving to a real Space later changes the host, not the data.
  2. An org's id: as Q10 decides (recommended: Spell's own org id, @family, like owen@spell).
  3. Writes as the org: each record is written by the space's own id, with a field naming the person who wrote it, called by (the word list's word for it in data). Kept out of what a program sees as its own fields.
  4. Who may write: "who writes" gets a second value, "its members", beside "only the owner"; and a refusal "your role can't write", so the page says why.
  5. Members: the app hands the kit's server each space's members and their roles; as Q9 decides, recommended: the org's own memberships, read by the people store's rolesOf() as they are today. Guest reads, member writes, admin also manages the list.
  6. Join, leave, admins: in the shape of AT Protocol's lists (T6): a membership added, removed, or given the admin role, written through the people store.
  7. Two edits at once: the existing "out of date" refusal and per-field merge settle them; nothing new.
  8. Starting it: a command for Spell's server (its name checked against the commands guide, and the commands page kept true).
  9. Checked by: tests over HTTP (a member writes, a guest is refused, an admin adds and removes, every record says by whom); the recipe box headless as Ana and Bo.
  10. Left out: reading direct (P5), finer rules than one list per space.
