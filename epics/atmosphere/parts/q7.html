Once Spell hosts people's accounts and spaces (Q1), Spell is the one answerable for what's in them, private or not.

  • What a host must do: take things down. Child abuse material, nude images shared without consent, copyright notices and doxxing (publishing someone's private details) are not optional.
  • It needs an abuse contact: an address people and other services can report to.
  • Ideally, it handles signed abuse notices by itself, as proposal 0012 suggests.
  • Reports and appeals: the moderation proposals (0009, 0007, 0005) describe reasons, routing, history and appeals.
  • The gap: every one of them is about public records. None covers private Spaces data, so this is ours.
  • An abuse address, and a way for Spell to hide or delete a record, a space or an account, with a log of why.
  • Enough for an invite-only start, where the groups are known.
  • Its cost: no report button or appeal yet; those come before opening to everyone.
  • A report button in programs, reasons, a queue, and appeals, as the proposals describe.
  • Its cost: a large piece of work before the first group is in, for a handful of invited groups.
  • The server phase adds an abuse contact and a takedown tool.
  • Reports and appeals become a todo for before Spell opens to everyone.
