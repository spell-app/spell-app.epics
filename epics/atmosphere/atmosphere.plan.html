Decides what family cookbook is saved in the cloud means when the goal is that people hold their own data, after a close look at AT Protocol Spaces. The proposal: a data folder on a small server anyone can run, so a first program needs no setup, with Spaces as a later place to keep it, once it's ready.

From epic `data`, on 2026-10-31. Owen, on its chunking review:

"This and 'the cloud' should move together into a new epic, `atmosphere`. I'm thinking that the atmosphere spaces are a very close analogy and should be thoroughly investigated before we make our own parallel-but-not-quite-the-same service which we need to own.

Goal is not that spell is holding people's data, but that people can hold their own data somewhere, so that's very compatible with atmosphere's goals. If we don't have to deal with hosting content that is fantastic. If we end up selling space as an atmosphere server, also great."

First: investigate AT Protocol's Spaces thoroughly. It's in alpha, from its August 2026 post, holding non-public data with access control: https://atproto.com/blog/atproto-spaces-alpha

Then decide what `family cookbook is saved in the cloud` is, given that goal.

Owen's answers on `epics/data/details/cloud-design.html` were for a server Spell runs, and hold unless the investigation changes them. Of its recommendation: "That is the goal".
