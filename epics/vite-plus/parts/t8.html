The Oxc extension used to read .oxfmtrc.json. Now it must find the format block of the root vite.config.ts, and the editor can't be checked unattended.

  • how you'd notice it's wrong: saving adds semicolons
  • the fix: update the Oxc extension, or point its config path at the root config (oxc.fmt.configPath: ./vite.config.ts)
