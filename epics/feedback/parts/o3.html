A woken session takes what was sent into phases, then answers every comment.

  1. epic comments gather: ONE PHASE PER PAGE, titled for the page, its comments the goal, each linked back to its block.
    • A page whose phase is still open gets an Updated block in that phase instead.
    • Each comment is marked taken: its thread says "Taken into feedback · <the page>".
  2. Each comment, one at a time (epic comments working <page>#M3 on), goes ONE of three ways:
    • Worked into the page: a content change (a guide's words, a table, an example), in the content folder. No code commit.
    • Sent on, when it asks to change how something WORKS (an element, the epic tool, a skill, a server) or belongs to an epic's running work: /send <epic> with Owen's words, and the answer names the request (epic-engine r7). An idea with no home: a future epic (/epic future).
    • Declined: the answer says why.
    Then epic comments answer <page>#M3 --file answer.html: the answer on his thread, his turn.
  3. A phase is DONE when every comment in it has its answer on its thread, whichever way it went: epic phase feedback#P3 done --done "<p>2 worked in, 1 sent to epic-engine (r7)</p>".
    • Not "when Owen says that's good": a standing epic's phases close on their own.
    • Nothing to merge: content is committed on its own; a request is the other epic's to build.
    • His reply on a thread after that is new work: the next gather opens a new phase for the page (or updates its open one).
  4. A question only Owen can answer: epic add feedback#Q, and the comment's answer points to it.
