While a <ui-root> loads, it shows grey placeholder shapes ("skeletons") for what's coming. They're close, not exact, so the page shifts a little when the real content appears.

  • The sizes (in em) were chosen by eye, from Fomantic's defaults.
  • The placeholders stack in the root's own shadow DOM, in page order (the skeleton decision, D61). So a grid of cards shows as a single column until ready.
  • Inline skeletons (buttons, labels, inputs) do sit side by side.
