D30 · Layout: dropdown menu popover is position: fixed

The .menu[popover] was position: absolute; webkit measured its position-area against the origin screenful of the page, so any dropdown below the first viewport height opened upwards despite room. fixed (the popover default) makes chromium, firefox and webkit agree (probe: dropdown at y=1100, scrolled into view, opens down in all three). A dropdown opened while its anchor is OFF-screen opens upwards in all three (viewport has no room below).
