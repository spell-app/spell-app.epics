D17 · Behaviour: toast pauses on a real pointer move, not pointerenter

A toast appearing under a resting pointer got a synthetic pointerenter and never left. UIToast now notes pointerenter and pauses only on a pointermove with nonzero movement. Fomantic pauses on mouseenter; this deviates on purpose. The CI pause-on-hover=false workaround was left in the toast life tests (Linux not verifiable here).
