• <ui-root> loads each family the first time its tag shows up, from a generated catalog, in an entry of its own.
  • It waits until everything inside is ready, or its timeout passes, then shows it and fires ui-ready; meanwhile a loading message or skeletons.
  • Theme, size, and an optional box: width / height and a fixed flag.
  • Its loading look is swappable: UIRoot.Loading and UIRoot.Skeleton.
