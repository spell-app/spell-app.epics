D26 · Decision: label colour matches Fomantic

Fomantic: a plain .ui.label inside .ui.red.segment (or any coloured owner) is NOT coloured; only its own colour, or its .ui.red.labels group's, paints it. Before: an un-coloured <ui-label> inherited --ui-color from any coloured ancestor. Now: :host in ui-label.css drops the inherited --ui-color* tokens and takes back its group's, handed down as --_labels-color* by the .ui.labels root (statistic's pattern). Class-grammar (light DOM) labels have no host, so a plain .ui.label there still inherits a coloured ancestor's colour. Kept open for Owen (T5).
