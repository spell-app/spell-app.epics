Add Details Now on an item that already has a More Details card: what happens to the first card.

  • chose: the new card replaces it, and the old card's text moves into the item's folded Original Discussion
  • over: stacking a second card under the first
  • why: the item stays short (Original Reply, then ONE card), and nothing is lost, as with every rewrite

Net effect:

  • an item never shows more than one More Details card
  • earlier ones are under Original Discussion, dated
