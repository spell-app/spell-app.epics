Two small limits of the fold button and sticky line (Q6) are left. Both come from how the browser lays the page out, not from bugs.

  • folding an item near the page's end can't keep its line in place once the page gets shorter than the window: the browser clamps the scroll. Example: I2, the last issue, at 700x500, drops about 30px
  • the review notice at the window's bottom (shown for 6 seconds, full width at 280px) can sit over the fold button while it shows

Fixed in P3 (Owen asked, 2026-10-06): the line and the fold button now stay in view down to the note box's end. The box is docked INSIDE the item's details, before the fold button, and the runtime keeps that same box when the item's body reloads from its part file, so typed text, focus and caret survive (check-review checks it).

Net effect:

  • reading or writing in an item's note box, you still see which item it is, and can fold it from there
  • the two limits above stay: both are rare and cosmetic
