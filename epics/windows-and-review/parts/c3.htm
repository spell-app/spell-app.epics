Two limits of Q6, both from how the page is built rather than from bugs.

  • the line sticks only while the item's details are on screen. It's sticky inside the accordion's <details>, and the note box is docked AFTER the accordion, so the line scrolls away as you reach the box. Keeping it would mean moving the box into the details
  • folding an item near the page's end can't keep its line in place when the page gets shorter than the window: the browser clamps the scroll. Example: I2, the last issue, at 700x500 drops about 30px
  • the review notice at the window's bottom (6 seconds, full width at 280px) can sit over the fold button while it shows

Net effect:

  • in an item's note box you see the section's title, not the item's
