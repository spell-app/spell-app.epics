Owen's asks from 2026-10-06, as built: what Owen sees change, and where it lives.

Replies you can't lose (first: replies are getting lost)

  • A note you type on the review page (Revisit, Make Todo) is saved to the epic's review inbox, as an unsent draft.
    • when: 10 seconds after you stop typing, or at once when you leave the box
    • a small floppy icon says it's saved
    • a reload, from either page server, brings it back
    • why notes got lost before: 1.2
  • An item you wrote in shows a speech-bubble mark on its line.
    • outlined while it's a draft, filled once sent
    • your words on hover

One button per action

  • Each item's single ⋯ button and its menu became separate buttons:
    • a group of three state buttons: Approve, Make Todo, Revisit
    • then Add Details Now, a round button of its own: an action, not a state
    • grey outlines until chosen
    • once chosen, filled in the Spell UI theme's green (decided) or orange (pending)
    • each one's name is its plain tooltip
  • Clicking the chosen one again clears it: no action.
  • Add Details Now and Revisit Now turn into a spinner while Claude works on them.
  • An opened item ends in its note box, its buttons stacked at its right (Q10):
    • Make Todo, green
    • Later, the comment dots, orange: talk it over in the next batch
    • Do Now, the wand, blue: Claude at once
    • every note box grows as you type
  • The four colours are chosen again, from live buttons (Q4).
  • Nevermind: clicking a running Add Details Now or Revisit Now again calls it off (Q5).

Plan docs that scan

  • The "Plan hung?" notice starts folded.
  • Add Details keeps the item's text on top, titled "Original Reply"; the new text goes under it in a white "More Details" card, open and foldable.
  • Phases read Symptom (one line), Changes (two or three lines), then the details.
  • An update to the plan is a fenced, dated line right under Symptom / Changes, not a word buried in the text.
    • a "Plan changes" box at the top of the phases lists those of phases still to do
  • An open item's line sticks under its section's title while you read it, and a round fold button floats at its lower right, down to the end of its note box (Q6).

Review Now, and where answers go

  • Review Now (a wand) sits beside Send (the paper plane) (Q2, J7).
    • Send queues your marks
    • Review Now sends them, and turns every revisit waiting into a request for now, answered in parallel
  • Every answer lands in its item on the page; the chat only links them (Q3).

Windows and sessions

  • Windows take a colour you choose, in Tomorrow Night Blue's look (Q1):
    • /epic new-thing -purple opens the epic's window in a whole purple colour scheme
    • /epic color teal recolours the window you're in
    • no colour: the current window's
  • A session that STAYS in its window (/epic x, /isolate x) retitles and tints that window at once.
    • the title: ⎇ x
    • put back at /isolate done
    • before, the window kept its old title: it said docs-sidebar (Workspace) while this session was in quick-open
  • A finished session's name starts with ✅ (✅ quick-open) after /isolate done merges.
    • from the next message to it on: the panel takes a title only from a hook (I4)
    • reopening it takes the ✅ off: /epic <name> again, /unpark, or /isolate <name>
