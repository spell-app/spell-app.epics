The fold button needs a place where, stuck at the window's bottom, it covers nothing. I gave open items' details a narrow right gutter and put the button on its own line at their end, rather than letting it float over the text or sit beside the last line.

  • over the text (no gutter): it would sit on whatever line is at the window's bottom while you read
  • beside the last line (a negative top margin, so the item ends where it did): tried it; sticky lets the button rise as far as its margin, so while an item's details first enter the window it rode up onto the item's line, over Add Details Now
  • the button can't leave the details, so it never reaches the note box docked after them (Make Todo / Do Now / Later at its right)
  • it hangs 8px past the item's right edge, into the section's 16px inset: the text gives up 22px, not 30
  • it sits UNDER the item's stuck line, so as the details leave the window it slides beneath the line, and the line's review buttons stay clickable

Net effect:

  • an open item's text is 22px narrower, and its details end in a 24px line holding the button
  • swept every 7px through an item with its note box at 280 / 700 / 1300px, light and dark: the button was never drawn over the text or any button
