What separates Review Now from Send (Q2 B: "Send queues, as today; Review Now sends and has the listening session work through the batch at once").

  • chose: both send every mark. Review Now also turns each revisit still waiting (unsent, or sent and still to talk over) into a request for now, as Do Now does: a background agent per item answers it into the item while its Revisit button spins
  • Send: approvals, picks and todos applied at once, as before; revisits answered one at a time by the session, into the items too (Q3), the chat only linking them
  • over: Review Now making the session skip its turn-by-turn talk some other way: the request-for-now path already has the spinner, "nevermind" and the late-write refusal, so nothing new to build or learn
  • the wand beside the paper plane: everything you marked, answered in parallel, in the items
  • a mark carrying a pick ("B, but ...") keeps it: the agent answers about B
