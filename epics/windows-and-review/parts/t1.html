On this plan doc, in the side bar's Review tab:

  • open an item, type a few words in the note box at its end: after 10 seconds (or at once when you leave the box) a small floppy icon says it's saved
  • reload the side bar: the box has your words again; the item's line shows an outline speech bubble
  • press Later (comment dots): the box empties and the note shows under the line, You · revisit soon · not sent yet, with Edit
  • Edit puts your words back in the box
