Ten asks from 2026-10-06, in the order they'll be built: what Owen sees change, and where it lives.

Replies you can't lose (first: replies are getting lost)

  • A note you type on the review page (Revisit, Make Todo) is saved to the epic's review inbox a moment after you stop typing, as an unsent draft; a small "Saved" shows by the box. A reload, from either page server, brings it back. See 1.2 for why notes get lost today.
  • An item you wrote in shows it on its line: a speech-bubble mark, outlined while it's a draft, filled once sent; your words on hover.

One button per action

  • Each item's single ⋯ button and its menu become four icon buttons: Approve, Make Todo, Add Details Now, Revisit Now (today: "Add to todo", "Add Details", "Revisit"). Grey outlines until chosen, coloured once chosen; the label is the tooltip.
  • Clicking the chosen one again clears it: no action.
  • Add Details Now and Revisit Now turn into a spinner while Claude works on them.
  • Make Todo opens a note box, as Revisit does; every note box grows as you type.
  • The four colours are chosen again (Q4).

Review Now, and where answers go

  • A "Review Now" button in the page header, where the paper plane (Send) is; the two take turns (Q2).
  • Answers to what you sent stop landing somewhere that doesn't scale (Q3).

Windows and sessions

  • /epic new-thing -purple opens the epic's window with a purple title bar; /epic color teal recolours the window you're in (Q1: which colours).
  • A session that STAYS in its window (/epic x, /isolate x) retitles and tints that window as ⎇ x on the spot, and puts it back at /isolate done. Today the window keeps its old title (it said docs-sidebar (Workspace) while this session was in quick-open).
  • A finished session's name starts with ✅ (✅ quick-open) after /isolate done or an epic's merge; reopening it (/epic <name> again, /unpark, /isolate <name>) takes the ✅ off.
