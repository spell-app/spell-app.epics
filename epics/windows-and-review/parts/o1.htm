Owen's asks from 2026-10-06, as built: what Owen sees change, and where it lives.

Replies you can't lose (first: replies are getting lost)

  • A note you type on the review page (Revisit, Make Todo) is saved to the epic's review inbox a moment after you stop typing (10 seconds, or at once on leaving the box), as an unsent draft; a small floppy icon says it's saved. A reload, from either page server, brings it back. See 1.2 for why notes get lost today.
  • An item you wrote in shows it on its line: a speech-bubble mark, outlined while it's a draft, filled once sent; your words on hover.

One button per action

  • Each item's single ⋯ button and its menu became a group of three state buttons, Approve, Make Todo, Revisit, then Add Details Now, a round button of its own (an action, not a state). Grey outlines until chosen, filled in the Spell UI theme's green (decided) or orange (pending) once chosen; the name is the plain tooltip.
  • Clicking the chosen one again clears it: no action.
  • Add Details Now and Revisit Now turn into a spinner while Claude works on them.
  • An opened item ends in its note box, its buttons stacked at its right: Make Todo (green), Later (comment dots, orange: talk it over in the next batch), Do Now (the wand, blue: Claude at once) (Q10). Every note box grows as you type.
  • The four colours are chosen again, from live buttons (Q4).
  • Nevermind: clicking a running Add Details Now or Revisit Now again calls it off (Q5).

Plan docs that scan

  • The "Plan hung?" notice starts folded.
  • Add Details keeps the item's text on top, titled "Original Reply"; the new text goes under it in a white "More Details" card, open and foldable.
  • Phases read Symptom (one line), Changes (two or three lines), then the details.
  • An update to the plan is a fenced, dated line right under Symptom / Changes, not a word buried in the text; a "Plan changes" box at the top of the phases lists those of phases still to do.
  • An open item's line sticks under its section's title while you read it, and a round fold button floats at its lower right, down to the end of its note box (Q6).

Review Now, and where answers go

  • Review Now (a wand) sits beside Send (the paper plane): Send queues your marks, Review Now sends them and turns every revisit waiting into a request for now, answered in parallel (Q2, J7).
  • Every answer lands in its item on the page; the chat only links them (Q3).

Windows and sessions

  • /epic new-thing -purple opens the epic's window in a whole purple colour scheme, in Tomorrow Night Blue's look; /epic color teal recolours the window you're in; no colour: the current window's (Q1).
  • A session that STAYS in its window (/epic x, /isolate x) retitles and tints that window as ⎇ x on the spot, and puts it back at /isolate done. Today the window keeps its old title (it said docs-sidebar (Workspace) while this session was in quick-open).
  • A finished session's name starts with ✅ (✅ quick-open) after /isolate done merges, from the next message to it on (the panel takes a title only from a hook: I4); reopening it (/epic <name> again, /unpark, /isolate <name>) takes the ✅ off.
