Which colour names /epic <name> -<color> and /epic color <color> accept. The colour is the title bar's; the theme stays the window's own.

  • Today a worktree's window gets a hue made from its name (tint() in scripts/window.mjs): every window different, none chosen.
  • The title bar needs a DARK shade, so its white text reads: "purple" means a dark purple there, not #800080.

Net effect:

  • /epic new-thing -purple opens a window with a dark purple title bar
  • /epic color teal recolours the window you're in, at once
  • 12 names, each a dark title-bar shade: red, orange, amber, green, teal, cyan, blue, indigo, purple, pink, brown, grey
  • an unknown name lists the 12
  • no colour given: the name's hue, as today

Why: short to type, always readable, the same purple every time.

  • the 12 names, plus any hex (-#5b2a86)
  • a light colour makes white title text hard to read
