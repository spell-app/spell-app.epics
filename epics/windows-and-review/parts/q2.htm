What "Review Now" in the page header does, and how it takes turns with Send (the paper plane).

  • Today the paper plane sends your marks to the Claude session reviewing the doc (/epic review <name>). With no session listening, its tooltip says so, and nothing happens with what you send until one starts.
A: start a review (recommended)
  • nobody reviewing: the header shows "Review Now"; clicking it opens a new Claude tab in this window with /epic review <name> typed in, for you to press enter
  • a session listening: the button becomes Send, as today
 B: send and act now
  • both buttons always there: Send queues your marks for the next batch; Review Now sends them AND asks the listening session to work through them at once
  • needs a session already listening

Net effect:

  • you can start reviewing an epic from its page, without typing a command
