The app's dialogs were rebuilt on <ui-modal> (P6f, part of P6), and behave a little differently from the React ones. You'd notice it when clicking outside a dialog, or in which button a confirm focuses first.

  • Text only: message, header, ok and cancel are strings.
    • no ReactNode, no SUI.ButtonProps
    • extra props no longer reach a modal
  • A click on the dimmed background no longer cancels (closedby=closerequest); Escape still does.
  • confirm() focuses Cancel first (ui's choice), not OK.
  • prompt() resolves undefined when the field is empty, as its doc said. React resolved "" after clearing.
  • promptForNumber() resolves the string, as its signature says. React resolved a number once one was typed (logged in agents/SUSPECTED-BUGS.md).
  • choose() is a list of <ui-radio> or <ui-checkbox>, not a searchable dropdown.
    • inputProps and allowAdditions are gone: unused, and ignored before
    • with several allowed, OK with nothing chosen resolves []
