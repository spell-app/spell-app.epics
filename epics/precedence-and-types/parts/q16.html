What it is. After P3, an expression is ONE of these, then any operators after it. This thing needs a name: it's the piece operators act on, and the slot a list prefix takes so it can't swallow an operator.

5, "hi", the deck x + 1

the cards in the deck where its color is red

So it is NOT "simple" and NOT "single" in the sense of "can't contain other expressions": it nests freely. What it lacks is an operator at its own top level.
