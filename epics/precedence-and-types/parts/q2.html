Today every bare word in a signature is a keyword; only parens make a parameter:

to give a card to a pile     =>  export function give_a_card_to_a_pile() {}
to give (a card) to (a pile) =>  Card.give_to_$pile(pile)

Proposed rule:

  • a / an + a KNOWN type = typed parameter ("any one of these"): to give a card to a pile ~== to give (a card) to (a pile)
  • not a type = words: to make a mess stays make_a_mess()
  • the + anything = words ("the one we all know"): to reset the stock pile
  • parens still work, and are needed for untyped (message), defaults, (with …)

Visible change: to create a card becomes Card.create(), not create_a_card() (one test pins the old reading; it flips). Plurals (to shuffle some cards) later.
