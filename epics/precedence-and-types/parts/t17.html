Owen, 2026-10-05: "not happy with the ExclusiveList thing -- too many changes". Say it from the ITEM's side, in English, and let a list guard what it takes. Supersedes the wording of P8 (a pile is an exclusive list of cards); goes with T12 (rethink exclusive-list semantics).

Owen's sketch

  • one at a time: a card can be (in|a member of) one pile at a time
  • the opposite, today's default: a card can be (in|a member of) many piles
  • a guard on the list: a tableau can add a card if: ..., overriding Tableau's add()
  • open: how the item's rule and the list's guard coordinate

How it's expressed today (P8)

  • on the LIST type: a pile is an exclusive list of cards -- TypeScope.exclusive, family = Pile and its subtypes; Card gets a read-only pile
  • runtime: one owner map per family; every write to a list's items (List.writeItems()) moves the item out of its old owner first
  • guards: none built in. Solitaire writes its own aliases -- a tableau "can play a card" if: ..., a stock-pile "can pick up a card" if: ... -- and its to play a card checks them by hand before move it to end-pile
