Binding power

  • Two numbers, two jobs (D26).
    • An operator's precedence, from the Precedence table, on suffix rules only (the values below).
    • A rule's priority (it was precedence) only breaks a tie between choices: highest, then longest, then earliest (D15). 
    *                     14
+                     13
comparisons           11
is                    10
and                    6
or                     5
X if C otherwise Y     4
  • compound_expression is THE expression rule.
    • an operand, then each suffix binding tighter than its bound
    • parsed once per level, not twice
    • a postfix pops like an infix: x + y is empty becomes isEmpty(x + y)
  • operand (D29): an expression with no operator at its top.
    • a prefix's last slot is {list:operand}, and so is there is a's (D23)
    • absolute value and round take the sum, {x:arithmetic_expression} (D3)
    • statements, and slots closed by a word, keep {expression}
  • Give-back in Sequence, the one change in the parser. A required word that the slot before swallowed re-parses that slot shorter: remove the card of the pile.
  • Calls and wait for (D24, D27, D33):
    • whole arguments as statements
    • inside an expression, their LAST slot is an operand: if double x is 4 becomes double_$n(x) == 4
    • one flag, operandInExpressions: a statement rule plus an operand twin, derived at registration (J17, J18)
  • The model: Parser Model §4.5. The mechanics: spell's PARSING.md, "Expressions".

A datatype on every match

  • match.datatype is rule.getDatatype(match), in spell's words (D16).
    • the type is a P.Datatype: text, choice, list of cards, Card ...
    • ONE normaliser for what a user writes, SP.typeName() (J13)
    • unknown fits everything
  • Lookups stay in parse(), into match.data. About 20 rules override getDatatype().
  • TypeScope: itemType, and the methods moved from the LSP.
    • the methods: chain(), isA(), getMember()
    • methods get P.ScopeMethod records, in the project's own methods when the type is built in or imported (J11)
  • Return types are inferred once a body parses (mutateScopeFromBody(), J20). A getter's type is its property's, so getters change scope (D9).
  • is a takes a KNOWN type (D32), declared anywhere in the project. A pre-scan of the lines stubs them first (J19).
  • Declarations gain params, returns and itemType, with no bump of the spell version (SPELL_VERSION; D37, J14).

Signatures, calls, members

  • Paren-free typed parameters (D2): 
    to give a card to a pile      ~==  to give (a card) to (a pile)
to create a card              =>  Card.create()
  • A call rejects an argument only when its type is known, and UNRELATED to the parameter's (D6, J16). So put the chip on the pot picks Chip's.
  • Members: member_words, 1..N words up to a stop word, skipping the blacklist (J25).
    • the X of Y is ONE rule: resolved through Y's type, else one loose word (D5, J23), at priority 1 (J24)
    • class_member replaces EnumerationRule
    • a new rule, list_count (J26)
  • Auto-declare at the first set the X of Y (D10):
    • on types the project declares only (J28)
    • at the file's top, or after the type's class (J29)
  • Built-in member tables, SP.BUILT_IN_TYPE_TABLE (D34): thing, list, app, text, date.
    • a member compiles per type (J35; examples below)
    • the Type Explorer is built from it (J34)
    • declaring or setting a built-in member is refused, with a reason (J33)
    the length of the name     =>  name.length
... of the deck            =>  spellCore.itemCountOf(deck)

Exclusive lists

  • a pile is an exclusive list of cards (D7): a card is in at most one list of the family, Pile and its sub-types. A deck is outside it.
  • the pile of a card: a read-only member of Card, declared by Pile's line (J39).
    • it's compiled as a getter, patched on after Pile's class: Pile.ownerOf(this)
    • set the pile of … is refused
  • Runtime: one WeakMap per family.
    • every write to a list's items goes through List.writeItems()
    • adding a held card moves it (J37)
    • scratch results (filter, copy, merge) own nothing (D8, J38)
  • Pile.spell's to move a card to a pile is now add + pause (D36).
