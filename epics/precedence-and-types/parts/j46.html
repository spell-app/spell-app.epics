move the card to the tableau => spellCore.move(card, tableau), a statement or a yes / no. At Priority.overridable (-1), so a project's own to move (a card) to (a pile) runs instead -- the frozen Solitaire fixture compiles unchanged. One consequence: where such a typed method refuses an argument (move card to deck, a deck isn't a pile), the built-in now reads it instead of a parse error.
