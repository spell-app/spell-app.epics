Chose: an exclusive list holds each item once -- add the card to the pile when the pile has it moves it to the end (addAtPosition() drops the old place first). As the old to move did (remove from its pile, then add).

  • Other option: allow duplicates within one list -- but then 'in at most one list' still lets a pile hold a card twice, and the pile of a card would survive removing one copy.
  • setItem() (reverse, shuffle) may hold one twice for a moment; owners are worked out from what's left, so they end right.
