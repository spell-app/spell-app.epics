Chose scope.couldBeA(actual, wanted): a call is rejected only when the argument's datatype is known and NEITHER type is the other or a sub-type of it -- a Deck for a Pile, text for a number, a Chip for a Card. A super-type fits (a list may hold a Pile, a number may be an integer, a thing may be a Card), as does nothing, an unknown type, or a chain with a stub or an unfound super-type. Why: D6 says reject only on a CONFIDENT mismatch, and a super-typed value may well be the sub-type.

  • chosen: unrelated types only
  • other: strict -- the argument must be the parameter type or a sub-type (rejects list for Pile)
