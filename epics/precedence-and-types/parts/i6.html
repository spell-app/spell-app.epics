A method whose call starts with its object can't be parsed: the whole project fails to parse.

  • its call rule, {thisArg:expression} flips, is aliased expression
  • so SpellParser.getNamesForRule() registers it as an operand, and throws (the left recursion guard, P3)
  • true of to (a card) flips since P3; paren-free signatures make it easier to write
  • logged in SUSPECTED-BUGS (spell)
  • the fix: register such a call as a statement only, or as an expression_suffix
  • not fixed in P5: no example does it
