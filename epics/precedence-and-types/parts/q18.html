From the review of J25. The stop list (of the a an is has ... as with whose ...) was a hedge: it decides where a multi-word member name ENDS when nothing else does.

  • Reads may not need it: the … of ends at of, and a read now resolves its words against the members the type declares (longest declared match).
  • Declarations still need SOME end: a card has short rank as text -- without as as a stop word, the name would swallow as text; the short rank of a card is: ends at of anyway.
  • Options: (A) keep it as is; (B) shrink it to the words a declaration's own syntax uses next (of as is :), resolving reads by declared members only; (C) no list: declarations end at the next literal word of their own rule.
