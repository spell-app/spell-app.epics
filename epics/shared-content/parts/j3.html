The goals tools used to find their goals folder by sitting inside it. Now they're in packages/docs/tools/goals, so targets.js reads GOALS_DIR when set, else <root>/goals; spell goals sets GOALS_DIR to the folder it found (the nearest goals.preferences.json5). The /goals skills' goals.sh no longer searches: it always uses its checkout's goals/.

  • a goals folder outside the checkout works through spell goals or GOALS_DIR, not through the skills' script
