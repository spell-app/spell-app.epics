The plan said the content stays as written, but the row under a notebook's tabs was a <menu>, which can't scroll: it was cut off at narrow widths. <toolbar> IS a menu that scrolls sideways with ‹ › keys, so each notebook's <menu class="ui-docs-section-tabs"> became <toolbar class="ui-docs-section-tabs">: a one-word change in 351 notebooks, and the TabBar notes' mention in 93. The other way, giving every <ui-menu> the toolbar's scrolling, would have moved the toolbar's whole scroll machinery into the menu.

The same move fixed the selected tab and the stray circle: the panel's two concave corners were drawn by the menu box's ::before, which is also the segmented thumb's, so the thumb was never drawn and its corner showed as a circle by the chosen tab. The corners now hang off the toolbar's box (docs-page.css), and the thumb shows.
