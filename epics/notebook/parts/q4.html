Dropping the cell tools leaves moving, adding and deleting cells to the editor's drag and drop (PartEdits): the app area, the Views tree and the palette.

  • It can't reach a cell today:
    • it moves only elements the running program drew, which carry a mark saying where they're written
    • a notebook's cells are drawn by a second renderer of its own, one compiled chunk each, with no marks
    • the running program is drawn too, but hidden
  • So the page has to be drawn by the running program itself, in the app area, and redrawn by the editor's edit-without-redraw (LiveEdit).
    • The notebook's own renderer goes (compileChunk(), its redraws and its keys: about 500 lines).
    • Add below becomes the palette's job; the selected cell becomes the Inspector's selection.
    • A run of prose has no tag to grab. The <markdown> block it reads as would carry a mark naming its text, so prose moves too.
    • To check: an example's code editor keeps its text and cursor while its example redraws.
 One drawing of the page; drag and drop, the Inspector and the palette work on it as on any app. Its cells can't be dragged: moving one means editing its code. A notebook page is the app, drawn like every other one; what moves things in an app moves its cells.
