The plan named one way: the twin making each section or example a generated view. P4 did it a smaller way, with nothing new in the spellx, the twin or the views.

  • The compiler writes each example as core.drawPart(this, () => h("spell-example", ...)): a memo of its own inside the page's drawing.
  • LiveEdit finds the examples that changed by their code, and the core draws only those again, from the new program.
  • Why not views: an example lifted into a view of its own would read its ... as the view, not the page; its name would need to be unique across the project (Q7); and the Views tree, palette and twin would show them.
