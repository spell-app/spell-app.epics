The Counter notebook is the one notebook that isn't a Spell UI component: a small page with a counter on it, written to show what a notebook is. It has to live somewhere once @spell/notebooks goes. Tests and prose name it (NotebookPageCells.test.ts, sideBarEdits.test.ts, the spellx README).

  • It can't go in the site: it isn't a component, and <counter> is a view of its own.
  • It can't join @spell/examples/Counter: that project also has a Counter.spellml and a view of that name.
  • A new example project, @spell/examples/CounterNotebook, its one file unchanged.
  • One more card in the home page's Examples row.
  • The two tests and the README point at the new address.
