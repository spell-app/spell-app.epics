A view defined inside a page (Q6) is lifted out to just before the page, so as built it would be an ordinary view of the project: usable from any file. 

<spell-page "counter page">
	<view counter> ... </view>
	<counter/>                     -- here, surely
</spell-page>

<spell-page "other page">
	<counter/>                     -- and here?
</spell-page>
 It's a view like any other, only written where it's first used; nothing new in the compiler. Local to the page: two pages can each have their own <counter>; the compiler needs scoped tag names. Writing a view inside a page is a convenience of placement, not a new kind of view.
