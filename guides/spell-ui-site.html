Spell UI Docs Site

Click a link in the Spell UI docs and only the page's main changes: the nav, the theme and the look controls never redraw. A first load shows nothing until every element is ready, then shows it all at once: a layout shift of 0.0002, measured locally, at about 250ms.

That's the docs site since the ui-docs-rework epic. Here's how it's put together, and why.

  • The pages: the shared ui/ folder.
  • Laid over them: the bundle and data from Spell UI's site/ folder.
  • Served at /components/ui/.
Epic page: epic ui-docs-rework Written for: anyone changing the site's chrome, a page, or the Spell theme Status: built, merged into main
  • The site wears a spell theme, made from the brand: the Spell UI docs' default.
    • The Spell hat is every site's favicon.
    • The other doc sites (docs, epic pages, saga pages) wore the theme too, until epic design-system moved them to spell-brand.
  • The chrome is written ONCE (layout.html).
    • A page's file is its <head> and one <main>.
    • Clicks swap only the main.
  • The page shows only once the layout, the theme and every element are ready.
    • No flash, no jump: CLS 0.0002, measured locally, shown at ~250ms.
  • Content is nested <ui-section>s with nested ids (#examples-types-emphasis).
    • They fold, and their titles stick under the sticky tabs.
    • A deep link lands with its sections unfolded.
  • The frame around the page:
    • Left: the brand nav panel, with the logo and the site search.
    • Right: the repo's site header (docked), the look controls, and "On this page".
    • No top bar on wide screens.
  • spell.css: a theme of Spell UI's own (listed in UI.themes.own).
    • Applied on top of classic, like every theme.
    • Its tokens come from the brand's tokens/.
  • Its colours:
    • Spell Purple primary: violet-600 in light, lilac in dark.
    • An aubergine dark mode, not black.
    • Ivory as the brown hue, lavender for info.
  • Fonts:
    • Headings: 'Spell Serif' is the INSTALLED Palatino family only (macOS / iOS, Windows, most Linux); elsewhere the generic serif.
    • Body: the system sans.
    • No font files ship: Owen's call (the brand's P052 fallback was ~540 KB, and AGPL).
  • Shape: pill buttons and labels, 12px inputs, 16px cards, 22px modals, layered soft shadows, round checkboxes.
  • From the interfaces.dev cheat sheet: 3px focus ring, buttons press to 0.97, no transitions while the scheme switches, tabular numbers, balanced headings, font smoothing, image outlines, scroll-margin-top.
  • It's the default on the Spell UI docs (ThemePreference).
    • The other docs' bundle (spell-ui.entry.js) applies spell-brand instead, since epic design-system.
    • The theme menu lists Spell, Spell Brand, Plain, Classic, then Fomantic's.
  • A plain left click on a link to a page of the site, or the nav's cancelable ui-navigate, starts a swap.
    • The router fetches the page (UI.sources, cached) and loads the families it uses.
    • Then it points a <ui-include select="main#main" page-styles> at it, inside a View Transition.
  • pushState keeps the page's real URL: reload, deep links, back / forward and live reload all work as they do on plain pages.
  • After a swap, these follow the new page:
    • the title, the nav's current row, "On this page"
    • the docked site header (spell-site:page), live reload's file
    • the page's inline scripts, which re-run
    • the flyout closes, focus moves to main, the hash lands
  • <ui-include>'s new ui-insert event hands over the fragment before it goes in.
    • The router keeps each example's authored markup (ExampleSource.keep()).
    • It makes every relative URL absolute.
  • Anything that fails falls back to a full load.
  • yarn site:new <tag|page> in spell-ui; rules in PAGES.md.
  • The body is ONE <main id="main" class="site-main" data-toc-header="...">.
  • Styles go in main or site.css, never <head>: a swap drops the head.

What sits around the page depends on the window's width:

Width Left Top Right
1200px+ nav panel: lockup, search, folding groups nothing docked site header, look controls, "On this page"
992-1199px nav panel slim bar: docked site header, look controls hidden
below 992px panel in the flyout slim bar: menu, search, brand, look controls (site header below 768px: in the flyout) hidden
  • <ui-docs-nav>: the brand panel, in the Color Set Chooser's look.
    • An ivory card, its header band holding the Spell lockup and the search field.
    • Folding group bands, and nav rows with a lavender current row.
    • Folds are remembered.
  • <spell-site-header docked>: the repo-wide bar as a compact row (no fixed bar, no crumbs, no scheme button).
  • The right column stays on every tab (the API tab too): the page's width never jumps.
  • Every tab's content is nested <ui-section sticky collapsible dividing>s, an example inside its own section.
  • Ids nest: <tab>-<section>-<example>.
    • yarn site:sections writes them INTO the markup. It's idempotent, and site:new, site:index and site:kitchen run it.
    • site:check fails on an id that doesn't follow its nesting.
  • A hash picks its tab, unfolds the sections around the target, and lands below the stuck titles (SiteSections.ts).
    • Old hashes (#types) still land, and the URL is rewritten.
  • The tabs bar sticks. Top-level titles stick below it, nested ones below their parents.
    • Folds start open, and are remembered per page.
  • "On this page" lists the nested sections.

  • One page per family (a folder: one chunk, one barrel) holds its tags together.
    • Button covers <ui-button>, <ui-buttons>, <ui-or>.
  • Tags that stand alone get their OWN page: ui-radio, ui-textarea, and the 12 Parts tags other than ui-header. 80 pages in all.
    • pages.json5's pages map names them.
    • The nav, search, index and kitchen sink follow its hrefs.
  • Downloads don't change: families still load as one chunk each.
  • <ui-docs-search>, in the nav panel's header: one field that jumps anywhere, and still filters the nav list.
  • Its groups:
    • On this page, read live
    • Components: every tag, by name, aka, topic
    • Pages
    • Sections: every page's, from the shared search.json5, built by yarn site:data
    • Attributes
  • / or ⌘K focuses it from anywhere; on phones it opens the flyout.
    • Arrows, Enter, and a staged Escape.
    • Results are plain links: the router swaps the page.
  • <ui-docs-themes>, two buttons:
    • a sun / moon showing the scheme in use: a click flips it, animated
    • a palette, opening a small overlay: the theme list and "Match system"
  • ONE scheme for every site: localStorage["spell-site:scheme"].
    • light, dark, or absent for the OS's.
    • Applied as ui-light / ui-dark AND color-scheme on <html>.
    • The docs' site header flips the same way.
  • The brand's app icon: a white hat on aubergine, 22.5% corners. yarn favicon in src/server generates it.
  • Who puts it on a page:
    • the page server, into every page it serves
    • <spell-site-header>, on file://
    • the app links its own copy
  • Real URLs with the layout injected, over a shell page with a route: every link, reload and live reload keeps working (Q1).
  • <ui-root display="when-ready"> over <ui-root hidden>, skeletons or a cloak: it was already built, and a failed script never leaves a blank page (Q2).
  • Family pages, with own pages for tags that stand alone (Q3, see the epic page's P7).
  • The site header docked in the right column, not a top bar (Q4).
  • Ids in the markup, not worked out at runtime: stable, grep-able, linkable.
  • Every choice made without Owen is a judgement call in the epic page (J1-J51).
  • A page opened from disk, or with scripts off, shows its main alone (no chrome).
  • A page's <head> styles and scripts don't follow a swap, and inline scripts in main re-run on every visit.
  • Where no Palatino-family face is installed (some Linux, Android), headings fall back to the generic serif.
  • On phones the stuck stack (top bar, tabs, two titles) takes about a fifth of the screen.
