As built, when it was parked. Every piece named here is in the zip now, unless it says it stayed.

  • The saga pages' folder (goals/) holds the preferences, a home page of every set, and a folder per set.
  • A SET (spell) has a contents page: its topics' cards, the next three months, "How we work".
  • A TOPIC has two files, with the same ids:
    • <topic>.html, the page for people, and the source of truth
    • <topic>.md, the same topic as a brief for agents
  • A topic's status: draft, in dialog, agreed (ready for agents), building, shipped.
  • A TARGET names a place: [set/]topic[/anchor]. motivation/G1 is item G1 on the active set's motivation page; spell/arc a section.

Each page's items, by kind. Ids never change and are never reused; a closed item stays, struck through.

id kind means
G1 goal something we want, on a horizon: now → December 2026, 2027, someday
I1 idea a way we might get there; not decided
Q1 question waiting on a conversation, with Claude's suggestion folded in it
R1 risk what's likely to bite us
D1 decision settled, with the why; agents don't relitigate it
W1 work a piece of work an agent, or you, can pick up
T1 thought your note, waiting to be worked in; digested ones stay, with what came of them
  1. Jot thoughts any time. The bubble beside any section or item, or /goals-thought motivation/G1: saved on the page, marked new.
  2. Talk it through. The Talk button, or /goals motivation/G1: Claude presents it, then asks one or two questions at a time, each with a suggestion.
  3. Write it down at once. After every answer: a decision added, the question closed, a history line, the .md brought in step.
  4. Digest the thoughts. The Update button, or /goals-update motivation: each thought becomes edits, decisions or questions, and is marked digested.
  5. Hand it off. Agreed work goes to agents, with the topic's .md as their brief; what they find comes back as history.

Five, linked into the repo's skills folder. Each ran the tool through one script, goals.sh.

  • /goals [target] | sets | use <set>: the talk-through.
    • It opens the page, presents it, asks, and writes each answer down before the next question.
    • It hands ready work to agents, and suggests /epic for anything with phases.
  • /goals-thought [target] [text]: saves your words verbatim, no dialog.
  • /goals-update [target]: works each new thought in, smallest change first, and marks it digested with what came of it. It never contradicts a decision silently: a thought against one becomes a question naming it.
  • /goals-open [target]: the page in a new browser window, starting the page server.
  • /goals-open-vs [target]: the page in VS Code's side bar, beside the code.

spell dev goals <verb> (and the older spell goals), from anywhere: it found the nearest goals folder. Every edit took the page's lock, so parallel agents queued instead of clobbering each other.
