Spell and AT Protocol Spaces

Communities will run themselves in Spell, in spaces they can reshape. AT Protocol will keep their data, so Spell doesn't have to reinvent hosting.

Written for: anyone working on or reviewing the epic atmosphere Status: mostly built the design; what's built is in Spell Hosts Your Data. Real mail, a public host and the phone app are still to come Researched on: 2026-11-01, by reading the specs. Nothing here has been run (see Sources)

Built so far: Spell's own server, shaped like a Space: project data folders in spaces, sign-in by an emailed code, the cloud line, takedowns and backups, in Spell Hosts Your Data. This page is the design for the org spaces still to come.

Spell Lexicon What to do about it
a recipe is a thing A record, named like space.spell.x.recipe A name belongs to whoever controls its domain, and spell.space is ours: the folder's address, reversed, then the type, space.spell.owen.recipes.recipe; a part a domain can't spell (events_team) becomes a hyphen
Text, a whole number, yes or no, date, list, one of String, integer, boolean, datetime, array, enum Direct; must not be empty is required, a length in letters one in graphemes, and a field's words are its description
A decimal number None: floats are banned Kept as text, "12.50", its description saying it's a decimal, and read back as a number in Spell; the editor warns on a published type. A whole number is an integer
A thing inside a thing, saved once, then referenced A nested object, or an at:// link Each record is capped at 1 MiB, and whole-then-reference has no direct equivalent
Rename a field, upgrade on read Not allowed once published: new fields must be optional, with no renames or type changes Field names freeze only on publishing: the editor warns at a published type's rename, and a version line only changes how old records read in Spell. A real change needs a new name
What it is Where it lives Its versions
A file: Spell code, a document, a picture A blob, and one record with its name, type and size, pointing to that blob Saved copies, kept as the org's policy says
The file you're editing A draft, saved over and over, in that team or project's private space None: it's replaced each time
Data in an app, e.g. messages, comments, sign-ups One record per thing, with a schema generated from the app's declarations None: sign-ups and posts just change
An app many orgs use One shared copy, with a record in each org's space pointing to one version Each published version

What Apple's rules ask of it
Apple's rule What it means for Spell
More than "a repackaged website" Real alerts, sign-in and a list of your orgs, not just a wrapper round the pages
User-generated content needs filtering, reporting, blocking and published contact details These have to exist before the first submission. Managers answer the reports, and the contact details are Spell's, as the app's publisher
HTML5 and JavaScript mini apps are allowed, and you're responsible for them Spell programs can run in it, but people's programs become Spell's problem with Apple
