Outline Spell

a card is a thing, then - rank is a number indented under it: that's Spell written as an OUTLINE. It compiles to the same javascript, character for character, as the sentences it stands for.

Sentence style says the subject on every line (a card has a suit as ...); the outline names it once, in a type's heading line, then lists what's true of it as bullets.

  • Words in quotes teach spell new language.
  • It's for people who glaze over at spell's "reads like a novel" sentence style.
  • Lauren's sketch of a card and a deck is the model.
