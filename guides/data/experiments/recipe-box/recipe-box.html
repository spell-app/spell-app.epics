The Recipe Box

Recipes on a real local server. Every change shows at once and is saved in the background, and nothing on the page knows which kind of server it's talking to.

  1. Start the servers, at the kit's root, and leave them running: 
    yarn tsx scripts/recipe-servers.ts
    They keep their recipes in memory, so restarting them starts over.
  2. Add a recipe, or change one. "saving…" then "saved" shows at the top.
  3. Open this page in a second window. What you change in one shows in the other within 5 seconds.
  4. For a clash, press Someone else renames it, then change that recipe's title within 5 seconds: yours is kept, and a notice holds what they wrote, with Use theirs. Change its notes instead, and both stand with no notice.
  5. Switch to GraphQL: change back-end="rest" to back-end="graphql" in this page, or open this address. It's the same recipes. GraphQL here has no versions, so a clash has no notice: the last save wins that field.
  6. Stop the servers and make a change. It stays on the page, says it's trying again, and goes through once they're back, even after a reload.

Last checked 2026-10-31 against branch data.
