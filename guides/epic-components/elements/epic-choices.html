<epic-choices>

A set of option cards (<epic-option>s): the options a question offers, or the options any item's text weighs. While open it draws the cards side by side; once answered, it folds them under a Choices heading, the chosen one marked.

For anyone reading or writing a plan doc's markup, or changing this element. built in packages/epics/components/epic-choices/. Its vocabulary (every table below comes from it): packages/epics/components/epic-choices/EpicChoices.en.ts; the markup rules: packages/epics/src/tool/PLAN-DOC.md.

Live: the real element, drawn by the epics pack this page loads. Click a line to unfold it. The review buttons don't show here: they show only on a plan doc being reviewed.

Its parents:

Anywhere prose goes (it's a flow element), so on any item kind:

  • <epic-item>: a question's own set, last in its text; or the options a judgement call or issue weighs. An item may hold several sets.
  • <epic-reply> and <epic-more>: options a reply or More Details weighs (test V1 of the epic-components epic has a reply ending in one)
  • <epic-version>: as it was, in an Original Discussion

A slot is a place in the drawing that a child goes to: a child with slot="name" goes to that name's place, every other child to the default one (shown as default).

SlotWhat goes there
defaultThe options.

A part is a piece of what the element draws in its shadow root (its own hidden markup), named so a stylesheet can reach it: epic-choices::part(header).

PartWhat it is
baseThe cards side by side; answered, the folded Choices aside.
toggleAnswered: the Choices <button> that folds the options.
panelsAnswered: the box of option panels, hidden while folded.

A state is a word the element sets on itself while something is true, for styling: epic-choices:state(open). Never written in the file.

StateWhen
answeredIts question is answered (chosen, or answered on its item): folded.
openAnswered, and its options unfolded.

The colours mean the same on every doc page: the whole scheme is on Plan Doc Colours (its source: templates/epics/plan-doc.md, "Colours").

  • Open (no chosen): the cards side by side, as many columns as fit. Each card folds by its header, and starts open.
  • Answered (chosen, or answered on its item): folded under a Choices heading on a warm aside fill; a click on it unfolds the options as panels. The chosen panel has a check before its letter and green text, and is the only one open.
  • While the page is reviewed, each card gets a Choose pill: the fill rule in green, dashed until sent, outlined once sent, solid on the chosen option (the pick applied). See <epic-option>.
  • Any command taking HTML (add --details, decide --details, details --file): an <epic-choices> is kept; an old option grid (ui-grid.spell-pros-cons, labels A · Title (recommended)) is turned into one on the way in (IncomingHtml)
  • plan-doc decide <name> <Q id> "answer" --option B: sets chosen on the question's set; a question has one answer, so no other set stays chosen
  • plan-doc inbox <name> apply, for Owen's pick: sets chosen on THAT set (picks name a set by its position in the item); a question is answered with the option, any other item approved with it
  • details --file on an answered question keeps the chosen letter when the new options still have it
  • older docs: the converter, from a question's own option grid
