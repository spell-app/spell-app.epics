<epic-version>

One earlier version of an item's text, inside its <epic-original>. It draws a small heading, "As first written" or "As of <date>", then the text as it was.

For anyone reading or writing a plan doc's markup, or changing this element. Its vocabulary (every name it uses): packages/epics/components/epic-original/EpicVersion.en.ts. Its component: packages/epics/components/epic-original/EpicVersion.tsx, with its .css and .test.tsx beside it.

Unfold each Original Discussion. The first holds ONE version, a real one (epic skillz, Q14), so it has no heading. The second holds two: "As first written", then "As of 10/4/26 20:49".

No live plan doc holds two versions yet; 75 hold one (epics/*/parts/).

Its parents:

  • <epic-original>: one or more, oldest first

Its children:

  • <epic-question>: at most one, a question's text as first asked, moved here by a rewrite
  • prose: the text, as it was
  • <epic-answer>: at most one, its answer card as it was
  • <epic-reply>: its replies, as they were

The tool writes the cards it moves here as the prose they said (Answer · Chrome, B · Chrome (recommended), chosen). The converter kept a few hand-written cards as <epic-answer> / <epic-reply> instead.

Name Values Meaning Required
as-of a time, YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM (2026-10-04 20:49) When it was replaced: drawn As of 10/4/26 20:49. Absent on the first version, as first written. no

A slot is where the element's own children show.

Slot Holds
(default) The text, as it was.

A part is a piece of the drawing a page's CSS can reach.

Part Is
base The version.
heading As first written / As of <as-of>: none on a lone first version.
body The text, as it was.
  • Its heading: small, muted, in capitals (an eyebrow over the text). No colour of its own.
  • "As first written" shows only once there's a second version: a lone version needs no heading. It follows its parent live: when a second version arrives, the first gets its heading.
  • Dates are written 2026-10-04 20:49 and drawn 10/4/26 20:49 (PlanDates).
  • It doesn't fold: its <epic-original> does.
  • Written with its <epic-original>: details --file, decide again, details --more again, original <name> <id> --file.
  • The first version is undated; each later one gets as-of the time it was replaced (now, or original --as-of), kept in date order.
  • Ids inside it become data-original-id (PlanItem.stripIds()).
