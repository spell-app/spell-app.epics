<epic-summary>

<epic-summary> is what the epic is, in two sentences, at the top of the Overview. It draws its text as a LEDE: the opening paragraph, a size up, in the headings' ink.

Written for: anyone reading or writing a plan doc's markup, or changing this element Status: built in packages/epics/components/epic-summary/ Its vocabulary (attributes, slots, parts, children): packages/epics/components/epic-summary/EpicSummary.en.ts; the component: packages/epics/components/epic-summary/EpicSummary.tsx Plan docs drawn by Solid web components (<epic-page>, <epic-item> ...) in a new package, packages/epics, loaded by <ui-root> as a component pack, so a doc holds only its data and prose. base The lede.
  • A paragraph in the body face, 17px, in the headings' ink, at most 72 characters wide.
  • The text stays the page's own: find-in-page and links in it work as anywhere else.
  • No colour, no fold: it shows whenever the Overview is open.
