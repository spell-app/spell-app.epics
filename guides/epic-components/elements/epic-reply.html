Its parents:

  • <epic-item>: after its answer and More Details, oldest first
  • <epic-version>: replies as they were, in an Original Discussion

Its children are prose, and may hold the prose elements, option cards (<epic-choices>) among them.

Its children:

Child How many What it holds
prose any number The reply.
Name Values Meaning Required
from text Who wrote it (Claude, Owen).
at a time, 2026-10-06 23:55 When: 2026-10-06 23:55, drawn 10/6/26 23:55 (PlanDates).
re text What it's about (as built (Doc Review), revisit soon).

A slot is a place in the drawing that a child goes to: a child with slot="name" goes to that name's place, every other child to the default one (shown as default).

Slot What goes there
default The reply.

A part is a piece of what the element draws in its shadow root (its own hidden markup), named so a stylesheet can reach it: epic-reply::part(header).

Part What it is
base The card: Claude's violet, Owen's ivory (from).
header Its heading band, a flex row: the fold chevron, who left, date right; a click folds it.
toggle The fold chevron, a <button>, first in the band; only with a body.
who The heading's left: <from> · re: <re>; wraps within itself.
date The heading's right: at, 10/6/26 23:55, on the top line; never wraps.
body The reply; not drawn when empty; hidden (until-found) while folded.

A state is a word the element sets on itself while something is true, for styling: epic-reply:state(open). Never written in the file.

State When
open Unfolded: it starts so (page state, never written).

The colours mean the same on every doc page: the whole scheme is on Plan Doc Colours (its source: plan-doc.md, "Colours").

  • Violet when from="Claude" (Claude's voice), ivory when from="Owen" (Owen's, as his answer cards and note box).
  • The band: Claude · re: as built (Doc Review) on the left, free to wrap; the date (10/6/26 23:55) on the right of the top line, never wrapping.

It folds by its heading band: a click anywhere on the band (but on a link or a button in it) folds or unfolds it, the chevron first in the band. It starts open, since it's the text being read; folding is the reader's own and never written into the file. Folded text is still found by the browser's find-in-page, which unfolds it.
