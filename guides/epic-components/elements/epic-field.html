<epic-field>

<epic-field> is one named field of a phase: Symptom, Changes, Goal, Done, Files, Verify or To review. It draws the field's icon and its bold label (Symptom:) before its prose. In any other prose, with label instead of name, it's a LABELLED BLOCK: Where: and its text, no icon.

<epic-phase> as name="symptom" one at most Symptom: what's wrong today, one line.
<epic-phase> as name="changes" one at most Changes: what changes, two or three lines.
<epic-phase> as name="goal" one at most Goal: a bullet per outcome.
<epic-phase> as name="done" one at most Done: what was built, once done.
<epic-phase> as name="files" one at most Files: what changes.
<epic-phase> as name="verify" one at most Verify: how we know it worked.
<epic-phase> as name="to-review" one at most To review: the items added during it still open and not reviewed (written by the script).

It's a PROSE element (flow): it goes anywhere prose goes, inside <epic-overview>, <epic-summary>, <epic-prompt>, <epic-section>, <epic-field>, <epic-updated>, <epic-item>, <epic-question>, <epic-option>, <epic-answer>, <epic-more>, <epic-reply>, <epic-status>, <epic-version>, <epic-commit>, <epic-event>, <epic-update>, <epic-net-effect>, <epic-note>, <epic-aside>, <epic-code>.

Prose: text, a <p>, a list. A first paragraph runs on after the label; lists and later paragraphs go below it.

Child Where How many What it is
prose any number The field's prose.
Name Values Meaning Required
name symptom, changes, goal, done, files, verify, to-review A phase's field (every field in a phase has one): symptom (one line), changes (two or three), goal (a bullet per outcome), done (what was built), files, verify (both hidden until the Phases title's toggles), to-review (the script's).
label text A labelled block's label, WITHOUT its colon (drawn): Where, What should happen, Step. For a field in prose, never in a phase: a field has name or label.

Where its children show: a child goes in a slot by its slot attribute.

Slot What goes in it
(default) The field's prose.

Pieces of what it draws, which a page's CSS can style from outside with ::part(name).

Part What it is
base The field: its icon column, then its label and prose.
icon Its icon, centred on its first line: none on a labelled block.
label Its label: Symptom:, Where: ...
  • The icon, grey, centred on the first line: Symptom an exclamation circle, Changes a magic wand, Goal a bullseye, Done a check circle, Files a folder, Verify a flask, To review a checklist.
  • Files and Verify are hidden until the Phases title's toggles show them.
  • To review: each #link shows as a chip in its item's state colour (the colour scheme): red needs Owen, blue Claude is working on it, yellow open, green decided or done, grey no longer relevant.
  • A labelled block: no icon column, a paragraph's spacing.
  • No fold: it shows whenever its phase (or item) is open.
  • spell dev plan-doc add-phase: Symptom, Changes, then Goal, Files, Verify (without --symptom / --changes: Goal, Files, Verify); a field not given reads TBD.
  • spell dev plan-doc phase-body <name> <N> --goal ...: sets a field, in its place; "" removes it.
  • spell dev plan-doc phase <name> <N> done --done "...": the Done field.
  • To review: written by the tool on every edit, LAST in the phase: the items added while it was active, still open and not reviewed.
  • Labelled blocks: by hand, in an item's text (add --details, details --file); spell dev plan-doc convert's second pass turned <p><b>Where:</b> ... into them.
