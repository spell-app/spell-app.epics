<epic-event>

One line of a plan doc's log. It draws an icon and the time, to the minute, then what happened.

Written for: anyone reading or writing a plan doc's markup, or changing this element Its vocabulary (every name it uses): packages/epics/components/epic-event/EpicEvent.en.ts Its component: packages/epics/components/epic-event/EpicEvent.tsx, with its .css and .test.tsx beside it

The first two are real lines from this epic's log. The third is given icon="flag" and a date alone, to show both.

In the doc they sit in <epic-section id="log" kind="log">; a split doc keeps them in that part file.

Name Values Meaning Required
at local time with its offset, 2026-10-06T08:12-04:00; a bare date works too When it happened. Drawn 2026-10-06 08:12. yes
icon a Spell UI icon name Its icon. Default pen to square: written only when it's another. no

A slot is where the element's own children show.

Slot Holds
(default) What happened, one line.

A part is a piece of the drawing a page's CSS can reach.

Part Is
base The line: icon, time, text.
icon Its icon, centred on its first line.
time Its time, to the minute (a <time datetime>).
  • The icon in a column of its own, muted, centred on the first line.
  • The time in mono, muted, in its own format, 2026-10-06 08:12. It's the ONE element that doesn't draw dates as 10/6/26 08:12 (PlanDates).
  • A hairline between lines. The text wraps beside the time; too narrow, it wraps under it.
  • No state colour, and it doesn't fold (the log section does).
  • spell dev plan-doc log <name> "<text>": a line of your own.
  • Every command that changes the doc writes one: phase (P2 active), add-phase, phase-body, estimate, updated, decide, close / reopen, cancel, review, defer, queue / unqueue, details, original, split / join, bedtime, inbox apply ...
  • spell dev plan-doc new: the template's first line, plan doc created.
  • The time is now, local, with its offset (PlanTime.isoMinutes()).
  • The converter turned the old log (a feed of ui-events, or a ul.plan-log) into these (LogConverter).
