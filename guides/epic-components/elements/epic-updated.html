<epic-updated>

<epic-updated> is one change to a phase's plan, after Owen's feedback or something found while building. It draws a dashed orange FENCE (a box around it) headed Updated and its date, with what changed under it.

Written for: anyone reading or writing a plan doc's markup, or changing this element.
Status: built, in the epic-phase folder of the epics package.
Its vocabulary (attributes, slots, parts, children): EpicUpdated.en.ts.
The component: EpicUpdated.tsx.

Where its children show: a child goes in a slot by its slot attribute.

Slot What goes in it
(default) What changed in the plan, and why.

Pieces of what it draws, which a page's CSS can style from outside with ::part(name).

Part What it is
base The fence: a dashed orange box.
toggle The fold chevron, a <button>, first: its heading row folds what changed.
icon Its icon, centred on its first line.
label Updated, then its time.
body What changed, under the heading; hidden (until-found) while folded.

Custom states: CSS can match them with epic-updated:state(name).

State When
:state(open) Unfolded: it starts so (page state, never written).
  • The fence: a dashed ORANGE box on a faint orange wash, its icon orange: orange means changed since you looked (the colour scheme).
  • Its heading: the fold chevron, the icon, Updated, the time and during P6 in small mono, muted.
  • A Plan changes copy (of): the same, led by a bold P3 link to the phase it changes.
  • Folding: open to start with; a click on its heading folds what changed.
    • Folded text is still found by find-in-page.
    • Whether it's open is the reader's choice, never written in the doc.
  • spell dev plan-doc updated <name> <N> "<html>" (or --file <path>): one line in phase N. Its phase is the phase under way then.
  • The Plan changes copies (slot="changes", of): the tool rewrites them on every edit, one per line of each phase still to do; never written by hand.
  • spell dev plan-doc add-phase ... --before N: renumbers the phases that move, in both attributes (phase, of).
  • spell dev plan-doc convert: from an old phase's Updated field, one per dated line.
