<epic-phase>

<epic-phase> is one phase of the plan, in the Phases section. Its attributes hold its id, title, status and estimate; its children are its fields, plan changes and commits. It draws the title line (P2 · Title) with its status icon and the estimate, and folds.

Written for: anyone reading or writing a plan doc's markup, or changing this element.
Status: built in packages/epics/components/epic-phase/.
Its vocabulary (attributes, slots, parts, children): packages/epics/components/epic-phase/EpicPhase.en.ts; the component: packages/epics/components/epic-phase/EpicPhase.tsx.

<epic-section> only when its kind is phases any number The phases, in order.

In THIS order, always: the tool puts each child where it belongs, and the check refuses another order.

Child Where How many What it is
prose slot="title" one at most A title with markup.
<epic-field name="symptom"> one at most Symptom: what's wrong today, one line.
<epic-field name="changes"> one at most Changes: what changes, two or three lines.
<epic-updated> any number Changes to the phase's plan, one dated line each, oldest first.
<epic-field name="goal"> one at most Goal: a bullet per outcome.
<epic-field name="done"> one at most Done: what was built, once done.
<epic-commit> any number Its commits, oldest first.
<epic-field name="files"> one at most Files: what changes.
<epic-field name="verify"> one at most Verify: how we know it worked.
<epic-field name="to-review"> one at most To review: the items added during it still open and not reviewed (written by the script).
Name Values Meaning Required
id
JS property: epicId 		text: phase id p1, p2 ...: shown as P2 · <title>; links use it (#p2). yes
title
JS property: epicTitle 		text Its short name, 2-4 words, WITHOUT its id (Saved Replies, drawn as P1 · Saved Replies). yes, or a slot="title" child
status todo, active, done todo, active (one at a time, blue: under way) or done (green). yes
estimate text Wall-clock time for Claude to do it, agents included, Owen's review not: 30m, 2h, 1h30m, 1-2h.
source text Its body's part file (parts/p2.html), loaded into its children when it opens.
part-ids text: ids The ids inside its part file: a link to one loads the body first, then lands.
commits boolean: there or not Its body lists commits (the page's git buttons).
open boolean: there or not Unfolded: PAGE state, never written in a doc (plan docs open folded). A click on the title, a link to it (or into it) and find-in-page set it; set it from code to open it.

Where its children show: a child goes in a slot by its slot attribute.

Slot What goes in it
(default) Its fields, plan updates and commits.
title A title with markup, in place of title.

Pieces of what it draws, which a page's CSS can style from outside with ::part(name).

Part What it is
base The element's box.
section The <ui-section> drawing the title bar and fold: its own parts through ::part() from inside.
body Around its children: sets --epic-stack for them.
note The Loads from parts/x.html ... line, when its part can't load.
status Its status icon, in its status's colour.

Custom states: CSS can match them with epic-phase:state(name).

State When
:state(open) Unfolded.
:state(loaded) Its source part is in.
:state(error) Its source part couldn't be loaded.
Event Cancelable When
ui-open yes About to unfold: its title was clicked. Cancel to stay folded. After the fact for a link.
ui-close yes About to fold: its title was clicked. Cancel to stay open.
ui-load The source part arrived and is in its children (as <ui-section source>'s).
ui-error yes The source part couldn't be loaded; its note says so unless cancelled.
  • The status icon, in the colour scheme: to do, an empty grey circle; under way, a half-filled BLUE circle (Claude is working on it); done, a GREEN circle with a check. A done phase's title reads quieter (muted).
  • The estimate as a badge at the title's end.
  • Its title bar on the row band, edge to edge, sticking under the Phases section's title while you read inside it.
  • Files and Verify stay hidden until the Phases title's toggles show them; commits until the page's git toggle does.
  • Folding: a click on the title folds it; on a plan doc it starts folded. With source, its body loads from its part file the first time it opens.
  • spell dev plan-doc add-phase <name> "Short Name" --symptom ... --changes ... [--goal ...] [--files ...] [--verify ...] [--estimate 1-2h] [--before N]: a new phase, todo; a field not given reads TBD. --before N inserts it as PN and renumbers the rest.
  • spell dev plan-doc phase-body <name> <N> --symptom ...: sets its fields.
  • spell dev plan-doc phase <name> <N> todo|active|done [--done ...]: its status, and its Done field.
  • spell dev plan-doc estimate <name> <N> "1-2h": its estimate (the Overview's total follows).
  • spell dev plan-doc updated <name> <N> "...": an <epic-updated> line.
  • spell dev plan-doc commit <name> <sha> --phase N, spell dev plan-doc commits --backfill: its commits, and commits.
  • Its To review field: rewritten by the tool on every edit.
  • spell dev plan-doc split: source and part-ids.
