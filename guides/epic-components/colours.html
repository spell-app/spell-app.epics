Epic Page Colours

Each colour means ONE thing on every doc page, so a page reads at a glance. The fill says how far along a button is.

Written for: anyone giving a colour to anything on a doc page Status: built decided in Q20 (Owen, 2026-10-08), built in P14 The table agents read: plan-doc.md, "Colours"
Colour Means For example
red needs Owen an open question's chip; an unreviewed judgement call or issue; the rail's count; an epic with errors
yellow open, still undecided (dark text on it) an open todo, caveat or test; a reviewed issue
blue do it now, or Claude is working on it Do Now, Revisit, Send everywhere; the Underway card; the active phase
green decided or done, however long ago Approve, Make Todo; a pick, the chosen option; a phase done; the Done card
orange ONLY changed since you looked, and warnings UPDATE labels, <epic-update> blocks and the pen on a changed phase's title; "nobody is listening"
violet Claude's voice his reply cards; "recommended", a thumbs-up with no word
ivory Owen's voice his note box, a marked note, his reply cards, the answer card; the rail
grey no longer relevant, inactive, not chosen a canceled item; buttons at rest; a phase to do; a future epic; a paused epic
  • No light blue and no light green: one shade per meaning.
  • Red and green are the pair colour-blind readers tell apart least: the chip's id, the icons and the words keep them readable.
  • A new element takes its colour from this table, never a colour that means something else.

On every button, pill and chip with a lifecycle: the review buttons, Send, the Choose pill, saga pages' topic labels.

Fill Means
grey outline available: nothing pressed
DASHED, in its colour Owen pressed it, not sent yet
OUTLINE, in its colour recorded (sent), or in progress
cleared handled: a review button is a grey outline again, and the id chip has the result
SOLID only on chips (an item's state), the step label's DONE, and a Choose pill once applied
  • It flips the older rule, where solid meant "not sent yet" (2026-10-06).
  • While Owen's mark is live, an item's id chip takes its chosen button's colour and fill.
  • Send never goes solid on a details page: the page can't tell when Claude is done with the answer.

An item's chip colour comes from its state, which the tool works out from its status and review marks on every edit. The FIRST row that applies wins.

state Colour When
progress blue Claude is working on it: an Underway card, or the spinner
old grey canceled: made moot, struck through
recent green decided or done, however long ago
open yellow work a review queued, not started
attention red an open question; an open judgement call or issue not reviewed, unless marked calm
recent green open, but settled by a review: approved as it stands, or made a todo
open yellow anything else open: todos, caveats, tests; a revisit Claude answered
  • Neither age nor a /bedtime run colours a chip any more: green stays green, and grey means only "no longer relevant".
  • The rail counts only attention items, in a red pill; none, no pill.
  • In CSS, two sets of tokens with the same values:
    • epic pages: <epic-page>'s --epic-state-*
    • the other doc pages: spell-doc.css's --spell-state-*
Page Colours
details pages recommended a violet thumbs-up; the chosen card green; Send blue, by the fill; a question's rail badge red (nothing yet), green (picked) or blue (Claude has more to do); the status label red, blue or green
saga pages items by state, as epic pages (the kind only in the id's letter); topic labels: draft grey, in dialog yellow, agreed green outline, building blue outline, shipped green
the Epics index in progress blue, errors red (every phase done, items need you), paused grey (untouched for 3 days), future grey, done green (Owen, 2026-10-10: no sleeping mark)
  • item chips and states: EpicItem.css, and <epic-page>'s tokens
  • review buttons, the note box: EpicReview.css
  • reply, answer and status cards: epic-answer/, epic-status/
  • the Choose pill and option cards: epic-choices/
  • the other doc pages: spell-doc.css, details.css, goals.css

Colours are drawn, never stored: a change recolours every doc with no doc edited.
  • Before, most colours meant several things. Orange alone meant about nine on an epic page: an item in progress, a choice not sent, Owen's note box, an UPDATE note, the active phase, a blocked agent ...
  • Buttons contradicted what they made: Make Todo was green, but the todo it made turned orange. Solid meant "not sent" on review buttons and "the current mark" in the note box.
  • Owen settled the scheme over four rounds on the colour-scheme page (Q20, 2026-10-08): one meaning per colour, and the fill for how far along.
  • Two later calls (J85, J86): decided things stay green however old, grey only for what no longer matters; review buttons clear once Claude has handled a mark, and the chip shows the result.
