The <brand-*> elements the copies are built from, each with a page in Spell UI's docs format: examples, usage, API and tokens.

Element What it is
<ui-panel> An inspector: a tinted box of fields under a header band, with sub-head bands that fold. Moved into Spell UI (was <ui-brand-panel>): its page is in Spell UI's docs.
<brand-field> One control with its label, a value readout, actions, an info tip, help and errors.
<brand-color> A square colour chip: its hex, OKLCH or name, a contrast mark, click to copy, a details tip.
<brand-color-set> A row or grid of chips, to show or to pick one from (keyboard included).
<brand-color-range> A 17-step ladder of shades from one base colour, as the Color Set Chooser makes it.
<brand-color-picker> Choose a colour by lightness and colourfulness for a hue, or type its hex or OKLCH.
<brand-logo> The Spell logo, outlined: the hat mark or a lockup, in one colour.
<brand-flourish> Page art: a generated swoop, blobs, curls, sparkle trail or rising wave behind the content.
<brand-blob> Page art: one soft lavender shape tucked into a corner.
<brand-composer> Write a spell and cast it: a serif text box, chips and a round cast button.
<brand-checklist> Round-marked lines: build progress (done, active, pending), or things to tick off.
<brand-phone> A phone frame with a status bar, around a live app preview.
